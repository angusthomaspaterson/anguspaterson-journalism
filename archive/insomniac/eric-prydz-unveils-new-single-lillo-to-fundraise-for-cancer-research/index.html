Eric Prydz Unveils New Single “Lillo” to Fundraise for Cancer Research

Angus Paterson · 28 December 2016 · Insomniac

It’s been an emotional month for Eric Prydz and his fans. Earlier in December, we heard the news that the Swedish superstar had planned to throw an exclusive show for James Lillo, a superfan suffering from stage-four cancer. However, the announcement was followed by the heartbreaking news shortly before the show that James had passed away. Deeply saddened by the news, Prydz got behind a raft of special fundraising initiatives on behalf of the Cancer Research Institute, and the latest initiative has just been properly unveiled.

On the most recent episode of his EPIC Radio show, Prydz announced he had just produced a track that he would now be releasing in memory of James and donating all the proceeds. The track is now out; listen below, and pick it up from Beatport to make your own donation to the cause.
