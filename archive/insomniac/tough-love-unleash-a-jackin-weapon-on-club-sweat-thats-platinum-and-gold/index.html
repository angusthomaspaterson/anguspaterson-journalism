Tough Love Unleash a Jackin’ Weapon on Club Sweat That’s “Platinum & Gold”

Angus Paterson · 7 June 2018 · Insomniac

Australia’s Sweat It Out! label is already a decade deep, and it has transformed into somewhat of a perspiration-soaked empire in that time. While they’ve got mega crossover stars like RÜFÜS DU SOL on their main label, it’s the sister Club Sweat imprint that is reserved for the grittier dancefloor effort. Today, we’re previewing its upcoming release from UK duo Tough Love.

“Platinum & Gold” is a record that lays out those vintage jackin’ vibes and locks them down real good. With the characteristic percussion that’s heavily loaded with hi-hats, plus a cheeky vocal sample talking tough alongside a deep bassline, things build to getting really, really wild in the second half.

Available June 8 via Club Sweat.
