Ralph Lawson Shows the Elrow Crew Some “Acid Love” Alongside Rui-Z

Angus Paterson · 25 October 2017 · Insomniac

While Ralph Lawson is more widely known for being the boss of the enduring, trailblazing 20/20 Visions label, next week the UK DJ/producer is showing some love to the Elrow family. Some Acid Love, to be precise, a four-track EP that sees Lawson teaming with Rui-Z. Ahead of the EP’s release next week, we’re previewing the title record as our Track of the Day.

“Acid Love” might as well be a love letter to classic acid house vibes, as it is an epic eight-minute extravaganza that takes its time building its 303 squelches into an intense peak. Complemented by its sexy vocal grabs, overall it’s tailor-made for late-night dancefloor mayhem.

Available October 30 via Elrow Music.
