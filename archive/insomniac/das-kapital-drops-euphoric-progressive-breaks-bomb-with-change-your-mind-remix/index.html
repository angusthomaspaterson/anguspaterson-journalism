Das Kapital Drops Euphoric Progressive Breaks Bomb With “Change Your Mind” Remix

Angus Paterson · 17 May 2018 · Insomniac

Saucy Records is an independent stable coming to you from the southeastern county of Berkshire in the UK. They’re currently riding high following the release of “Change Your Mind” from Affiliate, a rather epic melodic bass tune that balances thundering bottom-end, stuttering beats, and the honeyed vocals of Dakota Sixx. However, there’s a sneaky bomb on the B-side in the form of a remix from South African producer Das Kapital, and we’re featuring it as our Track of the Day.

Das Kapital is careful to keep the core melodic vibes of the original intact, as well as the rather seductive vocals from Dakota Sixx, though he works his magic by upping the tempo and launching it into proper progressive breaks territory. There’s enough broken-beat grandeur here to recall progressive breaks icons like Hybrid, who penned genre-defining classics like “If I Survive” back in the late ‘90s. Drop this one like it’s extremely hot.

Available May 18 via Saucy Records.
