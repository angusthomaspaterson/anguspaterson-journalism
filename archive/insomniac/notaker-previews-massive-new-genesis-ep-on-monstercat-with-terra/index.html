Notaker Previews Massive New ‘Genesis’ EP on Monstercat With “Terra”

Angus Paterson · 24 October 2017 · Insomniac

US electro/progressive hero Notaker has a new five-track EP out on Monstercat today, and it’s unquestionably his biggest musical moment to date. A mini album in its own right, Genesis travels across the spectrum from ambient introductions to vocal anthems to powerhouse progressive.

“Terra” closes out the EP on a deeper note, featuring cinematic ‘80s vibes that begin with an extended, powerful breakdown before eventually building to explode into the kind of epic, melodic progressive that Notaker has shown he’s a master at creating.

“Terra” was written to be the grand finale to the EP—the last track that contained melody, climax, and complexity of epic proportion,” he tells Insomniac. “It’s the epic return home from the long journey on the vessel.”

Available October 24 via Monstercat.
