Ryan Blyth Throws a Fresh Lick of Paint on the Robyn S Classic “Luv 4 Luv”

Angus Paterson · 12 April 2019 · Insomniac

There’s no denying the dancefloor impact a classic anthem can make when it’s been freshened up for modern crowds. Ryan Blyth does exactly that with his upcoming remix of Robin S’ “Luv 4 Luv,” which we’re previewing as our Track of the Day. It’s dropping on the very same imprint that originally released it on vinyl way back in 1993, Champion Records, and when it drops this Friday, it’ll represent the first time it’s ever been officially remixed.

Rising UK producer Ryan Blyth cements his reputation as a producer who’s on the rise by doing a bang-up job with his remix, making sure this new coat of paint is a slick one that does justice to those iconic Robyn S vocals, while suitably heating things up for the modern dancefloor. Blyth does this by keeping the vocals in the spotlight while supporting them with a particularly chunky, upbeat bassline. The key word here is “fun,” and the vocals of Robyn S, together with the polished production of Ryan Bluth, deliver this in spades.

Available April 12 via Champion Records.
