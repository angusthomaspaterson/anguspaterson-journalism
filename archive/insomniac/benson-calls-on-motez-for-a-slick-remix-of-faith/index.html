Benson Calls on Motez for a Slick Remix of “Faith”

Angus Paterson · 3 December 2018 · Insomniac

Australian producer Benson is rocketing at dangerous speeds toward the spotlight in his home country of Australia, earning himself airplay on national youth broadcaster Triple J earlier this year with his future house crossover hit “Faith,” which arrived on the Medium Rare imprint. An impressive remix package has been assembled to reflect the track’s hit status, with reworks from Stace Cadet, Yeah Boy, and his buddy Motez, who’s landed in the spotlight as our Track of the Day.

Motez increasingly finds himself in center focus of the international scene, having earned remix opportunities so far with huge names like Disclosure, Sam Smith, and Ellie Goulding. Naturally, he follows suit and does an incredible job with his “Faith” remix. Preserving the radio-friendly vocal charms of the original, he flips the energy into more of a main-room progressive direction with some nuanced studio work, a supercharged breakdown, and some particularly large drops that launch the listener straight into its tough, grinding bottom-end groove.

Available December 4 via Medium Rare Recordings.
