Free Download: Hardwell Explodes Into 2016 With “Blackout”

Angus Paterson · 7 January 2016 · Insomniac

There’s certainly some kind of musical seismic shift going on at the moment with the number of artists giving away free tracks, and Dutch titan Hardwell has joined his colleagues with the release of “Blackout” today. He is celebrating his birthday, though he’s doing it in the rather unusual fashion of giving us a free track. How does that work, exactly? As it turns out, with more stadium-filling hooks and towering drops than you can poke a stick at. “Blackout” represents Hardwell in fine form, so have a listen and download below.
