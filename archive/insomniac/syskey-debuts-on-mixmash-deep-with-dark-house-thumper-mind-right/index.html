Syskey Debuts on Mixmash Deep With Dark House Thumper “Mind Right”

Angus Paterson · 11 April 2018 · Insomniac

France’s Syskey has been slowly building his profile over the past several years with his unique brand of peak-time house, which blends big basslines with hip-hop acapellas aplenty. He was featured here with last year’s “Atypique,” and he’s hitting the big leagues with a release on Laidback Luke’s Mixmash Deep stable.

His new track is again a huge number that fuses tech-house elements with big-room flair for maximum impact. It fuses all of his signature elements with panache: heavy grooves, a huge bassline hook for unruly energy, and another one of his infectious hip-hop samples for some extra urban grit.

As Syskey himself says of the new track, “‘Mind Right’ is a real G-house track inspired by the dark side of Notorious B.I.G. It has a groovy and darkish feel to it, wrapped up with some tech-house sounds that are really my signature sound.”

Available April 13 via Mixmash Deep.
