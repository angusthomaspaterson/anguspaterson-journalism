So Schway Gets Into the ADE spirit With Fresh House Cut “G Sharp”

Angus Paterson · 20 October 2017 · Insomniac

The Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE) has taken over the Dutch capital this week, with thousands of industry players gathering to network and do business, alongside the hundreds of thousands of punters who descend upon the city simply to enjoy the hundreds of world-class parties.

To mark the annual occasion, our friends from the House of Hustle label have prepared a new Amsterdam Hustle compilation that arrives on Beatport next Monday. We’ve got a special preview of the 16 tracks of bangin’ house on offer with a fresh cut from So Schway. With “G Sharp,” he’s delivered a slice of jackin’, groovy house that ticks all the right boxes.

“For me, the vibe of a track originates from creating the key that resonates with you most,” So Schway says of the track and his decision to title it “G Sharp.”

Available October 23 via House of Hustle.
