The Golden Boy Drops Slamming Party Rocker “Street Right” for Elrow’s WMC Comp

Angus Paterson · 12 March 2018 · Insomniac

At the end of the month, the Winter Music Conference will return to sunny Miami. As one of the biggest annual gatherings of dance crews from around the world, Miami Music Week serves as a key time of the year when hot new releases and accompanying label compilations are showcased.

Famed party crew Elrow are getting in on the act with their Miami Off 2018 compilation, which they describe as “15 tracks of pure tech-house extravaganza.” Today, we’re featuring the lead offering from UK producer the Golden Boy.

“Street Right” is one of those records geared precisely for creating sweaty party vibes. Big, tough grooves, slamming kicks, and off-the-wall vocal samples create a hands-in-the-air tech house cut that is wholly uncomplicated about the fact of it being built for peak time.

Available March 16 via Elrow.
