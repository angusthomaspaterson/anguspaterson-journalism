Marsh Drops Silky-Smooth Deep House Record “Find Me”

Angus Paterson · 2 June 2017 · Insomniac

The Silk Music stable is known worldwide as one of the finest purveyors of deep, melodic progressive house. They’ve hosted excellent debut albums from a whole host of their family of producers in recent years, including Dan Sieg, Vintage & Morelli, and eleven.five. Next up is UK-based composer Marsh; we’ve got a killer selection from his Life on the Shore album.

The album is filled to the brim with seductively silky deep house and progressive, and its next single, “Find Me,” offers a great indication of what to expect when the album arrives on Silk Music next Friday. There are ethereal melodies aplenty, deep grooves that keep things moving, and some heavenly vocals from Katherine Amy on this particularly lush track.

Available June 12 via Silk.
