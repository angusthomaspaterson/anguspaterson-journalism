Franky Rizardo Bounces to Circus Recordings With the Rolling Tech of “Take Off”

Angus Paterson · 22 August 2017 · Insomniac

One of the rising acts from the Netherlands house and techno scene, Franky Rizardo is the brains behind his LTF Records label, as well as his own FLOW radio show and event concept. Shortly, he’ll be making a brief detour to the Circus Recordings stable for his three-track Take Off EP, and today we’re featuring its title track.

“Take Off” is groovy, functional tech house of the highest order, loaded to the hilt with intricate, irresistible grooves that Rizardo lays down as he gradually builds and weaves the track’s quirky, melodic hook over several minutes. It’s a main-room groover to keep the room moving.

“I’ve been spending loads of time in Ibiza hanging out,” Rizardo tells Insomniac. “After a crazy few days, I got back home and went straight in the studio. The inspiration for ‘Take Off’ was to try and create that moment where the party takes off. I’ve been playing in my sets and it’s been working well.”

Available August 28 via Circus Recordings.
