Sllash & Doppe Deliver Deep Groover “Lego”

Angus Paterson · 6 February 2019 · Insomniac

Tech house duo Sllash & Doppe are already fast favorites with stalwart stables like Toolroom Records, and their single from late last year, “Aguella,” is still holding strong in the Beatport charts. The Romanian twosome is making sure they’re firing up 2019 in similar style. They’re offering up their two-track Lego EP as their debut on Tube & Berger’s long-running Kittball label, and we’re bringing you a special preview of its title track before the full release drops this Friday.

“Lego” hits the spot just right with its deep, low-slung groove, sharpened up by some strong, stark percussive elements, as well as a super sexy vocal grab. Pushing through with plenty of dancefloor momentum, it’s punctuated with synths that rise and fall throughout its duration.

Available February 8 via Kittball.
