Weiss Returns to Toolroom With Soulful Tech-House Weapon “You Better Run”

Angus Paterson · 23 March 2017 · Insomniac

Toolroom Records is as consistent as ever when it comes to mainroom bangers from across the house music spectrum, the same territory it’s dominated for more than a decade. Here, label favorite Weiss returns to the fold, wielding a fresh tech-house slammer with a soulful edge—just in time for his upcoming Stateside tour that kicks off during the Miami Winter Conference.

“You Better Run” sees Weiss’ knack for working the dancefloor in full effect. At the top of the mix is a jacking tech-house groove that’ll offer plenty of momentum in the clubs—though it features a soulful edge, courtesy of its retro vocal sample and flashes of piano melody.

Available March 24 via Toolroom.
