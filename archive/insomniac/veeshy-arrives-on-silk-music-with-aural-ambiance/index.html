Veeshy Arrives on Silk Music With “Aural Ambiance”

Angus Paterson · 6 January 2018 · Insomniac

A staple of the melodic deep house and progressive scene, Silk Music isn’t wasting any time launching into the New Year with a string of releases their camp has already lined up. One of its first for 2018 is the label debut of Australian producer Veeshy, who steps up to Silk after years of building his profile in the progressive scene. His four-track Stimulate the Stars EP arrives in a few weeks, and we’re giving you a taste one of its offerings, “Aural Ambience.”

It’s a lush affair brimming with ethereal synths, deep basslines and angelic vocal samples, as well as sumptuous arpeggiations that rise to carry the lead. Here, Veeshy demonstrates without a doubt he is cut out for the label by delivering a track that’s wholeheartedly in the key of Silk.

Available January 15 via Silk Music.
