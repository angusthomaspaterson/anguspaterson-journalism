Mat.Joe Returns to Mother Records With the Raw Grooves of “I Go”

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2017 · Insomniac

Already regular names on the release schedule for German house specialists Mother Records, the Berlin-based duo Mat.Joe are back on the label with a full three-track EP of fresh, slamming house grooves. The Love Stream EP drops in the middle of October, and we’ve got the turbo-charged “I Go” as a special preview of what you can expect.

“I Go” brings a winning combination of raw, upfront grooves and gritty production. The intentionally rough-around-the-edges production gifts it with a proper live, organic vibe, pitched at a rollicking tempo with a sweaty energy that’s kicked up a notch by the arrival of a hip-hop acapella halfway through. Rawness, grittiness, and fun are in all the right measurements.

Available October 16 via Mother Recordings.
