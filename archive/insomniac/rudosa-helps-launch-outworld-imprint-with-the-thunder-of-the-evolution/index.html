Rudosa Helps Launch Outworld Imprint With the Thunder of “The Evolution”

Angus Paterson · 1 March 2019 · Insomniac

Berlin-based techno talent Klangkuenstler has forged his profile with a steady collection of releases on stalwart labels like Toolroom and Stil Vor Talent, though he’s decided it’s time to launch his own outfit. This week, he sets off his Outworld label with the three-track Outworld 001 compilation EP, featuring an offering from himself and two of his techno comrades. We’re featuring “The Evolution” from Rudosa as our Track of the Day.

“The Evolution” is an offering that slots in perfectly with the current punter appetite for techno that carries both a pounding drive and lashes of melody. Rudosa doesn’t waste any time laying down the foundations with a big, pounding warehouse kick, which is equally joined by brash, pulsing synths to give his production some proper energy for peak-time.

Klangkuenstler says the Outworld 01 EP sets the tone for what to expect from his new venture, “delivering a blueprint of techno that is futuristic in feel, yet firmly rooted in the analog aesthetics of its heritage… to take the listener deep into the outer realms and beyond.”

Available March 1 via Outworld.
