Noise Zoo Steers Toward the Euphoric Side on “Twister”

Angus Paterson · 18 August 2017 · Insomniac

The Enhanced Music stable and its diverse family of sublabels are increasingly placed alongside powerhouses such as Armada and Spinnin’ as one of the most important names in dance music. This month, they celebrated an impressive milestone in the form of its 300th release with label heads Tritonal’s “Good Thing.” Even after clocking so many miles in clubland, the team presses onward with a brand-new Noise Zoo cut as our TOTD.

Noise Zoo have already shown their flair for producing explosive anthems, like 2016’s “Namibia,” with a solid run of releases on Enhanced over the past year. Now, “Twister” sees them connecting with trance stalwart Cristina Soto for their first proper vocal collaboration.

Steering things a little more toward the euphoric side after their recent run of electro-driven cuts, Noise Zoo have constructed a particularly seductive, extended breakdown for “Twister,” and Soto’s vocal contributions are as accomplished as ever.

Available August 25 via Enhanced Music.
