PROFF Unleashes a Trancey Groove With “Innervate”

Angus Paterson · 1 November 2017 · Insomniac

Known worldwide as one of the finest purveyors of deep, melodic progressive house, Silk Music is based out of L.A., though it hosts a family of producers with international scope. This cast of artists will be spotlighted in early November when Silk Music Showcase 06 arrives, once again mixed by Jacob Henry and Tom Fall, and inspired by the weekly radio show of the same name. Before the compilation arrives, we’ve got a tasty preview from Russian progressive stalwart PROFF.

PROFF has proved himself the master of tough, melodic, groove-driven progressive for more than a decade, and with “Innervate,” he demonstrates again that the well never seems to run dry. This number is a little trancier than a lot of his recent work, but PROFF still ensures those crunchy grooves are present to kick the momentum along, showcasing his excellent percussion skills to full effect.

Available November 6 via Silk Music.
