Shiba San Unleashes a Bass-Infused Tech Monster With “Drop It”

Angus Paterson · 21 March 2017 · Insomniac

Just in time for his performance at the Beyond Wonderland SoCal this weekend, Shiba San is dropping the second installment of the Various Leaks EP series, where he puts the focus on the talent he’s hosting on his own Basement Leak label. For those who are keen to have a listen, we’ve got our hands on the EP’s standout track, which comes from Shiba San himself.

“Drop It” slams right down at the intersection between pumping mainstage grooves and tougher, tech-leaning sounds. Squelchy bass rumbles explode throughout the mix, recalling the wackier moments of Claude VonStroke and his Dirtybird label. Equally, though, it’s defined by its steelier techno rhythms used by Shiba San to give his record a tougher edge.

Available March 24 via Basement Leak.
