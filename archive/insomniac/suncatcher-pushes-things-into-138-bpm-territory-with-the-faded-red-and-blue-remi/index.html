Suncatcher Pushes Things Into 138-BPM Territory With “The Faded Red & Blue” Remix

Angus Paterson · 20 September 2017 · Insomniac

German trance stalwarts Kyau & Albert celebrated the 20th anniversary of their Euphonic label last year, and they have continued to show their commitment to quality trance in the days since. This week, the label unleashed the new Steve Brian single, “The Faded Red & Blue,” featuring the wonderful vocals of David Berkeley, and we’ve locked down the Suncatcher remix for your listening pleasure.

Suncatcher makes his intentions clear from the outset with his remix, pitching the tempo right into 138-BPM power trance territory. Holding off on showcasing Berkeley’s vocals until the breakdown several minutes in, he makes sure it’s otherwise garnished with plenty of lush melodic trance action.

Available now via Euphonic Records.
