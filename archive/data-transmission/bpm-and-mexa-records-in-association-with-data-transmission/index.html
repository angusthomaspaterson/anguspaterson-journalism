BPM & MEXA Records in association with Data Transmission

Angus Paterson · 6 January 2015 · Data Transmission

The start of BPM in Mexico is just around the corner and we are super excited to be teaming up with our friends at MEXA records for a killer party on Friday 10th January taking place at TABÚ, Playa Del Carmen, Quintana Roo. Heading the bill is of course label head-honcho Louie Fresco and joining him are Miguel Puente, Aquarius Heaven, Spacebyrdz, Justin Baulé, Zombies in Miami, Tara Brooks for a party not to be missed.
