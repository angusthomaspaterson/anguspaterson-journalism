Busy P [Audio]: Amping up the feedback, Part 2

Angus Paterson · 10 July 2008 · inthemix

France’s notorious Ed Banger Records have dominated the nightclubs over the past few years, and something that’s fundamental to all of the material coming from their roster is an undying love of rockin’ n roll feedback. While the Germans are obsessed with making their music deeper, more stripped back and minimal, over in the streets of Paris they’ve got a different focus altogether – ‘maximal’ is perhaps a better way to describe it. And the man behind it all is none other than Pedro Winters AKA Busy P; the very same guy who offered his guiding hand to a certain other mainstay in French dance music – Daft Punk, who he managed for 12 before only recently stepping down from duties.

And the same man who was working behind the scenes to push the first big wave of French house music out to the world in the 90s, in the new millennium he’s making sure that his country will remain in the spotlight for many years to come. “The responsibility is a big thing, but I’m not a politician. I’m more running away from Western civilities than accepting them,” he told ITM. “But of course I’m completely aware that I’ve been part of the French scene, and part of that dynamic… I’m working hard for my culture and country, and I’m glad we’ve managed to make some noise.”

Could Pedro Winters be the most influential man working in dance music today? In part 2 of our interview with the bearded enigma, ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to the man, the legend that is Busy P.
