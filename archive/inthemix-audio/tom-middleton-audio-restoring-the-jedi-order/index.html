Tom Middleton [Audio]: Restoring the Jedi Order

Angus Paterson · 8 July 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

You’d be a brave individual to go up against the Jedi mind tricks of the mighty Tom Middleton. One of the dance music’s great enigmas, the eclectic DJ/producer was only recently in Australia wowing audiences with an ‘acid house’ retrospective set at Playground Weekender that’s quickly become the stuff of legends. And that’s only the tip of the iceberg of what this musical chameleon has achieved over the years. Under pseudonyms like Cosmos, Global Communication, The Modwheel and AMBA, we’ve heard deep house, progressive, electro, ambient, experimental, techno, tribal and more. You name it, Tom Middleton has dabbled in it all.

But we’ll be seeing him in quite a different context when the Smirnoff Experience returns to Australia later this week. Prepare for the return of the Jedi Knights. The result of a long-running union with fellow DJ/producer Mark Pritchard, it’s been a number of years since the Jedi Knights used the force together and it’s usually something that’s reserved for the crowd at revered London nightclub Fabric. So anyone lucky enough to be heading along will be treated to an experience that money just can’t buy. “The force is going to be strong with this experience,” he told ITM.

ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to Tom Middleton about some of the breathtaking gigs he’s played for Smirnoff Experience around the world in recent years, what we can expect from the upcoming Australian tour and of course, on using the force.

Tom Middleton and the Jedi Knights play Smirnoff Experience, the only way in is to win so head to www.thewayin.com.au to get your hands on tickets…

Sydney – Thursday 10th July
Melbourne – Friday 11 July
Brisbane – Sunday 13th July
