Phil K [Audio]: Australian explorer

Angus Paterson · 24 January 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

When it comes to Aussie success stories internationally, they don’t come much bigger than Phil K. A DJ on the local scene for well over 20 years, he’s also made a name for himself across the globe, touring far and wide thanks to his stunning turntable skills and sublime production work as Lostep with Luke Chable.

He recently returned to Australia after a 2 year stint living in the UK and Greece, playing his first local show in almost a year. With a brand new mix CD about to hit stores, ‘Phil K’s Hi Fi’, ITM’s Angus Paterson tracked Phil down in Byron Bay to talk shop.

‘Phil K’s Hi Fi’ is out soon through Central Station/MRA. You can catch Phil touring Australia for the first time in almost a year to support its release:

Jan 27 – Famous, Melbourne
Feb 2 – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Feb 10 – James Hotel, Launceston
Feb 11 – Syrup, Hobart
Feb 17 – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Mar 9 – Cairns, Venue TBC
Mar 10 – Port Douglas, Venue TBC
Mar 17 – Moon Bar, Brisbane
Mar 18 – Elsewhere Bar, Gold Coast
