D Ramirez [Audio]: Meet him at the Discotek

Angus Paterson · 30 May 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

He’s fast become one of the most in-demand remixers and producers in the electro and tech scene, thanks in part to storming reworks of tracks by Bodyrox, Roger Sanchez, Ferry Corsten and the Plump DJs, and he’s had a string of releases on labels like Underwater, Yoshitoshi, Junior, Lot 49 and Toolroom.

After his first Australian tour in 2006 for Ministry Of Sound’s 15th anniversary, he’s coming back this June to launch his brand new compilation for the label; ‘Discotek’, which he’s mixed alongside Aussie favourite Mark Dynamix. ITM’s Angus Paterson spoke to him recently, and discovered his plans to move beyond the electro genre.

‘Discotek’, mixed by D Ramirez and Mark Dynamix, is out June 9th on Ministry Of Sound/EMI. He tours Australia in June:

Jun 1 – We Love Sounds, Adelaide
Jun 2 – Platinum, Gold Coast
Jun 3 – Ministry Of Sound Sessions, Perth
Jun 9 – Family, Brisbane
Jun 10 – We Love Sounds, Sydney
Jun 10 – Queensbridge, Melbourne
