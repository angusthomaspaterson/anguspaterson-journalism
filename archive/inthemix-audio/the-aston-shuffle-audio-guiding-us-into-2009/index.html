The Aston Shuffle [Audio]: Guiding us into 2009

Angus Paterson · 23 January 2009 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Canberra’s Aston Shuffle most certainly had a massive 2008. Forging a relationship with the all-powerful Ministry of Sound. Loads of their remixes and original tunes rocking the clubs. Their year booked solid with gigs and festivals. And let’s not forget, their amazing #6 placing in the Sony inthemix50, an unprecedented result that was the highest ranking ever for an ACT act.

“This year has been the biggest rollercoaster ride we’ve ever been on,” the Shuffle told ITM. “We never dreamed that we could achieve that in our lifetime, let alone what we did last year. We’re just really happy that our record label sees something in us, we got to play at pretty much all of the major festivals as well… We literally are pinching ourselves every day man, a year or two ago we were playing in lounge bars and now, at our last gig we closed the Fuzzy stage at Summafieldayze.”

If the opening weeks of 2009 are anything to go by, the next 12 months will prove to be even bigger for Canberra’s favourite sons, The Aston Shuffle. They were chosen by Ministry of Sound to compile one of the CDs for their extremely popular Clubbers Guide to 2009 CD, alongside Bass Kleph and the Stafford Brothers, and they’ll be hitting the road to take their wobbly, noisy sound to the nation. ITM does the shuffle with the boys form the ACT…
