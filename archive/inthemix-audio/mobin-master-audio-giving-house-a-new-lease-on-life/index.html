Mobin Master [Audio]: Giving house a new lease on life

Angus Paterson · 23 March 2009 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Soulful house may have largely been crushed under the heel of electro back in ‘05 when a new sensation stormed into clubland, with the wailing divas failing to win back the punters (beyond the hardcore faithful who still like their ‘ouse music with a bit of soul). However, Melbourne’s Mobin Master might very well be the one who helps turn back the tide, and show the way forward for soulful house.

He’s been working hard in the studio, with his tune from last year Show Me Love proving to be a massive hit that may just lay the groundwork for a soulful house revival. “The sound that I have now is house music vocals that please the girls, but when it kicks in it pleases the boys, because it’s quite energetic and tough,” he told ITM. “Gone are the days of soulful house being popular with the full vocals all the way through…. Now it’s just vocals in the breakdown, and cranking beats and synths when it kicks in. That’s the sound I’m creating at the moment.”

Mobin Master reckons it’s an approach that was needed to push house music forward, and put it back into the spotlight. “A few years ago house music was overtaken by the farty electro sound. There was no vocals, and it was a difficult time for house music producers to work out how to integrate both. I think Show Me Love was possibly a hallmark… it’s evolved to the stage where vocal house is coming back, but in a different way.”

It appears that Ministry of Sound agree with this perspective, as they’ve chosen the Melbourne DJ/producer to mix the latest edition of their Housexy series of compilations. Here Mobin Master talks to ITM’s Angus Paterson about his campaign to give house music a new lease on life.

Ministry of Sound’s Housexy Autumn 2009 is out now through Universal, mixed by Mobin Master and Those Usual Suspects.
