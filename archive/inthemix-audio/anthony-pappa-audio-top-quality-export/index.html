Anthony Pappa [Audio]: Top quality export

Angus Paterson · 17 July 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Steve Irwin, Nicole Kidman, Kylie Minogue… they’re all fine examples of top Australian exports to the world or entertainment. However, if you find yourself a little more interested in the underground side of things, you can’t go past Anthony Pappa. Born and bred in Melbourne, but UK based for the past 13 years, Pappa has been proudly flying the flag for Australian on the global progressive and underground house scene since relocating to the mother country.

Pappa’s list of achievements is impeccable; he won the Australian DMC Mixing Championship at the age of 15, moved to the UK in 1994, received acclaim for his production work as part of Freefall and Pappa & Gilbey, and he’s also mixed CDs for Global Underground (‘NuBreed 1’) and Australian label EQ (‘Balance 006’). ITM’s Angus Paterson recently spoke with Anthony about his forthcoming return to Australian shores for a national tour.
