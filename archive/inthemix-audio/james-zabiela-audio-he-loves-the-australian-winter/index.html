James Zabiela [Audio]: He loves the Australian winter

Angus Paterson · 3 June 2009 · inthemix

The UK’s James Zabiela is known as a DJ who’s developed such a strong affinity with the machines that help him make all those wonderful noises, that he’s very nearly become a robot himself. Little surprise then that he’s a guy (or cyborg, as the case may be) who shuns the daylight? His history with touring Australia during the ice-cold winter months would suggest exactly that, and he’ll be doing it again this June Long Weekend with a massive tour that will see him play We Love Sounds in Sydney and Adelaide, Winter Sound System in Melbourne and more.

“The last time when I came here I got absolutely cooked when I went to the beach,” James told ITM, talking about the one catastrophic time when he did actually come to Australia during our summer. “I looked like a beetroot. So yeah, I don’t really mind coming to Australia during the winter,” he laughed.

Whether he’s keeping out of the sun or not, anyone who’s been following his career closely over the past year or so would have noticed how much he’s stepped things up as a DJ, with a refined approach to programming that’s lifted him even further into the upper echelon of international DJs. He can now grip the attention of the dancefloor longer than ever before, and is a lot more restrained when it comes to his famous SFX and trickery. “It was probably touring with Nic Fanciulli, which really had a big influence on how I play. He’s got a totally different approach to DJing to what I have, so there was stuff to learn from him, especially in regards to how he paces his sets.”

Well, the June Long Weekend is nearly upon us. ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to everybody’s favourite dancefloor cyborg James Zabiela about stepping things up as a DJ, what it takes to hold back on all those the crazy sound effects as well as the crazy run of winter festival shows he’s got planned this weekend.

Catch James Zabiela at the following shows:

Saturday 6th June – We Love Sounds, Sydney
Sunday 7th June – Warehouse Festival, Canberra
Sunday 7th June – Winter Sound System, Melbourne
Monday 8th June – We Love Sounds, Adelaide
