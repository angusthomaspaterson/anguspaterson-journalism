Dirty South [Audio]: His disco is eating him

Angus Paterson · 14 March 2007 · inthemix

If looking at some of the Australian producers that are making a name for themselves overseas at the moment, you don’t get any hotter than Dirty South. Reaching critical mass last year with his smashing remix of Evermore’s It’s Too Late, he’s now heavily in demand right around the world and remixing tracks for artists as diverse as Depeche Mode, Roger Sanchez, Josh Wink and most recently Tiesto.

ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to Dirty South about his contribution to the latest One Love compilation Your Disco Will Eat You, how important international success and recognition is to him and why he thinks the electro house sound is so damn popular.

Dirty South has mixed the new Onelove album, ‘Your Disco Will Eat You’, alongside Denis The Menace & Jerry Ropero and Tommie Sunshine. It’s available now on SonyBMG.
