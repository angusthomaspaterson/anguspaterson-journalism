Adam Freeland [Audio]: Breaking away from the mould

Angus Paterson · 12 June 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Things have been a little quiet on the Adam Freeland front of late, with the British producer/DJ/label-owner having kept a low profile since the touring and associated commitments for his acclaimed debut album ‘Now & Them’ wound up a few years back. Once a recurring visitor to Australia, it’s been over 2 years since we last saw him on our shores. Of course, that’s got fans of his work salivating at the thought of his brand new release; ‘Global Underground 032: Mexico City’. The fact that he’s returning for a tour in September and October is just the icing on the cake!

This will be Freeland’s debut mix for the Global Underground label, and his first double CD in over 10 years. With a respected position carved out within the breakbeat scene globally, it’s also an interesting departure in sound for Freeland, as he steers his ship in the direction of house, tech and electro influences. Now based in Los Angeles, ITM’s Angus Paterson spoke with Freeland recently.
