Judge Jules [Audio]: Still a wild DJ

Angus Paterson · 29 August 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

He’s one of the most outspoken identities in dance music, and he’s also maintained an enviable position as one of the most popular DJs around the world for over a decade. Love him or hate him, you can’t ignore Judge Jules!

His weekly Judgement Sundays event in Ibiza is one of the island’s biggest parties, and throughout his exhaustive career he’s won awards like “Best DJ” (Muzik Magazine Awards), #1 DJ (Mixmag reader’s poll) and “Best International DJ” (Dancestar).

As a regular visitor to Australia, ordinarily visiting around Christmas time each year to soak in the sunshine of a Down Under Xmas, the Judge continues to reign as one of the last remaining ‘superstar DJs’ from the late 90s boom period.

‘Wild Weekends Vol 4’, mixed by Judge Jules and Smash N Grab, is out now through Central Station/MRA.
