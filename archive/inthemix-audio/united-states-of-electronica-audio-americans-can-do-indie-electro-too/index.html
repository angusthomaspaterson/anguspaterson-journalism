United States of Electronica [Audio]: Americans can do indie/electro too

Angus Paterson · 3 January 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Australia has become pretty damn famous for its indie/electro acts like Cut Copy, The Presets and Midnight Juggernauts and we’ve been able to stand shoulder to shoulder with UK heavyweights like The Klaxons and Hot Chip. But now the Americans are getting in on the act: as well as New York’s LCD Soundsystem, the other US band that’s been turning heads has been United States of Electronica.

Hailing from the normally rock-heavy city of Seattle, the band is far from just another synth-rock band who don’t bring anything new to the table. Their self-titled debut album has finally been released in Australia and tunes like Emerald City and Open Your Eyes have a joyous sense to them that recalls 60s psychedelia. So will we see their heavily-praised live show making its way to Australia? Angus Paterson from ITM has a chat to the next big thing in indie-electronica crossover.

United States of Electronica’s self titled album is available now through Original Recordings/Inertia.
