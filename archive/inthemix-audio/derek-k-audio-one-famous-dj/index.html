Derek {K} [Audio]: One Famous DJ

Angus Paterson · 12 February 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Hand-picked by Future Entertainment to be one of the main residents at their latest club venture Famous, Derek {K} is well on his way to fulfilling the prophecy of the club’s name, with his star well and truly on the ascent. Rocking upwards of 2,000 clubbers each and every week at Famous, since the party launched in September last year Derek has watched his profile climb alongside the club’s.

With plans now in place to take Famous interstate, the club set to launch at Home in Sydney very soon, we caught up with Derek to talk about how he’s found the ride so far. He tells Angus Paterson what it was like hearing Felix Da Housecat play his track at Space in Ibiza, how it felt being invited to reside at Future’s super-club venture, and how he approached his brand new mix CD for the Future Music Festival.

‘Future Music Festival 2007’, mixed by Chris Lake and Derek {K}, is out now through EQ/Stomp. You can catch Derek playing selected dates on the festival tour in March, as well as his own solo launch parties:

Feb 24 – Famous, Melbourne (CD Launch)
Mar 4 – Future Music Festival, Perth
Mar 11 – Future Music Festival, Melbourne
Mar 17 – Future Music Festival, Sydney
Mar 25 – Bedroom Nightclub, Gold Coast (CD Launch)
Apr 6 – Velvet Underground, Cairns (CD Launch)
