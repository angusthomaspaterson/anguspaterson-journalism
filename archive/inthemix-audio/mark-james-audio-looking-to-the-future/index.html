Mark James [Audio]: Looking to the Future

Angus Paterson · 7 December 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

As the head of one of Australia’s biggest dance music promotion companies, Mark James has got his sights set on the Australian dance music summer: while there’s the massive Future Music Festival tour on the horizon in early ’08, in the more immediate ‘Future’ he’s got the Summadayze festival coming up: Melbourne’s flagship New Years Day event which is celebrating its 10th Anniversary this year.

ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to the DJ, producer and Managing Director of Future Entertainment about sharing mixing duties with Dave Spoon on the new Summadayze CD, what he’s looking forward to in the New Year and what we can expect from Summadayze, Future Music Festival and beyond…

Summadayze 2008, mixed by Mark James & Dave Spoon, is in the stores now through Ministry of Sound/EMI. Check out the Summadayze and Summafieldayze dates across the country below…

Jan 1st – Summadayze, Melbourne
Jan 5th – Summafieldayze, Gold Coast
Jan 6th – Summadayze, Perth
