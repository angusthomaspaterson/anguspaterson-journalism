Stanton Warriors [Audio]: Bringing back their Good Vibrations

Angus Paterson · 18 November 2009 · inthemix

The last time UK breakbeat heavyweights the Stanton Warriors were in Australia, rocking the Laundry Tent at the Good Vibrations Festival with a ferocity matched only by Deadmau5, the duo of Mark Yardley and Dominic B decided they needed something a little special to throw into their sets. So they holed themselves up in their hotel room in Sydney prior to their first gig of the tour, and knocked together a bootleg of the Beach Boys classic Good Vibrations.

Predictable, perhaps? Well, the results speak for themselves as while it was originally intended only to serve as an introduction to their set, the results were so effective that it quickly became the biggest tune of their tour, as well as receiving loads of requests from their DJ colleagues.

The Harbourlife lineup announcement back in August popped the cork on the fact that the Stantons would be back in Australia in November, but it was eventually revealed to be only a small part of their whirlwind ‘Stanton Sessions 2009’ tour, which will see them spinning 8 sets in 8 days over 6 states. With the tour kicking off this weekend, ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to the Stantons about all the vibrating mayhem they’ll be creating over the next few weeks.

21st Nov – Harbourlife, Sydney
21st Nov – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
25th Nov – Halo, Hobart
26th Nov – Great Northern Hotel, Byron Bay
