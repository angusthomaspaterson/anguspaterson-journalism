Bexta [Audio]: She's digging the tech trance

Angus Paterson · 6 December 2007 · inthemix

STEAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Bexta has been smashing apart the clubs for a decade now with both her DJ sets and live performances, made up mostly of hard trance, hardstyle and hard house – you may have noticed a mighty big emphasis on the word ‘HARD’ in there! But if you’ve caught her out and about lately or had a listen to her new Mixology 10 compilation then you’ll be aware that Bexta, like a lot of other people out there, is really quite excited by the new wave of progressive/tech trance that’s out there at the moment.

“I absolutely love it; this kind of music feels like what I’ve been waiting my whole career for. Really driving beats, not too aggressive and beautiful melodies. There’s a nice balance there.” She’s out there on the road at the moment pushing that very sound, in addition to pushing forward with her new Mixology Digital label and a whole bunch of her own productions: here she has a chat with ITM’s Angus Paterson.

Mixology 10 is out now through Central Station/MRA, catch her playing at the following dates…

6th December: Platinum feat. Paul van Dyk, Gold Coast
8th December: Enchanted Festival, Adelaide
14th December: Home, Sydney
15th December: Holy Grail, Canberra
28th December: Viper Room, Melbourne
30th December: Mooloolabah, Queensland

And don’t forget she’ll also be heading out on the national Big Day Out tour throughout January and February, playing live shows around the country!
