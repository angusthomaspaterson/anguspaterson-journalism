Eric Prydz [Audio]: The leviathan lands, part 1

Angus Paterson · 24 December 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

For lovers of quality electronic music, this coming New Year season is going to be a special one. Underworld. Ferry Corsten. Digitalism. Eric Morillo. It’s an enviable lineup of artists who’ll be performing in the country, but you can make a fairly rock-solid argument that Swedish DJ/producer Eric Prydz effortlessly trumps them all. He’s simply one of the greatest dance music producers to ever walk the earth.

Anyone who’s been witness to the hysteria his tracks have created on the dancefloor over the past few years will understand the special connection that Prydz has with what works on the dancefloor. “When I make music, most of the time I try and picture myself being in a club on a dancefloor somewhere,” he told ITM. “And I make the music for dancefloors. When you listen to it at home, or on your iPod, it won’t have the same effect as when you hear it on a big soundsystem in a club… I know what I want to hear on a dancefloor, and that’s what I’m trying to accomplish when I make my tracks. And a lot of other people agree with me I think.”

It’s an approach that’s cut through all the different scenes, and you’ll be used to hearing tracks from Eric Prydz by now, no matter what style of dance music you’re into. “If I can see Sven Vath, Paul van Dyk and Erick Morillo all playing the same track, then I know I’ve made something really special.”

The anticipation has been building for year from his fans over when he’d finally make it to Australia, as he’s been kept away from our country by his widely reported fear of flying. But this New Year party season, it’s finally time for the leviathan to land. ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to one of dance music’s most elite DJ/producers about his long overdue tour of Australia that kicks off next week.
