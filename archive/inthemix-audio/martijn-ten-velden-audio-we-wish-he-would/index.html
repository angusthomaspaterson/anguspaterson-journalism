Martijn ten Velden [Audio]: We wish he would

Angus Paterson · 5 December 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

He’s been making electronic for more than 15 years under various guises, responsible for countless high-profile remixes and over the past few years, an extremely successful partnership with Mark Knight. But it was only just last year that the dance world finally put a face to the name of Martijn Ten Velden when he broke through to greater recognition with his massive hit I Wish You Would. Things have been steamrolling ahead ever since and he’s been taking his sugnature style of tribal, funky and electro-tinged house music right around the world.

So how well have things been going for the Dutchman in 2007? The best reflection of how in demand he’s been would be how many flights he’s embarked on: “It’s become so busy, I’ve counted my flights and I’ve been on 105 already. I’ve got another five this weekend, so I think by the end I would have had 130 flights this year.” Wow! He’s also right in the middle of his most extensive Australian tour yet, and he talks to ITM’s Angus Paterson about the year that’s just passed, his exciting new project with Mark Yardley from the Stanton Warriors as well as what’s going on in house music at the moment…
