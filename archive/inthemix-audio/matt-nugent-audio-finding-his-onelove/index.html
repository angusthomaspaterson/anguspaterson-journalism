Matt Nugent [Audio]: Finding his onelove

Angus Paterson · 19 March 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

While he’s known widely for his residency at Sydney’s hugely popular onelove Saturday night, Matt Nugent has a career as a DJ/producer that stretches back well before any association with an Australian superclub brand. Cutting his teeth in the Brisbane clubbing scene and scaling the heights to take on residencies at clubs like Family, Empire and Monastery, he eventually relocated to Sydney to enjoy similar success. He’s also enjoyed the fruits of a longtime partnership in the studio with his buddy Nick Gallea, another bright spark to emerge from the Brisbane clubbing scene.

He’s been one of the quiet achievers in Sydney clubbing for quite a while, building his reputation as a DJ who is equally adept at bigroom sets as he is warming up the crowd for an international visitor. But now it’s time for Matt Nugent to step it up a notch: he’s been handpicked by the onelove crew to share mixing duties with Grant Smillie and Italian fidget house sensations The Crookers on their new Sound Machine compilation. And you know what that means: getting his name out to a much wider national audience, as well as an extensive tour that’ll see him playing clubs in some of the most far-flung corners of the country.

So it’s all happening for Matt in ‘08. Here he talks to ITM’s Angus Paterson about working with onelove, what we can expect from his new CD and finally, that warmup slot he played for Paul van Dyk last year, when he proved his chops to hundreds of clubbers who were initially skeptical as to whether he would step up to the plate.
