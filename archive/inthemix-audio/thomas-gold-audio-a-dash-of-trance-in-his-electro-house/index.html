Thomas Gold [Audio]: A dash of trance in his electro house

Angus Paterson · 8 July 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Propelled by energy of the massive hit he enjoyed with Don’t You Want Me last year, Germany’s Thomas Gold is one of the latest house producers to break through into crossover club success. And he’s had a busy 2008 so far: gigs all over the world, remix offers aplenty and most recently, he’s been hired by the big guns over at Australia’s Onelove to mix a CD for their upcoming Neon Nights compilation.

Don’t You Want Me might have been the turning point of his career, but he’s since set up his own *S2G*record label together with his partners Chriss Ortega and Chris Montana. And while he’s made a name as a house music producer, he’s been broadening his horizons into unlikely territory – trance. He recently remixed the classic Silence from Delerium, which quickly snatched a Beatport #1 as well as a thrashing in the trance scene. He definitely nailed a unique house/trance crossover sound with the remix, and it’s something he’ll be exploring further in the future.

“I would say the trance world is connecting more and more with the electro and house world, and both of these things are getting closer and closer,” he said. “It was a great experience to try and connect both of these worlds.” ITM’s Angus Paterson speaks to Thomas Gold as he’s locked down in the studio compiling his mix CD for Onelove, and finds out what we can expect from his Australian tour this weekend.
