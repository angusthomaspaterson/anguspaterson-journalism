APSCI [Audio]: A union of sound

Angus Paterson · 30 November 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Comprised of New York MC/producer Raphael LaMotta and Philippino/Australian vocalist Dana Diaz-Tutaan, and dabbling in sounds that span across hip hop, electro and raw acoustics, APSCI are a musical duo with an interesting difference – they’re married.

They’re also signed to the prestigious Quannum label, have toured with Blackalicious and have already built up quite an impressive profile overseas with the success of their Thanks For Asking album. With their sophomore release due for completion and release next year, they’re on their way back to Diana’s hometown to show us what they’ve got. ITM’s Angus Paterson has a chat to them both.

As well as their solo show at Oxford Arts Factory on Wednesday December 5th, you can catch APSCI performing at HP’s Art in Motion at the Forum in Sydney on Dec 6th, along with Coldcut and DJ Numark. RSVP online for free entry at www.hpinteraction.com.
