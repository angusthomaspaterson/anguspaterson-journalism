Fedde Le Grand [Audio]: A new Sensation

Angus Paterson · 19 December 2008 · inthemix

It’s been a fast ride to the very top for Holland’s Fedde Le Grand. He’d been honing his craft for years before he was known to anybody outside of his inner circle, but it was with the release of his mega-anthem Put Your Hands Up For Detroit in 2006 that he was swiftly elevated to become a household name. There were some who came to associated him with the more predictable side of electro house, but not unlike Eric Prydz after his own mega-anthem Call On Me, his talent spoke for itself and the world gradually came to see the truth. Fedde Le Grand is the real deal.

Anyone who’s caught Fedde this year in a live setting will know how much he’s been killing it. Finding his groove on the tougher, more upbeat end of the tech house spectrum, he’s been impressing people all over the world with his performances. Including of course ID&T, the Holland production company responsible for the spectacular Sensation parties around the world. Fedde strikes the perfect balance between the accessible and the underground which is necessary for such massive events, and he’s been chosen by ID&T as a ‘resident’ of sorts for their revered series of parties.

“There’s just nothing like it,” Fedde told ITM. “It’s not only huge, and it’s not only the fact that people are in white. I think the whole show has evolved over the years into this amazing theatre slash Cirque De Soleil slash theme park… it’s more than a dance party, and they’ve really taken things to the next level.”

When Sensation reaches Telstra Dome in Melbourne for its mighty debut this NYE, Fedde will be there to show the 40,000+ crowd what he’s got. He’ll also shortly be embarking on his most extensive Australian tour to date, with club shows right across the country. Here ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to dance music’s biggest new sensation Fedde Le Grand about what he’s got in store this NYE.
