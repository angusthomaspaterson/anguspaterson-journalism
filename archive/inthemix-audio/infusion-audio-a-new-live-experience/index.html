Infusion [Audio]: A new live Experience

Angus Paterson · 9 July 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

You’d be putting it mildly if you classed Melbourne’s Infusion as one of the country’s great live electronic acts. Because after racking up a long list of achievements and accolades since forming in the 90s (recording an Essential Mix and playing at Glastonbury among them), they’re looked to as one of the premier examples of how electronic music can be taken to the live stage – from anywhere in the world, full stop. And now they’re back, and potentially bigger than ever.

After a brief hiatus from the circuit after touring extensively in support of 2004’s Six Feet Above Yesterday, Infusion will return to this stage this weekend for the Smirnoff Experience series of secret parties this weekend. They’ll also be taking the wraps of their new-and-improved live setup; we’ve seen the trio on stage with guitars in the past, but they’ve now incorporated live drumming into their sets as well as visuals courtesy of Tomato, the legendary design company who’ve provided visuals for the seminal Underworld. And better yet, it’ll also represent the first chance to listen to material from their new album which is due to drop early next year.

“It’s been a while since we’ve played a proper massive show,” says Infusion’s Jamie Stevens. “Because we just finished the new album, it gave us a chance to rethink how we do all this live.” And if you were lucky enough to get you hands on one of the highly sought after tickets, it’s all set to be an amazing Experience. ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to Infusion about the parties coming up this weekend, as well as what’s in store for the band in the remainder of 2008.

Infusion will be taking the wraps off their new live setup at the Smirnoff Experience, and the only way in is to win. Head to www.thewayin.com.au for details…

Sydney – Thursday 10th July
Melbourne – Friday 11 July
Brisbane – Sunday 13th July
