Jon O'Bir [Audio]: PVD protege

Angus Paterson · 30 September 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

If you’ve got the support trance of leviathan Paul van Dyk, then you must be doing something right. Such is the case for UK DJ/producer Jon O’Bir, who had already enjoyed an enviable level success before he won the approval of a certain Berlin dance music mainstay. He got his first big break back in 2002 when he beat out 300 other rivals to win a prestigious DJ competition, and not long after Godskitchen recognised the fledgling talent and offered him one of their highly-prized residencies. As the international gigs increased, so did his profile as a producer and late last year he put the finishing touches on his debut artist album From Within, with the intention of releasing it on his independent label. That was until PVD has a listen.

“I decided to send the album out to a few labels, with Paul’s imprint Vandit being one of them, and as soon as they got in touch there was only one way it was going,” he laughs. Things have certainly taken off since then. “Everything seems to be going so fast since joining Vandit, with the gigs around the world and especially playing alongside Paul which has been fantastic. I can’t ask for anything more, it’s been a real rollercoaster ride this year and it keeps going on the up and up.”

John will be arriving in Australia for the Godskitchen ‘10th Year Anniversary’ tour this weekend, so it’s somewhat fitting he’ll be visiting for the very first time under the banner that originally recognised his potential. Before Paul van Dyk, even. ITM chats with Jon O’Bir in anticipation of his opening show in Melbourne this weekend.
