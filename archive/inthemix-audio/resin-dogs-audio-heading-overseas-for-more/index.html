Resin Dogs [Audio]: Heading overseas for 'More'

Angus Paterson · 12 March 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

A reputation for tearing it up on the live stage. Years and years of international touring under their belts. Several solid albums in the can, including last year’s critically acclaimed More. So what’s next for Australia’s the Resin Dogs? Jetting off to Malibu in the States of course, to work with Britney Spears’ producer. Duh!

But it’s not quite what it seems. Late last year the band answered the call entered a competition run by the prestigious Document Room studios, and owner/producer J.T. Meskiel, who’s worked with everyone from the aforementioned Spears to Lauryn Hill and fellow uber-producer Rick Rubin. The band was answering the call to find the “best hip hop track producers on the planet,” and the Resin Dogs were deemed by Document Room as being worthy of an international audience.

So what happens now? They’ll shortly be off to Malibu to sit down with Meskiel for a three-day production stint at the million-dollar recording studio. The music that results from those sessions will be sent out to major and independent labels, publishers and music management, along with a prestigious ‘Stamp of Approval’ from Document Room studios. After over a decade in the game, the Dogs are perfectly placed to crack the tough US market, and not a moment too soon we reckon. ITM’s Angus Paterson has a chat with Katch before he heads overseas.

More is out now through the band’s own label Hydrofunk, distributed by MGM. You can catch the Resin Dogs playing at Soco if you’re in Adelaide this Saturday, otherwise they’ll be embarking on an extensive national tour when they return from the States…
