Mark Dynamix [Audio]: Pushing the Production

Angus Paterson · 14 March 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEWS

When shining the spotlight on Australian DJs, you just don’t find any more enduring and successful than Mark Dynamix. Rocking the clubs since the early 90’s with residencies at some of the biggest nights both in Sydney and around Australia, he’s seen his star shine even brighter since he became one of the chief names behind the Ministry of Sound’s presence in Australia.

ITM’s Angus Paterson visited Mark in his home studio in the Sydney suburb of Alexandria: taking a squiz at his technology setup, discovering the big plans he has production and remix wise for 2007 and talking a little about the latest minimal craze that’s huge around the world at the moment.

Mark Dynamix and Jaytech’s latest single ‘Destructor’ is out now on Hussle/EMI. To stay up to date on everything happening in the world of Mark Dynamix then be sure to check out his website – www.markdynamix.com.
