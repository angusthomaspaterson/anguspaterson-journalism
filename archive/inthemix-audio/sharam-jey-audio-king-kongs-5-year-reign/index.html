Sharam Jey [Audio]: King Kong's 5 year reign

Angus Paterson · 29 May 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEWS

Cologne based DJ and producer Sharam Jey will be in Australia to celebrate the 5th birthday of his record label King Kong this June, touring the country for the We Love Sounds Festival and Kink. A longtime favourite of the Kink crew, Sharam returns to Australia following the success of his 2006 mix CD and tour for the club. With a sound that spans house, tech, electro and even rock, Sharam has amassed a strong following in Australia, even causing clubbers to begin spontaneously crowd surfing at gigs in the past. ITM’s Angus Paterson talks shop with the leader of King Kong.

‘5 Years Of King Kong Records’, mixed by Sharam Jey and Lou Lou Players, is out soon through Central Station/MRA. Sharam Jey tours Australia in June for We Love Sounds and Kink:

1st Jun – Kink at Bustin, Wollongong
2nd Jun – Kink at 4Play, Geelong
3rd Jun – Ministry Of Sound Sessions, Perth
8th Jun – We Love Festival, Melbourne
9th June – We Love Festival, Brisbane
10th June – We Love Festival, Sydney
15th June – Kink at Platinum, Gold Coast
16th June – Kink at Arthouse, Sydney
17th June – Smut, Sydney
