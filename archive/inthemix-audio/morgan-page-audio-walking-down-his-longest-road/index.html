Morgan Page [Audio]: Walking down his longest road

Angus Paterson · 17 June 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

There are two sides to the persona of Canadian-born/LA-based producer Morgan Page. There’s his own original productions which have been given support from high-profile progressive DJs like Sasha, John Digweed and Hernan Cattaneo, but he’s also made a bit of a name for himself as a ‘go-to guy’ with his remixes of artists like of Nelly Furtado, Stevie Nicks and Ashlee Simpson. And both sides of his personality are on display on his debut album Elevate, which sees him fusing his punchy, melodic progressive sound with the pop, folk and rock sensibilities of the artists he’s so frequently been handed the responsibility of remixing.

And Elevate should see him suitably ‘elevated’ to the next level of recognition as an individual artist: with one of the album’s strongest tracks The Longest Road, he’s got a bonafide crossover anthem on his hands. Featuring the familiar vocals of Lissie as well as a remix from the ubiquitous Deadmau5 (that’s already proving to be massively popular), The Longest Road has the sort of wide appeal that could see Morgan Page breaching the mainstream in much the same way that Deep Dish did with Flashdance.

ITM’s Angus Paterson catches up with Morgan Page while he’s working in his studio “deep in the hills of LA,” and talks to him about his new album, what he’s got coming up next as well as the challenges in bridging the gap between the clubbing world and the mainstream.

Morgan Page’s Elevate is out now through Nettwerk/Stomp, and check out the clip for The Longest Road.
