TV Rock [Audio]: Coming at you from the underground (Part 2)

Angus Paterson · 10 September 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

What’s that you say? Yes that’s right, TV Rock are coming at you from the underground. At least, that’s where the DJ/producer duo responsible for the infamous smash hit Flaunt It found their roots, and it’s where they see their future. While onelove favourite Grant Smillie makes for a flashy frontman of sorts, the lion’s share of the studio work is done by Ivan Gough, an individual who’s no less than one of the most prolific and enduring producers in Australian dance music history. So you think you’ve got TV Rock pegged as cheap and cheesy electro? Think again.

“I’ve been making records for over ten years now,” Ivan told ITM. “If anybody thinks we’re just a mainroom electro group, that’s just completely wrong. I’ve made trance in the past, I’ve made a lot of progressive house and I made some of the first breaks records ever in this country in 97-98 with names like Phil K and Luke Chable. I’ve made a really broad spectrum of music. Electro was something we were doing for a while, I still think it has a big place in the music scene, but we’ve moved on.”

The current sound of TV Rock is more in line with the edgy mainroom house that’s pumping out of the clubs in Europe, from prestigious labels like Stealth and Toolroom Records with figureheads like Mark Knight and Laidback Luke and the Swedish House Mafia driving the sound forward. ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to super-producer Ivan Gough from TV Rock to find out more.
