4 Days In Geneva [Audio]: Meeting of the minds

Angus Paterson · 23 October 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

The term ‘super-group’ probably gets bandied about a little more than it should nowadays (did Tommy Lee et al actually sell any albums as Supernova?), but when it comes to underground hip-hop one would argue that the 4 Days In Geneva project could very well be fitting of the label. If not super-group, then at the very least it’s a great example of what happens when a collective of like-minded spontaneously have a meeting of the minds.

The 4 Days In Geneva collective is made up of Ohmega Watts, Braille, Surreal and DJ Idull, the foursome coming together in 2006 while on a tour of Europe. Traveling the continent via train, they’d spoken about the possibility of working on some tracks together, and when fate stepped in to provide them with fours days off in Geneva, Switzerland… well it doesn’t take a genius to work out what happened. ITM recently spoke with Braille about how the project unfolded.
