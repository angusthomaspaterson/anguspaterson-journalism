Splitloop [Audio]: Electro breaks revolution

Angus Paterson · 31 July 2008 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Is the breakbeat scene in need of a creative shot in the arm? According to Irish production duo Splitloop, electro is the answer. That’s been their approach at least, withy the fresh concoction of breakbeat sounds with a heavy electro influence that you can hear on their new artist album Pleasure Machine. It’s out now on Against the Grain, the seminal UK breaks imprint that’s run and operated by Krafty Kuts and Skool of Thought, and it’s already being touted as one of the year’s best dance releases. Look to the recent praise lavished upon it by DJ Mag, who awarded it with a five-star review.

As the Splitloop boys say themselves, “You can’t be looking only to the breaks scene to get inspiration for your music. That would just be a ridiculous feedback loop.” And it’s this fresh and open approach to their production that’s been getting them plenty of attention, hailed in some quarters as the saviors of breakbeat. “We’re playing a lot more nights that aren’t just breakbeat, we’re playing house nights and we’re playing electro nights. And everyone is loving the injection of breaks styles into that.”

ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to Splitloop ahead of the duo debuting their new tunes on an Australian tour.
