Jimmy Van M [Audio]: When too much Jimmy is barely enough

Angus Paterson · 17 October 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Following last year’s era defining 10th installment for the globally renowned Balance CD series, Jimmy Van M has kept himself busy with the requisite touring commitments, playing across the world to a rapturous response. Jimmy’s episode in the Balance series saw him contribute an epic 3 discs, journeying through the many sub-styles of the house music genre. Tech, progressive, minimal, electronica, downbeat… there’s probably more, but some things are better left unlabelled.

Not be outdone, Jimmy’s now found even more room for artistic expression, recently putting the final touches to a reprise of his ‘Balance 10’ triple CD set. If you’re of the opinion that too much Jimmy Van M is barely enough, then get your ears ready for ‘Balance 10.1’, a peaktime mix that’s very much akin to what you’d hear from the American when he tours Australia this month.

‘Balance 10.1’ is out soon on EQ/Stomp. Be sure to catch Jimmy when he tours Australia this month:

Fri 19th Oct – Sydney, Sweetchilli
Sat 20th Oct – Melbourne, Darkbeat
Sun 21st Oct – Gold Coast, Platinum
Fri 26th Oct – Perth, Ambar
Sat 27th Oct – Cairns, Velvet Underground
