Paul Van Dyk [Audio]: The world's DJ

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Much has been said about Paul Van Dyk’s status as the world’s reigning #1 DJ, and in 2007 Australia will get a double dose of the global dance superstar as he tours over two weekends; once this October and then again in December. This October’s run of dates are all proudly supported by inthemix, with the DJ playing some smaller club shows along the east coast.

Fresh from one of his frequent visits to New York, Paul took the time to speak with ITM recently while he relaxed at home in Berlin, talking about his brand new album ‘In Between’, and elaborating on how his current stage setup sees it border on being a live performance as opposed to traditional DJ set.

‘In Between’ is out now through Liberator/Warner. PVD visits Australia this weekend, before returning in early December for the remainder of his album launch tour!

Fri 12th Oct – Melbourne, Palace Metro Theatre [SOLD OUT]
Sat 13th Oct – Cairns, Generationext Festival [TIX ON ITM]
Sun 14th Oct – Sydney, Yu [SOLD OUT]
