Drapht [Audio]: Taking the nation, one show at a time

Angus Paterson · 4 March 2009 · inthemix

Fresh from the impressive critical and commercial success of his Brothers Grimm album last year, as well as an impressive top 10 listing in the Triple J Hottest 100 for his smash Jimmy Recard, you could safely assume that Aussie MC Drapht is cruising on somewhat of a high right now. But he’s far from kicking back and soaking it all in: it’s time to capitalise. This week he kicks his biggest national tour yet with more than 20 dates across the country, and even better yet he’ll be joined by veterans Downsyde for a show that promises to be full of unexpected surprises.

“These are the guys that I’ve grown up with, and they’ve sort of paved the way for my music ability as well, so to have the opportunity to tour with good friends for a month is gonna be crazy. I’m sure it’s gonna be messy,” Drapht told ITM. The plan is for the two acts to be onstage bouncing off each other for nearly the entire show. “It’s something different that’s never been done before… we’re really excited about it, we’ve been rehearsing four times a week… it’s come together really nicely.”

Brace yourselves for what’s set to be the biggest Aussie hip hop tour of the year. ITM’s Angus Paterson talks to Drapht ahead of the first gigs later this week.
