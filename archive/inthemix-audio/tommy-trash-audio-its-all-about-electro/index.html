Tommy Trash [Audio]: It's all about electro

Angus Paterson · 3 August 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

It’s likely you’re deaf, dumb and colourblind if you’ve missed the fact that electro house has well and truly taken over the mainstream clubbing scene. Not so much a revolution of house music – more like an evolution – it’s interesting to observe such a specific style of music mushroom in popularity so dramatically.

You hear it everywhere nowadays; in TV ads, on the radio, in clothing shops, and of course… on the dancefloor of most nightclubs and bars around the country. Ascribing to the logic that ‘too much is never enough’, Ministry Of Sound have jumped in with a brand new series of CDs that pay homage to the sound of now in clubland.

Tommy Trash is member of the exciting new breed of elite electro house producers emerging on the Australian scene. With several releases to his credit on the Hussle label, including ‘Slide’ and his collaboration with Goodwill ‘It’s A Swede Thing’, could you think of a better choice to mix Ministry’s first ever ‘Electro House Sessions’ CD? Didn’t think so!

‘Electro House Sessions’, mixed by Tommy Trash & The Stafford Brothers, is available August 11th through Ministry Of Sound/EMI. The boys will be hitting the road throughout August, September and October to launch the album, so check them out in a club near you:
