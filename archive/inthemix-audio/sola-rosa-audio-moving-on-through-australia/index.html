Sola Rosa [Audio]: Moving on through Australia

Angus Paterson · 3 July 2007 · inthemix

STREAMING AUDIO INTERVIEW

Covering the spectrum of funk, hip-hop, dub, reggae, breaks and brass, New Zealand’s Sola Rosa are heading back to Australia in July for a national tour in support of their fantastic new album ‘Moves On’, and it’s proudly brought to you by inthemix. The brainchild of Andrew Spraggon, Sola Rosa have released three albums to date, winning themselves a swag of prestigious awards in their native NZ.

The band’s most recent album, ‘Moves On’, has garnered praise from sources as diverse as New Zealand street press through to the Sydney Morning Herald, with inthemix’s own review of the album describing it as, “island sweet and full of funk but also effortlessly cool and ambitious in the right places.” When performing live, Sola Rosa is a six-piece act that features former Jah Wobble sideman Spikey Tee, and they’ll be hitting the road for four shows in July to showcase tracks from the new album.

If you’re into the sounds of New Zealand’s live dub and reggae scene, then be sure to check out Sola Rosa when they tour this July, proudly supported by inthemix:

Thursday July 19th: The Basement, Sydney
Friday July 20th: ANU Bar, Canberra
Saturday July 21st: The Evelyn Hotel, Melbourne
Sunday July 22nd: The Espy, Gershwin Room, Melbourne

‘Moves On’ is out now, available through Inertia.
