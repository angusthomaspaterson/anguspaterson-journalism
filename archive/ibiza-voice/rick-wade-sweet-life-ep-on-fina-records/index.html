Rick Wade - Sweet Life EP on FINA Records

Angus Paterson · 5 February 2014 · Ibiza Voice

The first release on FINA Records for 2014, the Sweet Life EP is a catalyst for some very classic house sounds. FINA Records was launched in 2011 by Simon Morell and Matt Long alongside 2020Vision boss Ralph Lawson, described by its heads as “deep house in various forms and influences”, and here the label solicits the skills of veteran US producer Rick Wade for a leap into the more classic side of the genre.

With his veteran approach in the studio, it’s hard to find fault with Wade’s execution. The original mix of Sweet Life sees him working in a soulful, organic setting, building the track’s hooks around some very warm violin harmonies and acoustic guitar work. There’s a few a few cheeky nods to dancefloor disco, though it still steers clear of an overly electronic sound. Mr Beatnik’s remix brings in a few synth flourishes, without deviating too much from the original template.

Meanwhile, funk becomes the focus on The Chateau, a groovy, funk-driven cut that’s driven by its funk guitars and slap bass, with a palatable sense of joy stirring from its vocal samples. There’s a masterful use of the sampled funk drumming, with a few straight-up 4/4 house beats scattered across its 6-minute running time. Another classic cut, it’s the best moment on the EP.

Jazz Militia goes for a more functional soulful experience, perhaps the least memorable of the originals here, though no less focused in its execution. Meanwhile, label head Simon Morell hooks up with Tom Taylor for a remix of Sweet Life, which again keeps Wade’s original template largely intact, though adding a bit of dancefloor drive for a more upbeat experience.

Perhaps short on surprises, Wade delivers on the Sweet Life EP in terms of classic sounds delivered with a veteran’s precision.
