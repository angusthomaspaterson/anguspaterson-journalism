Huxley & Sam Russo - Dont Undastand Remixes EP on Leftroom

Angus Paterson · 4 February 2014 · Ibiza Voice

Huxley & Sam Russo’s Dont Undastand shows how effective simplicity can sometimes be when it comes to shaping a sharp, hard-hitting DJ tool. The original mix is a lush, soulful and fairly traditional deep house track. The jazzy elements take the fore initially, before a tougher-edged bassline is deployed to drive the momentum of the track.

The fairly stock-standard vocal sample gives the song its title, and it does a fine enough job of helping build the hypnotic atmosphere, as strings build and cascade in the background. An impressive DJ tool that doesn’t feel the need to draw on big hooks in order to make an impact.

Meanwhile, although the remix from Leftroom Records label boss Matt Tolfrey doesn’t mess too mush with the essential elements of the original’s straight-up charms, he does an admirable job of throwing them into a less conventional setting.

Twisting the original track into an eight and a half minute broken-beat jam, he waits nearly 5 minutes to drop into any kind of 4/4 house beat. An inspired approach to retaining the spirit of the original.

On the flipside, the two Honey Dijon mixes don’t add anything overly spectacular to the proceedings, bringing the vocals and the bassline to the fore on the NYC and Chi-Town Boogie mixes respectively.

The original and Tolfrey mixes are the cuts to pay attention to here.
