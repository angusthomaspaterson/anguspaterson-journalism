Onelove relocates to The Bourbon

Angus Paterson · 29 October 2009 · inthemix

While plenty of regular nights have come and gone, Onelove is one of the last bastions of weekly clubbing that continues to regularly pack it out, more than three years after it first opened in Sydney – but there’s a big change in store. The party has been going down within the four walls of Tank Nightclub since the brand kicked off in Sydney back in 2006, but the organisers have just revealed their plans to relocate to the party central that is Kings Cross, taking over the three rooms of The Bourbon every Saturday night!

Onelove at Tank has closed for business, but it’ll be reopening at The Bourbon on Saturday 21st November with the exact same crew that regulars have come to know over the past few years. There’s some big events planned for the near future, including an extra-special Stereosonic afterparty, so watch this space.

Onelove reopens at The Bourbon in Kings Cross from Saturday November 21st.
