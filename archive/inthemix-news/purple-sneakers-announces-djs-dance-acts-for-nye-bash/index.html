Purple Sneakers announces DJs + dance acts for NYE bash

Angus Paterson · 17 November 2009 · inthemix

There’s gonna be parties aplenty wherever you are this New Years Eve, but if you’re planning on rocking out Purple Sneakers style, then the Purple Sneakers NYE House Party is returning once again to the three levels of The Manning Bar. The Grates will be the headliners, alongside international guests Ponytail and an assortment of other acts, however they’ve just taken the wraps off the acts on the DJ, hip hop and dance side of things.

Regular party starters Beni, PhDJ and the Purple Sneakers DJs will be joining Elefant Tracks posse Astronomy Class, as well as guest Los Angeles hipster Franki Chan and a bunch more.
