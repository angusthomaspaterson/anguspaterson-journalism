Aquasky break back into Australia

Angus Paterson · 10 September 2007 · inthemix

They’re one of the most recognisable names in breakbeat production, and the’ll be back in the country in October for a club tour across the country. Consisting of Kieron Bailey, Dave Wallace and Brent Newitt, the trio are known as one of the most prolific production crews working in breakbeat and have notched up countless releases on a whole variety of labels, as well as collaborating with the likes of big names like Freq Nasty, The Breakfastaz, Meat Katie, Sinden and many more.

Catch Aquasky when they return to Australia, playing at the following dates…

Friday 12th October – Ambar, Perth
Saturday 13th October – GPO, Brisbane
Friday 19th October – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Saturday 20th October – Revolver, Melbourne
Wednesday 24th October – Halo, Hobart
Friday 26th October, Canberra Uni Festival, Canberra
Saturday 27th October – Mojo West, Adelaide
