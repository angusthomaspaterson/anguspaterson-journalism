Field Day 2010 goes on sale at midday

Angus Paterson · 24 September 2009 · inthemix

Sydney’s most iconic party Field Day returns for its ninth round in the sunny surrounds of The Domain on January 1st 2010, and just in case you’ve forgotten, the party goes on sale at midday today, Thursday 24th September. It’s an event that has a long history of selling out very quickly, so don’t delay in getting yours!

This year we’ll be partying with mash-up kings 2manydjs, supported by one of Australia’s most successful live acts The Presets, as well as the super- duo of Switch + Diplo who will be performing as Major Lazer Soundsystem. That’s just the start of it though, and only just the other day it was announced that Cassius, Phenomenal Handclap Band and Aston Shuffle would also be joining the lineup.

Field Day is proudly supported by inthemix in 2010! Check out our Festival Page for more info. Tickets available midday Thursday 24th September from www.fuzzy.com.au.
