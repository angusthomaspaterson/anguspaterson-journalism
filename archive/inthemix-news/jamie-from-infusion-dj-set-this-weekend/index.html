Jamie from Infusion DJ set this weekend

Angus Paterson · 27 June 2007 · inthemix

The respective crews from Wanna Break and Sound Advice are teaming up for another massive party at the Burdekin this weekend, and to make it a special occasion they’ll be hosting an exclusive set from none other than Jamie Stevens from Infusion.

Infusion need little introduction – easily Australia’s premier electronic live act, Saturday night will offer a rare opportunity to see Jamie doing his thing solo behind the decks. The party will be held across two levels of the Burdekin and will also see performances from a tasty selection of Australian talent including breaks star Dopamine and all the regular Sound Advice and Wanna Break residents, bringing you all the tech house and broken beat action you can handle.

Things kick off from 9:00pm at The Burdekin on Saturday 30th June, $15 on the door.
