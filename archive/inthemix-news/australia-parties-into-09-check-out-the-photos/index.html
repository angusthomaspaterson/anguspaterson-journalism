Australia parties into '09 - check out the photos!

Angus Paterson · 2 January 2009 · inthemix

Tens of thousands of people partied across the nation this New Years, in what was proabably the most action-packed first 24 hours of January that we’ve ever seen; enough to surpass even the madness that we saw back in 99/00 crossover. It wasn’t only the tens of thousands of people from across the nation who made the pilgramage to Melbourne for Sensation’s debut at the Telstra Dome, it was also the mainstay events like Summadayze, the massive Shore Thing and Field Day in Sydney and newcomers like BBQ Beats in Brisbane.

ITM was at every single key event across the country, and we’ve got the photos to prove it. Check out some of the action that went down in the opening hours of 2009!
