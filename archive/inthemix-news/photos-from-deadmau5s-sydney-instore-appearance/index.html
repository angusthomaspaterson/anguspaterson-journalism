Photos from Deadmau5's Sydney instore appearance

Angus Paterson · 4 December 2009 · inthemix

It’s all Deadmau5 at the moment, with the Canadian electronic whiz headlining the Stereosonic shows in Sydney and Perth over the weekend and preparing to do the same in Melbourne, Adelaide and Brisbane over the next few days. Somehow in amongst all this, he managed to find the time to shake up the Sydney CBD this week when he made an appearance at George Street’s Virgin Megastore, with a 30-minute gig and a signing for the hundreds of fans who showed up.
