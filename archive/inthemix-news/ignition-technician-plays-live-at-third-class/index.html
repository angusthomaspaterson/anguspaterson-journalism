Ignition Technician plays live at Third Class

Angus Paterson · 12 October 2007 · inthemix

UK legend Ignition Technician will be returning for his third tour of Australia, bringing his hectic techno sounds to Third Class. Also known as Richard ‘Wilko’ Wilkinson, he’s thrilled audiences the world over in the eight years since he was picked out of UK club Orbit club by Carl Cox himself. As an extremely prolific producer he’s worked alongside names like Advent, Space DJs, Angel Alanis, Glenn Wilson, Cave, Marko Nastic, Jel Ford, DJ Bam Bam, DJ Urban, Rino Cerrone, Mike Humphries, Trevor Rockcliffe, DJ Preach and DJ Misjah, racking up a massive 180 releases under the Ignition Technician name.!

Catch Ignition Technician at Third Class, Saturday November 10. Entry is $15 and it kicks off at 10pm.
