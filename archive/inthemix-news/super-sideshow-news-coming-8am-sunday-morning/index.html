Super sideshow news coming 8am Sunday morning!

Angus Paterson · 31 October 2008 · inthemix

HERE IT IS… THE NEWS YOU’VE BEEN WAITING FOR!

This summer we’re looking forward to tours from several of the world’s most revered and explosive electronic live acts, and they’re all equally known for dominating the stages of whatever club, arena or festival they’ve been booked to smash out their heavy-duty synthetic beats. If you think you know who we might be talking about, make sure that you’re back at the site this Sunday morning from 8am. ITM will be able to exclusively reveal sideshow details in Sydney and Brisbane that one of these seminal live acts will be playing over the summer festival season.

Proudly presented by ITM, the shows are certain to be two of the most anticipated big-ticket dance gigs over the summer season, and it’ll give us all a chance to catch one of dance music’s favourite acts in a more intimate context outside of their slot at one of the summer festivals. Whoever could it be? The wait isn’t long now.

Be back at ITM at 8am Sunday morning for the news at which eagerly anticipated live electronic act will be giving us some sideshow lovin’ over the summer festival season…
