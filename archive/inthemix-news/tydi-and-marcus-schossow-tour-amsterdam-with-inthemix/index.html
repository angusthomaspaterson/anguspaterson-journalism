Tydi & Marcus Schossow tour Amsterdam with inthemix

Angus Paterson · 18 March 2009 · inthemix

It was the afternoon before the Holland edition of Trance Energy, dance fans from all over Europe (and the world) were flocking towards the Jaarbeurs Utrecht arena, and ITM was there to witness it all. We thought to ourselves, what better way to warm up for the madness to come than to hit the streets of Amsterdam (and the smoke and the sleaze of the red light district) for a little bit of an impromptu tour? We’d have to bring inthemix50 #1 Tydi and tech trance bad boy Marcus Schossow along for the ride, of course!

What happened when Tydi spotted the infamous Sex Museum and Banana Bar? Just how dashing did Marcus Schossow look when he hopped into that oversized novelty clog? And what the hell is “Febo” anyway? Find out all that and more with ITM’s Amsterdam walkabout in the afternoon leading up to Trance Energy…

Amsterdam Walkabout With Tydi & Marcus Schossow
