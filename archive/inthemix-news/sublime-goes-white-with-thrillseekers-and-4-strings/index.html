Sublime goes white with Thrillseekers & 4 Strings

Angus Paterson · 22 January 2009 · inthemix

Australia’s longest running club night has undergone somewhat of a renaissance in the past few months, and things look to continue in 2009. You might already know about the return of the veteran Beatfix and Cargo brands to Home Nightclub this Friday night, but the other big deal coming up soon is Sublime going ‘white’ for one special night! The Sublime White party is locked for Friday February 6th, and the organisers promise that that Home will be transformed into “a sea of white”, with plenty of decorations, large white stars and white sails to cover every inch of the club.

Sublime will also be welcoming two extra-special international guests for the evening, in the form of uplifting trance acts 4 Strings and The Thrillseekers, the latter of which has had a particularly large 12 months with massive anthems like Waiting Here for You and The Last Time. They’ve also been backed up with the new smash-hit in waiting City of Angels. Stay tuned for the ITM interview next week…

Get white with Sublime when they transform Home Nightclub on Friday February 6th, with special guests The Thrillseekers and 4 Strings.
