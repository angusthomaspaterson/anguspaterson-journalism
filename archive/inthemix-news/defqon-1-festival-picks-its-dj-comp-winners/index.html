Defqon.1 Festival picks its DJ comp winners

Angus Paterson · 7 September 2009 · inthemix

The team from Q-dance have selected the five winners who will play at the debut of the Defqon.1 Festival in Australia later this month – with a little help from hardstyle legend Brennan Heart on the judging panel, of course! It’s less than two weeks until the party hits Sydney, and over the past few weeks there’s been a tightly-contested national DJ competition (Check out the 50+ DJ mixes in ITM-FM) to decide which hard dance hopefuls would get to play at the party, and in turn be in the running to be flown to DJ at the main event in Holland in 2010. The heats took place last month, and the five winners have now been chosen from across Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Darwin and Adelaide!

Karpe-DM won the Melbourne heats, after DJing in his local scene for over six years and supporting some of hard dance’s finest. N-Tensify was Sydney’s chosen one, with his particular love of hard trance that was born out of listening to artists like Brooklyn Bounce and Scot Project from the age of 14, while Brisbane’s Hektic lived up to his reputation for amazing scratching skills and onstage antics by securing his city’s competition.

DJ Annihilate won the prize in Adelaide for his love of the tougher-edged hardcore and hardstyle. Slightly off the beaten track, ICue won the heats in Darwin after a year as resident at his city’s most popular hard dance club, demonstrating just how far his city punches above its weight with its scene.

The five winners will each play a 30 minute set at the Defqon.1 Festival on the Purple Stage, where they will be judged by some of the party’s renowned international DJ guests as well as the Q-dance Holland’s Music Manager – with one lucky winner chosen to do their thing next year in the spiritual heartland of hardstyle that is the Netherlands!

Make sure to catch the winners when the Defqon.1 Festival comes to the Sydney International Regatta Centre on Saturday September 19th. Tickets on sale now from tickets.q-dance.com.au, check out the trailer below…
