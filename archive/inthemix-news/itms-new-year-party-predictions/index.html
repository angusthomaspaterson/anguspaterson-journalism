ITM's New Year party predictions!

Angus Paterson · 30 December 2008 · inthemix

What are your plans for the opening days of 2009? We’re heading into one of the most spectacular periods of partying that Australia has ever seen, and during the opening days of the New Year we’ll be treated to performances from an incredible selection of international and homegrown dance talent. How can you pick and choose your favourite among such an amazing selection of events?

It’s very nearly become a matter of who isn’t coming, rather than who is: it all started with the news that revered Holland event Sensation would finally be making its grand debut in Australia, followed shortly by the news that Underworld would be paying us a visit at the end of the year. What about the possibility of Armin and Ferry going back to back in Gold Coast and Perth? Even with the devastating announcement that Eric Prydz has cancelled his Australian tour, there’s still a ridiculous amount of fun times coming up over the next week.

Whatever corner of the country you’re situated in, you’re in for more excellent music than you could ever hope to catch in such a short period of time. ITM have compiled a list of what we reckon will be remembered once the dust settles, and we all reluctantly return to work on Monday January 5th. Click below to find out what stood out…
