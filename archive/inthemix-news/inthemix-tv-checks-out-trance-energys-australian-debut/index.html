inthemix.tv checks out Trance Energy's Australian debut!

Angus Paterson · 23 April 2009 · inthemix

You’ve seen the photos, read the reviews, discussed it in the forums endlessly and played back endlessly in your head your fond memories of reaching for the lasers. But now it’s time to witness all the spectacle again, because inthemix.tv was there to capture it all! Trance Energy certainly owned the Easter Long Weekend, with events across four states, and you can relive it all right now with a special episode.

ITM spoke to some of the tour’s biggest international talent on the evening of the first gig in Sydney – Dutch veteran Marco V, ‘nu-trance’ ambassador Sander van Doorn, US heavyweight Sean Tyas as well as rising star John Askew. And that’s not to mention the party footage itself: ID&T did a bang-up job of authentically recreating the Dutch experience on the tour, and inthemix.tv was there to jump right smack into the middle of the BPM-synchronised spotlight.
