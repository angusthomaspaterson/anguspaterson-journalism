Set times for Playground Weekender

Angus Paterson · 27 February 2007 · inthemix

For everyone gearing up to head to the multi-day festival out at Wisemans Ferry next weekend, Playground have released set times for across the three days as well as announced a number of new acts. Those joining the Playground Weekender lineup will include local hip hop acts Astronomy Class and The Tongue & Diaz, The Mess Hall and Operator Please rounding up the rock lineup and The Versionaries and Rephrase adding to the festival’s eclectic taste.

The first release of weekend tickets have long since sold out, with the last batch now available for $150 +BF. Camping tickets are also available at $40 +BF, while single day tickets are on sale at $89 +BF. On the accommodation front, onsite cabins, tipis, and houseboats have now sold out. There are still fully equipped tents in the Playground Tent Hotel, a few yurts remaining, and general camping for those who bring their own gear.
