Kiss My Grass returns in 2010

Angus Paterson · 12 November 2009 · inthemix

Kiss My Grass has been a mainstay in the Melbourne dance scene for a number of years, a collaboration between Kiss FM and Future Entertainment that is a show of support for the station as well as a celebration of local dance music. It all goes down over the Australia Day Long Weekend and in 2010 it’ll be back. ITM now has the details on the party that’s scheduled for Saturday 23rd of January, as well as the extensive list of DJs and live acts that’ll be providing the tunes.

Once again we can expect a diverse range of musical genres covering everything from house, electro, techno, trance, breaks, hip hop and heaps more, with a typically massive list of DJs and live acts who will be there to dish it out. Kiss FM station manager Tim Byrne says he can’t wait for the 2010 edition of Kiss My Grass. “It’s so inspiring to see thousands of people turn up to support an all local line up of DJs and live acts and to show their support for Kiss FM.”
