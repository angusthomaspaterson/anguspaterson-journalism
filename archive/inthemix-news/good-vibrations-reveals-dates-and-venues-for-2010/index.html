Good Vibrations reveals dates and venues for 2010

Angus Paterson · 5 August 2009 · inthemix

There was never any doubt that the Good Vibrations Festival would be back for another run in 2010, but we’ve now got the proof with the announcement of the dates and venues of where the party will be touching down next February. Returning to the beautiful grounds of Centennial Park in Sydney, the Parklands Showgrounds on the Gold Coast and Flemington Racecourse in Melbourne, this year they’ll be inhabiting a new space in Perth’s Claremont Showgrounds.

Last year Good Vibrations accommodated more than 110,000 people with a sellout tour across the country, and this year is set to be just as big. We’ve been told we’ll have to wait a little while longer for lineup announcements. But for those extra keen there’s a limited number of presales available for Jam Music Members from Friday.

Good Vibrations Festival 2010:

Saturday 13 February – Centennial Park, Sydney
Sunday 14 February – Claremont Showground, Perth
Saturday 20 February – Parklands Showgrounds, Gold Coast
Sunday 21 February – The Nursery, Flemington Racecourse, Melbourne
