Get yer DJ Shadow set times here!

Angus Paterson · 30 May 2007 · inthemix

Looking forward to ITM’s special gig with DJ Shadow on Friday? Then check inside for details on when he’ll be hitting the stage, now with extra Gift of the Gab. That’s right, the infamous MC from Blackalicious will be joining DJ Shadow for all of his performances on his Australian tour.

While he’s headlining the We Love Sounds festival on the Queens Birthday long weekend, Shadow’s only Sydney sideshow will also be the smallest event he’s ever played in Australia, having performed at the Hordern Pavilion on his last two tours. Support on the night will come from Mo’Funk and tickets are onsale now exclusively from inthemix.

Set times are below, and there’s still a limited amount of tickets available to this intimate gig so get in quick!

7pm- 9pm: Mo’Funk
9pm-11pm: DJ Shadow
