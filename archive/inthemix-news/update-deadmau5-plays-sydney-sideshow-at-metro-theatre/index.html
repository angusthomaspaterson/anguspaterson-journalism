UPDATE: Deadmau5 plays Sydney sideshow at Metro Theatre

Angus Paterson · 28 November 2009 · inthemix

Stereosonic headliner Deadmau5 rocked it to tens of thousands of people in Sydney on Saturday, and if you’re keen for another chance to catch him doing his thing in a more intimate setting then you’re in luck. The Canadian master of electronic mayhem will be playing an ultra-exclusive sideshow this coming Thursday night at The Metro Theatre.

What can we expect? A massive mouse head for one, but also two hours of big-room electronic mayhem that he’s showcased so perfectly on his album from this year For Lack of a Better Name. He’ll be hitting the stage at 9.30pm, with Matt Nugent and Chris Fraser on board to offer local support.

Deadmau5 hits the Metro Theatre this Thursday 3rd December. Tickets on sale now at ITM, click HERE for your chance to win tickets.
