Get yer Tiga set times here!

Angus Paterson · 6 June 2007 · inthemix

Heading along to the intimate inthemix gig with Tiga and Bang Gang tonight? Check here for set times, and we’ve also been told the headliners will be going back to back until the wee early hours of the morning…

Each ‘inthemix with…’ night is an intimate musical adventure, the series started of with one of the biggest names in electronic music in the world – Laurent Garnier. Garnier played in March this year to a not so intimate but rocking crowd at the Metro and tonight you can continue to party on with Canadian electro maestro – Tiga.

Tiga is in town for We Love Sounds tour and this will be his only Melbourne sideshow. Tiga will be joined by Bang Gang DJs and locals Andee Van Damage and Harris Robotis for what will be a rare midweek occasion. There’s also the tantalizing suggesting that Tiga and Bang Gang will be going back to back from 1am onwards… Get amongst it!

Check below for set times, tickets will be available from ITM until 5pm and there will be tickets available on the door.

8.00-9.30: Andee Van Damage
9.30-11.00: Harris Robitis
11.00-1.00: Tiga
1.00-close: Tiga vs. Bang Gang
