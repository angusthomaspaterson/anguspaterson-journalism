Mark Dynamix gets aggressive at UN this Saturday

Angus Paterson · 5 February 2007 · inthemix

Aggressive Progressive returns to Oxford Street this Saturday, bringing withy them Mark Dynamix, one of the most influential and enduring figures in Australian dance music, for a special guest appearance behind the decks. Punters will also be treated to a set from Andrew James, another stalwart of the Sydney scene.

With such a roster of proven DJ talent on hand, the organisers promise a smashing night of excellent music. The combination of Mark’s technical prowess on the decks and inspired record selection, along with and Andrew’s impressive genre-crossing abilities, giving punters a look into the past, present and future of dance.

Aggressive Progressive feat. Mark Dynamix clocks in at UN Sydney this Saturday the 10th February.
