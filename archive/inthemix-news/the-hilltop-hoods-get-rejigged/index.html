The Hilltop Hoods get rejigged

Angus Paterson · 23 April 2007 · inthemix

The Hilltop Hoods made Australian hip hop history last year when their The Hard Road debuted at #1, now they’ve re-released it with a special twist – they’ve got the Adelaide Symphony Orchestra involved. Titled The Hard Road: Restrung, it saw them returning to the studio to re-compose the entire album with the assistance of their local orchestra and arranger Jamie Messenger.

The idea stemmed from the act’s own live performances, after they had worked with the ASO String Quartet at the 2006 ARIA Awards as well as The Falls and Southbound Festivals. To accommodate the different approach on the release, certain tracks have been adapted and reworked: we can expect to hear new takes on existing songs, a brand new track called Roll On Up as well as a new guest performance from US freestyler Okwerdz who joins in on Conversations From A Speakeasy.

If you’re in Adelaide on Saturday 12th May you’ll be able to see the Hilltop Hoods take to the stage to launch the new album, when they hit the Adelaide Entertainment Centre performing live with a 31-piece Adelaide Symphony Orchestra with conducting from Benjamin Northey.

The Hard Road: Restrung hits the record stores on Saturday 12th May.
