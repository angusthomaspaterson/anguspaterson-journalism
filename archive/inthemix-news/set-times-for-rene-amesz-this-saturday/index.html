Set times for Rene Amesz this Saturday

Angus Paterson · 5 December 2008 · inthemix

Dutch producer and DJ Rene Amesz, who got his break releasing Back Into The Future on Sander Kleinenberg’s record label Little Mountain, has established a reputation for his dirty approach to house music. He’s continued to release music over the years, on labels like La Factoria, Stereo Productions, Extrema, DK Records and Deep Records, with remixes also completed for Nic Fanciulli, Lexicon Avenue, Mark Knight and more.

Check out the set times for his gig at Civic Underground this weekend, and if you want to pick up pre-sale tickets remember you can get them here on ITM!
