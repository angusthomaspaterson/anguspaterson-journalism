Guetta, Angello & Ingrosso for Pacha NYE

Angus Paterson · 5 October 2007 · inthemix

Home Nightclub has another massive NYE lineup for the end of the year with the Pacha crew returning for another massive party, and the French maestro David Guetta and Swedish house mafia lynchpins Sebastian Ingrosso and Steve Angello will also be joined by hot UK DJ/producer Dave Spoon, responsible for the smash single Girls at Night. That’s one hot lineup!

Home first brought Pacha to Sydney in New Years Eve in 2005, with that gig and last year’s both selling out to excellent reviews. Bringing a touch of Ibiza glamour to Australia, you can also expect the traditional Pacha dancers, a top local lineup all mixed in with Home’s gorgeous waterfront location and a view of the fireworks.

Tickets on sale now though ITM, 1st Release are $80+BF, 2nd Release are $90+BF while 3rd Release are $100+BF. Stay tuned for more details!
