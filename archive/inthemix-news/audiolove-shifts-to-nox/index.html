Audiolove shifts to Nox

Angus Paterson · 31 October 2007 · inthemix

Powerhouse Productions has announced that Audiolove, its first foray into over-18 events with a hard-as-nails international lineup, has been shifted from its original venue of The Hordern in Moore park to Nox Nightclub in Liverpool. As a result they’ve lowered the ticket price for the event, and they’ve assured keen punters that the party will still feature the existing six international and five local acts.

Tickets will now be $55 on the door at the club, and it’s first in best dressed to all those who don’t have their tickets already. And for those who have purchased tickets already, if you’ve paid more than the new price then you’ll be given cash back on the door at Nox Nightclub upon presentation of your ticket. 1st and 2nd release tickets won’t require a refund while the 3rd release tickets will receive a $20 refund, and final release tickets will receive a $30 cash refund.

If you would prefer a full refund for your tickets, contact the ticket agency where you purchased them from before 1pm this Saturday. For everyone else, they’ll still be enjoying the same crackin’ lineup featuring Italy’s Tatanka, hard masters from the Netherlands Luna and Pavo, trance and tech names Randy Katana and DJ TOM as well as the UK’s Alex Kidd, rounded out by a local DJ lineup.

Audiolove hits Nox Nightlcub on Saturday November 3rd, presale tickets are available from www.powerhouseproductions.com.au.
