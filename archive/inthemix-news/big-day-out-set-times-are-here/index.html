Big Day Out set times are here

Angus Paterson · 16 January 2009 · inthemix

The country’s biggest and most popular festival is startlingly close, with the Gold Coast leg of the Big Day Out going down this weekend and the other cities to follow over the coming weeks. The lineup is as wide-reaching and comprehensive as ever, but for dance fans it’ll prove to be a particularly spectacular tour with names like The Prodigy, Pendulum, Simian Mobile Disco and Hot Chip making it one of the finest artist lineups ever as far as electronic music goes.

Wherever you in the country, if you’re heading to the Big Day Out then check out the set times so you can start planning out your day!
