Nas is coming

Angus Paterson · 9 August 2007 · inthemix

Is Nas the ‘King of New York’? That’s something that’s up for debate, but one thing that’s certain is that along with Jay-Z he’s one of hip hop’s seminal MCs. And you’ll be able to catch him when he heads down to Australia in September for the ‘Hip Hop is Dead’ tour.

With a career that extends over a decade with album sales of more than 12 million, Nas has earnt himself the reputation of one of hip hop’s most respected and revered performers. From his debut 1994 album Illmatic to last year’s Hip Hop Is Dead, he’s remembered for his weighty lyricism and characteristic poetic style.

Nas is coming, and you can catch him at the following dates during September…

Friday 14th September: The Metro, Melbourne
Saturday 15th September: Metro City, Perth
Thursday 20th September: The Big Top at Luna Park, Sydney
Friday 21st September: Thebarton, Adelaide
Saturday 22 September: Arena Entertainment Complex, Brisbane
