Freedom Festival is back in November

Angus Paterson · 30 July 2009 · inthemix

Last year’s Freedom Festival at the Hordern Pavilion was one the most unique new additions to Sydney’s party calendar, bringing with it a dazzling dose of spectacle that included, dancers, pyrotechnics, a rotating main stage and all manner of sensory overload; and it’ll be back on Saturday November 7th. The promoters have secured a brand new venue that’s being kept under wraps for the moment, but has never been used for an event of this kind before.

The theme for this year’s Freedom Festival will be ‘Theme Park’, and once again it’ll be shooting for a spectacular fusion of music and entertainment, inspired by some of Europe’s most extravagant parties. It’ll feature dancers, performers, pyrotechnics, fireworks, all manner of special effects, and a host of other surprises to be revealed on the night.

While the lineup is still being kept under wraps, several international DJs will be taking the flight over for the party. The main stage will be focused on big-room house, with the second arena pumping out trance while the third stage will feature hip hop and R&B.

Freedom Festival returns to Sydney on Saturday November 7th, stay tuned to ITM for venue and lineup details shortly. For more info on last year’s event, check out ITM’s Festival Page.
