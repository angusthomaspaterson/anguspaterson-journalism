The best of Parklife 'week one' on inthemix

Angus Paterson · 30 September 2009 · inthemix

The sun is out and the Parklife festival tour finally landed over the weekend, with the first sold out events in Brisbane and Perth featuring a massive lineup of international and local talent. It’s the kind of tour that’s described by artists on the bill as a ‘travelling circus’ – you land in one city, throw a party and then pack it all up and move onto the next town while barely taking a breathe. Sounds like a tough gig, right?

With the rest of the country preparing to join the party this weekend, inthemix’s team of crack photographers and reviewers were there to capture the first two shows in all their technicoloured glory, so we’ve compiled some of the finest moments in our ‘best of week one’ Snap galleries below…

inthemix.tv was also there for the first day of the tour in Brisbane to capture what went down. In addition to party footage we’ve got interviews with MSTRKRFT, Erol Alkan, La Roux, Claude VonStroke, The Rapture, Busy P and more.
