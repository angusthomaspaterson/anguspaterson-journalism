Australia's first Parklife photos reach ITM

Angus Paterson · 27 September 2009 · inthemix

One of the biggest moments on the annual dance music calendar finally landed over the weekend, with Parklife’s debut party in Brisbane officially signalling the start of the summer festival season. Tens of thousands of people got into the party spirit at Brisbane’s sprawling Riverside venue, and you can check out the photos now on ITM!

> Parklife, Brisbane gallery
> Parklife, Perth gallery

The 30+ degree temperatures meant the day was suitably scorching, with the massive lineup meaning there more music to choose from than could possibly all be seen. Early highlights were provided by Autokratz and a massive crowd for local boys Art vs. Science, while MSTRKRFT and Busy P both kept it pumping with upfront festival sets (with the latter successfully urging all the pretty ladies to jump on the shoulders of their nearest male companion).

Empire of the Sun was the massive drawcard for many, and while the show may not have been quite as ‘big’ as Luke Steele had hyped us to believe, it sure did deliver with the sheer beauty of the visuals on display, leaving thousands satisfied. Elsewhere, Tiga closed off the Air stage with some bouncing party vibes as gusts of wind caked every last person there in an orange layer of dust.

The national Parklife tour is proudly presented by ITM! Keep your eyes peeled for loads more coverage over the duration of the tour, and check out our Festival Page at inthemix.com.au/parklife for more news, interviews, clips and DJ mixes than you can poke a stick at.
