Anthony Pappa set to smash it at Chinese Laundry 11th Birthday

Angus Paterson · 13 June 2008 · inthemix

So it’s that time of year again, is it? That’s it, one of Sydney’s oldest clubbing institutions is celebrating its birthday again, throwing a bash to mark 11 years in providing excellent music to the Sydney scene. And they can finally take the wraps off who will be its special guest for the evening… None other than Anthony Pappa!

One of Australia’s biggest DJ exports and a true don of underground house, it was only last week that Anthony Pappa rocked the house for the Sweetchilli crew at the Civic Underground. Lovers of quality house will be overjoyed at his return, and what’s more you’ll be given the chance to catch him in a different setting as well: he’ll also be playing a intimate set from 8pm to 9pm for the first 150 people who RSVP to paul@jammusic.com.au, who will also be handed a free dink and BBQ.

Chinese Laundry fanboys and fangirls unite – this will be a big one!

Chinese Laundry 11th Birthday featuring Anthony Pappa hits next Saturday 21st June, the full lineup is as follows…
