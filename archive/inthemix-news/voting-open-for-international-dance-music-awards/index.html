Voting open for International Dance Music Awards

Angus Paterson · 1 March 2007 · inthemix

This year’s installment of the International Dance Music Awards (IDMA) is set to take place on March 22 during the massive Miami Winter Music Conference, and you can now cast your vote. Over 10,000 ballots are cast every year and the night seeks to recognise the most innovative and elite producers, promoters and record labels in the industry – with a distinctive international focus of course.

There’s a huge bunch of categories to vote in with the IDMA organisers aiming to be as comprehensive as possible: covering everything from dance tracks of all genres and persuasions, club promotion and branding, mix compilations, record labels, technology and software across a whopping 45 categories. On a local level, Australia’s Dirty South is up for a gong in the Alternative Dance Track category for his remix of Evermore’s It’s Too Late.
