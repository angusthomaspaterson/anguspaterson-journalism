Life hits the harbour

Angus Paterson · 22 November 2007 · inthemix

A regular at Kings Cross hotspot Favela, the Life crew will now be taking to the high seas. The Lady Rose will again play host to a dance party on the water, the first Life party for the summer with 3 levels of house music, Sydney Harbour, sunshine and a whole bunch of friendly faces.

While upstairs the focus will be on upfront house, with everything from deep funky tunes through to the anthems, downstairs will be blaring out the proper underground house that the Life crew is well known for.

It all takes place on Sunday December 16, tickets are $40 + BF so visit www.lifemusic.com.au for more details.
