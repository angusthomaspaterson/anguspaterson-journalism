Madox and Riva Starr’s touring identity crisis

Angus Paterson · 18 July 2008 · inthemix

So… We’ve got two hot new tours on the horizon, courtesy of the Destination? crew. Cutting-edge breaks hero Madox and the blog-tastic bleepy sounds of Riva Starr. Only thing is, they’re the same person. Say what?

That’s right, Italian DJ/producer has pioneered the electro breaks sound, been racking up the Breakspoll nominations and received props form everyone from Rogue Element, Tom Real, Elite Force to Baobinga & ID. But when he’s not concocting devastating broken beats, he’s moonlighting as the notorious Riva Starr, drawing on Brazilian and Latin rhythms and mashing together a sound that takes the best parts form nu-rave, burping synth lines and bleepy bass rockin’ tunes. And you’ll be able to catch him playing under both of his pseudonyms very soon.

Catch Madox at the following dates…

25 July – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
26 July – Major Break, Perth

And catch the naughty Riva Starr at the following shows…

31 July – Halo, Hobart
02 August – Empire, Brisbane
08 August – Destination @ Ambar, Perth
09 August – The Club Club @ Chinese Laundry, Sydney
