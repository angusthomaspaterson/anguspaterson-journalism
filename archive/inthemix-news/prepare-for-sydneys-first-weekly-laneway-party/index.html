Prepare for Sydney’s first weekly laneway party

Angus Paterson · 12 October 2007 · inthemix

Art, Bitch will be taking place every week, featuring DJs, live bands, cheap drinks and located in the amazing location of a licensed laneway in the middle of the city! Music wise you can expect a diverse mix of indie, hip hop, punk, electro, swing and everything else you can think of.

It’s all brought to you from the crew who brought you The Sandwich Club, Boom-Boom Mega Boom, Sosueme and more.

Art, Bitch takes place every Saturday from 7pm till late at the Loft at UTS, down the Alley and opposite The Clare.
