Sharam Jey plays Nevermind this Saturday

Angus Paterson · 16 September 2009 · inthemix

It seems that one visit to Sydney just wasn’t enough for German DJ/producer Sharam Jey. With a tour of Asia beckoning at the end of the month King Kong records kingpin is set to treat the sound system of Nevermind with an intimate encore show this Saturday 19 September, presented by weekly Saturday night Locodisco.

As his Australian tour culminates off the back of his latest release In My Blood featuring Nick Valentino of Aussie indie rockers Lost Valentinos, the soulful soundsmith will be backed up by an impressive support team including Ben Morris, Kato and Dan Cares for the evening. With the possibility on offer of tracks from his impending second album, this is sure to be an evening of intriguing delight.
