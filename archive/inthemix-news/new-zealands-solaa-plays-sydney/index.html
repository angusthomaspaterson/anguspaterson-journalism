New Zealand’s Solaa plays Sydney

Angus Paterson · 8 May 2008 · inthemix

Courtesy of the Nice Productions crew, New Zealand act Solaa will be returning to Sydney to showcase a night of the very best in soul, funk and hip hop. The 7-piece outfit threw down their debut album Steps in Time only a few years ago, featuring some of the best vocal talents NZ has to offer including Ladi6, Scribe and Hollie Smith, and they’ve toured it across the world and received airplay in both the UK and North America.

Niche Productions present Solaa, The Civic Underground on Saturday 17th May.
