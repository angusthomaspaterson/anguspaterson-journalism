Boy 8 Bit & Feadz making mayhem together

Angus Paterson · 15 September 2009 · inthemix

We’ve seen a delightful number of dance music duos announced to be touring together in recent weeks. First it was Fergie and Slam for techno fans, then it was Jon O’Bir and Mike Foyle for the trance crew, and now electro fans are getting a whirl with the news that Boy 8 Bit will be touring with Feadz during late October and early November! While they’ll be playing a number of solo gigs, they’ll be making mayhem as a duo under the same roof on a few separate occasions.

Boy 8 Bit made his Australian debut last year as part of the Parklife lineup, known for his slamming bleepy electro. He’s worked under a range of top-secret aliases, but Boy 8-Bit has gained him the most attention due to his memorable remixes for artists like The Black Ghosts, Burial, Chromeo, Armand Van Helden and more. Feadz on the other hand is one of the heavies for France’s Ed Banger crew, as well as being right-hand man for pop princess Uffie.

Boy 8 Bit & Feadz tour dates:

Thu Oct 22: Monastery, Brisbane (Boy 8 Bit)
Fri Oct 23: Villa, Perth (Boy 8 Bit & Feadz)
Sat Oct 24: The Grand Hotel, Wollongong (Boy 8 Bit & Feadz)
Fri Oct 30: Ruby Tramp, Gold Coast (Boy 8 Bit)
Fri Oct 30: Lick It 3rd b’day @ Empire Hotel, Brisbane (Feadz)
Sat Oct 31: St Paul’s Cathedral, Adelaide (Boy 8 Bit)
Sat Oct 31: The Club Club @ Chinese Laundry, Sydney (Feadz)
Sun Nov 1: Sundae Fundaze @ Clarendon Hotel, Newcastle (Boy 8 Bit & Feadz)
Mon Nov 2: STROBE @ Billboard, Melbourne (Boy 8 Bit & Feadz)
Sat Nov 7: The Club Club @ Chinese Laundry, Sydney (Boy 8 Bit)
Sat Nov 7: Cameo Disco @ Garage Bar, Adelaide (Feadz)
