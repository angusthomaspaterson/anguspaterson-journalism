Chat live One+One with Zabiela & Fanciulli on ITM

Angus Paterson · 22 May 2007 · inthemix

Their new musical partnership is one of the most exciting things happening in dance music at the moment. With a new CD in the stores and an Australian tour immanent, we’ll have James Zabiela and Nic Fanciulli live and online at 6pm EST on Wednesday June 6th to talk about the One+One concept.

The idea for One+One was actually born in Sydney back in ‘05 on Home Nightclub’s Terrace at a legendary Renaissance party, when Zabiela and Fanciulli – already loved and respected DJs in their own right – went back to back for an epic sunrise set. The first One+One CD is in the stores and their musical union is already being compared to that of the iconic Sasha & Digweed, but you’ll be able to see for yourself in June when they tour nationally as part of the We Love Sounds festival.

Fanboys rejoice as ITM will be hosting them for the next edition of ‘in the spotlight’, where you’ll get the chance to ask them whatever you fancy headed of their festival appearances over the Long Weekend. Everyone who logs in will have the chance to win some tasty One+One goodies, so mark it down in your diary and head to inthemix.com.au/chat/inthespotlight on Wednesday June 6th at 6pm EST to participate.
