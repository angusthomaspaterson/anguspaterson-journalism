Big Daddy plays Beware the Cat

Angus Paterson · 23 March 2007 · inthemix

YU’s weekly Saturday night keeps the big names coming with Big Daddy from Playtime Records to play a set of his trademark playful ‘wonky’ electro. Scheduled for Saturday March 31st after the V Festival has wound up, it’ll be another big night for Beware the Cat after Claude Von Stroke smashed the venue to pieces last weekend.

Big Daddy is credited as one of the founders of the ‘wonky electro’ scene in London: a collective that starts with his Playtime Records label and his two clubs Playtime and GEEK, as well as the worldwide group of DJs that like to give the electro sound a bit of a different spin. There’s Playtime co-founder Mike Monday and local boys *Cheshire Catz, as well as artists like King Roc, Cass and Mangan and New York’s Kerowack – all grooving to a single, wonkier beat.

Be at Beware the Cat at YU next Saturday 31st March to find out just what this ‘wonky’ electro sound is all about!
