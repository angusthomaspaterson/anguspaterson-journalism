Diesel-U-Music starts its search for Australia's best

Angus Paterson · 30 July 2009 · inthemix

The Diesel-U-Music initiative has already been responsible for digging up some excellent talent worldwide, bringing us massive names like Mylo, Diplo and The Cool Kids, and the Italian denim specialist has kicked off its search across Australia for our most talented and innovative musicians. D:U:M is looking to pick two winners to support the international winners at the Australian leg of the tour in November, and they’ll also be rewarded with a festival gig in 2010 along with $2500 worth of Diesel threads.

D:U:M has handpicked an impressive selection of judges to oversee the competition, including Grinspoon frontman Phil Jamieson, Nova presenter Maz Compton and a music writers from a range of publications like Rolling Stone, Vice and the Sunday Telegraph.

The competition is open now until the 13th September, with the winners announced on 1st October. The D:U:M event the two winners will perform at reaches Sydney on the Thursday 26th November.

Check out www.dieselumusic.com for your chance to be in a winner.
