More Trance Energy photos on ITM

Angus Paterson · 11 April 2009 · inthemix

The world’s biggest trance event continued its run debut through Australia on Friday, with Trance Event landing for a daytime event in Calder Park. Check out ITM’s photos of all the lights, lasers and international DJ action from both Sydney and Melbourne, and stay tuned for coverage on the remaining parties in Brisbane and Perth…

>Trance Energy photos Melbourne.
>Trance Energy photos Sydney.

Like the weekend’s first event in Sydney in Friday night, Melbourne punters enjoyed a diverse lineup that spread across all the trance scene has to offer – a mashup of deep progressive and hands-in-the-air anthems from BT, touch techy beats from Marco V and Sander van Doorn, and driving euphoria from the kings of the next generation Richard Durand, Simon Patterson and Sean Tyas.

The excitement surrounding Melbourne’s party was marred by a reported 26 people taken to hospital suffering from suspected drug overdoses, check HERE for more details.

Keep your eyes peeled to ITM over the next week for more Trance Energy coverage, and check out our Festival Page for loads of related news, interviews, mixes, clips and more.
