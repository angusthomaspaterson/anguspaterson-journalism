Argyle takes over the APEC long weekend

Angus Paterson · 31 August 2007 · inthemix

Are you a lover of the SHE brand of soulful house? Well you’ll have more than you could possibly handle next APEC long weekend as the crew along with Paul Strange and Ministry of Sound will be taking over The Argyle for a massive three days of partying. The ‘APEC Weekender’ will see three events take place across Friday, Saturday and Sunday and each event will start at midday and go right through to 3am. That’s a massive 15 hours of partying every day!

Friday will feature Mark John from Melbourne along with SHE resident DJs, Saturday will be a special Hed Kandi event featuring Jack McCord and a massive local roster while Sunday will feature La Fiesta Sound System, Alex Almeida and more. Get busy!

The organisers emphasise that The Argyle is outside the exclusion zones for APEC so there will be no blockades around the venue, allowing you to travel freely to and from the event.

Wanna know the best thing of all? It’s all free! Just head to www.paulstrange.com.au or www.shesydney.com to register.
