Good Vibrations reveals local performers

Angus Paterson · 8 December 2009 · inthemix

With the 2009 festival season drawing to a close, we’re all starting to look forward to what’s happening next year so it’s great timing that the Good Vibrations Festival has taken the wraps of the list of local performers who well be seeing on next year’s tour. There’s an extensive selection of local favourites locked for performances in each city, from Aston Shuffle and Cassette Kids in Sydney to Sampology and Kid Kenobi on the Gold Coast, to stalwarts Grant Smillie and John Course in Melbourne.

Check out the full list below, and Jam Music have also announced that Salt N Pepa turntable performer DJ Spinderella will also be delivering a solo performance at the festival.

Sydney locals: 1 Man Army, Act Yo Age, Agent 86, Cassette Kids, A-Tonez, Club Junque, Derty Rich vs Naany, DJ Just 1, Dolso, Graz, Gus Da Hoodrat, Jeff Drake, Jimmy 2 Sox & Johnny Pow!!, Kid Kenobi, Little Bambalam, Marky Mark & King Lee, Minx, Ro Sham Bo, Sam Scratch, Samrai, Spit Syndicate, Steve Lind, Tenzin, The Aston Shuffle, The Hump Day Project, Watussi, Wax Motif, Yolanda Be Cool

Perth locals: Agent 86, Bad Weather, Ben Mac, Charlie Bucket, Death Disco DJs, Fdel (live), Hickey, Kit Pop, Mama Says Yes!, Marty McFly, Micah, Mickey Juice, Mind Electric, Minx, Randa & the Soul Kingdom, Rekab, Samrai, Shazam, Signal Drivers, Steve Lind, Tenzin, Tim & Jean, Total Knowledge, Wax Motif

Gold Coast locals: Afro Dizzi Act, Agent 86, Aniki, Benny Electric, Chris Wilson, James Canning, Jason Morley, Jimmy Vegas, Kid Kenobi, Minx, Neroli, Nick Galea, Reekay Garcia, Rhythm & Cutloose, Sampology, Samrai, Skool of Thought, Steve Lind, Surecut Kids, Tenzin, The Cool Calm Collective, Tijuana Cartel, Vans V Cons, Wax Motif, Wongo

Melbourne locals: Agent 86, Andy Murphy, Boogs, Chardy, Denzal Park, Diafrix, DJ Kuya, DJ Perplex, Grant Smillie, Hercules in NY, John Course, Luke Chable, M.A.F.I.A., Matt Cant, Minx, Mobin Master, Ooh Ee, Phil K, Samrai, Slap n Dash, Steve Lind, Streetparty DJs, Tenzin, Those Usual Suspects, Wax Motif

For all your Good Vibrations needs, check out ITM’s Festival Page for loads of news, interviews and more. National dates below…
