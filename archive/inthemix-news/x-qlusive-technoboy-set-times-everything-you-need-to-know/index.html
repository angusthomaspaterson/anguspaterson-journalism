X-Qlusive Technoboy set times + everything you need to know!

Angus Paterson · 15 December 2009 · inthemix

This weekend, the Australian Q-dance team will be following up their Defqon.1 triumph from earlier this year with another installment of their X-Qlusive parties – where a particularly special member of the global hard dance fraternity is showcased, and given the reigns to host the evening. Italy’s Technoboy will be taking control at Sydney’s Hordern Pavilion this Saturday night, and ITM now has the set times for the party so you can start planning out your evening, as well as the essential ‘all you need to know’ information.

Known as a ‘god’ in hardstyle circles, Italian fashion maestro Technoboy definitely has a reputation as one of the best dressed DJ/producers working in hard dance, as well as one of the most proficient in the studio. He put these skills to good use when he hammered the European crowds at X-Qlusive Technoboy in Amsterdam earlier this year, and you can be sure he’ll be bringing his A-game for the Australian edition this Saturday night.
