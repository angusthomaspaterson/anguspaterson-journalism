Encore set for Tom Middleton this Sunday

Angus Paterson · 20 March 2008 · inthemix

Did you miss out on the chance to catch eclectic DJ master Tom Middleton playing gigs at either the Playground Weekender or the Renaissance party last weekend? Or maybe you’re just keen for an opportunity to catch the man playing another one of his rare acid house sets? Well your prayers have been answered, because he’s been locked in by the Lost Baggage crew to play at a special once-off Boutique Baggage event this Sunday night at the event’s home in The Cross.

Want another taste of Tom Middleton? Boutique Baggage hits The Cross on Sunday for some acid house beats, $20 on the door.
