Deadmau5's Sydney sideshow photos

Angus Paterson · 23 February 2009 · inthemix

Fans of the Beatport dominating Deadmau5 in Sydney were treated to an extra-special opportunity to catch him once again following his Good Vibrations performances last weekend. On Friday night he played to an absolutely rammed and heaving crowd at The Forum, culminating with a touch of crowd-surfing towards the end of the show and a special message to the crowd at the very end via his laptop. “Second best gig ever. That actually means a lot.” Check out ITM’s photo gallery.
