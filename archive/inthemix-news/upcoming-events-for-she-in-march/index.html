Upcoming events for SHE in March

Angus Paterson · 25 February 2008 · inthemix

Sydney’s premier soulful house crew SHE have a number of events coming up to keep their fans happy in March: firstly, a rooftop party in the scenic surrounds of Circular Quay, as well as a massive Easter bonanza just over a week later with several international headliners that should make for a massive long weekend gig.

First up is the ‘Rooftop Party’ at Circular Quay on the top floor of the Overseas Passenger Terminal, kicking off on the Saturday afternoon of March 15th from 4pm. Sporting spectacular panoramic views of Sydney Harbour as well as the CBD skyline, with regular residents such as Graham Cordery and Nick Vidal to be joined by international DJ/producer Richard Earnshaw.

But the big-ticket event happens at The Argyle the following Sunday March 23rd of the Easter Long Weekend, with a massive lineup of guests that includes Julie McKnight, famous for contributing her vocal chords to the Kings of Tomorrow classic Finally, a DJ set from Timmy Vegas in the UK as well as the return of the ‘Godfather of House’ himself Marshall Jefferson who will be playing his encore gig for SHE after visiting Australia for the very first time last year.

Keen for another dose of SHE? The crew hits the Overseas Passenger Terminal on Saturday March 15th, and then returns to The Argyle on the following Sunday March 23rd. Check the Whats On for more details.
