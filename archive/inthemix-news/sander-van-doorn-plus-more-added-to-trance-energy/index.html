Sander Van Doorn plus more added to Trance Energy

Angus Paterson · 19 January 2009 · inthemix

So you thought the Trance Energy lineup couldn’t get any hotter? How about the addition Sander Van Doorn, Agnelli & Nelson, John Askew, Leon Bolier and many more added to the lineup? All ITM can say is ‘holy crap’.

Even before the final announcement of details, Trance Energy was already gearing up to scorch your Easter holiday plans with some of the world’s hottest trance acts, along with all the over-the-top spectacle we’ve come to expect from the company behind Sensation. Earlier this month ID&T announced the first round lineup of acts who’d be playing at the Australian debut, and it’d be an understatement to say it received a positive response from ITMers. “Frothing at the mouth” would be a more appropriate way to describe the reaction, and we can now share full details of the tour!

Holland’s epic Trance Energy event has just reached the milestone of a decade, with some 30,000 punters rocking out to its sounds every year. Not unlike ID&T’s other flagship event Sensation, it’s just as much about the spectacle, the set pieces and the stunning production as it is the artists and music, but there’s little doubt they’ve pulled a hefty rabbit out of a hat with the lineup for the first Australian tour. It’s a genuine ‘who’s who’ of the global trance scene, with a solid mix of veteran performers and new talent that ticks all the boxes – euphoric, progressive, tech and much more.

Excited yet? Check out the national dates and venues…

Thur 9th April – Hordern Pavilion & Surrounds, Sydney
Fri 10th April – Calder Park Thunderdome, Melbourne
