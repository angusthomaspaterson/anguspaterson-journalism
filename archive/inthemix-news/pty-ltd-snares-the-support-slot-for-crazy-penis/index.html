PTY LTD snares the support slot for Crazy Penis

Angus Paterson · 21 December 2006 · inthemix

With a new album on the way, 2007 was already shaping up to be a big year for Brisbane three-piece live act PTY LTD. And it appears they’ll be kicking it off in style with the news that the group has scored the much coveted support act for Crazy Penis on their scheduled shows in Melbourne, Hobart and Sydney during January.

Made up of Tim Tainsh, Peter G. and Daniel Tainsh, PTY LTD have played the live circuit around Australia constantly over the last four years with a gig checklist that includes the Good Vibrations, Woodford, Falls and Parklife festivals. They can also boast to have supported wide range of overseas acts like Moloko, Dimitri from Paris, Mark Farina and JJ Hernandez. The band ahs just completed their new album Enticer Deluxe and are preparing their new single Make Amends to follow up Trying To Leave, which reached the Top 10 in the ARIA Club Charts.

PTY LTD can be seen supporting Crazy Penis at the following shows:
5th January: Melbourne, HiFi Bar
6th January: Hobart, Telegraph Hotel
7th January: Sydney, Hyde Park Barracks
