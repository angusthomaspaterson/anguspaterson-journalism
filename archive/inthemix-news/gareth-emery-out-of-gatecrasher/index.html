Gareth Emery out of Gatecrasher

Angus Paterson · 25 October 2007 · inthemix

Trance fans will be crying into their copy of the Podcast Annual 2007 with the news that rising trance star Gareth Emery (otherwise known as GTR) will be no longer appearing at the upcoming Gatecrasher shows across the country. His agents have sent their apologies, but ‘Crasher’ fans can rest easy with the assurance that remaining international stars Matt Hardwick, Ronald Van Gelderen and Dave Lee have promised to “smash it up” in true Gatecrasher style!

If you needed any other encouragement to head along to the shows, Ronald Van Gelderen himself called the Home Nightclub promoters this morning after hearing the news to touch base and tell them that he is “gonna rock Home this Friday!”
