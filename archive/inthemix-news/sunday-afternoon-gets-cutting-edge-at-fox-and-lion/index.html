Sunday afternoon gets cutting edge at Fox & Lion

Angus Paterson · 12 October 2007 · inthemix

Every Sunday afternoon at the Fox and Lion from 2pm will be the place to be if you’re after the latest slice of cutting edge European music, DJ Richie Valens providing the soundtrack. One of the residents and founding forces behind the legendary Late Night Shuffle, he’ll be bringing that same vibe to the Fox & Lion every week.

Rock on down to the Fox & Lion every Sunday from 2pm, DJ Angela Maison will be joining on the last Sunday of the month and there’s a free BBQ from 5pm.
