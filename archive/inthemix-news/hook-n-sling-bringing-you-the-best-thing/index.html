Hook N Sling bringing you the best thing

Angus Paterson · 7 July 2008 · inthemix

Digging up an old Boom Crash Opera classic that while everyone’s forgotten it, the hook still well and truly lurks in our collective consciousness? Inspired. In fact, it’s The Best Thing and Sydney producer extraordinaire Hook N Sling will be throwing a party at the Civic Underground on Saturday August 2nd to mark the release of the catchy new single.

Originally known for his presence in the breaks scene but since then successfully tried his hand at nearly every genre of house music under the sun, Hook N Sling AKA Anthony Maniscalco is one if the biggest recent successes in Australian dance music. Recruited by Ministry of Sound earlier this year and chosen to mix one half of the Clubbers Guide to 2008 CD, his powers of persuasion are nothing to be scoffed at; he deftly convinced Dale Ryder from Boom Crash Opera to re-record the vocals from The Best Thing for his new single.

Wanna know more? Catch Hook N Sling telling us all about it in the latest edition of inthemix.tv.

Catch Hook N Sling launching The Best Thing at Civic Underground on Saturday 2nd August, with support form Sam La More and Goodwill. $10 before 11pm and $20 thereafter.
