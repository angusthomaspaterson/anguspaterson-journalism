Get your mug plastered on a Good Vibrations poster!

Angus Paterson · 13 November 2009 · inthemix

If you’ve ever wanted to be one of those happy, smiley, sunnies-clad (not to mention colour filtered) girls or guys on the posters for the Good Vibrations Festival, the good folks over at Jam are now giving you the chance. A rather unusual (but very cool) promo over at the Good Vibrations website is allowing you to do exactly that; all you have to do it upload your photo, and hey presto… it’s your beautiful mug all over a Good Vibrations poster!
