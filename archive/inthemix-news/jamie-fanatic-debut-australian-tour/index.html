Jamie Fanatic debut Australian tour

Angus Paterson · 16 January 2009 · inthemix

Joining the growing list of Dutch house superstars that already includes the likes of Fedde Le Grand and Laidback Luke, fresh-faced DJ/producer Jamie Fanatic will be making his debut visit to Australia later this month. His work with Southern Fried Records has lead to remixes for Fat Boy Slim, Armand Van Helden and Calvin Harris, and he’s received plenty of support from a diverse selection of heavyhitters that includes the liks of Diplo, Junior Sanchez, Felix the Housecat, John Aquaviva and Crookers. How’s that for comphrehensive?

Catch Jamie Fanatic on the following tour dates…

23rd January – The Manor, Perth
24th January – One Love, Sydney
28th January – Whalers Inn, Warrnambool
30th January – Lot 33, Canberra
31st January – One Love, Melbourne
