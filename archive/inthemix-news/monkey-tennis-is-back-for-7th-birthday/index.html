Monkey Tennis is back for 7th Birthday

Angus Paterson · 28 May 2009 · inthemix

After a little time off after their last gigs at The Cross, the Monkey Tennis crew are back with their 7th Birthday Winter Ball and they’ll be bringing a different twist on their typical approach with an outdoor day event. On Saturday June 13th they’ll be taking over the rooftop terrace of The Blacket Hotel with an afternoon event that will go from 1pm to 11pm.

With a lineup that includes some of the best local talent that has helped Monkey Tennis hold court over the years, it should be enough to being the old regulars out of the closet as well as a few new faces too.

Monkey Tennis 7th Birthday Winter Ball hits the Blacket Hotel Saturday June 13th from 1pm to 11pm.
