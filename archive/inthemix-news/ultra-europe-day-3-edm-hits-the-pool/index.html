Ultra Europe Day 3: EDM hits the pool

Angus Paterson · 15 July 2013 · inthemix

After two long nights at the Poljud Stadium in Split, which entertained tens of thousands of partiers from around the world, Ultra Europe shifted on Sunday over to the pristine island of Hvar for a more exclusive event. Set by the pool of a luxury hotel, an intimate crowd of several thousand were treated to some of the most amped-up, turbo-charged music heard all weekend.

With proceedings kicking off at 3pm, Swedish duo Cazzette stepped up early on for a repeat performance after their main stage showing on Friday, setting the tempo for what was to come. Next up were Australian sisters Nervo who delivered the surprise set of the day – main-room euphoria, packed with all the hands-in-the-air moments of a trance set.

From there, bombastic sounds were the theme for the rest of the afternoon, as the pool heaved with bodies. After Dada Life and Chuckie, it was over to L.A. showman Steve Aoki, who played possibly the most over-the-top set of Ultra Europe. If Saturday night was about the different sounds invading the mainstage, then Sunday made no concessions: it was high-octane EDM all the way. For the sprawl of buffed, tanned and bikini-ed dancers, that was exactly the soundtrack the occasion called for.
