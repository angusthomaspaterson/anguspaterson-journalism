Pole Folder back in May

Angus Paterson · 7 April 2009 · inthemix

Belgium progressive stalwart Pole Folder will be back in the country alter this month for another round of club dates. He’ll be coming hot on the heels of starting his own label called La Tour, the release schedule of which has so far included his track The Way, championed by Sasha at the WMC, with releases to follow shortly from Charlie May among others. Get down to catch one of progressive’s most enduring DJ/producers…

Pole Folder tour dates…

Saturday April 18th – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Friday May 1st – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Saturday May 2nd – Shape, Perth
