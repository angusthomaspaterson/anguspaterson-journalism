Check out the Field Day photos & review!

Angus Paterson · 2 January 2008 · inthemix

Were you at Field Day, Sydney’s biggest and most iconic New Years Day event? The inthemix photographers were, and we’ve already got hundreds of photos online along with the definitive review from ITM’s own Jack Tregoning. Check em out now! You can search for your mug in the photo gallery while you can relive the event in the written word format HERE.

Following in the tradition that’s been building for a few years, in 2008 there were more live acts at Field Day than ever before, along with a broad range of musical styles. There was something for everybody on January 1st – from house and electro through to hip-hop, breaks, tech and more. Punters enjoyed live sets from Canadian electro-funk duo Chromeo, UK indie rockers the Go! Team, Baltimore’s finest Spank Rock along with a bunch of world-class DJs like Angello & Ingrosso, Claude Von Stroke, Felix Da Housecat, Stanton Warriors and more. And from all reports, a massive amount of fun was had by all!

Can’t let the past? Check out our Festival Page with a whole bunch of news, DJ mixes and interviews with the acts that performed at Field Day. And check out his clip from Felix Da Housecat’s performance…
