Set times revealed for Sunbeats NYD at Coogee

Angus Paterson · 19 December 2006 · inthemix

UK superstar DJ Judge Jules is the big drawcard for this New Years Day party at Selinas near Cooogee Beach, and set times have just been released for punters who plan to be in attendance. The venue is only 20 metres away from Coogee Beach, and the promoters are billing the event as the perfect setting to welcome in the New Year with a huge indoor main room and a gorgeous outdoor courtyard.

Judge Jules is no doubt one of the most recognised DJs in the world, with a successful show over many years on the UK’s seminal Radio 1 network. He’s also headlined every major dance event across the globe from Creamfields, Glastonbury, Homelands, Gatecrasher, Godskitchen and so on.

Tickets are $70 including booking fee and can be purchased on inthemix HERE, and the set times are as follows:

12:00-1:00: Damien Spencer
1:00-3:00: Trix V Godfella
3:00-4:00: John Devecchis
4:00-5:00: Illya
5:00-6:00: Ben Morris
6:00-7:00: John Glover
7:00-9:00: Homegrown
9:00-10:00: Potbellez
10:00-11:00: Scott Richardson V Cameron O’Shea
11:00-1:00: Judge Jules
1:00-2:00: Nik Fish V Amber Savage
2:00-3:00: Jumping Jack v Pee Wee Ferris
