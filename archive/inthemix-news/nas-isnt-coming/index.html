Nas isn’t coming

Angus Paterson · 9 November 2007 · inthemix

Hip hop fans aren‘t having the best of weeks, after discovering that New York hip hop star Nas aint coming to Australia after all, rescheduling his tour for the second time this year. The reasons given for the cancellation have been related to commitments for his upcoming album release. Dang!

New dates for March and April next year will be apparently announced shortly, and all tickets will remain valid for the rescheduled tour. Stay tuned.
