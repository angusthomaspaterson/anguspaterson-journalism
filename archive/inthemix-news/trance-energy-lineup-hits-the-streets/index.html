Trance Energy lineup hits the streets!

Angus Paterson · 5 January 2009 · inthemix

Barely did we even get the chance to recover from all our NY shenanigans, before being called to cast our mind forward to Easter for what will no doubt be one of 2009’s most anticipated tours. Check below for the first round of the Trance Energy lineup for details on who’ll we’ll be listening to over the Easter long weekend!

For any of the 40,000 punters who rocked it at the Telstra Dome in Melbourne for Sensation on NYE, but would have liked a little (or a lot) more ‘trance’ on the lineup, their dreams are set to come true this Easter. Trance Energy comes direct from legendary Dutch production ID&T, the same crew responsible for Sensation, and with Trance Energy’s debut lineup in Australia they’ll be packing a lineup that well exceed everybody’s expectations.

Sensation resident Marco V will be returning for the tour in April, along with an appearance from big-room veteran Judge Jules and electronic pioneer BT; but he’ll also be joined by an unbeatable posse of ‘nu-trance’ heroes that includes Simon Patterson, Sean Tyas, Richard Durand and many more. That’s only the beginning though, because ID&T are of course famous for employing some of the most cutting-edge production, lasers and special effects seen anywhere in the world; and anyone who was at Sensation on NYE would have seen them in full effect. Start building up those biceps, because come April you’ll be reaching for the lasers…
