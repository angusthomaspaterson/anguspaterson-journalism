Second Sydney Big Day Out announced!

Angus Paterson · 7 October 2009 · inthemix

After the rapid sell-out of Big Day Out in Sydney this morning, organisers have announced they’re adding a second show. The same line-up will return to Sydney Showgrounds the following day – Saturday 23 January – for another day in the sun.

It’s only the second time in the festival’s history that two Big Day Outs have run in one city. 23 January will be the 100th Big Day Out staged, making this quite a momentous occasion. Expect a big party atmosphere.

Stay tuned to ITM’s Big Day Out Festival Page as the tour approaches, check out the details for the Sydney shows below…

Friday January 22nd – SYDNEY – Sydney Showground – SOLD OUT!
Saturday January 23rd – SYDNEY – Sydney Showground – ON SALE MON OCT 12
