Danny Tenaglia locked for epic 6-hour set this Thursday!

Angus Paterson · 5 January 2010 · inthemix

Future Entertainment and Nevermind have made all our New Years dreams come true. This Thursday the inimitable Danny Tenaglia will be flying into Sydney for an intimate set at one of Sydney’s finest clubbing establishments, courtesy of the One Night Only brand, and we won’t be getting no measly two-hour set. Six hours of Danny Tenaglia, baby.

Danny will be riding high after his sets on the Summadayze tour over the weekend, as well as a special NYE performance at Sydney’s Shore Thing. Before that, it’d been more than a decade since we’d seen him in the country. The news of his tour was met with joy when it was announced late last year, but we’ve all been hanging out for a chance to catch him in an intimate setting. Well here’s your chance!

Danny got his start in New York way back in the 70s and has been a seminal name across dance culture since then, but he’s showing no signs of slowing down yet. He went into detail recently in his interview with ITM. “There is no doubt that I will continue to share my love of music with the world in some way, shape or form. There are so many directions that I could go with this and really, who’s to tell what technologies or other outlets there will be available for me to do this in the future.”

Danny Tenaglia will play Nevermind this Thursday 7th January. Tickets go on sale from 10am Wednesday 6th January with a strict limit of four tickets per person, exclusively on ITM!
