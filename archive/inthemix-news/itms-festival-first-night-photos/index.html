ITM's Festival First Night photos

Angus Paterson · 12 January 2009 · inthemix

It was equal parts exhilarating fun and borderline mayhem on the streets of Sydney on Saturday night, when Festival First Night returned to launch the hugely popular Sydney Festival celebrations for the month of January. Estimated crowds of 300,000 flocked to party in the streets to check out the action, which saw Grace Jones hit the stage in The Domain to huge acclaim, as well as local promoters Fuzzy hosting two dance stages nearby that saw performances from Santogold, A-Trak and Busy P among others. Check out the ITM photo gallery!

> Sydney Festival First Night photo gallery

While the vast sea of punters gathered in The Domain were for the most part civilised, it was a slightly more anarchic affair across the other city streets. The party threatened to descend into chaos when a huge amount of people descended on College Street prior to Santogold performing, leading police and security to shut the stage down, before the barriers eventually collapsed and saw hundreds of people streaming into the space. But what’s a street party without a touch of excitement anyway?
