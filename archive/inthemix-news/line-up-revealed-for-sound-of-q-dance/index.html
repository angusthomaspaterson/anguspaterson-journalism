Line-up revealed for Sound of Q-Dance

Angus Paterson · 5 February 2009 · inthemix

The Q-Dance brand has well and truly risen to dominate the hard dance scene in Australia over the past 12 months, following its introduction to the hardstyle masses over the 2008 Easter break, and then the X-Qlusive tour that followed in December. Now, with little warning it’ll back in late March for a one-off Sound of Q-Dance show in Sydney, again featuring the cream of the world’s hard dance crop.

Taking the “Harder Styles in Dance Music” to the world, this year Q-Dance will be celebrating the 10th anniversary of its debut in the Netherlands. Their World Tour 2009 has already kicked off in France and there’s dates scheduled for countries right across the world including Ireland, Scotland, Italy, Norway, Sweden and more. The next Australian event will hit the Hordern Pavilion in Sydney on Saturday 21st of March, and it promises to be yet another banging party.

The Sound of Q-Dance takes over the Hordern Pavilion in Sydney on Saturday 21st of March, tickets on sale now. Check below for the lineup:
