Fuzzy & Fag Tag present Wildlife this NYE

Angus Paterson · 22 October 2007 · inthemix

In an event that will see Sydney’s leading dance music promoter Fuzzy teaming up with one of its most successful gay & lesbian crews Fag Tag, Wildlife will be taking over the Hordern this New Years Eve and will feature headline sets from Frankie Knuckles, Freemasons and Stonebridge.

Yes, the godfather of house himself Frankie Knuckles is returning to Sydney. As the pioneer of the genre that he helped create, Knuckles has sat at the forefront of house music for the past few decades, knocking out hit after hit and travelling all over the globe. Knuckles was recently inducted into the Dance Music Hall of Fame, has won Grammy Awards, revolutionised the clubbing scene in New York and Chicago, and will be bringing this world of experience to Fag Tag and Fuzzy present Wildlife on NYE and take you on a journey as only he know how.

Joining Frankie Knuckles on the main stage at the Hordern are crowd favourites, the Grammy award-winning Freemasons. Comprised of producers James Wiltshire and Russell Small (also from Phats & Small ), the Freemasons have had a string of number one hits from Watchin to Rain Down Love, and created some of the hottest remixes to ever hit a dance floor, like Beyonce’s Ring The Alarm and Most Precious Love by Blaze feat. Barbara Tucker. They’ll be heating up the dancefloor at Wildlife on NYE at the Hordern.

Joining Frankie Knukles and Freemasons is Stonebridge. Best known for for his seminal club anthems Put ‘Em High and Take Me Away, the Swedish DJ knows how to get a party started and will be bringing this skill to the Hordern this New Year’s Eve. Joining them will be a host of local DJs, sexy entertainment and other wild surprises.

Wildlife is on at the Hordern on NYE from 10pm to 8am. Tickets go on sale Thursday November 1st, check out ITM’s sibling site (or unspecified gender) Same Same for all the details!
