ITM's The Likes of You photos

Angus Paterson · 18 May 2009 · inthemix

Future Entertainment’s flagship progressive/minimal brand of events made an adventure outside of its hometown of Melbourne over the weekend, with The Likes You debuting in Sydney on Saturday night alongside Friday night’s party at Brown Alley, with Hernan Cattaneo and Loco Dice providing the tunes. Check out ITM’s photo galleries capturing all the action!

> Melbourne
> Sydney

The visits of both Hernan Cattaneo and Loco Dice was the subject of much hype of the leadup to last weekend. Hernan inspires a certain kind of reverence and adulation in his fans, who were salivating at the thought of seeing him again after enduring nearly a two-year wait between tours, while for Loco Dice it counted as the first time he’d ever visited Australia, mentioned in February as one of ITM’s Most Wanted Tours of 2009.
