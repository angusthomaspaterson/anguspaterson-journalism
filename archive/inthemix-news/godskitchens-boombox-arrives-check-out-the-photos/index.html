Godskitchen's Boombox arrives, check out the photos!

Angus Paterson · 6 October 2009 · inthemix

Global clubbing brand Godskitchen revealed it’s entry into the audio-visual spectacle status-quo earlier with the ‘Boombox’, and on Sunday night it arrived in Australia with its first gig in Sydney. The soundtrack was provided by Cosmic Gate, John ‘00’ Fleming and Blank & Jones (who all also appeared at an intimate club gig in Brisbane on Friday), and from all reports the Boombox lived up to all the hype.
