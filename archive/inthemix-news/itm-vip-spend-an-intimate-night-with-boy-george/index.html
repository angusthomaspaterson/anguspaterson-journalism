ITM-VIP: Spend an intimate night with... Boy George

Angus Paterson · 28 February 2007 · inthemix

The big star of this year’s Mardigras festival will be playing only one other gig while he is in Sydney – and the only way you’re gonna get to be part of it is if you’re an ITM VIP member. Boy George will be playing to an exclusive industry crowd Sunday night at the Privilege Bar, the event is not open to the public but ITM-VIP members (and their friends) can gain entry simply by showing their ITM-VIP card at the door. Boy George will be supported by Lisa Loud, one of the UK’s top female DJs.

But that’s not all… ITM is also running a competition that will give VIP members the chance to meet Boy George, click HERE to enter.

Boy George came to fame with Culture Club during the 1980s, turning out a massive line of hits including Do You Really Want to Hurt Me? and Karma Chameleon. He started DJing in the early 90’s and has been responsible for a huge number of successful mix compilations for legendary rave/house promoters Fantazia as well as Ministry of Sound. A gay icon in every sense of the word, he’s the guest of honour at this year’s Mardigas Festival.

The night kicks off at 8pm at the Privilege Bar. Tickets are $20 each, you must show your valid ITM-VIP card at the door or be accompanied by someone with an ITM-VIP card to gain entry.
