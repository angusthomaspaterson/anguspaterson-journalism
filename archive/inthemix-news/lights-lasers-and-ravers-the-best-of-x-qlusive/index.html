Lights, lasers & ravers - the best of X-Qlusive!

Angus Paterson · 23 December 2008 · inthemix

Were you at one of the X-Qlusive events that went down over the weekend? For fans of the harder styles of dance it was their equivalent of all the hype and hoopla surrounding Sensation’s debut in Australia, but even if hardstyle isn’t your cup of tea it’s not hard to appreciate all the spectacle that surrounds the parties from the Dutch Q-Dance crew. Giant visual screens, lights, lasers and of course, thousands of stomping ravers!

ITM had some of its finest snappers along at the Sydney leg of X-Qlusive last Friday night, and they caught the hardstyle crowd getting amongst it as well as plenty of the production spectacle and manic musical moments from headliners Showtek who played alongside acts like Zany, Southstylers, Dutch Master and more. Check out some of the finest moments from the X-Qlusive tour on ITM!
