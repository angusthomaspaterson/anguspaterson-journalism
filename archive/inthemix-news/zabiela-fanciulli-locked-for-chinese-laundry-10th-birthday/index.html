Zabiela + Fanciulli locked for Chinese Laundry 10th Birthday

Angus Paterson · 24 May 2007 · inthemix

It’s been Sydney’s worst kept secret, but ITM can now (officially) confirm that the power couple of James Zabiela and Nic Fanciulli have been locked for a massive ‘One+One’ set at the Chinese Laundry’s mega 10th Birthday bash next weekend. That’s a massive decade in Sydney clubbing and as well all know, over the past two years in particular the club has truly solidified itself as one of the city’s greatest venues for kickin’ underground dance music, so it’s sure to be a huge shindig.

Zabiela and Fanciulli have both in our faces a lot at the moment, billed as the biggest partnership in dance music since Sasha & Digweed started spinning records together. The idea was actually born in Sydney back in ‘05 on Home Nightclub’s Terrace at a legendary Renaissance party, when the pair went back to back for an epic sunrise set. As well as a CD release promoting their One+One collaboration they’re touring the nation as part of the We Love Sounds festival circus and now, a sideshow in Sydney that will certainly go down as a gig to remember.

There’s a limited amount of second release tickets left from ITM, and those interested in having a chat with the duo should log onto in the spotlight on Wednesday at June the 6th at 6pm EST when we host them for a live webchat.

The party kicks off at the Chinese Laundry on Saturday 2nd June, get yer tickets while you still can…
