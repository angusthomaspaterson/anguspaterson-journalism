Audion, Oxia & Omar S set to devastate in September

Angus Paterson · 24 July 2009 · inthemix

If you need an extra dose of techno amphetamine to blast you through to the end of winter, then this will probably do the trick. Audion will be performing live at Future Entertainment favourite The Likes of You in both Melbourne and Sydney during September alongside Oxia, with Omar S also joining the massive Melbourne show. And to treat the rest of the nation’s tech fanatics, all three will be playing select gigs around the rest of the country.

Audion is otherwise known as the unbeatable Mathew Dear, and this year he’ll be bringing this particular alias to select venues worldwide to coincide with the release of new music, which he’ll be debuting alongside anthems from the past like Kisses and Mouth to Mouth. Joining Audion at The Likes of You events in Sydney and Melbourne will be techno’s newest French identity Oxia.

Omar S on the other hand is known as the ‘Grandson of Detroit Techno’, a massively respected DJ/producer from the motor city who’s made it his mission statement to put “a new wave of real Detroit techno soul back on the map”. With all this tech to choose from, September might end up being a little bit larger than we anticipated.

Audion (live) tour dates:
Fri Sept 11th – The Likes Of You, Melbourne
Sat Sept 12th – The Likes Of You, Sydney
Sun Sept 13th – Giesha, Perth,

Oxia tour dates:

Sat Sept 12th – The Likes Of You, Sydney

Omar S tour dates:
