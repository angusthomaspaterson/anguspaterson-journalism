Paolo Mojo tours for Renaissance

Angus Paterson · 21 June 2007 · inthemix

He’s one of house and progressive’s rising stars, and after wowing the punters with his tour last year he’ll be back soon for another jaunt of dates across the country on the back of a new mix compilation for Renaissance.

Paolo has been doing his thing for a number of years, but his unique DJing style was brought to the attention of a whole bunch of new faces last year when he released his installment in the Balance series of compilations. His sound is difficult to pin down but it takes in house in all of its different variations with a broad ‘progressive’ thread holding it all together, and he’s definitely among the nu-breed of house stars. This was reflected in the fact that he was picked up by Sasha’s agency Excession at the start of ’05.

On the production side he’s well remembered for his hit 1983, with the Eric Prydz reworking of the tune becoming one of the biggest club smashes from the second half of last year.

Check out Renaissance feat. Paolo Mojo at the following dates…

Saturday 7th July: Home Nightclub, Sydney. Famous presents Renaissance
Friday 13th July: The Lounge, Adelaide
Saturday 14th July: Queensbridge Hotel, Melbourne. Famous presents Renaissance
Sunday 15th July: The Bedroom, Gold Coast
