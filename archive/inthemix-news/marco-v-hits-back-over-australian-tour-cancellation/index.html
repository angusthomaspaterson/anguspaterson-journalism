Marco V hits back over Australian tour cancellation

Angus Paterson · 28 October 2009 · inthemix

Disappointment was rife last week with the news Marco V would no longer be making it to Australia for his November tour, which included a headline spot at Sydney’s Freedom Festival as well as a gig for Melbourne’s Darkbeat crew. The tech trance icon had indicated on his Twitter account that he was less than happy with the circumstances surrounding the cancellation, but this week he’s issued a statement where he’s spoken out further on these frustrations. Not happy Jan!

“I was really looking forward to playing out in Australia. I moved my entire diary around in good faith to fit this event in. It’s bad that these promoters just cancelled me instead of contacting us and seeing how we could make this event happen. I wouldn’t mind flying over for only one event, I already did that for Sensation NYE! I will definitely see everyone out there some other time soon.”

Marco and his management have pointed the finger squarely at the Australian tour promoters; however, they’ve responded to the criticism and emphasised their great disappointment at being forced to cancel his dates, reiterating last week’s statement that it was the last-minute shift of venue to Luna Park that forced some tough business decisions.

“Allegations, rumours and talk that suggest this was a publicity stunt from our end to announce Marco v just to win ticket sales without the intention of bringing him out is completely untrue,” the statement says.

“It is certainly no reflection on Marco’s genuine talent which we highly respect and value.”
