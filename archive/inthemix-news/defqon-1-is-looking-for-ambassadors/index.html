Defqon.1 is looking for ambassadors

Angus Paterson · 28 April 2009 · inthemix

How much do you love your hardstyle? Any raver worth their fluffy leggings would already know that the team from Q-dance will be bringing the mighty Defqon.1 Festival to Australia late this year, and as the original Holland event is regarded as one of the finest hard dance parties staged anywhere in the world, it’s cause for excitement that Sydney will be treated to the full experience in September. For the fanatics out there, Q-dance are offering you the chance to be a part of it all…

Are you keen to help make the international debut of the Defqon.1 Festival an unforgettable occassion? Do you want to be a ‘Defqon.1 ambassador’? Do you want to represent the brand in your street, suburb, city, sports club or university? Q-dance are currently looking for dedicated hardstyle lovers to help them represent the brand in Australia, and they’re urging the faithful to get in touch.

Do you want to be a Defqon.1 ambassador? Email your details, as well as some information on your connection to the hard dance scene, to info@q-dance.com.au. And stay tuned to ITM for more news on Defqon.1’s debut as it comes to hand…
