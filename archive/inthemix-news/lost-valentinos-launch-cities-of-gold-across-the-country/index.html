Lost Valentinos launch 'Cities of Gold' across the country

Angus Paterson · 2 November 2009 · inthemix

This year Sydney act Lost Valentinos finally released their long-awaited debut album Cities of Gold, overseen in the studio by none other than uber-producer Ewan Pearson, and the band are finally ready to take it on the road to launch it in their home country. A moody and textured effort, the album unites a brooding indie-rock jangle with a swirly electronic focus, and it’s a sound that’s been getting the band major props in the UK where they made their first forays this year.

Lost Valentinos tour dates:

Thu Nov 12th – Neverland Bar, Gold Coast
Fri Nov 13th – The Zoo, Brisbane
Sat Nov 14th – Great Northern, Byron
Fri Nov 20th – Revolver, Melbourne
Fri Nov 27th – The Wall, Sydney
