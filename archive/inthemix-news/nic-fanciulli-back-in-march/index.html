Nic Fanciulli back in March

Angus Paterson · 10 January 2008 · inthemix

Had a little bit of fun with the One+One experience last year? Well in a matter of a few short weeks after his partner in crime James Zabiela will be in the country for the Good Vibrations tour, Nic Fanciulli will also be back for a series of dates. He certainly made an impact last time he was here as part of the We Love Sounds roadshow and whether you saw sharing duties behind the decks with Zabiela or instead playing by himself, the results were the same: crankin’ tech house at its absolute finest. Bring him back we say…

Here are the Nic Faniculli dates that are known so far, stay tuned for more…

14th March: Platinum, Gold Coast
15th March: Empire, Brisbane
20th March: TBA, Sydney
21st March: Brown Alley, Melbourne
22nd March: Chinese Laundry, Sydney
