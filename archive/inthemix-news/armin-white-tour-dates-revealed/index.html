Armin 'White Tour' dates revealed

Angus Paterson · 4 April 2007 · inthemix

As the hype builds for the Dutch trance superstar’s return to Australia over the June long weekend, we now know when and where he’ll be playing around the country. Armin Van Buuren recently revealed exclusively to ITM that he would be returning to Australia in June, playing extended ‘Armin Only’ style events. “I’m not traveling with a festival this time, I’m doing it on my own,” he told national editor Tim Hardaker.

Consistently ranked as one of the world’s top dance music identities in the DJ Mag Top 100 Poll, Armin last toured Australia in March 2006, headlining the Two Tribes tour and inaugural Future Music Festival in Sydney. Fans are already frothing at the mouth in anticipation of the upcoming ‘arena’ tour, confirmed in Sydney and Melbourne as Ibiza-style ‘White Parties’ featuring Armin playing for a massive 6 hours!

Dates for the Armin Only tour are below, and for more info check out ITM’s recent interview where he talked about the upcoming shows.

Thursday 7 – Gold Coast (The Bedroom)
Friday 8 – Perth (Metro City)
Saturday 9 – Sydney (Olympic Park)
Sunday 10 – Melbourne (Vodafone Arena)
