fRew fronts up for Vacation Records national tour

Angus Paterson · 25 August 2009 · inthemix

To get an idea of what a massive year Vacation Records has had, you only need to look at frontman Bass Kleph’s performance in the Sony inthemix50 which saw him moving up a hefty 26 spots in the poll’s biggest overall increase. The label has chosen DJ/producer fRew to represent them on their brand new compilation Wish You Were Here 2009, a collection of their biggest hits from the past year, and he’ll be taking it on the road for a national tour.

fRew has been pegged as “one to watch” by Vacation Records, .with a style that’s described as classic big room house with a unique twist that’s all his own. He’s made an impact with Shake With It, Call of the Crow, Deep Love and recently earning praise with My Heart Stops featuring Chris Arnott and Rosie.

Vacation Records’ Wish You Were Here 2009 is out now through iTunes and Beatport, and you can catch fRew on his remaining national dates…

Friday 28 August – Ambar, Perth
Saturday 5 September – Escobar, Melbourne
Friday 11 September – King Street Hotel, Newcastle
Saturday 12 September – New York, Launceston
Friday 18 September – Lalaland, Byron
Saturday 19 September – Empire, Brisbane
Saturday 26 September – Syrup, Hobart
Saturday 3 October – HQ, Adelaide
