Eric Morillo set times

Angus Paterson · 5 April 2007 · inthemix

Eric Morillo is promising to deliver a mammoth set at Home on Sunday night, check below for set times. The club owners have agreed that if Eric wants to play until lunchtime then he will be free to do so, so there’s no telling how long he’ll play for! Expect a massive night to celebrate the 10th Anniversary of Subliminal.

Main:
10 – 11:30 Dirty South
11:30 – 1: Carl Kennedy
1 – close: Erick Morillo

Terrace:
11 – 2.30: Daniel Crocetti & Crispin Kerr
2.30 – 4: Robbie Lowe
4 – close: John Devecchis
