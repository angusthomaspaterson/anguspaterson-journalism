Do you want to present on FBi?

Angus Paterson · 19 January 2009 · inthemix

Well the time is now! Here’s your chance… Sydney’s favourite community radio station FBi is auditioning for new presenters to join the stable. You don’t need radio experience, you just have to like talking about music and be available to do a show at least fortnightly. FBi are offering you the chance take our presenters training course, teaching you all you need to know to present your own radio show.

Auditions take place on Saturday February 14th and Monday February 16th at FBI offices, 44 Botany Road in Alexandria. To register for the audition, email your name, age and phone number to info@fbiradio.com and include one paragraph on who you are and what you do, a short review of a gig you saw recently and the top 5 tracks you can’t live without

Entries close Friday 6th February! FBi will let you know if you made the auditions by Wednesday 11th February.
