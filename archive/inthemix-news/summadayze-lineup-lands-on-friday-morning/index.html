Summadayze lineup lands on Friday morning!

Angus Paterson · 3 September 2009 · inthemix

UPDATED – CHECK OUT THE SUMMADAYZE LINEUP HERE!

We knew the major summer announcements were imminent, but it’s even closer than you may have first thought… This Friday morning Future Entertainment will be announcing the lineup for their long-reigning New Years Day favourite Summadayze in Melbourne. While we’ll have to wait a little longer to find out the lineups for the rest of the country, the Melbourne event has traditionally offered a good indication of who we’ll be listening to in the opening days of the new year.

There’s been plenty of speculation as to who will be playing this year (you check out some of them in ITM’s summer hitlist and predictions whatever eventuates, Future Entertainment never fail to pull a whopping rabbit out of their hat for their New Years celebrations, so we don’t expect the crossover into 2010 to prove any different.

Be back at ITM early Friday morning for the full list of acts playing at Summadayze in Melbourne!
