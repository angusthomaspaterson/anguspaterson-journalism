Beck's Berlin Sessions announces its locals

Angus Paterson · 22 October 2009 · inthemix

After wowing the crowds earlier this year, Becks’s Berlin Sessions will be returning in November for a special tour commemorating the 20th anniversary of the fall of the Berlin Wall. We already knew Âme, Benji Frohlich and Move D would be the internationals representing the Berlin sound, but the organisers have just lifted the curtain on the local performers who will be providing the support.

Brisbane: Jad Lee, Audun, Mike Redfern, Nathan McLay
Perth: Ben Swan, Massiv Trav
Melbourne: Christian Vance (live), Miles Mac vs. Andy Hart, Unfettered
Sydney: Simon Caldwell, Nathan McLay, Murat Killic, Ben Korbel, Jad Lee, Co-Op DJs

“Inspiration Through Unification” is the theme for November’s Beck’s Berlin Sessions tour. It’ll be looking back to the fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989, which saw the city reborn as a source of creative inspiration for the rest of the world – as well as developing an unrivalled electronic music underground, of course. The national shows will be celebrating 20 years of inspiration through music, art, design and cultural innovation.

If you want a taste of what’s to come, have a listen to Move D’s DJ mix in ITM-FM, as well as a set from Benji Frohlich.

Beck’s Berlin Sessions returns in November:

Friday 6 November – Ambar, Perth
Saturday 7 November – Barsoma, Brisbane
Friday 13 November – Prince Bandroom, Melbourne
Saturday 14 November – The Civic, Sydney
