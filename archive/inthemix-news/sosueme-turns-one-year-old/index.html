Sosueme turns one year old!

Angus Paterson · 8 May 2008 · inthemix

That’s right, it’s time to party… Since starting its monthly smashdown at Fringe bar and moving onto its weekly residence at Q Bar, Sosueme has left a lot of messiness and mayhem in its wake and you can bet this will be reflected in its first birthday bash. The organisers say to expect, and I quote… “Clowns, balloons, GameBoy/GameGirl, tails, donkeys, Soft Tigers, asses, drinks, Bagraiders, cake, Art Vs Science, lollies, twister, Miami Horror, showbags, streamers, Hey Now, musical chairs and more”. And don’t forget resident DJs Mixtape:Mafia, misc.disc.dj’s, The Whaattt?? Mailer Daemon and Boom! DJs. Fire up boys and girls!

Sosueme turns one year old this Friday May 9th at Q Bar, ticketsd on sale now from ITM.
