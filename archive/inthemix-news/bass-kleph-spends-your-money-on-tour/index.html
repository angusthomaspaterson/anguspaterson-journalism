Bass Kleph spends your money on tour

Angus Paterson · 22 May 2009 · inthemix

Vacation Records head honcho Bass Kleph has established himself as one of Australia’s most consistent dance music names, but he’s got a particular buzz behind him at the moment due to the release of his new single $pend My Money with Stellar MC on the vocals! He’ll be making the most out of the buzz and taking it on the road with a 14-stop tour of Australia that will see him touching down in all the city centres, as well as a few stops off the beaten track too,

$pend My Money has been holding strong in the top 10 of the ARIA Club Charts the for the past eight weeks, currently sitting comfortably at #7 and even earning some impressive airplay on commercial radiowaves. You can hear it for yourself pumping out over our country’s club sound systems during June and July.
