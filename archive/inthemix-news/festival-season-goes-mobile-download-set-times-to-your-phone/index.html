Festival season goes mobile! Download set times to your phone

Angus Paterson · 31 December 2007 · inthemix

Heading out for a big one this New Year’s period? Smirnoff Experience and inthemix have teamed up to bring you the Smirnoff Festival Guide, is a Java-based application for your mobile phone that will allow you to download set times, maps and other info for some of the biggest events coming up. It’ll be catering for all the big parties over the festival season, but just in time for launch we’ve got info on some of the biggest New Year events going down over the next week – Shore Thing and Field Day in Sydney, Summadayze in Melbourne and Summafieldayze in Queensland.

The Smirnoff Festival Guide is a Java-based application for your mobile, and once you download it to your phone it runs like a program, rather than connecting to a website. It only accesses the internet when it updates your info, meaning you can run the application anytime, regardless of your phone’s signal strength. The app is approximately 337kb and you download it from a mobile website, with updates sized at around 100kb. For both downloads, your normal data and carrier charges apply.

In addition, punters going to either Summadayze or Summafielddayze will be able to get the guide directly via Bluetooth units at Smirnoff Experience’s Area 21 stage. It’s an area a bunch of high-quality acts handpicked from the main festival line up will be performing unexpected sets you won’t experience at any other event anywhere in the world, and in this case you’ll have sets from international guests Joachim Garraud and Sister Bliss to look forward to, as well as an excellent assortment of local DJs. If you miss out this time, never fear because the Area 21 stage will also be making an appearance at Kiss My Grass in Melbourne and Future Music Festival in Brisbane and Sydney, while you’ll be able to check out video footage, streaming sets and photos right here at inthemix after each event.

If you have one of the below mobile phones then you can download the application, but it’ll become available for a bunch more handsets as the summer rolls on. Happy New Year shenanigans!

Download the Smirnoff Festival Guide for free by visiting www.smirnoff.com.au, and heading to the Smirnoff Experience section. Normal data and carrier charges apply
