JDS breaks out across the nation

Angus Paterson · 8 February 2007 · inthemix

The powerhouse breaks duo will be carving it up at the Future Music Festival around the country, as well as a number of solo dates to ensure none of the other states are left out. The festival gigs for JDS will held in Brisbane, Melbourne, Adelaide and Sydney while Hobart and Perth will be treated to headline shows.

The duo initially grabbed attention a few years ago with their track 9 Ways, caned in the clubs and remixed by the Plump DJs. Their reputation as a killer production outfit was solidified with the release of Purple Funky Monkey on the TCR label, which delivered the hits Rockstar, Blackout, Don’t Be Alone and Disco Rockers.

Since then they’ve mixed a compilation for the Beatz and Bobz series and dropped the new singles Some Kinda Funky Shit, The Spice and Shake It. Darren is the DJ of the outfit and he’s returning after a successful tour with Krafty Kuts two years ago.

Check below for details of the JDS tour across the country:

Friday 9th March: Halo, Hobart
Saturday 10th March: Future Music Festival, Brisbane
Sunday 11th March: Future Music Festival, Melbourne
Monday 12th March: Future Music Festival, Adelaide
Friday 16th March: Ambar, Perth
Saturday 17th March: Future Music Festival, Sydney
