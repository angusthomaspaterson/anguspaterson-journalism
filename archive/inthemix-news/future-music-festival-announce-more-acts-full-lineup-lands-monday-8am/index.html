Future Music Festival announce more acts, full lineup lands Monday 8am

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2009 · inthemix

Future Entertainment’s flagship annual tour the Future Music Festival has been a favourite of ITMers since it fired up across the country several years ago, regularly scooping the awards in the Sony inthemix50. So it’s a forgone conclusion that we’ll be eagerly awaiting news on what artists will be playing on the 2010 tour. Well, there’s not long to go now! ITM will have the first-round announcement of acts at 8am on Monday morning, so make sure you’re back at the site.

Details about the festival’s lineup have been trickling out for a few months now with Empire of the Sun, David Guetta and Dubfire all appearing as just some of the artists to be playing at the festival. And indeed, just yesterday, crossover dance-rockers Franz Ferdinand revealed live on triple j radio that they’d be a part of the monolithic festival, as well as hinting that club legends The Prodigy would be making the trip as well.

ITM can can assure you though, that’s only the tip of the iceberg as next year’s FMF lineup is arguably one of the biggest Future Entertainment have ever pulled together!
