Go on tour with Stereosonic with ITM's live festival reporter!

Angus Paterson · 30 November 2009 · inthemix

Stereosonic kicked off in Sydney and Perth over the weekend, and ITM’s live festival reporter John Treadgold was there to cover the behind-the-scenes action every step of the way. Whether it was standing behind the Bloody Beetroots as they performed and witnessing the mayhem in the crowd, getting up close and personal with Deadmau5’s Oz flag head or simply hanging around backstage, our live festival reporter was there. Check out the ‘week one’ coverage now!
