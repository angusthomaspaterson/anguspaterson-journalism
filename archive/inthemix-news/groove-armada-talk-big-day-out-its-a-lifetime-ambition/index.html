Groove Armada talk Big Day Out - "it’s a lifetime ambition"

Angus Paterson · 8 January 2010 · inthemix

The start of the epic Big Day Out tour is just around the corner, and UK live powerhouse Groove Armada will be then ones closing the Boiler Room to 25,000 strong crowds across the country, playing alongside the likes of Sasha, Calvin Harris and Simian Mobile Disco. And the band themselves are just as excited as the rest of us, with Tom Findlay revealing this week that playing the Big Day Out has been a dream of the band for many years – comparing it to the likes of the revered Glastonbury Festival in the UK.

“It’s been a real lifetime ambition, I don’t think I could have lived with it and got to the end of my career and not played Big Day Out,” Findlay told tvnz.co.nz this week. “Sometimes when you tour, you play just dance events, and you’re preaching to the converted a little bit. In my opinion, Big Day Out is more like a rock show and it’ll be great for us to get up there and do what we do in front of a really varied, music-loving audience.”

For all the fans who are also heading along to Groove Armada’s sideshows during the tour, they’ll be treated to an exclusive sneak peak of material off their new album Black Light. With the restrictions of festival set times lifted, they’ll be given the chance to roam around the new material. “There’s no doubt that it’s the best music we’ve ever made,” says Tom. Big claim, that.
