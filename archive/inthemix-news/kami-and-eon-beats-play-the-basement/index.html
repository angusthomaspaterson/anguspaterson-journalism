Kami and Eon Beats play The Basement

Angus Paterson · 12 October 2007 · inthemix

Two of Sydney’s finest soul and groove bands will be combining for a great double bill on Friday 9th November, with support from DJ Simon Caldwell.

Eon Beats Project are favourites at The Basement and have released a number of successful albums, including About To Blow, and they can often be seen on the bill for festival and clubs all over the country. They’ll be previewing some new tracks from their forthcoming album. Kami on the other hand brings a fusion of hip hop, reggae, disco and funk, backed up by soulful vocals and harmonies. Their debut CD For All You Clowns is out now.

Catch Eon Beats Project and Kami on Friday 9th November at The Basement, and the show is $24.
