John Course & Grant Smillie take over the Adelaide airwaves

Angus Paterson · 28 August 2007 · inthemix

The massively popular Overdrive program is taking over Adelaide radio, and every Saturday night from 7-10pm DJs John Course and Grant Smillie will be bringing you the biggest and best dance music from across the world. And for the next couple of weeks they’ll be featuring guest mixes from some of Australia’s biggest jocks that they have provided for inthemix50 DJ Poll.

It’s already been a massive success around the country so far: in the latest radio survey, Nova’s Saturday night dance show hosted clearly won it’s timeslot with a 21.3 per cent share of the marketplace. The show has been running in Melbourne for the past 18 months and has seen its popularity skyrocket over that time.

Overdrive airs from 7pm-10pm Saturday nights in NOVA 91.9 Adelaide, make sure you tune in and keep your ears to the ground for some excellent inhthemix50 DJ sets!
