Subsonic Festival set times + maps for this weekend

Angus Paterson · 2 December 2009 · inthemix

The newest and most unique entry to the Sydney festival calendar kicks off its first event this weekend, with the Subsonic Festival set to hit the Mountain Valley Resort in Barrington Tops with a cracking lineup of underground electronic talent that includes Alex Smoke Format:B, Tom Clark, as well as an extensive selection of local names that includes Jamie Stevens, Simon Caldwell, Shades of Gray, Matt Aubusson, Diatribe and heaps, heaps more. If you’re making the pilgrimage this weekend, check out the festival page now as we’ve got the set times and maps so you can start planning out your three days!

CLICK HERE TO CHECK OUT SUBSONIC FESTIVAL SET TIMES PLUS MAPS.

Subsonic Music Festival takes over the Mountain Valley Resort in Barrington Tops from Friday 4th to Sunday 6th December, tickets on sale now at ITM.
