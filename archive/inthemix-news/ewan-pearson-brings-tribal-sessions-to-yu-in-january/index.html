Ewan Pearson brings Tribal Sessions to YU in January

Angus Paterson · 13 December 2006 · inthemix

No rest for the wicked as YU’s weekly Saturday night Beware the Cat presents the prolific DJ/producer on the first weekend of the New Year.

Ewan Pearson has been making records for a living since 1998 and is famous for his remixes for the likes Depeche Mode, Chemical Brothers, Seelenluft, Franz Ferdinand and Goldfrapp, elevating him to the status of one of the most respected producers working in dance music.

Recently Pearson has shifted into producing other artists, working on The Rapture’s new album Pieces of the People We Love as well as other artists like Ladytron, Chikiniki, Envoy, Jeb Loy Nichols and even Gwen Stefani. He has also just completed work on a number of tracks for Tracey Thorn’s forthcoming LP Out of the Woods that is due in February next year.

Ewan continues to DJ regularly right throughout Europe and appears regularly at Fabric in London, Manumission in Ibiza, Pulp in Paris and Panoramabar in his current hometown of Berlin. He wi8ll be headlining when Beware the Cat presents *Tribal Sessions * and will be supported by Bjorn Wilke, Ben Korbel and Declan Lee.

For more information on Ewan Pearson check out his website at www.ewanpearson.com.
