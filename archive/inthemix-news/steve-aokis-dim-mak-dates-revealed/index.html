Steve Aoki's Dim Mak dates revealed

Angus Paterson · 14 September 2009 · inthemix

We already knew Steve Aoki would be touring Australia for a series of shows themed around his Dim Mak imprint in October, and Future Entertainment have now revealed the dates of when you’ll be able to catch him in your neck of the woods. He’ll be hitting all the major hotspots over two weekends under the new banner ‘Late Night Date presents…’

What to say about Steve Aoki that hasn’t already been said? Talked about more than ever in 2009, he’s kept himself in the dance music headlines not only with his wildly successful Dim Mak imprint, home to the likes of MSTRKRFT and Bloody Beetroots, but also through his ability to earn the respect of a raft of high-profile collaborators like Armand Van Helden, Laidback Luke, will.i.am and loads more. Can this man do no wrong?

Steve Aoki tour dates:

Thu Oct 15 – Brisbane, Family.
Fri Oct 16 – Perth, Villa
Sat Oct 17 – Canberra, The Academy
Sun Oct 18 – Adelaide, Electric Circus
Fri Oct 23 – Melbourne, Roxanne Parlour.
Sat Oct 24 – Sydney, The Metro
Sun Oct 25 – Gold Coast, Elsewhere
