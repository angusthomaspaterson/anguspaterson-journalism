UPDATE: Stereosonic reveals Brisbane locals + arenas

Angus Paterson · 23 October 2009 · inthemix

If your weekends are feeling a little a little quiet after all the action around Parklife, Defqon.1 and Godskitchen recently, never fear because in around a month’s time the massive Stereosonic tour will be kicking off around the country. Deadmaa5. Axwell. Laurent Garnier. Fedde Le Grand. John Dahlbäck. Need we say more?

To get that anticipation bubbling, the organisers have just revealed the locals who will be offering support to that massive, massive lineup of international guests, as well as the different arenas for the respective cities. Check them out below!

Also, are you keen to go on tour with Stereosonic this year as ITM’s official live social networking reporter? One lucky winner will be flown across the country to Brisbane, Perth, Sydney and Melbourne with AAA pass around their neck to post reports and updates on the action as it happens, and if you want to be ‘the one’ then you’ve only got a limited time to enter the competition. Get cracking!
