inthemix is going to Tomorrowland 2013

Angus Paterson · 25 July 2013 · inthemix

Last year, inthemix took the pilgrimage to Belgium for Tomorrowland and over the course of three days found out why it’s possibly the world’s most-hyped event. This weekend, inthemix will be returning to the town of Boom (its population of 16,000 swells each year with nearly 200,000 Tomorrowland travellers) to experience it all over again. How will the spectacle of 2012 be bettered?

The powerhouse behind Tomorrowland, ID&T have already dropped a few cryptic clues as to what we can expect this weekend, unveiling earlier in July the theme of this year’s event, The Arising of Life, a chapter that will apparently “take us back to ancient time”. Ultimately though, all the talk of a “mystical land between the shadows of wonder” is an excuse for ID&T to go all-out on stage design and production.

Away from the biggest amphitheatre, there’s the 20 or so different stages filled with DJs from across the spectrum. In addition to the big-leaguers like Tiesto, Axwell, Armin van Buuren and the Sunday night tag team of David Guetta, Afrojack and Nicky Romero who dominate the Main Stage, there’s a corner of the site to satisfy pretty much everybody’s tastes. Arenas will be hosted over the weekend by Carl Cox, Sven Vath, Richie Hawtin, Derrick Carter and Dave Clarke, while elsewhere trance don Markus Schulz will be manning his own arena for a massive 12-hour marathon set. And that’s only the tip of the iceberg…

Stay tuned to inthemix over the weekend, with daily updates, photos, interviews, live sets and more. Tomorrowland TV will stream from from 9PM to 2AM CEST each day of the festival, 26, 27 and 28 July. Check out the promo video below.
