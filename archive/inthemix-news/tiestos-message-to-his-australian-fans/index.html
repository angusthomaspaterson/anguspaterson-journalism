Tiesto's message to his Australian fans

Angus Paterson · 17 April 2008 · inthemix

There’s only one week to go until Tiesto finally returns to Australia after a massive four-year break, when he’ll be debuting his massive ‘Elements of Life’ stadium show in all its glory (in addition to a select few intimate club shows, of course). ITM recently spoke to the man himself, and he had the following message for his Australian fans.

“It’s been four years since I was last in Australia, so I think Australian crowds and Tiesto have a lot of catching up to do,” he told ITM. “My message is that I’m really excited to finally have the time to go back to Australia, and I’m really excited to play for you guys. I was really happy to see that all the shows sold out so fast, and so many too. We were only meant to do one show in Melbourne and one in Sydney, so I’m really happy that everyone is so excited! Thank you for all of your support.”

For the Sydney and Melbourne shows, Tiesto says that we can expect the same full-blown production and pyrotechnics that he took across the United States last year: while it’s been scaled back in terms of size to reflect the fact that he’ll be playing to crowds of around 6,000 rather than 30,000, it’s the same massive ‘Elements of Life’ show and all the dazzling spectacle that goes with it. But first, he’ll be kicking things off in a more intimate style with two shows at Brisbane’s Family late next week: stay tuned to ITM as we bring you all the up to date photos, reviews and other coverage!
