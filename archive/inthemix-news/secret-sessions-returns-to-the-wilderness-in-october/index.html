Secret Sessions returns to the wilderness in October

Angus Paterson · 27 August 2009 · inthemix

To mark the fading of winter and the onset of the summer sun, Secret Sessions will be returning to South East Queensland for its 5th consecutive year this October, once again offering a fine selection of underground electronic music in the Numinbah Valley. With a pristine location that’s wedged between two of the area’s most beautiful National Parks, there will again be a fine selection of both international and local DJs and live acts to get the vibe happening.

This year’s event takes place from the 9th to the 11th October, and it takes a drive of around an hour and a half from Brisbane to reach the location. Secret Sessions will offer two stages of music and live entertainment as well as a host of other treats like camping, roaming performances, food stalls, workshops, arts and creative spaces, chill areas and everything else you’d expect from a quality ‘bush doof’.

First release tickets are on sale now for $70, with second release going on sale 28th for $80. Tickets will be on sale at the gate from Friday through to Sunday for $90.
