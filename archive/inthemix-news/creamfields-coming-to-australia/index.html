Creamfields coming to Australia?

Angus Paterson · 30 April 2008 · inthemix

Cast your minds back to late 2007 when Future Entertainment head honcho Mark James spilt the beans that his company had just picked up the rights to the Cream brand in Australia (read about it HERE). At the time Mark said it would be making its debut in Easter ‘08 when they’d be kicking things off with a national club tour. While obviously that tour never eventuated, you can be sure that Future still has massive plans for the Cream franchise – which may even include the renowned Creamfields festival coming to our shores!

Future Entertainment have a habit of dropping tantalizing clues for what they’ve got coming up, and following the recent announcement on their website that they’ve become the official representative of the Cream brand in Australia, they’ve dropped another bombshell once again. Featuring a timeline of the Cream brand that stretches back to its birth at the Nation nightclub in Liverpool in 1992, when it reaches 2008 it’s followed by the headline of ‘CREAMFIELDS’, as well as the copy; “Creamfields is the only festival concept that has been successfully established in the global market,” it says, continuing on shortly after to say that, “Tours are already confirmed in the UK, Spain, Greece, Cyprus, Malta and Australia,” with the final word auspiciously highlighted in blue.

While it’s far from a concrete announcement, Mark James himself might have actually made a made a reference to Creamfields visiting Oz when he spoke with ITM late last year. “We’ve just taken over the Cream brand, and we’ll be looking after that from now on in Australia. That might turn into a festival, you never know,” he said to inthemix. “But we’re currently looking at another couple of festivals next year and one specifically that’s going to be around the end of November, which is a big overseas brand that will be coming to Australia for the very first time.” Creamfields coming to Australia this November? Guess we’ll just have to wait and see!

Since Creamfields was launched in 2000 it’s been taken to more than 15 countries worldwide, including Spain, Portugal, Chile, Brazil, Ireland, Mexico, Poland, the Czech Republic and Argentina. And with Australia currently mad for music festivals, there’s no denying Creamfields would make a fantastic fit.

Creamfields reaching our shores before the end of the year? Stay tuned to ITM for news!
