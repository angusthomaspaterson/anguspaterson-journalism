Australia's first Big Day Out photos!

Angus Paterson · 19 January 2009 · inthemix

Australia’s largest festival the Big Day Out kicked off the national party on Sunday in the Gold Coast, with Perth drum n’ bass heroes Pendulum taking the main stage while reigning veterans The Prodigy returned to their old stomping grounds of the Boiler Room.
