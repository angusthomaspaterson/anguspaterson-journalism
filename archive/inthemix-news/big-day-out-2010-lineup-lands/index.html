Big Day Out 2010 lineup lands!

Angus Paterson · 29 September 2009 · inthemix

CHECK OUT OUR BIG DAY OUT LINEUP SHOWCASE GALLERY

With rumours flying all around the net it’s obvious the hunger is huge for news on Big Day Out, Australia’s biggest and most successful national tour, and the first round announcement for the 2010 tour has finally landed. Check it out below!

Heading up the lineup or this year’s Big Day Out tour are the ‘Knights of Cydonia’ themselves Muse, known for sporting a live show big enough to shake Wembley Stadium to its very foundations. They’re joining by an ensemble of local and international rock acts that includes The Mars Volta, Powderfinger, Eskimo Joe and heaps more.

On the dance music tip, live heavyweights Groove Armada will be making their Big Day Out debut as well as Calvin Harris and mashup king Girl Talk, who will also both be bringing their party vibes to the festival for the first time. On the indie-dance vibe there’s also plenty to offer with Ladyhawke, Midnight Juggernauts, Tame Impala and more playing the tour.
