Shore Thing set times wash ashore

Angus Paterson · 22 December 2009 · inthemix

As New Years Eve inches closer and closer, the set times keep rolling in and the latest to unveil itself to the public is an event that’ll be part of the plans of many a partygoer – Bondi Beach’s Shore Thing, which this year sports the massive trio of DJs that is Danny Tenaglia, Carl Cox and Roger Sanchez. Huge, and what’s even bigger is that Mr Tenaglia has been given a whole two and a half hours to do his thing, as well as being given the honour of bringing the punters into the New Year. Together with the ITM competition winner S Angelsi who’ll be performing live early in the evening, it should make for another cracking New Years Eve courtesy of Fuzzy.

Shore Thing returns to Bondi Beach on Thursday 31st December 2009, check out the SET TIMES and VENUE MAP for more info.
