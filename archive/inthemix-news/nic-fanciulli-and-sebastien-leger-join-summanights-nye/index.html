Nic Fanciulli & Sebastien Leger join Summanights NYE

Angus Paterson · 2 November 2009 · inthemix

As if the lineup for the debut of the Summadayze brand into Sydney wasn’t big enough already, Future Entertainment have announced that their NYE Summanights bash will also be hosting the talents of Nic Fanciulli and Sebastien Leger. It’s also been confirmed that both Leger and Josh Wink have been locked in for extended three-hour sets, with the latter set to bring it home with the closing set on the terrace in the wee hours of the morning.

It’s all set to go down at Home Nightclub on New Years Eve, and Fanciulli and Leger will be joining an all-star cast of dance music names that includes Sharam, Eddie Halliwell, Mark Knight, Menno De Jong and heaps more.

Summanights comes to Home Nightclub at Darling Harbour, Sydney on Thursday December 31st 2009. Check lineup across the different rooms below…
