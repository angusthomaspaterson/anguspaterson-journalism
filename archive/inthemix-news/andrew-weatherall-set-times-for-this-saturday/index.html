Andrew Weatherall set times for this Saturday

Angus Paterson · 11 January 2008 · inthemix

Legendary DJ, producer and remixer Andrew Weatherall will be playing his first ever Sydney show this Saturday night at Oxford Art Factory, check below for set times. If you’re locked and loaded for this weekend’s gig, you’ll finally be given the chance to discover how he’s neatly managed to straddle both rock music and straight-up techno all these years. Brought to you by Civil Society and presented by inthemix, he’ll be putting his years of experience to work and putting the funk back into tech house…

Andrew Weatherall hits the Oxford Art Factory, you can check out the recent ITM interview HERE and set times are below…

9.00-10.30: Tim Ritchie
10.30-12.00: Mad Racket & Nathan Mclay
12.00-4.00: Andrew Weatherall
4.00-Close: Mad Racket
