The very best of Easter's clubbing comeback

Angus Paterson · 17 April 2009 · inthemix

So you thought last weekend was all about reaching for the lasers? Think again, because there was a whole bunch other dancefloor rockin’ stuff going on, with a phenomenal shift that saw the action moving back to the clubs. Sinden, Fake Blood, Trentemoller, Nick Warren, Satoshi Tommie… they were all in the country, playing extended sets within the dark confines of some of our finest clubs! Here at ITM, we’ve been overjoyed to see a fresh enthusiasm for some of our more intimate spaces.

ITM had photographers at every last event, and we’ve scoured the galleries to bring you the very ‘best of’ moments from all the nation’s events. Whatever corner of the country you’re from, check em out now…
