Joakim tours for Future Classic

Angus Paterson · 15 October 2008 · inthemix

While the flashy crew from the Ed Banger and Kitsune labels might be the ones dominating the limelight, it was Joakim who was dubbed the “Godfather of French Electro” by I-D Magazine. He’s indeed considered royalty in the Parisian electronic scene, and this November he’ll be touring Australia for the very first time. Original tracks like Lonely Hearts and I Wish You Were Gone were responsible for building his reputation, and most recently his rework of DJ Mehdi’s Pocket Piano is looking to prove a dancefloor hit.

National Joakim dates…

Sydney – Friday 14 November @ Civic Underground
Brisbane – Saturday 15 November @ Barsoma
Melbourne – Friday 21 November @ La Di Da
Perth – Saturday 22 November @ Ocean Beach Hotel
Adelaide – Sunday 23 November @ Sugar
