Paint the town red with Pure Pleasure

Angus Paterson · 8 November 2007 · inthemix

The next installment of the Pure Pleasure parties will be coloured a soft crimson glow – come dressed in your favourite red outfit for the chance to win prizes on the night! Onboard for night will be Craig Obey and Brendan Fing, who will both be playing special funky house sets alongside resident Ange. And again it’ll be returning to the second level of the Equilibrium Bar.

For a dose of funky house, head along to Pure Pleasure Experience when it returns to Equilibrium Bar on Saturday 17th November.
