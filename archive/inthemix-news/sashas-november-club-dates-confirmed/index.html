Sasha's November club dates confirmed

Angus Paterson · 22 July 2009 · inthemix

Earlier this week it was revealed that dance music icon Sasha would be returning to Australia in November for a tour of Australia’s clubs, with his gig in Sydney at the Chinese Laundry the first to be announced. ITM can now confirm the remaining dates for the tour, with Sasha confirmed to play intimate spaces in both Melbourne and Brisbane, courtesy of our friends at Future Entertainment.

It’s no understatement to label Sasha as one of dance music’s most enigmatic figures. With nearly two decades in the scene and the one responsible for leading the charge when progressive house was at its most epic and successful back in the late 90s, he’s since kept himself on the cutting edge of house and electronic music, both with his use of Ableton in his live sets as well as his remarkable consistency as a producer. While his releases can often be few and far between, they’re leapt on immediately by fans and last year’s Invol2ver was good enough to take home the #2 spot in ITM’s Top 20 Releases of 2008!.

Sasha’s last visit to Australia was part of the Global Gathering festival, but this time he’ll be playing to the kind of more intimate spaces he’s arguably most well-suited for. Check out ITM’s feature interview to get a sense of what he’s been up to in 2009.

Sasha returns to Australia in November:

Thurs Nov 19th – The Met, Brisbane
Fri Nov 20th – Billboards, Melbourne
Sat Nov 21st – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
