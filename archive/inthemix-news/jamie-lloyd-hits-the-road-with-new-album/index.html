Jamie Lloyd hits the road with new album

Angus Paterson · 29 October 2009 · inthemix

Beware of the Light is the soulful new album from Future Classic favourite Jamie Lloyd, the followup to his superbly reviewed Trouble Within and this week’s Review of the Week for ITM. This weekend he’ll be kicking off a tour that stretches right across the country throughout November.

Jamie is loved for his ability to bring a soulful edge to his electronic music, and this is well on display on Beware of the Light, a release that showcases his ability to bring strong songwriting and vocal performances together with leftfield electronic music. Jamie has said the bulk of the album was also written with his live shows in mind, a reflection of the fact that he’s also already become known as a hugely accomplished performer.

If you’d like a taste of Beware of the Light then check out ITM’s latest podcast episode, and catch him on his national tour below:

Fri 30 Oct – Sydney, Civic Underground
Sat 7 Nov – Adelaide, Sugar
Fri 13 Nov – Canberra, Lot 33
Sat 14 Nov – Brisbane, Bar Soma
Sat 21 Nov – Perth, Shape Bar
Sat 28 Nov – Melbourne, Sorry Grandma
Sun 29 Nov – Hobart, The Bay Hotel
