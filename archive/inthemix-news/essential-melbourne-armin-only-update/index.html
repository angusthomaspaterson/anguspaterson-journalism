Essential Melbourne 'Armin Only' update

Angus Paterson · 20 May 2008 · inthemix

For those who are planning on heading along to the upcoming ‘Armin Only’ shows over the June long weekend, there’s been some important info come to hand on the parties taking place. Future Entertainment has regretfully informed ITM that the show on Monday June 9th has been cancelled. They apologise for any inconvenience and disappointment that may have caused to punters, and have confirmed that all tickets purchased for the show will be fully refunded from the place of purchase.

The other update is related to the remaining sold-out show on Sunday June 8th. Due to a new liquor-licensing policy being implemented by the Victorian Government, the venue will only licensed to serve alcohol up until 3am. The event will still continue until 7am as previously advertised, but only non-alcoholic drinks will be served from 3am onwards. Sounds like a massive dampener on the fun of the evening hey? Future are urging clubbers that if they share their opposing views on the new lockout and licensing laws, to register their complaints at www.2amlockout.com.au.

Getting yourself in a flap about the unfortunate new lockout and licensing laws? Stay tuned to ITM as we keep you up to date on the issue.
