Sinden plus more join Harbour Party lineup

Angus Paterson · 16 November 2009 · inthemix

If you’re planning on spending your New Years Eve under the fireworks at Harbour Party over Luna Park, then alongside the already announced lineup you can also expect to be hearing the fidgety, wobbly beats of Sinden who’s just been added to the lineup. A member of A-Trak’s rising Fools Gold label, he’s been breaking down the genre boundaries for several years now and making lots of noise, not the least of which was his infamous anthem Beeper.

Also joining the lineup will be local delights Hey Now DJs, Sampology and Alison Wonderland, not to mention the cast of international talent announced last month that includes Pendulum, Ian Carey Project, JD Samson, Ajax, Bass Kleph and heaps more.

Harbour Party NYE returns to Luna Park on Thursday 31st December, tickets onsale now at ITM.
