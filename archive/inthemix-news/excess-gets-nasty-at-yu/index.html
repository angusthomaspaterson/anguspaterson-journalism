Excess gets nasty at Yu

Angus Paterson · 1 November 2007 · inthemix

Hungering for another taste of the nasty electro/tech sounds that Excess has become famous for, across its many different venues? This Friday at Yu will be massive with an appearance from inthemix50 #10 DJ Adam Bartas, who will be playing with partner in crime Dean Paps who together form the production outfit Orchestrated.

Excess kicked off just late last year at the old Gaff on Oxford Street, with a music policy of dirty electro/tech and with the intention of giving interstate DJs a shot in the Sydney club scene. Since then it’s delivered a wide assortment of fine national talent including the likes of Aston Shuffle, Luke Chable, Andy Murphy, Chardy, Nick Galea, Raye Antonelli along with some of Sydney’s including Mark Dynamix, Bass Kleph, Hook N Sling and more. Coming up to their 15th monthly event, the organisers promise they’ve got heaps of fun times in store.

Excess returns to Yu this Friday, look out for Midori giveaways and it’s $15 on the door or $10 if you email excessnights@gmail.com for guestlist.
