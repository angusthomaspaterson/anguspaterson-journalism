Trickski for Future Classic

Angus Paterson · 31 August 2007 · inthemix

Future Classic return to their favourite new venue the Supper Club for a night of excellent tunes courtesy of Berlin DJ/production act Trickski. Their first visit to Australian, it coincides with the release of their new compilation CD Members of the Trick, a collection of their 12” releases on the seminal Sonar Kollektiv label.

They’ll be joined by local heroes Nathan McLay as well as Jamie Lloyd, who will be playing live on an intricate combination of laptops, analogue synths and effects coupled with his live vocal stylings and samples.

Check out Future Classic Label Night from 10pm on Saturday 22 September at The Supper Club.
