Wanna rub shoulders with music industry heavyweights?

Angus Paterson · 10 September 2007 · inthemix

If you’re an up-and-coming musician or industry wannbe, here’s your chance. V-RAW, an initiative designed to assist people realise their dreams in the music, fashion and design, will be hosting a free music networking session in Sydney this week. If you’re an aspiring artist, promoter or music industry enthusiast then it will likely be up your alley as you’ll get the chance to hear from Fuzzy dusiness director Ming Gan, and Sony BMG artist and repertoire manager Adam Gould as well as Grinspoon frontman Phil Jamieson.

The V-Raw network sessions are aiming to offer people who want to work in music an opportunity to learn from true industry professionals, so if it sounds like your thing then shoot an email off to vraw@making-waves.com.au to register for attendance.

The V-Raw network session takes place on Tuesday 11th September 2007 from 5:30pm Sydney University’s Manning Bar. Email vraw@making-waves.com.au for more details.
