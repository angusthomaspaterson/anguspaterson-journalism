Josh Wink to tear roof off Chinese Laundry

Angus Paterson · 7 March 2007 · inthemix

With the Future Music Festival drawing near, punters will be given another chance to experience one of dance music’s most enduring names in an intimate set at the Chinese Laundry. It’ll be his first time Josh Wink has played Australia in over five years and Saturday March 17th will be his exclusive club show in our city.

To call Josh Wink a longstanding name in dance would be a gross understatement. Releasing his first record way back in 1989, he has been a revered name on the global electronic scene ever since and most memorably, releasing seminal hits like Higher State Of Consciousness and Don’t Laugh. He continues to be a big DJ name with his combination of techno and house and as fans would agree, will be ideally suited to play the Chinese Laundry on a Saturday night.

Josh Wink will be heating up the Chinese Laundry next Saturday 17th after Future Music Festival.
