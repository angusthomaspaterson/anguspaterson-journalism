Sharam leaves Playground Weekender lineup

Angus Paterson · 7 February 2007 · inthemix

Progressive house don Sharam, best known as part of the production and DJ duo Deep Dish, will no longer be playing at Playground’s massive March festival. But he’ll still be landing in Australia for a national tour that will include a Ministry of Sound gig in Sydney on March 3rd.

Sharam is best known for role in Deep Dish, who have been mainstays in the progressive house scene for over a decade but had a breakthrough hit with Flashdance in 2004. But lately Sharam has been striking out on his own: as well as completing the latest Global Underground release and playing solo DJ gigs, he’s also been earning big props for his single PATT (Party All The Time).
