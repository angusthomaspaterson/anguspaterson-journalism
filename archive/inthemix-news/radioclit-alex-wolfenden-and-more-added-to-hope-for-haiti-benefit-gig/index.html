Radioclit, Alex Wolfenden & more added to Hope for Haiti benefit gig

Angus Paterson · 1 February 2010 · inthemix

There’s a rather large benefit gig going down at World Bar tonight, Monday February 1st, with more than 60 acts confirmed to be onboard to offer their support for Espere Hope 4 Haiti. It was a rather large event to begin with, but it’s looking like the stakes have just been raised significantly. The organisers have just announced that international performers Radioclit, Alex Wolfenden, Namito and Dan Ward will be joining the bill. Things just got even more massive!

Radioclit have remixed everyone from M.I.A to Metronomy to TV On The Radio, and coming off the back of performances at Laneway and Beck’s Festival Bar, you’ll have a chance to catch them in a more intimate setting (while doing your bit for charity at the same time).

Namito on the other hand is a Berlin mainstay, having released tunes on the likes of Great Stuff, Kling Klong and Systematic, while Alex Wolfenden is well known for his residency at We Love… Sundays in Ibiza, among his other achievements.

Espere Hope 4 Haiti is part of a series of events taking place around the world simultaneously to raise money for a select group of NGOs. For the Sydney edition, 100 per cent of door and ticket sales, as well as profits from the bar, will go directly to Medecins Sans Frontieres, an organisation working with Haiti since 1991.

Espere Hope 4 Haiti hits The World Bar tonight, Monday February 1st. Tickets still on sale HERE, otherwise grab em on the door. Get down there!
