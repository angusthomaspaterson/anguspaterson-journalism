Mark Dynamix gets Deep as Fu*k

Angus Paterson · 24 July 2007 · inthemix

One of Australia’s finest Mark Dynamix will be joining the Deep as Fu*k crew this Thursday night at Moulin Rouge, shortly before he jets off to Berlin. Fresh talent Rob Ferre will be offering support while resident DJs Marcotix and RifRaf will be playing straight afterwards for those who are looking for a late night.

Get on down to Moulin Rouge on Thursday 2nd August, free before 11pm and $5 after.
