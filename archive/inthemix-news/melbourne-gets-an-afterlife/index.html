Melbourne gets an Afterlife

Angus Paterson · 21 August 2009 · inthemix

If you were looking for somewhere to party after Parklife, you’ve now got the perfect choice. The lineup and details for the official afterparty Afterlife have just been announced, and it’s a tasty lineup indeed featuring Candian electro pinup Tiga, playing alongside locals Andree Frost, Harris Robotis, Nick Foley, Generic and more.

This will be the place to be for all those who managed to get a ticket to the sold out festival, or those who missed out but still want to sample a few tasty Parklife delights.

Afterlife hits Roxanne on Saturday 3rd October from 10pm. inthemix is a proud presenting partner of Parklife in 2009! Keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page at inthemix.com.au/parklife.
