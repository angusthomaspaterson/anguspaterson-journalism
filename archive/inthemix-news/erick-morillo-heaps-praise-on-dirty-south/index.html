Erick Morillo heaps praise on Dirty South

Angus Paterson · 16 October 2008 · inthemix

This week ITM chatted with Subliminal Records veteran Erick Morillo about his upcoming NYE jaunt in Australia, that’ll see him DJing as one of the headliners at Sensation’s mighty debut in Melbourne before hopping in a Lear Jet to fly to Sydney, to drive it home at the Pacha NYE gig at Home Nightclub with a performance he promises will go well into the late hours of the morning. Among other things he chatted about, Morillo spoke of his recent season in Ibiza which he labels as his most successful to date, and it saw him sharing the decks at his renowned Subliminal night at Pacha with none other than our own Dirty South. And the house legend had plenty of nice things to say about our fast-rising production whiz.

“He’s someone I met two years ago when I played a party in Sydney, it was the one that was at the racetrack,” Morillo told ITM, referring to his ‘06 Future Music Festival closing set at Randwick Racecourse. “He met me and he gave me a demo, and I remember playing it that same day and thinking, ‘wow, this is really good.’ And to see him now become one of the reckoning forces in the industry is truly amazing. I always said it’s only a matter of time before producers out of Australia start taking over the industry, so it’s good to see people like him enjoying some great success.”

It’s high praise for the Melbourne ex-pat who’s currently based over in Europe, and rubbing shoulders with the likes of the Swedish House Mafia among others. But according to Morillo, it’s Dirty South’s fine work in the studio that speaks for itself. “He’s just doing great records, and that’s what it’s all about. He’s doing smokin’ remixes and great records, and everytime you have somebody with the kind of output that he’s had in the last 12 to 16 months, and quality output, then you go to the next level. He’s done a really great job of staying focused in the studio, not letting his DJing come and really delivering some great records. And that’s what it’s all about.”
