DJ Perplex retains the crown in DMC Championships

Angus Paterson · 23 August 2007 · inthemix

With six of the country’s best turntablists vying for the crown at the national DMC Championship finals in Melbourne, DJ Perplex managed to retain the crown for the second year in a row. He dished up cuts, scratches and beat juggles that wowed the audience as well as the judges, with 10 of the 12 placing him in 1st place. His win of 34 points put him a whopping 19 points ahead of his closest rival!

Known by his family and friends as Ollie Winter, he’s all set to return to London do some more damage (and represent his country) at the ‘World Finals’ this October.

Check out the final results…

1st: DJ Perplex – 34 points
2nd: DJ Snair – 15 points
3rd: B.2WO – 14 pints
4th: DJ Skoob – 7 points
5th: A-Style – 5 points
6th: DJ Zeke – 3 points
