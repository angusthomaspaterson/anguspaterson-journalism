Best of Sydney's epic Festival First street party

Angus Paterson · 14 January 2009 · inthemix

For a few short weeks in January, Sydney briefly gets to snatch away the title of ‘Cultural Capital’ from its southern cousin Melbourne. The Sydney Festival lights up the city and keeps the excitement levels pumping well after we’ve all waved goodbye to our New Years celebrations, with tasty cultural delights to satisfy lovers of theatre, film, live bands and of course dance music, with Becks’s Festival Bar at Hyde Park Barracks hosting more quality performances you could ever squeeze in.

To launch the festivities though, anyone living in (or traveling to) Sydney gets treated to something extra special. Festival First Night sees the CBD’s streets, laneways and parks cleared of all cars and transformed into a massive dancefloor with a free street festival that approaches the scale of Melbourne’s massive St Kilda Festival. The cultural treats are diverse, but of particular interest to ITMers are the two stages hosted by promoters Fuzzy. Last Saturday saw acts like Santogold, Busy P, DJ Medhi, A-Trak and Ajax creating mayhem in the otherwise calm city streets.

Were you there making mayhem? Or maybe you were looking on at all the craziness from interstate when an estimated 300,000 flocked to party in the Sydney streets. Check out ITM’s Snap! gallery of Festival First Night’s most exhilarating moments.
