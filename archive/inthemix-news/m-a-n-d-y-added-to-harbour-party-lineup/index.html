M.A.N.D.Y. added to Harbour Party lineup

Angus Paterson · 17 October 2007 · inthemix

They were just in Sydney over the weekend to headline the latest Killer party, but news just in is that Get Physical stars M.A.N.D.Y. will be back at the end of the year to play Harbour Party, the massive New Years Eve bash at Luna Park. The other new additions to the lineup are Sydney electro outfit The Heat as well as the eclectic party-loving duo Funktrust.

They’ll be joining an existing lineup that includes Australia’s own Cut Copy and Pnau playing live while Sneaky Sound System will be DJing. And on the international front, Sister Bliss and Joachim Garraud will also be DJing.

It all takes place on New Years Eve at Luna Park, so if you’re keen then get in quick cause tickets are selling extremely quickly!
