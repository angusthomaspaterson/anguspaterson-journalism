Cosmic Gate to rock Sydney on NYE

Angus Paterson · 2 November 2007 · inthemix

If you’re looking for a touch of trance to rock your world on New Years Eve, we’ve got just the thing – seminal DJ/production duo Cosmic Gate have been confirmed to play a Sydney gig. Tranceport NYE will feature them playing a 4-hour set, and it’ll be the only Sydney show they’ll be playing while on their world tour promoting their upcoming album.

It all takes place at Space on NYE, stay tuned to ITM for more details!
