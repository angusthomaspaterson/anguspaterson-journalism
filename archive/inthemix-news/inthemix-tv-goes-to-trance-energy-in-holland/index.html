inthemix.tv goes to Trance Energy in Holland!

Angus Paterson · 31 March 2009 · inthemix

Can you believe it’s only a little more than a week before the inaugural Trance Energy tour kicks off across Australia? As you might already know, ITM was there in early March to witness the original Trance Energy event in Holland (along with 35,000 other enthusiastic punters), and we’ve captured all the buildups, breakdowns and lasers in an extra-special episode of inthemix.TV.

Join us as we hit the streets of Amsterdam with inthemix50 #1 Tydi and Marcus Schossow only hours before they’re due to hit the stage, and hang around as we head over to Jaarbeurs Utrecht to witness one of the world’s most spectacular dance parties. We catch Tydi in full flight during his mainstage opening set, interview trance luminaries like Armin van Buuren, Paul van Dyk and Judge Jules and get dazzled by the out-of this-world production. Check it out now!
