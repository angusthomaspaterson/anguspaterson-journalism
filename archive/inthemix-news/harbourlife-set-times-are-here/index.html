Harbourlife set times are here

Angus Paterson · 18 November 2009 · inthemix

Harbourlife sold out in record time again this year, showing that demand is just as high as ever to party in the stunning harbourside surrounds of Mrs Macquarie’s Chair near the Royal Botanic Gardens, and it’s set to go down again this weekend. For those who are looking to plan out their sun-drenched day, Fuzzy have just released the set times for your perusal!

There’s been a few upset dance fans today with the news that New York City disco dons Holy Ghost! have been forced to pull out of their Australian tour, with the group still reeling from the tragic death of The Juan MacLean drummer Jerry Fuchs last week, however Fuzzy have locked down trusted party starters Beni and KIM from The Presets to step in as replacements. They’ll be playing alongside French house master Martin Solveig, as well as breakbeat heroes the Stanton Warriors.
