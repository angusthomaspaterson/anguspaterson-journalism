Kevin Griffiths to slam Lost Baggage this Saturday

Angus Paterson · 14 February 2008 · inthemix

Feel like another fix of uncompromising, no-holds-barred techno? Well lucky for Sydney, the Lost Baggage crew will be making its way back to Kings Cross this Saturday. And the special international guest this time around will be Kevin Griffiths. Formerly on staff at Berlin’s Fine Records, he launched his Tsuba label in early 2006 and since then has sought to cover the best in European-flavoured cutting-edge club music. Since then it’s gone onto turn many heads – naturally ,with an artist roster like Oxia & Eric Borgo, David K, Steve Lawler, Danton Eeprom, Motorcitysoul, Geddes, Aural Distortion and more, that’s hardly surprising. Catch Keven Griffiths bringing the Tsuba sound to Lost baggage this weekend.
