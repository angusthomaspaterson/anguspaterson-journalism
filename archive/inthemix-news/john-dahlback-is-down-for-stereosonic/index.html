John Dahlback is down for Stereosonic

Angus Paterson · 17 July 2009 · inthemix

Stereosonic is tipped to be the country’s biggest festival experience for the closing months of the year, coming back bigger and better than ever and now there’s another act who can be confirmed for the lineup – Sweden’s John Dahlback, one of the strongest new entries into the ranks of house DJ./producers from the past few years.

With the squeaky electro grooves of Pyramid getting his name out there in a big way last year, Dahwlback can point to his own label Pickadoll as the biggest measure of his success. With artists like Sebastien Leger, Dada Life, Zoo Brazil, Robbie Rivera and Laidback Luke all putting out releases on the imprint, it’s only further cemented Dahlback’s reputation as a solid hand in the studio.

The announcement of John Dahlback’s place on the lineup backs up last month’s announcement that Italian noise fiends The Bloody Beetroots would be returning for the tour (read about it here). Word on the street is there’s some pretty massive names still to be announced, so stay tuned…

Stereosonic national tour dates, presented by ITM:

Sat 28th November – Sydney
Sun 29th November – Perth
Sat 5th December – Adelaide
Sat 5th December – Melbourne
Sun 6th December – Brisbane
