Summadayze, Field Day, BBQ Beats - ITM's 'best of' NYD galleries!

Angus Paterson · 7 January 2009 · inthemix

The fine selection of parties that go down across the country on New Years Day are of such a high standard that they can often make parties on New Years Eve seem redundant – witness the amazing posse of parties we saw in the opening hours of ‘09 this year, with Field Day, Summadayze, BBQ Beats and Groove on a New Years Day keeping the punters happy in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Adelaide respectively. While partying on NYE may have been brought back with a bang with Sensation’s debut in Melbourne this year, it just gave everyone a reason to back it up and do the double! Never have dance fans had more fun than in the 2008/2009 crossover.

Were you getting down to Ferry and Underworld at Summadayze in Melbourne? Or perhaps worshipping the fine performances of Santolgold and Boys Noize at Field Day in Sydney? Maybe dropping science with Public Enemy at BBQ Beats in Brisbane, or putting your hands up with Fedde Le Grand at Groove on a New Years Day in Adelaide? Or you might have even rocked up to Summafieldayze or Summadayze in Gold Coast and Perth in the hope of catching Ferry Corsten and Armin van Buuren going back to back behind the decks.

Whether you’re keen to relive the magic, or just see what you missed out on, you’re in luck because ITM was there to catch all the special moments and we’ve compiled them all in a special NYD Snap! gallery roundup… Check out the most spectacular party action that went down across Australia at the beginning of ‘09…
