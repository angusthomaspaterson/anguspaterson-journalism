Muscles goes cruising for guns, babes and lemonade

Angus Paterson · 16 November 2007 · inthemix

Right across the country that is… You’ve probably caught him playing at least several times at festivals this year, but here’s your chance to catch the Modular wonder-kid Muscles doing his own solo shows! He’s been omnipresent over the last little while, with his debut album Guns Babes Lemonade released in late September and going straight to #14 in the ARIA charts, with tracks like Ice Cream, Sweaty and One Inch Badge Pin getting heard all over the place. Now you can catch him in the flesh.

Muscles will be playing the following gigs around the country!

Sat Nov 24 – The Beach Party, University of Newcastle
Sun Nov 25 – The Corner, Melbourne (Under 18s Afternoon Rave)
Sun Nov 25 – The Corner, Melbourne (Over 18’s)
Tues Nov 27 – The Corner, Melbourne
Thu Nov 29 – Rocket Bar, Adelaide
Sat Dec 1 – Foreshore Festival, Canberra
Thu Dec 6 – Family, Brisbane
Sat Dec 8 – Homebake, Sydney
Sun Dec 9 – The Forum, Sydney (All Ages)
