Will Saul tours for Balance 015

Angus Paterson · 15 September 2009 · inthemix

Last month it was announced that EQ Recordings would be following up the homerun they smashed with Joris Voorn’s entry into the Balance series earlier this year, with an entry from UK deep house maestro Will Saul. The response from dance music fans was that Will was indeed a worthy successor, and in late October he’ll be touring the country in support of the new mix.

Will Saul is the DJ/producer responsible for the Simple Records and Aus Music labels, describing his sound as ‘deepdubdiscohousetechno’ with a soulful edge. Some of the acts he’s remixed have included Jesse Rose, Solomun, Steve Lawler, Phonique and Marc Romboy, and he was also resident at legendary London underground haunt The End before the club shut its doors earlier this year.

Will Saul’s Balance 015 will be released on October 9th, check him out at the following shows…

23th Oct – Logik, Brisbane
24th Oct – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
3rd Nov – Alumbra, Melbourne (with Steve Bug)
