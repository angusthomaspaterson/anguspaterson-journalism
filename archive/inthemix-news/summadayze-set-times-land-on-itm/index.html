Summadayze set times land on ITM

Angus Paterson · 29 December 2009 · inthemix

As we all know, this New Year period Future Entertainment are hitting the road again to usher in 2010 in grand style with a truly unique nationwide tour, the most extensive Summadayze tour that we’ve seen yet. For punters who are hitting the enduring New Years Day party in Melbourne, as well as the debut of the brand in Adelaide and the party in Perth on the first weekend of 2010, the set times are in for your perusal. It’s a long day with an absolutely massive assortment of DJs across multiple stages, so you’d be wise to start planning your day out now!
