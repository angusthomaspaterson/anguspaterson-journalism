X-Qlusive Technoboy unleashes full lineup

Angus Paterson · 22 October 2009 · inthemix

Last week it was reported on ITM the Australian Q-dance team would be following up their recent Defqon.1 triumph with another installment of their X-Qlusive parties – where a particularly special member of the global hard dance fraternity is showcased, and given the reigns to host the evening. Italy’s Technoboy will be here for X-Qlusive Technoboy in December, and ITM can now reveal the international lineup of guests who will be joining him at Sydney’s Hordern Pavilion.

Q-dance are well known for the larger-than-life spectacle they bring to their events, an approach that was well on display at the outdoor Defqon.1 Festival last month but they’re equally able to blow minds with their indoor parties. Last year’s X-Qlusive Showtek in Sydney was definitely an event that won over the local hard dance fraternity, so check out some of ITM’s past coverage for an idea of what to expect.

Italian fashion maestro Technoboy definitely has a reputation as one of the best dressed DJ/producers working in hard dance, but this is one that’s matched by his consistency in the studio with his hardstyle anthems reaching every corner of the globe. He put these skills to good use when he hammered the European crowds at X-Qlusive Technoboy in Amsterdam earlier this year, and you can be sure he’ll be bringing his A-game for the Australian edition.

X-Qlusive Technoboy hits Hordern Pavilion in Sydney on Saturday December 19th, check out the full lineup below:

Technoboy
Headhunterz
Isaac
T ‘n’ T aka Technoboy & Tuneboy
Stephanie
Hardstyle Masterz (LIVE)
Hosted by Ruffian
