Sydney Stereosonic details emerge!

Angus Paterson · 18 September 2008 · inthemix

We’ve been hanging on the line the past few weeks. When would Stereosonic be making its way to Sydney, and what shape or form would it take? ITM can finally reveal the ‘satellite’ concept, with a series of shows to take place across four different locations across two weekends.

Satellite v1.0 will hit the Enmore Theatre on Saturday 22nd November with Get Physical stars Booka Shade. The Japanese Pop Stars are on support duties, and they’ve been hailed as the ‘future kings of dance’ (they’re from Northern Ireland, just in case you were wondering). Satellite v2.0 will be headed up by DJ legend Carl Cox at The Arthouse also on Saturday 22nd November, joined by Eric Powell and more.

For the trance fans out there, Satellite v3.0 will be a stupendously huge affair when it visits Home Nightclub for Sublime on Friday 28th November. Paul van Dyk, his Vandit Records sidekick Giuseppe Ottaviani, as well as psytrance stars Infected Mushroom all on the bill. The final Satellite v4.0 show will be a bang-for-your-buck affair at the Manning Bar featuring all manner of techno, fidget house and Ed Banger style electro-funk. It’ll sport the jam-packed line-up of The Crookers, DJ Hell, Vitalic, DJ Funk, Devlin & Darko, Tommie Sunshine, Headman, Dave Nada and Mr Maqs. Phew!

Late November surely is packed with dance music madness, check below for full details on the Sydney Stereosonic satellite shows.
