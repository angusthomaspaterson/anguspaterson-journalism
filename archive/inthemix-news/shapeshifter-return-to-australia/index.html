Shapeshifter return to Australia

Angus Paterson · 12 April 2007 · inthemix

Shapeshifter already wowed audiences earlier this year as part of the national Big Day Out tour, also winning an unprecedented support slot for Tool that saw them performing in front of 15,000 rowdy punters at the Myer Music Bowl. But news just in reveals that you’ll have another chance to catch the New Zealand live drum n’ bass act when they return for a string of shows in late May and early June.

Their latest album Soulstice has been praised by fans and fresh converts alike, but it is their ripping live shows that have been increasingly throwing them into the spotlight. Fellow country men Solaa will be joining them for support on the tour.

Catch Shapeshifter at the following dates…

Melbourne: Prince of Wales – Friday 25th May
Sydney: The Forum – Saturday 27th May
Newcastle: Cambridge Hotel – Thursday 31st May
Brisbane: The Columbian – Friday 1st June
Adelaide: Fowlers Live – Saturday 2nd June
Perth: Capital – MOS Festival – Sunday 3rd June
