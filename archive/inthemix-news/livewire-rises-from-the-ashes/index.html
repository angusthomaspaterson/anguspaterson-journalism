Livewire rises from the ashes

Angus Paterson · 14 March 2008 · inthemix

The Livewire party at Home in Easter ’06 was probably one of the greatest progressive/tech parties that Sydney has ever seen, featuring a massive international lineup that spread right over the four levels of the club. While nobody ever expected it to rear it’s head ever again, on the Anzac Day long weekend it’ll be back!

It’ll all be going down on Saturday April 26th at Home Nightclub, brought to you by Sweetchilli and Future entertainment who will be pulling together five headline international acts at the one party. Paolo Mojo will be returning and he’ll be joined by Petter, Abe Duque playing both a live and DJ set as well as the massive partnership of Supermayer, a project that sees Michael Mayer and Superpitcher working together both in studio and in the live setting.

Wanna know more? Ticketing information and full event details will be announced this coming Wednesday, stay tuned to ITM!
