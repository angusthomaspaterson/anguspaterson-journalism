Transmission Magic City draws it wildcard

Angus Paterson · 3 May 2007 · inthemix

With only three weeks to go until the party, the up and comers competing in the Next Gen DJ Competition have been given another chance to get behind the decks. The Chakra Balance Arena will feature sets from all the DJs who competed at the semi final competition last Sunday night at The Temple, and a bunch of other contenders will be given a second chance to compete too.

Voting is now open for the ‘wildcard’ DJs. The top seven DJs with the most votes will be selected to play, and there are three separate selection systems open now on the Transmission forums for you to cast your vote. Voting will close Sunday 13th May so make sure you tell all your friends to come and vote.

DJs in the first voting system include Shaw, Ink, O!, Syn, Malfunction, Reiivax, Raven, Misao, Burner and Gysi. Cast your nomination HERE.

DJs in the second voting system include Aiigee, Escsape, EanEktik, Matt Dawson, Genesis, Gebstar, RossMIG, SkINNER, Ezy, Bisho and Duress. Cast your nomination HERE.

DJs in the third voting system include Demolition, SRDJ, The Doctor, Frankie_Fix_It, Gavatron, Boos, Dutch, Easty, Phelon E and Technik. Cast your nomination HERE.

In other Transmission news, the semi final DJ competition went off with a bang on Sunday night with the four DJs across both the trance house categories selected to compete in the upcoming finals. The Anarchist, The Horror, Spinout and Toxic are all in the running to play a set on the main trance stage at Transmission Magic City while Gavin DeMyer, Robbie Jackson, Nik Mila and Sean Noreiks will be competing to play on the house stage.

Get on down to The Temple on Sunday 13th May to see the remaining finalists battle it out, or check out our Transmission FULL COVERAGE page for more details.
