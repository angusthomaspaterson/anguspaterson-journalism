Must-watch: Matinee Ibiza goes viral with insane, eye-popping ad

Angus Paterson · 2 June 2013 · inthemix

After a cracker of an Ibiza season last year, promoters are working harder than ever to secure the punter dollars. And with a stack of shake-ups this year, in terms of DJ defectors, residency overhauls and new clubs opening, competition is looking fierce. Fittingly, Amnesia’s Saturday night Matinée decided to come out all guns blazing, in a less than subtle attempt in grabbing the attention of clubbers holidaying on the White Isle this year. Scroll down to see it for yourself.

The club night has gone down the rather unconventional route for promoting its 2013 season – via a rather expensive looking mock commercial, spruiking a non-existent energy drink known extravagantly as Ibizious. The ad is so gloriously over the top, and utterly perfect in how it channels the ludicrous excesses of the ‘80s, that it’s basically impossible to resist.

Beyond extremely clever commercials, though, Matinée Ibiza is otherwise gloriously unremarkable, in terms of a residency that barely raises an eyebrow as far as musical talent goes. There’s no David Guetta or Tiesto on the line-up, and definitely no Solomun or Guy Gerber either, with a majority of the main-room DJ duties taken care of by Andre Vicenzzo and Flavio over the entirety of the residency’s three-month run.

With a history stretching back 15 years though, the Matinée brand seems instead to trade on its embrace of Ibiza-style hedonism and excess, selling its residency as an “ambitious audiovisual engineering project” that offers “the biggest showcase of talent and grandeur to be found on the island”.
