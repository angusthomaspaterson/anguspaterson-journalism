Big Day Out launches for 2010, check out the photos!

Angus Paterson · 18 January 2010 · inthemix

What’s arguably the biggest, most popular and most exciting festival tour is here, with the Big Day Out kicking off its Australian run on the Gold Coast over the weekend. As per usual it’s a massive lineup of international artists, and while rock might dominate the mainstage, some of electronic music’s finest can be seen in the Boiler Room including Groove Armada and UK DJ veteran Sasha, who debuted his spectacular new audio-visual show to the masses. Check out the photos now!

> Big Day Out, Gold Coast

The Gold Coast was just the beginning though, as there’s still the whole rest of the country to come as well as an assortment of sideshows. There are two massive events in Sydney this weekend, followed by Melbourne, Adelaide and Perth after that. Make sure you stay tuned into ITM for all your essential Big Day Out coverage!

Check out ITM’s Festival Page for more Big Day Out info, and check out the remaining national dates below:

Fri Jan 22 – Big Day Out, Sydney
Sat Jan 23 – Big Day Out, Sydney
Tue Jan 26 – Big Day Out, Melbourne
Fri Jan 29 – Big Day Out, Adelaide
Sun Jan 31 – Big Day Out, Perth
