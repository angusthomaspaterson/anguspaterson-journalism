Japanese dubstep king Goth-Trad lands next weekend

Angus Paterson · 22 May 2009 · inthemix

Not only has Goth-Trad been given credit for shaping the sound known as ‘mad rave’ (an insane mishmash of techno, house, jungle crunk, grime, drum n’ bass, 2-step and more), his abstract and twisted approach to his music has earned him the title of his country’s biggest name in dubstep. Following up his successful tour of Australia last year, next weekend Goth-Trad will be bringing his abstract electronic experiments back to Australia for another national tour.

Goth-Trad tour dates:

Friday May 29 – Perth
Friday June 5 – Sydney
Saturday June 6 – Melbourne
