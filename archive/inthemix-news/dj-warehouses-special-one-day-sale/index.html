DJ Warehouse's special 'one day' sale

Angus Paterson · 18 June 2009 · inthemix

Gearheads in Sydney and Melbourne take note… DJ Warehouse is holding another one of their special ‘one day sale’ this Saturday 20th June in both cities, to mark the end of the financial year. They’ve dropped their prices on their full range of DJ, studio and PA gear, as well as inking deals on open box and ex-hire stock, so there will be some good deals available for sure.

The one day sale kicks off at 11am on Saturday and ends at 5pm. You can find the DJ Warehouse store in Sydney at 641 Parramatta Road in Leichhardt, while in Melbourne it’s found at 431 Victoria Road in Abbotsford.

Head to www.djwarehouse.com.au for the complete sale list and all the details.
