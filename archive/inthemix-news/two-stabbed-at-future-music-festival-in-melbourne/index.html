Two stabbed at Future Music Festival in Melbourne

Angus Paterson · 12 March 2007 · inthemix

Sunday’s event marred by a fight that left two in hospital with multiple stab wounds that were inflicted by an attacker who has yet to identified by the police. A man from the Melbourne suburb of Thomastown was stabbed seven times and 21-year-old man from NSW was stabbed twice, with the male perpetrator fleeing after the attack.

The fight happened close to 8pm near gate two of the Sidney Myer Music Bowl, and both men were taken to Alfred Hospital where the victim with seven stab wounds underwent surgery and the other received stitches. While no arrests have been made yet, police have confirmed that a 21-year-old male from Broadmeadows is assisting with inquiries, and they are also hoping to speak with more witnesses.

The day was also blighted by a heavy presence of drug dogs that led to 36 arrests for possession of amphetamines, ecstasy, GBH and cannabis. Two will be interviewed for drug trafficking offences, while the remaining arrests will be put through the diversion program.
