Check the lineup for free Smirnoff Ice inthemix ‘Selector’ party

Angus Paterson · 14 May 2007 · inthemix

We’re throwing another free party for the ITM community at Sounds on Sunday this weekend, check below to see the DJs that YOU voted for to play a set on the day. The party will be hitting the Greenwood from 2pm onwards this Sunday and the lineup was selected from votes tallied during the RSVP process.

The voting was split into the DJs that would be playing in each different area including the courtyard, the chapel and the cocktail bar, as well as a category for the up-and-coming DJs. The results are now in and we can reveal that because of your votes, Miss Gabby and Ember will both be playing sets in the Courtyard, Marc Us will be spinning in the funk/hiphop Side Bar while the deep and funky house room in the Chapel will host the Fowl Play posse of DJs.

RSVPs are still open for the party and entry is free for the first 1000 ITMers through the door. Get there early to guarantee quick and easy entry, as well as to show your support for the up and comers! It’ll make for a bangin’ afternoon so you love inthemix online, then make sure you come and get involved offline.

The Smirnoff Ice inthemix ‘Selector’ party hits Sounds on Sunday on May 20th, check below for the lineup and register HERE for free entry.
