Ableton takes wraps off v.8 with free courses

Angus Paterson · 21 May 2009 · inthemix

Version 8 of the massively influential Ableton Live is nearly upon us, and to help mark the occasion its local distributor Musiclink Australia have announced a series of free events that will offer an in-depth rundown on the new software. You won’t have to pay a cent to take part in the seminars, and they’re presented by Ableton specialists and feature exclusive live sets by Ableton-endorsed artists.

Taking place at several of Australia’s hottest clubs, each event will be kicking on after the demonstrations are all wound up with a proper after-party. The first of the events takes place at the Chinese Laundry this Saturday night, after which is the club’s 12th Birthday celebrations featuring Paolo Mojo! Definitely worth checking out if you’re an electronic music enthusiast.

Australian Ableton Live 8 launch events:

23rd May – Chinese Laundry from 3pm, Sydney
Anthony Garvin: Manager Studios 301
Damien Goundrie: SAE Liveschool
Neon Stereo: Ableton Alpha Tester
DIZZ1: Ableton Endorsee
Jono Fernandez: Ableton Endorsee

28th May – Miss Libertine from 7.30pm, Melbourne
Tom Cosm: Ableton Endorsee
Danny Bonnici: Ableton Endorsee
DIZZ1: Ableton Endorsee
Jono Fernandez: Ableton Endorsee

6th June – Stepp Inn from 6pm, Brisbane
Clinic presentations by:
Craig McCullough: Ableton Certified Trainer
Tom Hall: Brisbane: Ableton Collective
Paddy Free: Pitch Black
