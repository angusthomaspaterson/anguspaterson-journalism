Sam Lamore is Rising High over Easter

Angus Paterson · 16 March 2007 · inthemix

Sam Lamore, best known for his part in the short-lived Tonite Only musical project, will be hitting the decks at the next Rising High party on Easter Saturday. The organisers promise the Easter installment will deliver an extra dose of an underground vibe in a unique setting – their mission statement for the Rising High parties.

Also playing will be the Potbelleez, Off Siders (Mike Mcgrath and Ron Moore), Other One and Lil A, as well as and Rising High’s very own band project The Lullabyes and Spit Syndicate for funky soul and hip hop and party mash ups.

Rising High kicks off on Easter Saturday April 7th from 8pm at the Blacket Hotel.
