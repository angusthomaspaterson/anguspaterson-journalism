Rogue Element coming back for more

Angus Paterson · 30 May 2007 · inthemix

The Rogue Element aka Ben Medcalf is one of breakbeat’s hottest producers and after last year’s successful tour, he’ll be heading back downunder for another series of club dates this June.

Since signing a two-album deal with Exceptional Records in 2004, The Rogue Element has taken the breakbeat world by storm. A string of singles received instant support from heavyweights like the Plump DJs, Freestylers, Stanton Warriors, Krafty Kuts and Rennie Pilgrem and culminated in him being awarded the ‘Best Breakthrough Producer’ award at the prestigious Breakspoll Awards.

2006 was again a massive year for him, seeing Rogue Element lend his remixing abilities to tracks from again, some of the biggest names in breakbeat, as well aas earning himself another Breakspoll Award – the coveted ‘Best Producer’ gong. A second studio album is currently in its early stages and is expected to be released in around September 2007.

Catch The Rogue Element playing at the following dates…

Friday 8th June: Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Saturday 9th June: GPO, Brisbane
Sunday 10th June: Baja, Coolangatta
Wednesday 13th June: Halo, Hobart
Thurs 14th June: Como Hotel, Perth
Friday 15th June: Brown Alley, Melbourne
