Rastawookie gear up for album launch

Angus Paterson · 14 March 2007 · inthemix

Aussie ‘global sounds’ act Rastawookie are about to release their debut album Perfectly Ordinary to the public, and they’ll be hitting the Metro in April for a special show in support. The album will hit the streets on April 21st and the titular single will be available from a week before that.

Rastawookie are known for bringing an electronica influence their mashup of multicultural sounds, and always carrying a massive horn section with their live shows. Their music is influenced by culture from all over the world, from South America to the Caribbean to the modern western world, so you can expect a mix of reggae roots with dub, drum & bass, hip hop, funk, bossa and ska all thrown into the mix.

The ‘Perfectly Ordinary’ album launch takes place Saturday April 21st at the Metro Theatre, with support from The Versionaries & The Sunshine Brothers.
