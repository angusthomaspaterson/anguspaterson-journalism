Tiesto selling out all over the country

Angus Paterson · 16 September 2009 · inthemix

As the hype builds for the release of Tiesto’s hugely controversial Kaleidoscope album in early October, news has reached ITM that all of his shows in Sydney, Melbourne and Perth have sold out – with promoters Hardware dropping the tantalising hint that we can expect another big announcement over the next couple of days.

Kaleidoscope is shaping up to be one of the year’s most talked about dance albums – both criticized and embraced with open arms in equal measures. ITM has been lucky enough to have had an advance listen, and we reckon it’s gonna be a mighty big smash hit that wins him a whole new legion of fans…

Stay tuned for more Tiesto news shortly! Kaleidoscope Australian tour dates below:

Fri Jan 29th – Sydney, Hordern Pavilion (SOLD OUT)
Sun Jan 31st – Brisbane, Riverstage
Fri Feb 5th – Melbourne, Hisense Arena (SOLD OUT)
Sat Feb 6th – Melbourne, Hisense Arena (SOLD OUT)
Thu Feb 11th – Perth, Metro City (SOLD OUT)
Fri Feb 12th – Perth, Metro City (SOLD OUT)
