Australia's first Sensation photos

Angus Paterson · 3 January 2010 · inthemix

Sensation returned to Melbourne’s Etihad Stadium on New Years Eve with ‘Ocean of White’, an event that was even more spectacular in every way than last year’s debut party. While the spectacle was held back and restrained until ‘The Mix’ dropped at 2am, after that it was switched on in full force – giant fountains, fireworks, special effects, stunning images projected in water mist and a laser show to rival anything you’ve ever seen before. Spectacular.

Where you there in Melbourne to check it out, or are you interested to see what you missed out on? Check out the first photos on ITM…
