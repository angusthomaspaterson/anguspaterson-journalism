Modular mentoring opportunity up for grabs

Angus Paterson · 25 June 2009 · inthemix

Looking for new opportunities in your creative endeavours? Qantas’s Spirit of Youth Awards, also known as SOYA, is known as one of the country’s leading grants programs for new creative talent, providing unique mentorship opportunities to young creative hopefuls. Registrations are now open, with the opportunity for a young music hopeful to learn the ropes with the brains behind Modular Records.

SOYA is now in its sixth year, and gives winners aged 30 and under the chance to win a 12-month mentorship with industry leaders across the seven creative fields of fashion, film, visual arts, visual communications, industrial design, photography and of course music. Winners will receive financial support in the form of $5000 in cash and $5000 in flights from Qantas across the seven SOYA categories.

And who’s the mentor for the luck music winner? None other than Stephen Pavlovic from Modular, who’s been involved with helping shape the careers of acts like The Avalanches, Ben Lee, The Living End, Wolfmother, Jack Johnson, Cut Copy and The Presets over the past decade, as well as being behind more than a few festivals and tours. Who better to give you a helping hand into the music industry?

Submissions are open until the end of July, get yourself to www.soya.com.au for more details!
