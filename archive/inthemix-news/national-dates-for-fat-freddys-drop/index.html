National dates for Fat Freddy's Drop

Angus Paterson · 12 February 2009 · inthemix

Brought to you by our good friends over at Niche Productions, New Zealand’s Fat Freddy’s Drop will be returning to Australia in March to help fill the gap for fans who are desperately (and impatiently) awaiting the release of their new album. They’ve won over a legion of admirers over the past three years with their fusion of soul, jazz, reggae and hi-tech sounds, with a wildly successful debut album Based On A True Story and an unbeatable live show, and on their upcoming tour you’ll get the chance to catch them roadtesting tunes off their sophomore Big BW album.

Fat Freddy’s Drop tour dates during March…

Thursday 19th – Palace Metro, Melbourne
Friday 20th – HQ, Adelaide
Saturday 21st – Enmore Theatre, Sydney
Friday 27th – The Tivoli, Brisbane
Saturday 28th – The Great Northern, Byron Bay
Sunday 29th – The Great Northern, Byron Bay
