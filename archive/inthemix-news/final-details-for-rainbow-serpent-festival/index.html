Final details for Rainbow Serpent Festival

Angus Paterson · 19 January 2009 · inthemix

The Rainbow Serpent Festival returns this weekend to Western Victoria, and it promises one of the best events in its 11-year history. While at its heart it might be an electronic music festival, you can expect a lot more still: art, spiritual education, relaxation, healing and much more. Add to this roving performance artists, giant sculpture and light installations, workshops and speakers on a multitude of topics, and you’ve got one of the best outdoor festivals of its type in the Southern Hemisphere.

Leading the charge on the lineup will be the acclaimed D-Nox and Beckers, one of Germany’s most highly acclaimed producer duos of progressive house, and Canadian born producer Mathew Jonson from the Wagon Repair will also be making himself known at the festival. That’s just getting started, and there’s a list of underground names that stretch across a variety of genres.

Looking to the ‘arts’ side of the festival, the organiser’s Community Arts Grants Rainbow allowed them to push the push the boundaries a little bit more. Alongside the music there’ll be a massive sideshow of circus, cabaret, puppetry, theatre and fire performances on the sidelines, as well as Gypsy and Dub live bands. And slottingn into the ‘lifestyle’ category will be a three-day program of personal growth, with workshops on Energy and Ceremony, Sound and Percussion, Indigenous Culture and much more, as well as guests speaking on topics like Health and Wellbeing, Spirituality and Ceremony and so forth.

Wanna find out more? Check out the Rainbow Serpent Festival Page for more info on what you can expect this weekend.
