Akon's Melbourne gig spirals into brawl

Angus Paterson · 29 October 2009 · inthemix

Look, when we heard Sexy Bitch for the first time we also wanted to punch someone in the face as well, but Melbourne, we think you’ve taken things a little too far. With a rap-sheet that boasts nearly 20 people treated for injuries, assaults on security guards, several arrests and Akon himself leaping into the crowd to try and quell the mayhem, Melbourne is starting to sport a pretty nasty reputation. Yep, all this went down at the Akon’s Melbourne show at the Hisense Arena earlier this week. Geez…

The trouble erupted when a 50-strong crowd tried to storm the entrance to the venue, with two security guards suffering serious injuries and police confirming that that 18 people required medical treatment. Akon was forced to halt his performance as the mayhem erupted in the crowd, with the rapper taking the rather risky choice of jumping down into the crowd and urging them to “calm down”.

Shadow Attorney-General Robert Clark told the Herald Sun the incident was another example of Melbourne’s night-life violence “People can’t even go to a concert without violence breaking out. It’s now getting to the stage where overseas visitors are remarking on the fact that Melbourne’s not safe.”

Akon & T-Pain’s subsequent gig up in Sydney reportedly went off without a hitch.
