inthemix gets intimate with Paul van Dyk

Angus Paterson · 31 July 2007 · inthemix

Following last week’s massive news that the world’s #1 DJ Paul van Dyk will be touring Australia not once but twice in 2007, inthemix is proud to announce that it will be bringing you the once-in-a-lifetime chance to catch the man in an intimate environment. inthemix with… Paul van Dyk will be taking place on Sunday 14th October in the cosy surrounds of Yu. Tickets are on sale now and are strictly limited to 500 people, so don’t miss out!

A fixture on the global dance scene since the early 90s, Paul van Dyk is riding high at the moment on the back of his new single White Lies featuring Jessica Sutta of the Pussycat Dolls. The single is lifted from van Dyk’s fantastic upcoming new album In Between, and we’re told to expect everything from house, electro, progressive and of course trance, in all its different shades and styles.

He’ll be back in Australia in December, but this could very well be your only chance to ever catch Paul van Dyk in such an intimate environment. It’s the next in a series of “inthemix with…” events that in the past has featured massive names like Deep Dish, Grandmaster Flash and only just earlier this year, DJ Shadow and Tiga. It’s gonna be another pearler of an event, so don’t miss out!

inthemix with… Paul van Dyk takes place on Sunday 14th October at Yu, tickets available now from ITM for an early-bird price of $49+BF and $59+BF thereafter.
