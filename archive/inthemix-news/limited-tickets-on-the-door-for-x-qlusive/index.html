Limited tickets on the door for X-Qlusive

Angus Paterson · 19 December 2008 · inthemix

With the big night nearly upon us, word has trickled through that presales to X-Qlusive have completely sold out. However, for hard dance fanatics who’ve made the last minute decision that they need to go, there will be 100 door tickets available at the Box Office tonight from 9pm at a cost of $149.65, and it’s a matter of first in best dressed!

For everyone else who’s got tickets already, it’s now just a matter of a few hours wait…
