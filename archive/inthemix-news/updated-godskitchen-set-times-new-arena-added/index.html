UPDATED: Godskitchen set times + new arena added

Angus Paterson · 2 October 2009 · inthemix

The awe-inspiring ‘Boombox’ has finally landed in the country, with the annual Godskitchen tour set to return to Australia this weekend, and the set times have arrived for when the various DJs will be taking stepping up to take their place within the mighty structure. What’s that you say, you heard John ‘00’ Fleming was getting a whole two and a half hours to do his thing? You heard right.

Due to an overwhelming last-minute rush for tickets, Future Entertainment have also announced that a new ‘Old Skool’ arena has been added. Check out the set times for both rooms below…

Godskitchen ‘Boombox’ set times:

10.00-10.30: DJ Competition Winner
10.30-11.15: Robbie Lowe
11.15-12.00: Pee Wee
12.00-1.30: Blank & Jones
01.30-02.00: JES (Live)
02.00-03.30 :Cosmic Gate
03.30-06.00: John 00 Fleming

Godskitchen Old Skool Arena:

10.00-11.00: Nick Muller
11.00-12.00: Sasha Vatoff
12.00-01.00: Tom-e
01.00-02.00: Nik Fish
02.00-3.00: Matrix
03.00-05.00: Dave Kirkpatrick

Along with J00F, trance superstars Cosmic Gate will be also headlining the proceedings (check out the ITM interview from this week), and they’ll be joined by fellow veterans Blank & Jones along with a live performance from Jes. For anyone who’s still yet to see the Boombox in action, check out the gallery below…
