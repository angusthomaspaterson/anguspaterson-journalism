Good Vibrations sideshows keep rolling in!

Angus Paterson · 5 December 2007 · inthemix

Following our announcement earlier today that Calvin Harris would be playing sideshows when he visits Australia for the Good Vibrations tour early next year, we’ve got a whole other bunch to unveil… The Rapture, Thievery Corporation and K-OS! Looks like February is going to be an extremely busy month…

Most of you will already be familiar with The Rapture – we saw them earlier this year on the V Festival tour, and you just can’t beat their Ewan Pearson produced indie/disco crossover sound. Thievery Corporation would have to be one of the world’s greatest ever ‘chillout’ act (dispel from your mind any negative connotations that go along with that word) and they’ll be stripping it back from their 20 piece band for a special DJ set at the Basement in Sydney. Meanwhile the MC/Vocalist/multi-instrumentalist/producer K-OS will be touring Australia for the very first time, and if you’re in Sydney you’ll be able to catch him at a special sideshow at the Gaelic Club.

Want some special Good Vibrations sideshow action? Check the dates below for details…

Calvin Harris – Friday, February 8th: Hi Fi Bar, Melbourne
Calvin Harris – Thursday, February 14: The Forum, Sydney
The Rapture – Thursday, February 14th: Prince of Wales, Melbourne
The Rapture – Friday, February 15th: The Metro, Sydney
Thievery Corporation (DJ Set) – Friday, February 15th: The Basement, Sydney
K-OS – Thursday, February 14th: Gaelic Club, Sydney
