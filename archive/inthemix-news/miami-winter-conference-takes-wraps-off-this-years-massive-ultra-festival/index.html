Miami Winter Conference takes wraps off this year’s massive Ultra Festival

Angus Paterson · 13 February 2007 · inthemix

Everyone who means anything in global dance will be at the Miami Winter Conference this March, one of the big-ticket annual events for electronic and music worldwide. The lineup for the massive weekender windup party has just been announced, spread over two days for the first time ever. And this year inthemix will be there to capture it all in a special series of reports.

The Ultra Music Festival returns again to downtown Miami’s waterfront Bicentennial Park on Friday 23rd and Saturday 24th of March. The focus on the first day will be live acts with the Ultra Live! Main Stage showcasing The Cure, a special live performance from Tiesto, Shiny Toy Guns and Miss Kittin. The Ultra Tents will be headlined by Deep Dish and Junkie XL with many more to be announced.

Saturday will drive it home with more of a straight forward clubbing sound, featuring Paul van Dyk alongside a diverse selection of heavyweights including Carl Cox, Erick Morillo, Danny Tenaglia, Ferry Corsten and Richie Hawtin.

The organisers are promising more than 150 DJs, bands and producers performing across nine with more than 50,000 music fans expected to be rocking up for the party.

Check out www.ultramusicfestival.com for more details, and stay tuned to ITM as we reveal more details about our special coverage of the Miami Winter Music Conference in 2007.
