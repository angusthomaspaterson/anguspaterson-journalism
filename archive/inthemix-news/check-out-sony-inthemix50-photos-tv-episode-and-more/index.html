Check out Sony inthemix50 photos, TV episode & more

Angus Paterson · 20 August 2009 · inthemix

On Wednesday night the cream of Australia’s dance music industry gathered together at Sydney’s Nevermind to witness the unveiling of the 2009 results for the Sony inthemix50. There was professional rivalry, dreams were realised, hearts were broken and there was a whole lot of booze consumed, both before and after the trophies were handed out. For those who were there, and for anyone who wants to have a look at what went down, we’ve got plenty of photos of all the DJs, promoters and industry heads who made it along, as well as a special episode of inthemix.tv.
