Spank Records get silly at 5th Birthday celebrations

Angus Paterson · 8 May 2007 · inthemix

It seems like only yesterday that Mark Murphy, Chris Lo and Leanne Clegg opened a small record store in Darlinghurst called Spank Records. Believe it or not that was five years ago and it’s become a Sydney dance music institution in that time. If you rock along to the Terrace at Home Nightclub on Saturday May 19th, as part of its weekly night Famous, you’ll be able to help them celebrate.

There will be a slew of DJs playing in party mode including Mark Murphy and Chris Lo naturally, but also getting silly on the evening will be Tim Sea, Trix, J-Flex, Spank worker bee Custard Jim and other surprise guests.

When Spank Records first opened it had four turntables, no air conditioning and two browsers of records… Five years down the track and they now occupy a store five times the original size that stocks a lot more than just vinyl – everything from music production software and hardware to DJ equipment and accessories. They were the first dance music store in Sydney to have an online store and they’ll also be making the big shift into digital distributions in a couple of short weeks.

Come and help Spank Records celebrate on the Terrace at Home on Saturday May 19, and check out the Spank Records site for more details.
