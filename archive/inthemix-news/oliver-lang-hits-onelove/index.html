Oliver Lang hits Onelove

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2007 · inthemix

Onelove will be bringing a little slice of Ibiza to Australia when they host a DJ performance from Oliver Lang on Saturday October 27th. Described as the “future hero of house” by none other than Erick Morillo, he’s been making waves with his residency at Ibiza beach bar *Bora Bora*as well as a high-profile series of remixes and a compilation for Bar Grooves. Spinning chunky house and electro with an edge of funk and tribal, get ready to see what he’s got to offer.

Oliver Lang hits Onelove on Saturday October 27th at The Prince Bandroom, with support from Tommy Trash (Syd) and Disco Mafia.
