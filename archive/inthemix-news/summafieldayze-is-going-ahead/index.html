Summafieldayze is going ahead!

Angus Paterson · 4 January 2008 · inthemix

Have you been concerned about all the terrible weather, worried that Queensland’s biggest New Year event Summafieldayze won’t be going ahead? Well never fear, because the promoters have assured us that the party will be definitely be happening this Saturday! Future Entertainment, Fuzzy and Cross Promotions have again teamed up to put the event on and they’ve issued the following statement…

“The Summafieldayze event site is currently being upgraded to minimize any issues that may arise with wild weather. This includes the restructure of the event stages and the move from circus style tents to dome stages. There will be plenty of undercover and shaded areas for shelter but please bring suitable clothing for all weather conditions.” You can check out the upgraded map at the Future Entertainment website.

So the party’s happening after all! Check out the ITM Full Coverage page for a whole heap of interviews, news and DJ sets to get you in the mood.
