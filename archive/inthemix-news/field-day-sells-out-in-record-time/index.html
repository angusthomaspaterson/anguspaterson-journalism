Field Day sells out in record time

Angus Paterson · 9 October 2007 · inthemix

In what’s proving to be a crazy week for festival announcements and sell-out events, things are rolling on with the news that Fuzzy’s annual Field Day event has completely sold out after being on sale for less than an hour! The punters pounced at midday today when tickets officially went on sale, and before 1pm the tickets had already all officially been snapped up. That makes it a record for the iconic Sydney event!

For those who were lucky enough to get tickets, you’ll be enjoying a lineup with more live acts that ever before including the likes of Bonde Do Role, Chromeo, Go! Team, Malente, People Under the Stairs and more.

All up there’s a diversity in musical styles that covers everything from house to electro to hip-hop, breaks and tech, and some of the international DJ names that will be playing are Swedish housemeisters Angello & Ingrosso, ‘Dirty Bird’ tech house star Claude Von Stroke, electro pioneer Felix Da Housecat as well as Stanton Warriors, one of the classic ‘Fuzzy Breaks’ acts who have held a slot at Field Day in years gone by.

it’s all scheduled to happen again in the Domain on January 1st, 2008. Did you manage to get tickets?
