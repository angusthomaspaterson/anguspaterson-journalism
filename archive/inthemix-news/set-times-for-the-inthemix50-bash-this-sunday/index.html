Set times for the inthemix50 bash this Sunday!

Angus Paterson · 14 September 2007 · inthemix

Following much anticipation and a massive industry bash on Wednesday night the results are in – check them out HERE. To celebrate inthemix will be throwing a massive bash at Sounds on Sunday this weekend. Check below for the set times!

Taking over the whole venue, the ‘Cream of the Crop’ in the Courtyard will be featuring performances from some of the biggest names in Australian dance music including Ben Morris, Grant Smillie, Hook n Sling, Gus da Hoodrat and more. And to mark the new ‘Producer’ category in this year’s poll the Chapel will play host to some of our most impressive production talents including Bass Kleph, Deepchild and Stick Figures.

It should be a massive day with sunny weather forecast to boot, so we hope to see you all there! Tickets are on sale now for $20+BF or $15+BF for ITM-VIPs.

The inthemix50 party will be landing at Sounds on Sunday this weekend, peek below for set times. Check out the FULL COVERAGE page for a huge assortment of inthemix50 DJ mixes.
