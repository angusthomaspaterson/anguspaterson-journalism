De Jah Groove take their mellow sounds across Australia

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2007 · inthemix

Six-piece act De Jah Groove have a new single out called Mellow Stages, a blazing fusion of rock, roots, horns, percussion and drum ‘n bass and rocking guitars. You’ll get the chance to experience it in the live setting as they take it across the country as they prepare to unleash their new album on the public.

Doing all of the hard work on their own back, the band released their debut EP Renegade Reggae in late 2006 and have already attracted a nationwide following and airplay on Triple J. While their new single is in the shops already, their debut album Rock Bottom Jackpot will be ready for release this November.

Catch De Jah Groove at the launch of their Rock Bottom Jackpot album this November.
