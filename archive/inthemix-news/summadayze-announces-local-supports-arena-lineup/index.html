Summadayze announces local supports + arena lineup

Angus Paterson · 14 December 2009 · inthemix

Melbourne’s key New Years Day event returns to the Myer Music Bowl in 2010, featuring a typically stunning array of international DJ talent, but of course there’s going to be a fair few of Australia’s biggest dance music players helping hold the day down with some local produce. Future Entertainment have just released details on who will be clocking in for a set on New Years Day, as well as giving us an idea of what DJs will be playing in what arena during the day.

Summadayze will certainly be packing one huge lineup in 2010. While the main stage will be packing a massive posses of names like Carl Cox, Danny Tenaglia, Sharam, Eddie Halliwell and 2manydjs, the ‘Yellow’ stage will be hosting quality underground names like Mark Knight, Sebastian Leger, Danny Howells and more.

And that’s just getting started, as the music will be spread across a massive eight stages, with the ‘Sorry Grandma’ stage in particular boasting a list of acts that stretches from LCD Soundsystem DJs to Josh Wink to Ian Carey, while the ‘Strobe’ stage is set to feature some most excellent noise from the likes of Sinden to Fake Blood to Josh Wink.
