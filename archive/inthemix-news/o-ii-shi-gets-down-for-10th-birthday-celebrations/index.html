O-ii-shi gets down for 10th birthday celebrations

Angus Paterson · 8 March 2007 · inthemix

The O-ii-shi series of local hip-hop parties will be marking its entry into double digits with an impressive hat trick of international guests. With three shows lined up from now until the end of Easter, we’ll be treated to sets from DJ Revolution, the Expansion Team Sound System and DJ Shortkut.

DJ Revolution is a turntablist based in LA, California who is responsible for one of the most syndicated hip hip radio programs in the world, The Wake Up Show, reaching a massive 12 million listeners weekly. A swell as being a popular DJ he also has a highly regarded place in the US music industry, and he’ll be playing Friday 30th March at Candy’s Apartment.

DJ Babu and Rakaa are the Expansion Team Sound System, also hailing from LA as a ‘party rocking’ duo that still manages to push the boundaries. Famous for their series of Nightlife parties in Hollywood, they’ve released three successful albums under the Dilated Peoples banner as well as countless numbers of collaborations. They’ll hit the Gaelic Club on Saturday 7th of April.

Lucky last, DJ Shortkut will be playing Anzac Eve, Tuesday 24th April at a venue yet to be determined. He’s yet another celebrated turntablist from the States who has won a whole stack of awards for his mixing skills.

Keep an eye out for the three O-ii-shi parties coming up over the next month.
