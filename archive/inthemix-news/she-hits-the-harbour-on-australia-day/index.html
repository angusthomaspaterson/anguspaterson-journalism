SHE hits the harbour on Australia Day

Angus Paterson · 15 January 2008 · inthemix

One of Sydney’s favourite sources of soulful house SHE will be taking to the harbour this Australia day for their annual summer boat party, on a 3-level luxury cruiser DJ Jorj from the Defected Records crew along to help create the vibe. Jorj has also released production on imprints like Distant Records in the US and Curious in the UK and he’s know as a a master behind the decks, bringing the party with his latin and soulful beats.

DJ Jorj will be hitting the harbour with the SHE crew on January 26th, tickets available now on ITM!
