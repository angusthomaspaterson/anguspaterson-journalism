Big Day Out set times released

Angus Paterson · 3 January 2008 · inthemix

Recovered from all your New Year shenanigans yet? Well there’s no rest of the wicked because it’s already time to start thinking about how you’re going to plan your adventures when the Big Day Out rolls into town later this month. With a bit of a 90s resurgence on the cards with Bjork and Rage Against the Machine toplining the bill, and what’s turned out to be quite an impressive range of electronic acts with UNKLE, LCD Soundsystem, Krafty Kuts and Carl Cox all set to smash the Boiler Room, it’s set to be an event to remember.
