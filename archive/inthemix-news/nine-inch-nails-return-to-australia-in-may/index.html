Nine Inch Nails return to Australia in May

Angus Paterson · 5 February 2007 · inthemix

The brainchild of musical genius Trent Reznor, US act Nine Inch Nails are simply one of the greatest electronic/rock acts ever – and Australians will get another chance to taste one of their searing live performances this May. The band’s website has revealed they will soon be back to play shows across Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane.

Trent Reznor first began pushing his unique and angst-ridden brand of electro/industrial/metal in the late 80’s, but he really took the world by storm with the release of that masterpiece The Downward Spiral in the mid 90’s. After taking a break for a few years and taking stock of his personal issues and problems with addiction, he returned in 2005 with the critically acclaimed With Teeth album. The band is due to release a live DVD later this month.

Tickets go on sale 23rd February from Ticketek, or pre-sale on 19th February from the official NIN website. Check below for dates.
– May 7th: Riverstage, Brisbane.
– May 9th: Luna Park, Sydney.
– May 13th: Metro, Melbourne
