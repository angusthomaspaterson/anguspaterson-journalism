inthemix.tv goes to Sensation 'Wicked Wonderland'!

Angus Paterson · 22 July 2009 · inthemix

Earlier this month mega promotions company ID&T debuted its spectacular Sensation ‘Wicked Wonderland’ event in Amsterdam over two sold-out evenings, giving the world the first taste of the spectacle that will be toured all over Europe and possibly eventually to Australia too. inthemix.tv was there to capture all of the wonder, with an episode that’ll make it just a little bit easier to hold out for Sensation’s ‘Ocean of White’ event in Melbourne this NYE…

Not nearly enough ‘Wicked Wonderland’? Check out our gallery of the event to get a glimpse of dramatic sights that ID&T brought together for the event. Sander Van Doorn, Fedde Le Grande and Sebastian Ingrosso were all there to lay down the tunes, but the Sensation experience is about so much more than just the music…
