Deadmau5 & Stantons play Good Vibrations afterparty

Angus Paterson · 13 January 2009 · inthemix

The crowned king of Beatport and the lynchpin duo of breakbeat will both be on deck to keep the party going after Good Vibrations winds up on Saturday February 14th, with the festival’s official afterparty taking place at the Chinese Laundry. That’s right, both Deadmau5 and the Stanton Warriors are confirmed to play, as well as a performance from Rico Tubbs so it’ll make it an evening of pure mayhem.

In other related news on Good Vibrations acts who’ll be playing club shows when in town, drum n’ bass icon Roni Size and his partner on the mic Dynamite MC have been confirmed for an exclusive show at Chinese Laundry’s weekly Yum Cha night on Friday the 13th February. Tickets for both nights will only be available on the door, with no pre-sales available so punter discretion is advised…

Stay tuned to ITM for interviews with Deadmau5, Stanton Warriors and Roni Size in the lead up to the Good Vibrations tour.
