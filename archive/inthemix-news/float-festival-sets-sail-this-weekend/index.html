Float Festival sets sail this weekend

Angus Paterson · 22 January 2009 · inthemix

For anyone wanting to take advantage of the great weather this Australia Day Long Weekend, how does stepping (or sailing) into Sydney Harbour on a luxurious 3-level cruiser sound? Float Festival will be doing just that, with the tuines being provided by acts from both the house and hi-NRG scene. Sunny times ahoy!

Headlining on the hi-NRG side will be Sunset Brothers, with support form Micky D, Andre Jay and Charlie Brown. On the house side of things, Ministry of Sound fave Goodwill will be playing alongside Tom Piper, Matt Nukewood and Tass with Bongolicious George on vocals. Time to hit the water people!

Float Festival sets sail this Saturday January 24th at 3pm from King Street Wharf in front of Cargo Bar, tickets on sale now!
