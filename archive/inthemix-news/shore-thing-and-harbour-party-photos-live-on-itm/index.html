Shore Thing & Harbour Party photos live on ITM

Angus Paterson · 2 January 2008 · inthemix

With the dust settling from all the NY madness, check out the fireworks-heavy coverage from Lunar Park’s Harbour Party HERE, as well as the photos from the epic Bondi event featuring Groove Armada HERE. The sold out event went off with a bang, and was marred only by a punter who was punished dearly for having a crack at the timeworn tradition of jumping the fence!

According to the SMH, a 21-year-old man is in a serious condition in St Vincent’s Hospital after an attempt to gatecrash the party went horribly wrong. Allegedly, he was walking along a wall beside a restaurant shortly before midnight before climbing onto a bunch of stacked beer kegs and grabbing an air-conditioning unit. When the unit broke and the man lost his balance, he fell two metres to the ground where he was impaled on a piece of metal that penetrated his neck, jaw and shoulder region. Police were on hand to quickly administer first aid, prior to the arrival of the ambulance.

Beyond any aborted attempts to crash the 15,000 strong party, all reports indicate that Shore Thing managed to live up to its reputation of providing a massive way to bring in the new year. According to our own forums HERE, “Everyone was up for an awesome time… the music from where I was standing was clear and crisp without reverberation or delay, and GA’s set, flanked by their lightening sequence from their newly (relatively anyway) lighting technician, suited the surrounds ideally.”

Stay tuned to ITM for a stack of New Year photos and reviews, and if you’re keen to relive the Groove Armada experience then check out the NEWS about the rumored DJ gig in Sydney this week.
