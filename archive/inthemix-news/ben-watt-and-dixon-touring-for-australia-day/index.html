Ben Watt & Dixon touring for Australia Day

Angus Paterson · 30 December 2009 · inthemix

Buzzin Fly versus Innervision? At one special Australia Day boat party in Sydney at least, that’ll definitely be the case. Either way, we’ll be treated to tours from two of underground house and techno’s finest names in late January when both Ben Watt and Dixon land for a series of shows. Thought you already had too much to do on Tuesday 26th January? Well think again!

DJ/producer and head of the revered Buzzin Fly stable, Ben Watt has long since transcended his status as part of the Everything But The Girl duo, since going on to become one of underground dance music’s most loved figures in the UK. Dixon on the other hand holds a similar status over in Germany. As well as his involvement with the Innervisions label, and an impressive reputation as a producer, he’s keen to emphasise that he’s a DJ first and foremost, specializing in house on the deeper end of the spectrum.

Ben Watt tour dates:

Tue 26th Jan – Agwa Yacht Club, Buzzin Fly vs. Innervisions, Sydney
Fri Jan 29th – Darkbeat, Melbourne
Sat Jan 30th – Barsoma, Brisbane

Dixon tour dates (more TBA):

Mon 25th Jan – New Guernica, Melbourne
Tue 26th Jan – Agwa Yacht Club, Buzzin Fly vs. Innervisions, Sydney
