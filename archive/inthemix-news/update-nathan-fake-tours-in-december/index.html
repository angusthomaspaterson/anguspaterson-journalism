UPDATE: Nathan Fake tours in December

Angus Paterson · 6 October 2009 · inthemix

Promoters Future Entertainment have updated details on Nathan Fake’s tour, confirming his appearance in Perth but clarifying that he won’t be appearing at Sydney’s The Likes of You, playing instead at Chinese Laundry at a date TBA.

The Border Community star himself Nathan Fake will be back in the country before the year is out, with gigs so far announced for Melbourne, Sydney and Perth. He’ll be playing live, appearing in Melbourne at with The Likes of You with a stunning all-star cast of supporting techno aficionados that includes Format:B, Alex Smoke and Arnaud le Texier! It’s set to be one of the biggest Likes of You parties of the year.

Nathan Fake returned this year with Hard Islands, the follow up to his debut album Drowning In a Sea of Love and he’s been delivering fuzzy-edged synths and maximal acidic techno beats since he was still in his teens. The last time he was here was way back in early 2007, so he’s well overdue for another visit.

Updated Nathan Fake tour dates, stay tuned for further details…

Fri Dec 11th – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Fri Dec 18th – Democracy, Perth
Date TBA – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
