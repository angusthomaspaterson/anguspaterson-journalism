Yuksek plays some Splendour sideshows

Angus Paterson · 23 June 2009 · inthemix

We already knew that French electro rocker Yuksek AKA Pierre Alexandre-Claude Busson would be in the country for a live performance at Byron Bay’s beloved Splendour in the Grass festival in July, but it’s now been revealed he’s also locked in for a series of DJ gigs around the country. From Canberra to Perth, he’ll be taking his distorted sound from corner to corner!

Yuksek will be here in promotion of his debut full length album from last year Away From The Sea, which saw him put his classical musical training put to work with a release that combined distorted electro with pop hooks, indie riffs and whatever else he decided to throw into the equation. He’s backed it up in 2009 with recent remixes for Peaches, Tiga and The Teenagers with his longtime studio partner Brodinski, as well as further musical reinterpretations of the likes of Phoenix’s Lizstomania, Lady Gaga’s Paparazzi and Autokratz’s Always More.

It’ll be Yuksek’s third time in Australia, following his debut club tour in 2007 and his presence on the Parklife lineup last year.

Yuksek tour dates:

Fri July 24th – Boomtick presents @ Villa, Perth (DJ set)
Sat July 25th – Splendour in the Grass @ Belongil Fields, Byron Bay (live)
Sun July 26th – Pang! @ Lot 33, Canberra (DJ set)
Fri July 31st – Lick It @ Empire Hotel, Brisbane (DJ set)
Sat August 1st – The Club Club @ Chinese Laundry, Sydney (DJ set)
Sun August 2nd – Roxanne Parlour, Melbourne (DJ set)
