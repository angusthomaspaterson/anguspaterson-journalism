Luke Fair gets Famous next Saturday with encore set

Angus Paterson · 16 April 2007 · inthemix

So you enjoyed Luke Fair’s bangin’ set at Sweetchilli last Friday night? Here’s your chance to see him again as he takes over the main floor of Famous on April 28th. In Australia to launch the latest installment of the Balance compilation series, the funky progressive don has well and truly rocked’ the house wherever he has played and he’s a perfect fit for Home Nightclub’s brand new Saturday night.

Luke Fair has carved out quite a reputation for himself as a producer and DJ over the past few years, releasing mix compilations on labels like Yoshitoshi and Bedrock with music that can be described as funky, housey and techy all at the same time. Renowned for playing his own re-edits of tracks in his sets, in the studio, in the studio his productions run the gamut from dubby house to melodic progressive.

Catch Luke Fair at Famous on Saturday 28th April, with main floor support from Carl Kennedy and Goodwill.
