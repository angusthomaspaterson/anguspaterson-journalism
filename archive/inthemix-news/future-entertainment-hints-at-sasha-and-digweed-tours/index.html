Future Entertainment hints at Sasha & Digweed tours

Angus Paterson · 14 July 2009 · inthemix

Future Entertainment main man Mark James has a history of regularly dropping bombs on the ITM forums, and he’s done it again with the revelation that we can look forward to appearances from both Sasha and John Digweed. Mark spoke out in a thread in the Melbourne forums this week confirming the respective tours from the dance music icons, with Sasha scheduled to reach Australia in November while Digweed will be here for Future Music Festival in 2010.

This buzz on the street is that Sasha will be flying in to Australia for a series of club appearances, in contrast to his mainstage festival appearances last year at Global Gathering, while Digweed will be returning for another headline slot at Future’s flagship summer festival tour after playing some extremely memorable gigs in 2007.

Want to know more? So do we, so stay tuned to ITM for further updates as soon as they come to hand.
