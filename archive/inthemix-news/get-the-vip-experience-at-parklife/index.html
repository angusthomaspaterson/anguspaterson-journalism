Get the VIP experience at Parklife!

Angus Paterson · 27 June 2007 · inthemix

Got your credit card at the ready to buy Parklife tickets when they go on sale? If you’re buying your tickets from ITM then we have a special offer exclusively for ITM-VIP members that is sure to put some extra razzle dazzle into your festival experience. You’ll also receive a pass to Fuzzy’s Festival VIP Area on the day, where you can chill from the crowds and mingle with your favourite artists.

As you’ll already be fully aware, Parklife 2007 has an awesome lineup and more live acts on the bill than we’ve ever seen before. It’s held in spectacular outdoor venues across the country so it’ll be the hottest ticket this coming September and October. To take advantage of this special offer you need to be logged in as an ITM-VIP member when you purchase your Parklife tickets, and it’s limited until 23rd August or alternatively, when the VIP passes sell out.

And as an extra little bonus for anyone buying their Parklife tickets thrrough inthemix, if you pay using Paypal then you’ll be given a copy of the ITM ‘06 CD featuring Ben Watt and Ivan Gough at no extra cost!

Not an ITM-VIP yet but want access to the VIP area at Parklife? Join ITM-VIP so you can be part of the fun, and remember tickets go on sale in Sydney Thursday 28th June, Perth and Adelaide on 5th July and Melbourne and Brisbane TBC.
