Get a taste of Weird Science at the Phoenix

Angus Paterson · 13 August 2007 · inthemix

In the spirit of ‘Weird Science’, Oxford Street’s Phoenix Bar will play host to new monthly night Geek Sheik that will bring you a whole bunch of excellent dancefloor oddities that normally get passed by at your average club night. Expect an eclectic selection of indie/electro, disco-punk and assorted tech.

Expect performances from Tommy Trash, Johnny Rad, The Famous (Live), Qubism, Kid Kay Ferris, Roksteady and there will also be extensive effort paid to decorating the venue in a true ‘Weird Science’ fashion.

Sound like your thing? Get on down to the Phoenix on Friday August 31 and don’t forget to bring your labcoat…
