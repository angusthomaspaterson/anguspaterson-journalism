LA Riots starting fights in Australia

Angus Paterson · 25 September 2009 · inthemix

Prepare yourselves for noise... The LA Riots are among the new wave of electro heavies to have emerged from the heady Los Angeles scene, ranking alongside the now defunct Guns N’ Bombs (check them here talking about their city’s electro mayhem), and in October they’ll be bringing it to Australia.

Taking their name from the infamous riots that ripped throughout the city back in 1992, so far they’ve remixed artists like VHS or Beta, Choromeo, Photek and Justice alongside their own heavy club bangers. With remixes still to come from Datarock, Kid Sister and The Cure, expect plenty more noise from these guys.

LA Riots tour dates:

30th Oct – Home, Sydney
31st Oct – Ruby Tramp, Gold Coast
5th Nov – Monastary,Brisbane
6th Nov – Villa, Perth
7th Nov – Eurotrash, Melbourne
