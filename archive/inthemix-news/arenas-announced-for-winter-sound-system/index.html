Arenas announced for Winter Sound System

Angus Paterson · 21 May 2009 · inthemix

The interest in Future Entertainment’s brand new June Long Weekend event Winter Sound System has been huge, and with a lineup featuring the likes of Armin Van Buuren, Armand Van Helden, James Zabiela, Laidback Luke and much more, it’s not surprising at all. Taking place at the the Rod Laver Arena, Hisense Arena and the surrounding areas at Melbourne Park of Sunday June 7th, the arena lineups for the event have just been announced. Check them out below…
