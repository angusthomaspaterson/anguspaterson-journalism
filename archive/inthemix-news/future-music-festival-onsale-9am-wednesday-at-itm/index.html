Future Music Festival onsale 9am Wednesday at ITM

Angus Paterson · 21 October 2009 · inthemix

Keen for a bit of Future Music Festival action in the opening months of 2010 after looking upon last week’s bigger-than-big lineup announcement? Tickets go onsale right around the country from 9am on Wednesday 21st October at ITM, so if you’re looking to snap up some tickets at 1st release prices then make sure you’re locked and loaded.

If you’re a fan of dance and live music then there’s plenty to like about next year’s Future Music Festival lineup. Top lining the proceedings are live titans The Prodigy who will be returning to the country a whole lot faster than any of us expected (much to our delight). They’ll be backed up by Empire Of The Sun, Booka Shade and more.

Add to that some of the world’s most elite DJ talent, including John Digweed, Sven Vath and Above & Beyond, it’s a lineup that’s a whole lot of massive. Big big big.

Future Music Festival goes onsale at ITM from 9am Wednesday October 21st. Check out the Whats Ons for ticketing details!
