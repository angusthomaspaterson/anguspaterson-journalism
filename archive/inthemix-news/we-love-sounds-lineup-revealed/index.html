We Love Sounds lineup revealed!

Angus Paterson · 4 April 2007 · inthemix

Anticipation driving you crazy? Well wait no longer, because below we’ve got all the details of who you’ll be rocking out to over the June Long Weekend. We’ve just finished up with the biggest summer festival season that Sydney has ever seen, but never fear as we’ve got something to look forward to over the freezing winter months. We Love Sounds will be hitting the Hordern Complex on Sunday June 10, and there’s one hell of a lineup locked down for the party.

We Love Sounds was a sellout success in 2006, but this year it is set to almost double in size with the addition of the entire Royal Hall of Industries alongside the Hordern Pavilion and the Forum – the same setup that is used for the massive Mardi Gras parties every March. In fact, Sounds have even secured assistance from the technical producers of that annual event to help make this year’s winter festival the very best that it can be.

Tickets go on sale Tuesday 10 April from ITM, with first release costing $89.90 inc. BF and final release costing $99.90 inc. BF. Peek below for the lineup!
