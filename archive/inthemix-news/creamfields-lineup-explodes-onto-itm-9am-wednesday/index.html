Creamfields lineup explodes onto ITM 9am Wednesday!

Angus Paterson · 9 February 2010 · inthemix

THE LINEUP HAS BEEN RELEASED – READ THE FULL STORY HERE!

The biggest entry to the Australian festival calendar for 2010 is here, and tomorrow morning you’ll be able to find out the massive posse of dance talent who’ll be rocking up to the party alongside you in May. The Creamfields brand is set to make its Australian debut in a mere three months, and it’ll be bringing with it a lineup of artists to match the unbeatable heritage it’s built up globally since it kicked off in Liverpool in the late 90s. Make sure you’re back at ITM at 9am to check it out!

We’ve already been offered a few teasers, of course. Last week promoters Hardware and Onelove lifted the lid that those Italian crazies The Bloody Beetroots would be roaring back for the festival, this time with their six-piece band in tow they’ve dubbed the Death Crew 77. They’ll be joined by rising stars Riva Starr and Bingo Players, making up the hat-trick of acts confirmed so far.

Another massive name who’s rumored to be joining the ruckus is Swedish house don Steve Angello, revealing on Twitter recently he’d be in the country around the time of the festival. ITM has also put together a shortlist of artists with impressive odds to appear at Creamfields, so it’s time to place your bets people …
