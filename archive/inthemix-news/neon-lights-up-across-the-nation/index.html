Neon lights up across the nation

Angus Paterson · 27 April 2009 · inthemix

There’s a new kid in town that’s set to go head-to-head with some of Australia’s biggest clubbing brands. The Neon Records label is a brand new project for Warner Music, and they’ve already enjoyed several #1 hits on the ARIA Club Chart including Sidney Samson’s Riverside, which reigned in the top spot for a massive six weeks. The brand is now launching volume one of their mix compilation series Neon Essential, and they’ll be touring it extensively across the country between now and late June.

Mixed by TV Rock as well as Melbourne newcomer DJ Chardy, Neon Essential vol. 1 features 35 of the most upfront club hits with a tracklisting including names like Deadmau5, Sébastien Léger, Dirty South, Pryda, Armand Van Helden, Crookers and loads more. The tour will be touching in every corner of the country, and it’ll be the first of much more that’s planned to come for the Neon Records label.

Neon Essential vol. 1 is in the stores now, and you can catch it lighting up at the following shows across the country…
