Countdown for Field Day begins!

Angus Paterson · 21 September 2007 · inthemix

That’s right, the festival season is well and truly kicking into gear now as summer draws near, and ITM can reveal the date that Sydney’s most iconic and popular New Years party will be going on sale. If you’re planning on celebrating the New Year at Field Day in the Domain again this year, be back at ITM and have your credit cards ready at midday on Tuesday 9th October.

Since it debuted in Sydney on the first day of 2002 nearly six years ago, Field Day became one of the city’s premier festivals and the party to be at over the New Years period, one of the first festivals to begin selling out in record time and pretty much setting the trend for the rest of the massive events around the country.

Field Day has been intrinsically tied in with Sydney’s house and breaks scenes but in recent years has hosted a growing presence of the tougher-edged electro and tech sounds at the Killer stage. Who knows what lineup they’ve got in story for this year? Fuzzy confirms that we can expect a lineup announcement late next week.

Field Day goes on sale at ITM at midday on Tuesday 9th October. Check back next week for the lineup and have a look below at a clip of last year’s event!
