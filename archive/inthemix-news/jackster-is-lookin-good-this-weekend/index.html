Jackster is Lookin’ Good this weekend

Angus Paterson · 3 August 2007 · inthemix

Lookin’ Good returns to Candys Apartment this Saturday with the legendary Jackster emerging out of retirement for one massive night of classic house music. Having bowed out of the DJ game in 2005, the former Home resident and Clean and Serene founder will be returning for a special two-hour set.

Check it out at Candys Apartment this Saturday, $15 before 11pm and $20 after. ITM VIPs receive a $5 discount with valid membership card, set times are below…

Main Room
10.00 – 12.00: Mike Silver
12.00 – 01.30: Tom da Silva
01.30 – 03.30: Jackster
03.30 – 06.00: Adam Coverdale

Bulletproof Room
11.00 – 01.00: Jay Smalls
01.00 – 03.00: Allan Marshall
03.00 -05.00: Sam Serene
