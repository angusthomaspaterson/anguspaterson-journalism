Watch: Richie Hawtin & Giorgio Moroder go deep at RBMA New York

Angus Paterson · 29 May 2013 · inthemix

The Red Bull Music Academy is currently holed up in New York City, hosting a typically impressive cast of DJs, producers and otherwise artists for a series of lectures, showcases and performances that is sure to pique any dance fan’s curiosity.

Two of the keynote lectures for the NYC residency have been disco and electronic music icon Giorgio Moroder, who has recently been thrown back onto the spotlight via his heavily hyped collaborations with Daft Punk, as well as Richie Hawtin, a techno luminary who’s been increasingly taking his sound, and his Minus label, to a much wider audience. Rejoice, because there’s more than three hours of lectures that can be viewed in full online.

“My name is Giovanni Giorgio, but they call me Giorgio,” the legendary Moroder cheekily opens his lecture with, a reference his collaboration on Random Access Memories. “I was quite happy just doing my crosswords, and then Daft Punk came and pulled me back in,” he tells the audience. It’s a fascinating lecture where he offers some genuinely illuminating insights into his creative process as a producer over the decades.

Lecture: Giorgio Moroder (New York, 2013) from Red Bull Music Academy on Vimeo.

Hawtin’s lecture beings with his Plastikman classic Spastik being played in full to the crowd, followed by him offering a full breakdown of how he originally created the track in a live take using analogue equipment; in less than an hour, no less. He also ventures back to the early techno days in the ‘80s, when he was regularly venturing to the record shops of Detroit from his home in Canada, as well as speaking on his recent ‘Beyond EDM’ tour of the US. You can watch that one at the RBMA Vimeo page along with others from luminaries like Francois K.
