Final ever Funktrust on Easter Thursday

Angus Paterson · 16 March 2007 · inthemix

One of Sydney’s seminal and most enduring Thursday night events is wrapping up, with the Funktrust boys inviting everyone to come and boogie on down at the Hunter Bar for the very last time on Thursday April 6. All the old-school regulars are called upon to experience the weekly block party vibe in the next couple of weeks leading up to the big closing party.

The Funktrust crew looks forward to seeing you there! Will, Aaron, Mike, Ben & Sam will all be behind the decks at Hunter Baron Thursday from 10pm – unconscious of course.

The final Funktrust night club events EVER will take place on…

Thursday 22 March
Thursday 29 March
5 APRIL 2007 – 6TH BIRTHDAY FUNKTRUST FINALE
