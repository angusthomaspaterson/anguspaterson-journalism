Festival fave Golden Plains reveals 2010 lineup

Angus Paterson · 5 November 2009 · inthemix

One of the Australia’s most dearly loved events for those who like to look beyond the major festivals, Golden Plains has revealed its lineup for 2010. Taking place at the Meredith Amphitheatre in Victoria during March, the festival has proved a big winner in the annual Festival Awards, dominating the ‘Australia’s Favourite Festival’ category for two years running.

While the lineup isn’t hugely tailored for ITMers, with this year’s international guests mostly consisting of indie heavyweights like Pavement and Dinosaur Jr, there’s a sizable contingent of indie-dance crossover acts on the bill like the UK’s The Big Pink, and Aussie names like Midnight Juggernauts and Tame Impala, who show how much the lines have blurred between rock and dance events in recent years

Golden Plains returns to the Meredith Supernatural Amphitheatre in Victoria, from March 6-8 2010. Check out the lineup below…
