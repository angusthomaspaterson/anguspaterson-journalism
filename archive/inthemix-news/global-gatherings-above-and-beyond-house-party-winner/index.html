Global Gathering's Above & Beyond house party winner

Angus Paterson · 22 October 2008 · inthemix

It was one of the most impressive and highly sought after prizes we’ve ever been able to offer here at inthemix – the opportunity for one lucky punter to have UK trance heavyweights Above & Beyond come and play an exclusive set at their houseparty, with an intimate crowd of 30 friends. We can now announce that Marek from Sydney is the lucky winner of the competition, and you can have a listen to his response below.!

So how would you expect such an evening to unfold? ITM recently spoke with Above & Beyond about their upcoming tour as part of Global Gathering (have a listen to the full interview HERE), where Tony McGuinness revealed they view it as an opportunity to step back to the days before they reached their current heights as trance superstars.

“The thing about afterparties is you need to sit down and talk to your new best friend sitting next to you. While I suspect initially there’ll be a lot of people taking pictures of us, hopefully after 10 minutes they’ll realise that we’re just normal people. That’ll make it a bit more for us as well!”
