Krafty Kuts cleans up at the Breakspoll Awards

Angus Paterson · 28 February 2007 · inthemix

The big man of breakbeat has a massive night at the annual awards for the breaks community, taking home gongs for Best Album, Best DJ and Outstanding Contribution. Krafty Kuts finally got his Freakshow album out to the public in 2006 and the verdict was that it was pretty damn good. Also taking home the prestigious Outstanding Contribution elevates him to the level of the likes of Rennie Pilgrim for the impact that he’s had on the scene over the years.

Though the breakbeat scene floundered a little in 2006, the Breakspoll Awards defiantly insisted there was still plenty of excellent work done by DJs and producers. The Stanton Warriors in particular bucked the trend by enjoying what was probably their biggest year yet, with a smokin’ hot range of productions and remixes along with an artist album and several mix CDs. While they missed out on any production gongs, they took home the award for Best Compilation for their excellent Fabric Live CD.

2006 was also a breakthrough year for Rogue Element, which was reflected in him taking home the award for Best Producer as a shared remix nod with Tom Real for their reinterpretation of Noisa’s Gutterpump.

Here is the full list of results…

Best Album: Krafty Kuts – Freakshow
Outstanding Contribution: Krafty Kuts
Best DJ: Krafty Kuts
Best Magazine: Mofo
Best Remix: Noisa – Gutterpump (Rogue Element vs Tom Real Remix)
Best Club/Event: Spectrum
Best Compilation: Stanton Warriors – Fabric Live
Best Producer: Rogue Element
Breakthru DJ: Hexadecimal
Breakthru label: Sound Of Spectrum
Best Radio Show: Annie Nighingale
Best Retailer: Know How
Best Website: Nuskoolbreaks.co.uk
Best Label: Mantra Breaks
Breakthru Producer: Groove Diggerz
Best Radio Station: Breaks FM
Best Single: Far Too Loud – Get High/Midnight
