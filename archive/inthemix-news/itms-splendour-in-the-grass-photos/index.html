ITM's Splendour in the Grass photos

Angus Paterson · 27 July 2009 · inthemix

One of the country’s most beloved festivals returned to the pristine green fields of Belongil Fields in Byron Bay over the weekend, and ITM was there to capture all the action, which included performances from Bloc Party, MGMT, The Flaming Lips, Friendly Fires and many more. Check out the photos now on ITM…

> ITM’s Splendour in the Grass photos

So how did the weekend pan out? According to ITM’s sibling site Fasterlouder, it was mighty fine indeed. “After a spell of rain on Friday night, the sun was out as the Splendour festivities got going on Saturday morning,” the site reported over the weekend. Returning heroes Bloc Party represented the highlight of day one’s festivities, capping things off with a greatest hits set in the Supertop. “The band was in tight form throughout the set, dishing out their urgent indie-rock to an overflowing crowd… and the mood was certainly ecstatic.”

MGMT also delivered the goods on day two with the band’s only Australian performance. “The elfin superstars surprised some at their December shows last year by being more jam band than electro anthem-makers. However, at Splendour they were in unabashed crowd-pleasing mode. Two new songs made an appearance in the set-list, but they were overwhelmed by the likes of Weekend Wars, Electric Feel, Time To Pretend and galvanising closer Kids.”
