We Love Sounds tears across the country - photos live on ITM!

Angus Paterson · 10 June 2008 · inthemix

After hitting the ground in Perth and Canberra last week, the We Love Sounds circus made its way across the rest of the country over the weekend with a massively diverse lineup of DJs and live acts in tow, with massive events in Sydney, Melbourne and Adelaide. The most interesting thing about this year’s lineup was that beyond the world-class selection of DJ talent that included Steve Lawler, Dubfire, Derrick Carter and Mr Oizo, there was also an impressive selection of electronic live acts like Modeselektor, Lindstrom and the M_Nus Experience, and for the first time for the festival a massive showing of indie/electro acts – bands like !!!, Grand National, The Whip and The Bravery brought a distinctly different feel to the proceedings.

The lineup varied across all the states, but the flagship Sydney event (where the festival debuted five years ago) was bigger than ever before, stretching out to take up the entire Fox Studios Entertainment Complex. While reports flooding in from the Melbourne event suggest that some issues were experienced with the sound bleeding between the stages, however an entertaining day was still had by all and this was reflected in both the Adelaide and Brisbane events, which featured a lineup that was more squarely focused on the DJ and electronic acts on the bill.
