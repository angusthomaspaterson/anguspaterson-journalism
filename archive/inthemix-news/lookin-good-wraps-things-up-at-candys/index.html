Lookin' Good! wraps things up at Candys

Angus Paterson · 29 July 2008 · inthemix

After nearly five years of residency and over 50 events, classic house night Lookin’ Good! will be throwing its last party at Candys Apartment this Saturday night. While they’ll be taking a two month break from festivities, they’ll be returning to their regular monthly events on Saturday 22 November at a much larger venue.

The organisers promise details on the new venue will be released soon, but to help them go out in style they’ll be enlisting the services of Fowl Play for a main room live performance. Also joining them on the night will be Sydney legend Scott Pullen, as well as residents Mark Dynamix, Adam Coverdale and Tom da Silva. And for those who like it a bit deeper, the ‘Bulletproof Room’ will also be down some tech and electro from Mozzi, Sam Serene and Ben Kelly one more time.

Lookin’ Good! wraps things up at Candys Apartment this Saturday 2nd August, 8pm-6am.
