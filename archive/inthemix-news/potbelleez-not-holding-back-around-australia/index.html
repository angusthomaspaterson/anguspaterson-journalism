Potbelleez not holding back around Australia

Angus Paterson · 17 October 2007 · inthemix

To celebrate the release of their new single Don’t Hold Back, mashup DJ kings The Potbelleez will be taking their raucous party vibes across Australia with a series of shows. With the duo playing with their signature six-deck setup, they’ll be showing the rest of the country what Sydney has known for a while. These boys bring the noise!

Don’t Hold Back was co-written and produced by none other than Sam La More from Tonite Only fame, and it’s already making waves. Holding the #1 spot on the ARIA Club Charts for two weeks, it was performed live to an audience of 1.9 Million viewers during the Big Brother finale! How’s that for massive exposure?
