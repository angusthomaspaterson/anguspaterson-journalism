Locodisco launches this weekend

Angus Paterson · 4 June 2009 · inthemix

While Kink may be no more, a new chapter in Nevermind’s reign as one of Sydney’s finest clubbing spaces has begun. The club’s new Saturday night residency Locodisco launches this Saturday night, headed up by an appearance by Swiss dance hero Headman alongside Neoteric from Dubsided Records.The city will be going off after the We Love Sounds festival, and the team from Nevermind are intent on delivering an excellent kick-on option.

With the emphasis placed firmly on the “party”, Locodisco will feature a mainroom of eclectic house flavours that covers fidget, electro, techno and more, served up week in week out from the likes of Ajax, Ro Sham Bo, Jaime Doom, Gus Da Hoodrat, Mark Murphy, Ben Morris and more. The back room on the other hand will offer some slightly more subdued party flavours, with the music stretching across disco, indie-dance, classic party and hip hop tunes selected by Tim CC, Vivi Not Disco, Cassette, Kali, Ash Le Rouge and many others.

Locodisco launches this Saturday 6th June, kicking off at 10pm with free entry before 11pm and $20 thereafter, with the party continuing well into Sunday morning.

Locodisco hits Nevermind every Saturday, check out the headliners in store for June and July…
