John Dahlback's Onelove mix excursion

Angus Paterson · 3 November 2009 · inthemix

Sweden’s John Dahlback was a heavily-hyped name on this year’s Stereosonic lineup, with an extensive history already as a techno producer before he reinvented himself last year as a house producer in the vein of the Swedish House Mafia, and his skills have now been recruited by the Onelove stable for their next mix compilation. Mobile Disco 2010 will hit the shops on November 27th, just in time for the start of the Stereosonic festival and he’ll be joined on mixing duties by Sony inthemix50 #2 Andy Murphy, as well as the Bang Gang posse.

Traditionally, the release of Onelove’s mixed compilations have been accompanied by national club tours by the DJs responsible for putting the CDs together. All three acts will be doing the rounds with the Stereosonic tour in late November and early December, but it waits to be seen if we’ll have Dahlback playing some intimate club shows along the way. Fingers crossed!

Onelove: Mobile Disco 2010 is out November 27th, mixed by John Dahlback, Andy Murphy and Bang Gang. Catch all three acts on the Stereosonic tour, keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page at inthemix.com.au/stereosonic and check out the national tour dates below…

Sat 28th November – Moore Park, Hordern, RHI & Surrounds, Sydney
Sun 29th November – Claremont Showground, Perth
Sat 5th December – Royal Melbourne Showgrounds, Melbourne
Sat 5th December – Bonython Park, Adelaide
Sun 6th December – Eagle Farm Racecourse, Brisbane
