ITM's national Trance Energy gallery wrapup

Angus Paterson · 13 April 2009 · inthemix

The Australian debut of Trance Energy ripped across the country over the weekend, with four huge dates across Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth. Australian punters were treated to the the authentic experience of the world’s largest trance event, and ITM has now sent live the photo galleries from across all four states…

>Trance Energy photos Perth.

The general consensus across the country was that ID&T did a stellar job of importing the Trance Nation experience to Australia for the first time, with Sydney punters enjoying the party that most closely replicated the spectacular Utrecht event. The tour had a trance lineup to die for, but the amount of DJs on the lineup was almost too much of a good thing: the one consistent grumble across all the different cities was the inherant limitations of the one-hour sets. Overall though, there was enough reaching for the lasers to keep an entire nation of trance lovers happy, with BT and Sander van Doorn proving to be the star performers over the four days.

Keep your eyes peeled to ITM this week for more Trance Energy coverage, and if you’re in a retrospective mood then check out our Festival Page for plenty of news, interviews, DJ mixes, clips and more.
