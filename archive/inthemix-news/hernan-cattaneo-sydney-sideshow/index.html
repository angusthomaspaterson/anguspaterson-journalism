Hernan Cattaneo Sydney sideshow?

Angus Paterson · 30 April 2007 · inthemix

Word is spreading that the South American progressive house maestro Hernan Cattaneo will be playing another gig on Sunday 10th June following his performance at the massive We Love Sounds festival – venue details to be confirmed!

The last couple of years has seen Hernan’s star rising exponentially: best know for his unique brand of melodic progressive and his long term association with the Renaissance brand, he has spent the past three years in the top ten of the DJ Mag Poll. He’s now well and truly ranked up there with the likes of Sasha and Digweed and in June he’ll be touring on the back of the release of his Renaissance presents Sequential 2 compilation.

Stay tuned to ITM for more details as they come to hand…
