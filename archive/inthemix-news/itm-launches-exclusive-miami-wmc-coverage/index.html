ITM launches exclusive Miami WMC coverage!

Angus Paterson · 20 March 2007 · inthemix

Always dreamt of attending the mega Miami Winter Music Conference event? It ranks up there with the Ibiza party season as one of the coveted destinations for dance music lovers, but it remains out of reach for most of us. But never fear because ITM’s national editor Tim Hardaker is there to bring us all the news.

We‘ve already had a taste with his interview with Dutch trance god Armin Van Buuren where it was exclusively revealed that he’ll be touring Australia over the July long weekend. Tim will also be publishing a special Miami Blog during his time at the conference which already includes details on his trip to Miami in a stretch Hummer with the boys from Random Soul.

As well as the daily panels, showcases and parties, the WMC also hosts the International Dance Music Awards (IDMAs) and the Ultra Music Festival, so it’s sure to be a massive week. Tim will be endeavoring to bring it all back to us in Australia with streaming audio interviews, live DJ sets, news updates, photo galleries and more.

Be sure to check the inthemix WMC Full Coverage Page on the site for the latest news on what’s going on in Miami!
