26 overdoses reported in Melbourne

Angus Paterson · 11 April 2009 · inthemix

The excitement surrounding Trance Energy’s debut in Melbourne on Friday was marred by a reported 26 people taken to hospital suffering from suspected drug overdoses, with four of the patients deemed to be critically ill upon transportation. All patients were discharged from several hospitals across Melbourne by early Saturday morning.

Paul Holman, operations manager for Ambulance Victoria, told the Herald Sun last night that the drug known as GHB is suspected to be the cause of the illness. He also claimed he was at a loss as to what could be done to better address the issue beyond strip-searching punters as they enter venues.

“Government, venue operators and organisers can do what they can do but it’s about the individuals and young people taking responsibility for their actions and behaviour,’’ he said. “If they choose to ignore the facts and the warnings the consequences are they could die or cause themselves serious harm. I don’t know what more we can do.”
