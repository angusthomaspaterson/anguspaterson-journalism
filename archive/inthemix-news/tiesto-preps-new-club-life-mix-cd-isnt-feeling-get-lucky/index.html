Tiesto preps new 'Club Life' mix-CD; isn't feeling 'Get Lucky'

Angus Paterson · 15 May 2013 · inthemix

“I heard Get Lucky. It’s just not my taste.” Dutch DJ superstar and ‘trouse’ specialist Tiesto has dared to express something less than undying love for the heavily-hyped return of Daft Punk to the spotlight, while chatting to Billboard this week. “It’s great what Daft Punk does and the sound quality is great, but that whole disco vibe is not really my thing.”

Even though Daft Punk’s return to early days of disco isn’t to his taste, luckily Tiesto has his own new music to spruik. He’s currently readying the third instalment of his Club Life series of compilations for release next month, this time themed around the Swedish capital of Stockholm, after previous volumes set in Miami and Las Vegas.

Tiesto has revealed some info on what inspired him to choose Stockholm as the location for his latest mix. “I moved to Stockholm in February 2009 and now spend several months there every year. The Swedes are enormously talented in fashion, music and art, which is extremely inspiring for me when I work on new tracks. There are also lots of great clubs within walking distance from the city center that makes Stockholm a very friendly place to party.”

He also spoke to Billboard about his new single Take Me that will be included on the mix. “It’s a combination of the old and the new Tiësto,” he revealed. “It’s housey but has this trance-y vocal in it, and the melody is great…It’s definitely the perfect definition of what I stand for at the moment – from the past until now.”

Otherwise, it’s business as usual for the bigger-than-huge Tiesto, who’s popularity has inflated even further following mainstream America’s recent embrace of EDM. He’s now based in New York City, and has also swapped out his long-running Ibiza residency with a brand new one at the Hakkasan nightclub in Las Vegas.

Tiesto also told Billboard about the joys of working with his new management company; the same crew responsible for artists like Dave Mathews Band, naturally. He confirmed the new arrangement is “going great. If I can quote one of the artists who’s also there, ‘They make other management companies look like amateurs.’”
