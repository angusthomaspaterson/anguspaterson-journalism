Armin van Buuren earlybird tickets exclusive on ITM

Angus Paterson · 4 April 2008 · inthemix

Armin van Buuren’s ‘White Party’ tour was a runaway success last year, and this June Long Weekend we’ll be getting a chance to relive it again when he returns to Brisbane for another extended set at a brand new venue the Commerce Building. Proudly presented by inthemix, we’ll be selling an exclusive round of 300 earlybird tickets for only $59+BF. And they’ll be going on sale Tuesday 15th April.

The festival silly festival may be coming to an end, but it’s already time to start getting excited about the wintertime events. Holland’s Armin van Buuren rose to seize the #1 spot in the DJ Mag Top 100 poll last year, and this year he’ll be cementing his strong relationship with his Australian audience with another national tour. The promoters have promised “spectacular visuals and sophisticated sound production,” and when it comes to extended sets, Armin hasn’t let us down yet so expect him to go well beyond the call of duty…

Armin van Buuren returns to Brisbane on Saturday 14th June. Earlybird tickets are on sale exclusively on ITM Tuesday 15th April for only $59+BF. Check out our Artist in-depth page for everything Armin related, and stay tuned to ITM!
