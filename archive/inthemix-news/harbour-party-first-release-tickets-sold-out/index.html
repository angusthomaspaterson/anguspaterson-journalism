Harbour Party first release tickets sold out

Angus Paterson · 16 October 2008 · inthemix

It’s become one of Sydney’s most legendary NYE parties, with an unrivaled position right on the edge of the Harbour giving party people the best position to experience the annual fireworks spectacle. Yes, it’s Luna Park’s Harbour Party NYE, and if you’re keen you better get in quick as first release tickets have already sold out.

Heading up the lineup this year will be 2007’s electro-pop breakthrough sensation Calvin Harris, joined by Boys Noize and Steve Aoki as well as a local lineup of The Potbelleez playing live and Ajax, Aston Shuffle and Burns. Final release tickets are onsale now at ITM for $159+BF, and there’s also a range of VIP options available, and they start at $209+BF.

Stay tuned to ITM in the leadup to NYE.
