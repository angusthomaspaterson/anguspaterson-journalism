Stereosonic hits Melbourne this December

Angus Paterson · 20 September 2007 · inthemix

Onelove and Hardware are teaming up to bring a massive new festival to Melbourne this December called Stereosonic. And you heard it here first on inthemix! It’ll all be happening at the Royal Melbourne Showgrounds on Saturday 1st December, taking advantage of the venue’s recent $140m refurbishment. The Showgrounds will be transformed into a multi-stage festival utilising the grandstand and new green grounds, and the organiser’s say to expect an event like you’ve never before seen at the venue.

It’ll be bringing a whole range of international and local acts that showcase a diverse number of styles including electro, house, indie dance, electronica, detroit funk, tech phunk and dance-pop. And as you can tell if you’ve had a look at the lineup below, there’s a massive cast of international talent that have been recruited for the party!

1st release tickets are available from ITM this Monday 24th September, and the full lineup details will be released on Friday 28th September. Meanwhile, check out the massive cast that have been confirmed so far!
