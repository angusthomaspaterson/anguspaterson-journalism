Second round of acts announced for Great Escape festival

Angus Paterson · 13 December 2006 · inthemix

A whole new slew of acts joins an already impressive bill for the three-day festival set to take place at the Newington Armory over the Easter long weekend.

The acts joining the lineup include John Butler Trio, Little Birdy, The Cops, The Temper Trap, Bliss N Eso, Darren Hanlon, Holly Throsby, Angus & Julia Stone, Deerhoff, Stick Figures, Flogging Molly, Gogol Bodello, Ooh La La and Operator Please.

Following on from the success of last year’s inaugural event, the Great Escape will again be held right at Newington Armory, right on the riverfront and adjacent to Sydney Olympic Park. As before, festival goers are promised a diverse experience over the three days that will take in music, art and on-site camping. Tickets go on-sale this Thursday December 14 at 9am and costs $165 + BF for a three-day pass, $75 + BF for a single-day pass and $60 + BF for a camping pass.

Acts already announced for the Great Escape include Wolfmother, Gomez, Missy Higgins, The Living End, Ben Kweller, Fat Freddy’s Drop, Ziggy Marley, The Hilltop Hoods, The Vines, Bob Evans, The Fumes, Fishbone, Blue King Brown and Lee Scratch Perry.

For festival info, head to thegreatescape.net.au or call 1300 369 227.
