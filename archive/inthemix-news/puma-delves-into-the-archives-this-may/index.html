Puma delves into the archives this May

Angus Paterson · 17 April 2009 · inthemix

To accompany the release of the new Puma Archive range of footwear to the sneaker-buying public, Puma will be hosting a series of retro-themed parties during May that will be bringing the theme alive – paying homage to the 70s, 80s and 90s through the colour, pattern and design. As Puma themselves put it, it’s a “modern execution inspired by heritage”.

The Puma Archive Parties will take place across Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane and will celebrate the retro-electro music of the afore mentioned decades, with an impressive range of DJs on the turntables including SGT Slick, Those Usual Suspects, Goodwill, Carl Kennedy, Hook N Sling, Kate Monroe, Tydi, Baby G and more. Tickets to these special one-off events are absolutely free for anyone who purchases any Puma Archive gear from a Footlocker store.
