Wayside Chapel makes charity chic

Angus Paterson · 25 October 2007 · inthemix

Kings Cross-based charity Wayside Chapel will be taking fashion, music and art to a whole new level on Thursday November 1st, holding an event at the Gaelic Theatre in Surry Hills to raise much needed funds for the homeless, victims of abuse and substance addiction and people with mental health issues.

Local designers & stylists Marnie Skillings and Fleur Wood along with YEN fashion editor Imogene Barron will be styling and customizing clothing from the charity organisation’s own Beatniks & Bohemians Op Shop, which began in 1996 and has become a massive to the charity’s overall revenue.

The event aims to capture the attention of music and art lovers, so you can expect funky tunes from the likes of Ripping Dylans and Tokyo Blonde as well as a bunch of one-off canvasses on sale on the evening for $150.

Tickets for the event are only $22 and all the funds raised will go towards helping the Wayside Chapel to continue its good work. So get on down to the Gaelic Theatre on Thursday November 1 from 7pm, check HERE for info on pre-booked tickets.
