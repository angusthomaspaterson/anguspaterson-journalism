Sharam hitting the decks at Home

Angus Paterson · 6 February 2007 · inthemix

Sharam, One half of progressive house duo Deep Dish and responsible for latest Global Underground release, will be playing at Sydney’s biggest club on Saturday March 3rd for the Ministry of Sound’s #04 party. He’ll also be joined by John Course and Hook n Sling in the main room, Sweetchilli DJs on the terrace and Alan Thompson with the Potbelleez in the icon room.

Sharam is best known for his production work and DJing as part of Deep Dish, who have been mainstays in the progressive house scene for over a decade but had a breakthrough hit with Flashdance in 2004. But lately Sharam has been striking out on his own: as well as completing the latest Global Underground release and playing solo DJ gigs, he’s also been earning big props for his single PATT (Party All The Time).

Presale tickets are $35 and will be available from ITM, below are the lineup details for the evening:
