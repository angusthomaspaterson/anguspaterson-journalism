Wanna work for Sublime?

Angus Paterson · 5 September 2007 · inthemix

If you think you have what it takes to the head of promotions at Australia’s longest running club night, here’s your chance! Sublime are on the hunt for a fresh new face to take control of their promotions crew.

The organisers behind Home’s regular Friday night are now recruiting, and bare looking for someone with the passion, hardworking ‘can do’ attitude and inspirational skills to lead a promotions crew of like-minded music lovers. Just in time as we head into the heady days of the Australian summer.

Think you‘ve got what it takes? Email your resume to info@homesydney.com.
