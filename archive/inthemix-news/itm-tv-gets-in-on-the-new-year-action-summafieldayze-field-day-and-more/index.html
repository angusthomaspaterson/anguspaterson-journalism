ITM-TV gets in on the New Year action – Summafieldayze, Field Day & more!

Angus Paterson · 15 January 2010 · inthemix

There were more parties going down than ever before over the New Year season as we clocked over into 2010, and ITM-TV was there to cover a fair few of them. Want to relive some of the magic? We’ve got special episodes online capturing all the epic moments from Summanights on New Years Eve in Sydney, Field Day on New Years Day as well as the national favourite Summafieldayze that went down in the Gold Coast last weekend. Check it all out now!
