Who Made Who to rip it up live at Boobytrap

Angus Paterson · 27 February 2007 · inthemix

Expect an intimate show as the Denmark trio brings their live performance to the Melt Bar next Friday night, in the leadup to their performance that weekend at the Playground Weekender festival.

Who Made Who released their self-titled album late last year, featuring a unique concoction of disco that brings a wide range of influences into the fold like folk, funk, pop and big dash of Italodisco. Space for Rent has been the album’s big single and they’re known for having a cracking live performance, getting them gigs all over Europe.

Who Made Who live, Friday 9th March at Boobytrap, $25 +BF. Tickets on sale shortly from ITM.
