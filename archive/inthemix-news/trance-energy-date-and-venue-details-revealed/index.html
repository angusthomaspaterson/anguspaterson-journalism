Trance Energy date & venue details revealed!

Angus Paterson · 2 December 2008 · inthemix

Check out our Trance Energy featured gallery from the Dutch event!

If the news this year of Sensation’s debut in Melbourne wasn’t exciting enough, trance fans around the country collectively wet themselves when it was exclusively revealed on ITM last month that promoter ID&T’s renowned Trance Energy event would be venturing outside of Holland for the very first time in Easter 2009, with dates across Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth. ITM can now reveal date and venue details for the Sydney leg of the Trance Energy tour!

Trance Energy will be touching down in Sydney for the very first time on Thursday 9th April, and it’ll be taking place at the Hordern Pavilion and surrounds with an all-night party. And it’s the event that trance fans from around the globe dream about at night – featuring performances from some of the genre’s biggest names, the lineup represents just one part of the overall package as promoters ID&T are famous for employing some of the most cutting-edge production, lasers and special effects seen anywhere in the world. We’ve got little doubt Trance Energy will be one of the most spectacular events on the dance music calendar in 2009

Check out our Trance Energy featured gallery from the Dutch event!

Trance Energy 2009 national tour dates:

Thu Apr 9th – Hordern Pavilion, Sydney
Fri Apr 10th – Melbourne Showgrounds, Melbourne
Sat Apr 11th – Riverstage, Brisbane
Sun Apr 12th – Venue TBA, Perth
