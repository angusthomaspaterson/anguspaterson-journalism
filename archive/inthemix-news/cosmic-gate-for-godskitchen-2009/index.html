Cosmic Gate for Godskitchen 2009

Angus Paterson · 6 May 2009 · inthemix

Have you started thinking yet about what your party plans will be for this October? It might just be time to start giving it some serious thought, as Future Entertainment have confirmed that trance icons Cosmic Gate will be a part of this October’s Godskitchen tour around the country. What’s equally as exciting is that it’s been officially dubbed the ‘Boombox tour’, referring to the stunning audio/visual setup that Godskitchen debuted at an event in the Ukraine earlier this year. Looks like we’ll be witnessing the Boombox in all its glory when Godskitchen returns in October!

As far as Cosmic Gate goes, there’s arguably no better time for the German duo to return to Australia. Namechecked in ITM’s Most Wanted Tours Of 2009 back in February, they’ve been one of the scene’s most consistent producers since forming in ’99, constantly reinventing themselves and keeping pace with the trance scene as it developed into the unstoppable beast it is today. However, it’s the release of their new artist album Sign of the Times that’s put them back in the spotlight in the opening months of ‘09.

Details are scant so far on the other DJs who might be making the trip over in October, but Future have indicated that NYC songstress Jess will be on the Godskitchen lineup. She’s a vocalist who’s worked with some of the top names in trance including Tiesto, and she’ll be performing live with her band on first visit to Australia.

Stay tuned to ITM for more details on the Godskitchen ‘Boombox tour’ as they come to hand!
