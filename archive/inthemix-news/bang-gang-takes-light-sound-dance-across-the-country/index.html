Bang Gang takes Light, Sound, Dance across the country

Angus Paterson · 27 June 2007 · inthemix

Australia’s most popular DJ posse Bang Gang will be taking it on the road during July and August to launch their new Light Sound Dance compilation. Featuring original tracks from Simian Mobile Disco, S

oulwax, Cut Copy, Justice, Futureheads, Klaxons, Midnight Juggernauts, The Rapture and many, many more, the boys will be promoting it in an extensive tour that stretches right across the country.
