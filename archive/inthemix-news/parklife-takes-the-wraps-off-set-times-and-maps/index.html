Parklife takes the wraps off set times & maps

Angus Paterson · 17 September 2009 · inthemix

Can you believe the start of summer is so close? The massive Parklife tour kicks off in the final weekend of September, and the kind folks at Fuzzy have provided us with the set times and maps for the entire Australian tour. This one is gonna be big, people! It’s time to start planning your day now, because there are a hell of a lot of quality acts that you’ll wanna catch… Head to the inthemix festival page for the full rundown, and be sure to also check out all the latest Parklife related news, interviews, DJ mixes, videos and more!
