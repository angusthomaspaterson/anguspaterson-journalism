Pharmacy to shatter Sydney on Easter Sunday

Angus Paterson · 4 April 2007 · inthemix

It’s time to be very, very afraid as Melbourne’s hardest, darkest and dirtiest club hits Sydney for one night of crippling hardstyle, hard acid and tech trance – the official warmup for Transmission Magic City. Classic Sydney venue the Gaelic Club will be the spot and it’ll make for the perfect space for all the typical stylings of a Pharmacy party with its gothic staging and high cathedral ceilings.

The party will be host to a range of excellent international talent including Holland hardstyle maniac DJ Isaac, rising tech and euphoric trance stars Kyau & Albert, hard trance hero Kan Cold of Hennes & Cold fame, a live performance by Dark by Design and a hairstyle mashup from the Straight on DJ Team featuring Pila & The Scientist.

The party will also see a massive three-way back2back set between Hellraiser, Steve Hill and Amber Savage. DJ support will also be given by Nervous, Steve Hill, Patrik Carrera and Slim.

Set times below, for more info check out the Transmission website or have a look at the ITM’s full coverage page for the upcoming Transmission event.

8.30pm – 10pm: Patrik v Slim
10pm – 11.00pm: Nervous
11pm – 1am: Kyau and Albert
1am – 1.30am: Dark by Design
1.30am – 3am: Derb
3am – 4am: Isaac
4am – 5am: Pilla & Scientist
5 – 6am: Amber v Steve Hill v Hell Raiser
