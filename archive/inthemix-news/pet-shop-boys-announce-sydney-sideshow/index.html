Pet Shop Boys announce Sydney sideshow

Angus Paterson · 23 March 2007 · inthemix

For those of you who can’t make it along to V Festival next weekend, the UK kings of disco camp will be playing a solo Sydney show April 6 at the Hordern Pavillion. The show will be their last performance after their festival commitments wind up.

The pet Shop Boys return to Australia on the back of the release of their ninth studio album Fundamental,
released in May last year. You’ll know them for hits like West End Girls and You’re So Flamboyant, but they’ve managed to navigate the constantly shifting world of music with their own distinctive image remaining completely intact: satiric and irreverent at all times.

Tickets on sale Monday – book through Ticketek 132 849.
