Alex Kidd bounces back for national tour

Angus Paterson · 12 February 2009 · inthemix

Hold onto your phat pants, because one of hard dance’s biggest stars will be ripping across the country before the month is out. Alex Kidd will be arriving next weekend for a jaunt that takes in gigs at the F#@K the Love 1st Birthday in Melbourne, as well as Sydney’s longest running club night Sublime and its little foray into the ‘Black’ side of the clubbing equation.

Alex Kidd will be arriving on the back of what’s proved to be an incredibly successful 12 months, securing his status even further as a hard dance icon. In 2008 he became one of the scene’s few DJ/producers to record a set for BBC’s Essential Mix, the world’s most widely listened to dance radio show. That’s not to mention gigs all over the world, as well as residencies and ties with a wide range of elite clubbing brands and events that includes Goodgreef, Global Gathering, Colourfest, Planet Love and more. Is Alex Kidd hard dance’s biggest personality? See for yourself later this month.

Stay tuned for ITM’s interview with Alex Kidd next week, and catch him at the following tour dates…

Fri Feb 20th – Sublime Black, Home Sydney
Sat Feb 21th – F#@K the Love 1st Birthday at Chasers, Melbourne
Fri Feb 27th – Syrup, Hobart
Sat Feb 28th – Time, Darwin
