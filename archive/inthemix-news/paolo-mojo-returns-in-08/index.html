Paolo Mojo returns in '08

Angus Paterson · 14 March 2008 · inthemix

Paolo Mojo had enjoyed a really strong showing in Australia over the past few years, making his Australian debut in 2006 and then returning the following year for a triumphant showing in some of our country’s bigger clubs. And if you’ve been hanging for another dose, you can rest easy, because he’s on his way back again.

Regarded as one of the house and progressive scene’s rising stars, Paolo was doing his thing for a number of years before he was brought to the collective attention of the dance music world with the release of his entry in the Balance series. Last year he followed it up with a compilation on the Renaissance label and while his sound is difficult to pin down, but takes in house in all of its different variations, with a broad ‘progressive’ thread holding it together.

Check it out for yourself when he plays the following dates this April…

24 Apr – The Likes Of You, Melbourne
26 Apr – Livewire, Sydney
4 May – Sunday Roast, Brisbane
