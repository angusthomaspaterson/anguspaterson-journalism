inthemix.TV goes to Sensation

Angus Paterson · 12 January 2009 · inthemix

The enigmatic ‘Oak of Love’ made its debut appearance in Australia at Dutch event Sensation on NYE, and 40,000+ punters were stunned by it’s epic beauty. Or something like that. Whether you were there or not, either way you should turn your attention to the latest episode of inthemix.tv as we’ve captured the spectacle in all its glory.

Check out the sexy white outfits, the pyrotechnics, the spectacular NYE countdown and of course the ‘Oak of Love’ itself. We’ve also got interviews with DJ headliner Fedde Le Grand who gives an insight on what it’s like to play at such a massive event, as well as event organiser Duncan Stutterheim who chats on some of the logistics of taking the Sensation experience around the world.
