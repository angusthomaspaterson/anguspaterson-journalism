Surreal Sounds hits Sydney Uni tonight

Angus Paterson · 20 March 2007 · inthemix

Kicking off at the campus at 8pm tonight, Surreal Sounds is set to be one of the best ‘back-to-school’ parties of the season. The lineup is locked and loaded to feature Martini Club, John Glover, Ben Morris, Tenzin, Timmy Trumpet, Elroy, Luke Calder and Le Monde, with the artists pumping out sweet beats across two massive stages in the quadrangle of St Paul’s College.

Surreal Sounds is recognised as one of the biggest and best uni bashes going on and in the past has showcased the likes of Kid Kenobi, Sneaky Sound System, Bang Gang DJs, Mark Dynamix, Rogue Traders, Jimmy Z, Illya and Groove Terminator. This year’s event is set to be bigger than ever

Tickets to Surreal Sounds can be picked up between 12 and 2pm at Manning House.

Make sure you’re at Sydney Uni on Wednesday night and check out www.surrealsounds.net for more details.
