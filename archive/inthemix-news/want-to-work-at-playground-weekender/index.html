Want to work at Playground Weekender?

Angus Paterson · 29 February 2008 · inthemix

If you’re interested in picking up some extra work out at Wisemans Ferry when the Playground Weekender Festival returns next weekend, look no further. Employment agency PloyMe specialises in recruiting for large-scale music events, and only last month they filled over 200 positions for the Good Vibrations Festival. They’ve now got a whole bunch of roles available for next weekend’s event, with positions ranging from bar staff to production crew, and a myriad of jobs in-between. Get to it quickly though as the jobs need to be filled ASAP.

If you’re interested in working out at the festival next weekend, check out www.ployme.com.au for more info.
