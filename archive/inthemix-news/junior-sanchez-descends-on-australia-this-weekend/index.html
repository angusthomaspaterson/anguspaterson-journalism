Junior Sanchez descends on Australia this weekend

Angus Paterson · 12 May 2008 · inthemix

Here’s a little something that caught us all by surprise… With little or no warning, one of NY’s finest Junior Sanchez will be in then country this weekend to play a whirlwind set of gigs around the country. He was just here last year for the Smash Your Stereo tour, but he’s back again for another series of dates for the Onelove crew.

Another example of a New Yorker doing it his way, Junior Sanchez is widely known as a DJ who’s not willing to tow the line when it comes to his craft. Famous for being kicked off the turntables at Pacha in Ibiza when he played a song by indie rockers The Faint, he was also known for pushing the electro sound long before it reached critical mass – but don’t expect him to be charting the latest ‘lectro floorfillers, as he’s a lot more unpredictable than that. Check him out this weekend.

Junior Sanchez will be back in Austrlaia in a few days, check him out at the following shows…

Friday May 16th – Limelight, Perth
Saturday May 17th – Tank Nightclub, Sydney
Sunday May 18th – Bedroom, Gold Coast
Friday May 23rd – Electric Circus, Adelaide
Saturday May 24th – The Prince Bandroom, Melbourne
