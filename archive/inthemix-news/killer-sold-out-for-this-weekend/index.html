Killer sold out for this weekend

Angus Paterson · 1 March 2007 · inthemix

Fuzzy’s electro party for this Saturday night featuring Frenchmen SebastiAn and Kavinsky has sold out.

Electro fans slow in getting their act together will no doubt be sobbing into their 80’s fashions, but they’ll have even more reason to be upset if they don’t get get a move on getting tickets to the next Killer party featuring Booka Shade on Easter Thursday – because Fuzzy warns us that tickets are mighty close to selling out.
