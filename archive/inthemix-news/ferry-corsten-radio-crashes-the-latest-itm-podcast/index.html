Ferry Corsten radio crashes the latest ITM podcast!

Angus Paterson · 19 December 2008 · inthemix

The Dutch trance master is back with his brand new album Twice in a Blue Moon and is soon to arrive on our shores for a massive series of gigs across the country this New Year period. Check out ITM’s recent feature interview for more info, but he’s also heading up ITM’s final podcast for the year with a remix of his club smash Radio Crash to get you in the mood for his upcoming tour.

That’s only scratching the surface though, as the inthemix podcast session 72 also features new stuff from Italo disco icon Serge Santiago, locals Bass Kleph, The Temper Trap and D’Opus & Roshambo, ZZZ from the Bang Gang 12 Inches label and much more to help you wrap your year up in style.
