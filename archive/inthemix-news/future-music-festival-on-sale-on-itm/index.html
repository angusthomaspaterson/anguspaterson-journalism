Future Music Festival on sale on ITM!

Angus Paterson · 8 December 2008 · inthemix

Future Music Festival is the favourite event of many a dance fan around the country, and next year’s lineup will feature what’s easily the strongest selection of live acts the national tour has ever showcased, as well as a typically strong selection of elite DJ talent from across the globe. Tickets are on sale today on ITM!

Legendary rap/funk/rock act N.E.R.D will head up the 2009 festival alongside the mighty Basement Jaxx. Also on the bill, Brazilian sensations CSS and France’s Etienne De Crecy. They’ll be joined by DJs Paul Oakenfold, Markus Schulz, Christopher Lawrence, Swedish House Mafia bosses Steve Angello and Sebastian Ingrosso and a whole bunch more.

In 2009 the Sydney leg of the tour will have a reduced capacity of only 25,000 punters. Considering the festival sold out all 35,000 tickets weeks in advance last time around, you can be assured it’ll do the same thing next time, and at an even quicker pace. Don’t delay getting your tickets or you might be left crying!

Future Music Festival returns to Randwick Racecourse on Saturday 28th February, tickets on sale now! Stay tuned to ITM for all the essential updates, and stay tuned to our Festival Page for interviews, clips, DJ mixes and more!
