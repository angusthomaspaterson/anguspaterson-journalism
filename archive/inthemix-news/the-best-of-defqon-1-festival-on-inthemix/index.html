The best of Defqon.1 Festival on inthemix

Angus Paterson · 23 September 2009 · inthemix

There are 15,000 ravers out there who are still shaking their heads in disbelief at what they saw at the Defqon.1 Festival in Sydney on Saturday. If there was ever any doubt that we’d be getting the full Dutch experience, they were dispelled for evermore when fireworks began exploding from the towering structure of the mainstage. There was spectacle aplenty, and inthemix was there to see (and photograph) it all – check out the most spectacular moments in our Snap! gallery roundup!
