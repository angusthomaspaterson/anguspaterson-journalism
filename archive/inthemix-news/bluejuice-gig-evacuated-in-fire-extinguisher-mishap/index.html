Bluejuice gig evacuated in fire extinguisher mishap

Angus Paterson · 15 June 2009 · inthemix

In an incident reminiscent of an unfortunate turn of events in a Townsville nightclub a few weeks ago (read all about it here), the Friday night gig of the ever so danceable Bluejuice at the Zoo in Brisbane was dramatically cut short due to a punter getting a little loose and wild with a fire extinguisher. It is believed that a member of the crowd started spraying the venue with a fire extinguisher about halfway through the band’s set.

Most patrons thought it was a smoke machine at first, until the cloud of powder quickly descended on them, restricting vision and breathing. The Zoo had to be evacuated immediately.

The band says that it was a very stupid and dangerous stunt for someone to pull, but assure fans that this will not deter them from coming back to Brisbane in the near future.
