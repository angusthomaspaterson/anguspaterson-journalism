Levity Nights launches later this month

Angus Paterson · 8 May 2008 · inthemix

Levity is an initiative designed to help unearth excellent indie talent, and it’s the label that was responsible for kickstarting the careers of Cut Off Your Hands and Mercy Arms. In this same spirit, Levity Nights will be launching and taking place on Thursday evening across fourteen consecutive weeks. To be held at the Hollywood Hotel it’ll be brought to you in conjunction with Boundary Sounds, who also are responsible for the legendary Purple Sneakers.

Levity Nights will feature PhDJ, Vivienne Kingswood and Nick Findlay on resident DJ duties, and it’s launch will be firing up on Thursday May 29th with Sydney band The Holidays as the special guest, while future guests will include Cut Off Your Hands, Miami Horror, 2onajoyride and many more to be announced.

Levity Nights launches at the Hollywood Hotel in Surry Hills Thursday May 29th and runs to 28th August, 8pm to 2am and $5 entry on the door.
