The full Lee ‘Scratch’ Perry experience returns to Australia

Angus Paterson · 12 March 2007 · inthemix

The reggae/dub veteran will be bringing his full band back to Australia and will be performing at a number of sideshows in Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide addition to his festival appearances at the Blues and Roots festivals and The Great Escape.

The much respected and revered Jamaican producer, performer and shaman will be dropping old-school sounds and classic cuts dug up from his infamous Black Ark period right through to the latest album. His musical career spans almost five decades, instructing and collaborating with the likes of Coxsone Dodd, Peter Tosh, Bunny Wailer and Bob Marley to The Clash, Zodiac Mindwarp and Adrian Sherwood.

Check below for dates and details…

SUNDAY 1 APRIL: Adelaide, Fowlers Live
MONDAY 2 APRIL: Melbourne, The Prince Bandroom
WEDNESDAY 4 APRIL: Sydney, Metro Theatre
