We Love Sounds 1st release tickets snapped up

Angus Paterson · 12 April 2007 · inthemix

A record response for the Sounds Group with more than a quarter of the tickets to their Sydney We Love Sounds show selling within two days of being on sale. While it took last year’s festival three weeks to reach this point, all 1st Release have now been purchased.

The festival is taking it across the country this year, with dates in Brisbane, Adelaide and Melbourne. The Sydney show boasts more than 45 international and local live acts and DJs, including DJ Shadow, Tiga, James Zabiela & Nic Fanciulli, The Infadels and Daddy G among them.
