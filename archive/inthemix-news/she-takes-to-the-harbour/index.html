SHE takes to the harbour

Angus Paterson · 3 October 2007 · inthemix

Sydney’s premier soulful house crew will be jetting off on a 3-Level luxury cruiser on Saturday November 10, bringing the SHE experience to Sydney Harbour with a guest appearance from Silky, direct from the UK.

Silky is one half of the Soulfuric Records duo Seminal Grooves, a hot production crew in the house world and responsible for the club smash Cuban Blues. Silky’s latest remix for the legendary Frankie Knuckles called Good People was a popular track at this year’s Miami WMC, and he’ll be showing the SHE regulars what he’s made of. Out on the harbour no less!

We’ll also be seeing performances from a whole bunch of regular SHE talent including Random Soul, Graham Cordery, Dave 54, Nick Vidal, Monkey Tennis DJs and much more, as well live sax, vocals and percussion. In such a unique setting, it should make for one of the best soulful house events of the year.

The party goes from 11am to 5pm on Saturday November 10, pre-sale tickets are available from www.shesydney.com.
