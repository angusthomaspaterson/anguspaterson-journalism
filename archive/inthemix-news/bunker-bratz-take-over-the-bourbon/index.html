Bunker Bratz take over the Bourbon

Angus Paterson · 29 August 2007 · inthemix

Sydney legend Robbie Lowe along with Tim Culbert will be joining forces to bring you a night of premium underground house music in the perfect intimate settings of ‘The Bunker’, located The Bourbon in Kings Cross. They’ll be joined by Carlos Zarate for yet another dose of quality underground tunes.

Catch it at the Bunker Bar, downstairs at The Bourbon on Friday 14th September from 10pm.
