Wiley's new album streamed live on ITM

Angus Paterson · 15 January 2009 · inthemix

Got grime? UK MC superstar Wiley is about to land in Australia for a national tour, and he’s also about to drop his new album See Clear Now which hits the stores this weekend. It’s earning him props for bravely entering new musical territory, for switching up the grimey beats with house and techno, with a diverse range of guests that includes Hot Chip, Kano, Mark Ronson and many more. And you can check it out now as it’s streaming live on ITM!

The Ronson produced track is the album’s first single Ca$h In My Pocket, Wiley’s own cheeky ode to the effects of the Global Financial Crisis. Coming from the same guy who released last year’s smash hit Wearing My Rolex, which also appears on the new album. Otherwise, See Clear Now is set to further cement Wiley’s status as the ‘Godfather of Grime’, alongside his membership in the Pay As U Go and Roll Deep crews.
