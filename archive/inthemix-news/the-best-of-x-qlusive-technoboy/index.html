The best of X-Qlusive Technoboy

Angus Paterson · 23 December 2009 · inthemix

The next chapter of Q-dance’s Australian adventures arrived in Sydney over the weekend, with X-Qlusive Technoboy handing the hardstyle baton to the Italian style maestro of the same name for an evening that shone the light on his musical achievements. It was fashion music and hardstyle combined for an evening that was sporting a massive setup, as per usual, along with performances by fellow hard dance heavies Stephanie, Tuneboy, Hardstyle Masterz and more.
