Rob Kay hits The Metro this Saturday

Angus Paterson · 7 June 2007 · inthemix

Rob Kay is one of Sydney’s rising DJs with residencies at a number of its biggest clubs like Tank, Moulin Rouge and YU. This Saturday you’ll be able to check him out when he hits The Metro.

Rob is also cultivating a growing profile as a producer under the production guise of Aviator, delivering remixes for the likes of Craig Obey and Hed Kandi and labels such as Ministry of Sound UK and the US imprint Soulfuric.

See for yourself when Rob The Metro this Saturday, 20 – 30 Bourke Street. Doors open 9pm.
