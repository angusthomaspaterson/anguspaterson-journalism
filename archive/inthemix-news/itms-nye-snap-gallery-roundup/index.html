ITM's NYE Snap! gallery roundup

Angus Paterson · 6 January 2009 · inthemix

Not even a last minute cancellation from Eric Prydz could slow us down. New Years Eve 08/09 went off across the country like a firework over the Sydney Harbour Bridge, and while the debut of Sensation at Telstra Dome drew the largest numbers, every last corner of our sprawling nation got a taste of the action. There was Shore Thing, Pacha and Ed Banger NYE in Sydney, the massive Origin festival in Perth and Empire in Brisbane to name but a few.

Even if you party hopped like a madman, it would have been impossible to catch it all. Well you’re in luck, because ITM was there to catch all the special moments and we’ve compiled them all in a special NYE Snap! gallery roundup. Check out the ‘muffin tops’ from the most spectacular NYE moments from across Australia!
