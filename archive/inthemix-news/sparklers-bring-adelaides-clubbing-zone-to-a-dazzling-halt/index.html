Sparklers bring Adelaide's clubbing zone to a dazzling halt

Angus Paterson · 19 May 2009 · inthemix

Yes, you read correctly. While Sydney’s nightclub district of Kings Cross had the streets closed due to a violent shooting over the weekend, things came to a halt for Adelaide clubbers for an entirely different reason: a bundle of luminous sparklers, wrapped in electrical tape. The “suspicious package”, as it was identified by The Independent Weekly, was responsible for the police’s decision to lock down the western end of Hindley Street at about 9am for nearly two hours on Sunday morning.

Adelaide’s police provided details to The Independent Weekly on the heady challenge they faced in neutralising the threat. “As a matter of course, the finding of any item that is not readily identifiable will be properly investigated and all precautions will be taken to ensure the safety of the public,” a police spokesman said.

The street was later opened again just before 11am when it was deemed to be “not a threat,” with the contents of the parcel identified as sparklers bound together with electrical tape. And all was well again in the streets of Adelaide.
