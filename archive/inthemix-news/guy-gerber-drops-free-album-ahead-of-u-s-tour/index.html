Guy Gerber drops free album ahead of U.S. tour

Angus Paterson · 16 June 2013 · inthemix

You’d think Israel’s Guy Gerber would have had his hands full turning Pacha Ibiza on its head with his edgy new high-profile residency, Wisdom Of The Glove. However, it appears the tech-houser is keeping extra busy in the studio too, in addition to planning a Stateside tour in July.

Stacked with his own fresh material, Gerber’s superb Fabric 64 mix last year almost doubled as an artist album. Now he’s back with a surprise brand new LP called Who’s Stalking Who. Not only has he taken it upon himself to give it away for free as a CD covermount with an issue of Pacha Magazine, he’s also made it freely available to everybody on his SoundCloud.

Clocking in just over an hour, and completely composed by Gerber while he was on the road over the past year, it’s an impressive ode to the producer’s prolific and polished studio output, and explores a lot of the same silky, melodic textures that fans loved so much on his Fabric 64 mix. In short, it’ll go down a treat.

Gerber will also be taking a short break from his Pacha residency in Ibiza, and embarking on a five-date ‘Independence Day’ tour of the U.S. Check out the dates after the Who’s Stalking Who embed. On the July 4 holiday in Brooklyn, New York, he’ll play on the Williamsburg Waterfront alongside Sasha and Maya Jane Coles, before continuing on to the Wavefront Festival in Chicago.

Guy Gerber U.S. tour dates:

July 1 – Island Party Toronto
July 3 – Wavefront Pre-Party Castle, Chicago
July 4 – Verboten presents NeverSayNever at StageONE, Brooklyn, NY
July 5 – Wavefront Festival, Chicago
July 6 – Story, Miami
July 7 – Piknic Electronik, Montreal
