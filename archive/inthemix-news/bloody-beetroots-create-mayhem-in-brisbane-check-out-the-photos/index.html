Bloody Beetroots create mayhem in Brisbane - check out the photos!

Angus Paterson · 26 June 2009 · inthemix

Italy’s The Bloody Beetroots have landed in Australia, and last night they played their very first gig for the tour in Brisbane at Family Nightclub. And by the looks of the photos, they brought the kind of mayhem they’re already well famous for. Check out Australia’s first photos on ITM!

Outrage feat. The Bloody Beetroots – Family Nightclub, Brisbane

Arriving on a wave of hype touting them as the next-big-thing in dance music, The Bloody Beetroots have achieved much in their relatively short career. The studio/DJ outfit of producer Bob Rifo and traveling DJ Tommy Tea are one of the most successful acts of the blog era, utilising online platforms to spread music and reach new fans. They’ll be back for Stereosonic later this year, but meanwhile we’ve got a slew of manic club gigs to go…

Want to know more about The Bloody Beetroots? Check out the recent ITM interview with the ‘Warped Warriors’, and be sure to catch them on one of their remaining gigs around the country…
