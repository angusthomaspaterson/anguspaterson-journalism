Firefox & Boogs hit the road

Angus Paterson · 16 May 2007 · inthemix

With a new mix CD due to hit the stores this week, Melbourne DJ Boogs and rising electro production duo Firefox are about to hit the road for a national tour. The Pure compilation is the follow up release from Vinyl Pusher Records.

Check em out at the following dates, with more to come…

Friday 18th May: Gold Coast, Elsewhere Bar (Boogs)
Saturday 19th May: Melbourne, Lounge (Boogs)
Sunday 20th May: (morning 7-11am) Melbourne – Revolver (Boogs)
Friday 1st June: Albury, Roi Bar (Boogs)
Friday 29th June: Melbourne, Brown Alley (Boogs)
Friday 6th July: Port Douglas – Fluid Nightclub (Firefox)
Saturday 21st July: Sydney, Chinese Laundry (Boogs & T-Rek)
Saturday 21st July: Melbourne, Metro (Firefox)
Friday 27th July: Port Douglas, Fluid Nightclub (Boogs)
Saturday 28th July: Brisbane, Family (Firefox & Boogs)
