Astrix cancels Australian tour due to illness

Angus Paterson · 27 November 2009 · inthemix

In news that will devastate psytrance fans who were rearing up to check out Israel’s Astrix this weekend at the Strawberry Fields festivals in Melbourne and Byron Bay, as well as his club show in Sydney, it’s been advised that he’ll no longer be able to make it. The local promoters were informed he has been hospitalised due to severe illness, and with him not being clearing him for flying by doctors, he’s been forced to remain in hospital and has therefore been forced to pull out of his tour.

The promoters of the Strawberry Fields Festival have expressed severe regret over the circumstances. “This move is an utter devastation to us and we are shattered that we are not able to deliver on our promises,” they said in a statement. “Whilst this issue is somewhat out of our hands, we accept full responsibility for the outcome of this event and hope that in future you trust in us to deliver the goods.”

While the Byron Bay edition of Strawberry Fields has been cancelled, the party will go on in Melbourne as well as the club show in Sydney, and Alex UP from UP Records will be stepping up to take Astrix’s place. The promoters have also promised they’re in talks with Astrix’s Hommega stable to get an extra-special surprise happening early next year.

“We look forward to working with them to deliver it to you, the fans, the party people and our friends, the tour we promised and the tour that your deserve. We won’t forget you missed Astrix this weekend and we will make it up to you next tour!”

Strawberry Fields Festival take place in the Victorian bush over the weekend of Saturday 28th November, and the Sydney club show this Friday November 27th will also go ahead too as planned.
