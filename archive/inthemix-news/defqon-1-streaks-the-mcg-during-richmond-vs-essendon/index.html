Defqon.1 streaks the MCG during Richmond vs Essendon!

Angus Paterson · 25 May 2009 · inthemix

> See more pics of the streaking moment here!

Ladies and gentlemen, we have what could be the most daring entry yet for the Defqon.1 Maximum Exposure competition… On Saturday evening a crowd of tens of thousands gathered at Melbourne’s hallowed MCG to watch Essendon score its comeback win against Richmond, but during the game an extra keen Q-dance ambassador made sure the audience had no choice but to look at something else entirely – the logo for the Deqon.1 Festival of course!

The streaker in question stormed the field wearing a Q-dance t-shirt, brandishing a white sheet trailing behind him emblazoned with the Defqon.1 logo. He also proceeded to drop his pants and bolt around the sports field, until he was crash tackled by security not long after (check out the clip below for evidence). How’s that for dedication to the cause? It’s the latest in a series of attempts to get the logo out to as many people as possible, all for the chance of winning a trip for you and your best mate to Holland for the Defqon.1 Festival next month.
