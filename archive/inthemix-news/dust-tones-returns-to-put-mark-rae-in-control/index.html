Dust Tones returns to put Mark Rae in control

Angus Paterson · 22 January 2009 · inthemix

One of Sydney’s favourite underground crews Dust Tones returns in early February with some more quality music, this time with former Grand Central Records main-man Mark Rae. Also one half the acclaimed Rae & Christian duo, since Grand Central Records wound up its run Mark has been based in Los Angeles.

Mark recently teamed up with his longtime partner Steve Christian again to form the Yes King label, and last they released the debut album from, the band of the same name called Rock This World. Hip hop, dancehall, raga, ska… Mark Rae digs (and plays) it all.

Dust Tones returns on Friday 6th February at the Beach Road Hotel in Bondi, with Mark Rae in control. And best of all it’s FREE!
