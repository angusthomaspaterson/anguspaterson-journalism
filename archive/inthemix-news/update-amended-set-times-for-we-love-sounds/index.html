UPDATE: Amended set times for We Love Sounds

Angus Paterson · 1 June 2009 · inthemix

CHECK OUT THE AMENDED SET TIMES FOR WE LOVE SOUNDS BELOW.

Sydney’s favourite winter festival We Love Sounds returns to the Hordern Pavilion and Surrounds this Saturday 6th June for a sold out party that will be the biggest yet in the event’s history. With massive names like Armin van Buuren, Armand Van Helden, James Zabiela and Laidback Luke set to provide the entertainment, it’ll make for a massive June Long Weekend – set times are in, check them out below!

The revised set times come packaged with some important information from the organisers, related to capacity for the event. “Please be aware that the capacity of each arena at We Love Sounds is limited. As in all previous years, throughout the day to ensure your safety and dancing comfort, arenas may be closed off temporarily when they reach capacity. Please be patient or check out other arenas if you are unable to enter. If there is an artist that you particularly want to see, make sure you arrive with plenty of time before their set. “

Want more We Love Sounds news, interviews, DJ mixes and more? Check out our Festival Page, amended set times for the festival below…
