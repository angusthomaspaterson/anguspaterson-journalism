Rejected clubber opens fire at Melbourne's Viper Room

Angus Paterson · 7 November 2007 · inthemix

We’ve all shared our stories of getting knocked back from the hottest clubs for not wearing ‘funky’ enough shoes, but yesterday it took a serious turn when a dejected clubber returned to Melbourne’s Viper Room venue packing a fully-loaded firearm. The man had been turfed out onto the streets by security at around 6am after they became alarmed by his behaviour, only for him to quickly retrieve a handgun from nearby before returning to confront those who had booted him out of the club.

Security staff noticed that he had a gun tucked into his trousers upon his return to the entrance of the venue, and he then proceeded to fire up to five shots in the air according to news.com.au. The gun was then wrestled away by security in what has been described as a “frantic” struggle, wrenching it out of his hand before he managed to slip free and race to a waiting car that was parked in a nearby side street.

Luckily no one was injured in the shootings. The gun was seized examination after police arrived at the scene minutes later, and security footage captured by nearby businesses will also be looked at in the hope of locating the shooter.

The Viper Room is located in the exclusive Melbourne suburb of Prahran. It’s been the second incident at the venue involving firearms in the last six months, after a 35-year-old man was seriously wounded in April after being shot by a gunman.

Anyone who may have witnessed or have information on the latest incident can call Crime Stoppers on 1800 333 000.
