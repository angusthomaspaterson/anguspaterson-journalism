UK ravers run out of town by police

Angus Paterson · 18 December 2008 · inthemix

Norfolk top policeman chief Supt Tony Cherington announced this week that he’s eradicated free parties in his county this week by adopting a policy of hurting organizers financially. “We took away their profit by seizing equipment and shutting down events. Now they know it’s not worth their while coming to Norfolk,” he told local news site EDP.24.co.uk. “Illegal raves have been completely driven out of Norfolk, we aren’t even getting the kind of small-scale events which caused problems in the past,” he boasted.

His approach differed markedly to rave cops in Goa, who were this week accused of being involved in assaulting two Russian property developers planning to build a resort on land used for parties on Palolem beach. Police told Indian news portal SIFY.com that they were investigating the vicious beating of Russians Dan Sorokin and Sergey Korbantkov as a ‘civil dispute’ though Mr Sorokin claimed ‘drug mafia’ trance promoters and corrupt cops were to blame.

“One of those who assaulted us was a policeman in civilian clothes. It’s been almost a week since we filed the complaint. We are even willing to identify the assailants, but no police official has bothered to enquire with us even once,” he complained. “They could have taken my life, but fear will not keep me from coming to Goa, which I love,” Sorokin added.

Goa tourist chief Ralf D’Souza, meanwhile, said numbers of visitors has so far dropped 20 per cent following the Mumbai attacks while State tourism minister Francisco Pacheco said armed forces are on high alert following a Al Qaeda terror threat. “We don’t mind tourists not coming for this season if they are wary of security but we don’t want a terror attack to happen,” he told press agency AP, “That will entirely finish Goa’s image,” he added.
