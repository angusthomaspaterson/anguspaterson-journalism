Paul Strange keeps the house vibe kickin’ at The Arthouse

Angus Paterson · 1 April 2008 · inthemix

As you probably already know, longstanding Sydney promoter Paul Strange has been keeping a pretty strong house music vibe going at The Arthouse on Saturdays as of late with nights like Hed Kandi, Defected, Ministry Of Sound, OM, Renaissance and more. After these massive summer events, Strange has announced he’ll now be moving forward with the weekly Together At The Arthouse launching on 19th April. The aim here is to bring consistency, but to still stay true to the same house vibe mixed with an atmosphere that’s still ‘up-for-it’ and stylish.

Together at The Arthouse will feature several weekly residents over two rooms. In the main room ‘Upfront’, Jack McCord from Hed Kandi will be joining local young gun Tony Venuto for some quality big room house, while in the side gallery bar they’ll be pushing what’s been termed as “feelgood, cheeky grooves” with DJ Matt Cahill over from the UK as a resident. And yep ,you can expect the big brands such as Hed Kandi, Defected and defected to be making a regular appearance.

Together At The Arthouse launches on Saturday 19th April.
