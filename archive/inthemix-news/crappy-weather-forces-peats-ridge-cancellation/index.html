Crappy weather forces Peats Ridge cancellation

Angus Paterson · 20 December 2007 · inthemix

Been noticing the gloomy skies out there at the moment? For those planning of heading out to the Peats Ridge Festival this New Year period it’s got a whole lot gloomier, with the news that the event will no longer go ahead.

“Mother Nature has made her call and as a sustainable Festival, we have had to listen,” say the festival organisers. “It is with the greatest regret that we have to announce the cancellation of this year’s Peats Ridge Festival.”

It’s been the highest December rainfall on record, coupled with six months of consistently wet weather, which have been blamed for the cancellation, resulting in ground conditions that have made it impossible for the Festival to proceed. “Although the team has done everything in its power to deliver the Festival, at the end of the day the wellbeing of our patrons must come first. In the interests of public safety the organisers and venue owner have had to make this extremely difficult decision.”

Peats Ridge organisers have assured their fans that although this year’s Festival isn’t going ahead, they’ll be back “brighter and better” in 2008. All tickets bought via credit card will automatically have the value of their ticket refunded, and for all other refunds or ticketing queries you should contact your point of purchase directly.

Bummer hey! But you can’t ignore Mother Nature. If you’ve got any queries about the festival’s cancellation, check out www.peatsridgefestival.com.au or give them a buzz on (02) 9281 3316.
