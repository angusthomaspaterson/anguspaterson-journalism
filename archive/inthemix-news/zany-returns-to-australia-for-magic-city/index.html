Zany returns to Australia for Magic City

Angus Paterson · 12 February 2010 · inthemix

The spirit of Transmission will return when Magic City unleashes the dragon in May, and ITM has just got word on one of the headliners who will be taking to the stage for the event. Hardstyle kingpin Zany will be returning to Australia following his triumphant closing performance at the Defqon.1 Festival last year, and he’ll be packing a record box of new hard dance weapons, along with the tantalizing promise from the organisers that there will back-to-back sets at the party never before be seen anywhere in the world!

Zany says to expect something bigger than we’ve ever seen before when he returns in May. “We will bring our Australian fans the sound of Fusion,” he told ITM. “I really hope that they never will forget this evening. We will do our very best to make it a very successful night!”

However, Zany has remained tightlipped on the special surprises in store for the punters at Magic City. “Expect the best of the best… The rest is a surprise!”

Magic City: Unleash the Dragon, hits the Sydney Olympic Sports Centre on Saturday May 29th. Check www.magiccity.net.au for info, and stay tuned to ITM for more details!
