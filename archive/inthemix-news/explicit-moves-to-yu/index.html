Explicit moves to Yu

Angus Paterson · 29 August 2007 · inthemix

Following the recent news that the Trashbags crew will be relocating to Kings Cross club Yu, the next clubbing posse to make the jump over will be Explicit who this time are moving from their old home at Oxford Street’s Havana.

Recently celebrating its massive 1st Birthday, Explicit is well known among the Sydney clubbing community for its party atmosphere and monthly servings of underground ‘dirty, sleazy, twisted electro-tech’, as it’s termed by the organisers. After 13 massive months at Havana they’re looking forward to a bright new future at Yu and residents Ben Morris, Reno, Matttt & Tomass will be coming along for the ride.

Along with regular appearances from past Explicit favourites like Mark Dynamix, Hook n Sling, Mark Muphy, James Taylor, Telefunken and Robbie Lowe, there will special guest stars and other performances from fresh young talent from all around Australia. Expect the party vibes to keep on rocking!

The launch party kicks off on Friday October 12th at Yu!
