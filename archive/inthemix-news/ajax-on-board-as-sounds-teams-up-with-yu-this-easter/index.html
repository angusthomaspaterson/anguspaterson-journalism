Ajax on board as Sounds teams up with Yu this Easter

Angus Paterson · 12 March 2008 · inthemix

ITM50 #1 Ajax will be on board to drive the madness levels through the roof when the Sounds let loose for a massive bash across both Yu and Soho this Easter Sunday. They’ll be delivering a showcase of the best local talent as well as a touch of the old school, and in addition to Australia’s favourite they’ll be a whole bunch of other quality music selectors on board.

In long weekend style it’ll be kicking off early… so you can just keep on kicking on, of course. Doors open across both floors at 9pm with the likes of Funktrust DJs, Elroy, Spruce Lee, Shamus Ben Morris, Ajax, DJ Belgium (of Riot in Belgium fame) and a whole bunch more. It’ll keep the party going in the Cross well into the messy wee hours of Sunday morning…

Tickets are $20+BF and available now through ITM. Check out the fill lineup below…
