ITM gets merry with free inthemiXmas bash!

Angus Paterson · 28 November 2008 · inthemix

We’re on a mad dash towards the end of the year, with everyone starting to scratch their heads and ask, “Where the hell did the year go?” That’s right, all the retailers are breaking out their trees and tinsel we’re all making plans with our family as to where we’ll be on December 25th. That’s right, Christmas is nearly here! And to celebrate the year that was, ITM will be throwing a free bash to help you reflect on all what a massive year in dance music we’ve enjoyed, to catch up with all your mates in the ITM community and of course, boogie on down to some quality tunes with an impressive DJ lineup.

The Sydney leg of inthemiXmas will be taking place at our city’s hottest new club Nevermind on Friday 5th December, and it’ll be featuring one smokin’ hot secret guest who’ll be jumping behind the decks. All we can say is that he’s played a seminal part in the development of Australian electronic music over the past decade… go on, try and figure that one out! He’ll be joined by one of our hottest exports DJ Mo’ Funk along with Mark Dynamix in full ‘MDX’ mode, and Nevermind resident Ben Morris.

That’s not to forget the DJ set from an up-and-comer in the ITM community, which will be announced shortly. Get some Xmas spirit up ya!

inthemiXmas hits Nevermind on Friday 5th December, click here to RSVP! Free for the first 200 people to arrive before midnight, $15 otherwise.
