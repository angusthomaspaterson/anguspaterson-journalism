Famous set times unleashed

Angus Paterson · 22 March 2007 · inthemix

The Future Entertainment superclub experience launches in Sydney this Saturday night. It had a massive success last weekend with a 20,000 strong at the Future Music Festival, so the punters are eagerly waiting to see what’s to come with their partnership with Home Nightclub, Sydney’s biggest dance music venue.

Doors open from 10pm, here’s who you’ll be listening to on the evening…
