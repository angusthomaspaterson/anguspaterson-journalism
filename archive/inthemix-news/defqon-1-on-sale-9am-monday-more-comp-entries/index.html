Defqon.1 on sale 9am Monday + more comp entries

Angus Paterson · 29 May 2009 · inthemix

The Defqon.1 Festival has been the talk of the dance scene since the announcement it would be held at the spectacular Sydney International Regatta Centre, and the party’s iconic logo has also been plastered everywhere thanks to ITM’s Defqon.1 Maximum Exposure competition. On Monday morning at 9am you’ll have your chance to grab your tickets through q-dance.com.au, and there will be a few special rewards offered for the early birds who get in quickly.

If you’re one of the first 50 ticket buyers then you’ll receive a free VIP upgrade, while the first 500 tickets purchased will also include a complementary ticket to the Australian Hard Dance DJ Championships, a national tour that’s penciled in for August in the leadup to the Defqon.1 Festival. The tour will feature special guest Blademasterz AKA Brennan Heart, stay tuned for more details!

In other Defqon.1 news, entries to ITM’s Maximum Exposure competition closes on Monday and the brave attempts to win the trip to Holland have been flying in thick and fast, with hardstyle fans spurred on by the notoriety of the ‘Defqon.1 streaker’ who stormed the field of the MCG during the Richmond vs Essendon match last Saturday.
