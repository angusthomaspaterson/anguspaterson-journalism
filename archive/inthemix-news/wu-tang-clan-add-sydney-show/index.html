Wu-Tang Clan add Sydney show

Angus Paterson · 19 October 2009 · inthemix

It was reported last week that icon US rap group Wu-Tang Clan would still be making it to Astralia in spite of the cancellation of the bizarre looking Megarampage festival (where they shared the bill with the likes of Good Charlotte and The Offspring), and they’ve just added a show at Sydney’s Enmore Theatre as their first stop on their Australian tour.

The legendary hip-hop troupe’s concert will be held on the 29th of October, and confirmed for the trip are GZA, Raekwon, RZA, Ghostface, U God and Inspectah Deck, with more members to be confirmed. Fans will have to get in quick though: tickets went on sale last week, and it will surely be a sell-out concert.

The Wu-Tang Clan play the Enmore Theatre on the 29th of October, supported by Lil Jon. Tickets on sale now!
