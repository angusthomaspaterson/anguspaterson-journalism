Magic City unleashes the dragon in May

Angus Paterson · 2 February 2010 · inthemix

Transmission may have called it a day after fourteen years, but its legacy lives on… in a little place called Magic City. Set to debut at Transmission’s spiritual home of the Sydney Olympic Sports Centre on May 29th, and brought to you by the same team behind what was the city’s leading hard dance event for over a decade, Magic City brings with it the promise of being transported away to a faraway place – that showcases all types of dance music with a harder edge, of course!

While details have yet to leak out on what acts we’ll be seeing at Magic City, the organisers have hinted they’ll be hosting one of the world’s biggest hard dance acts to headline the party. Whoever could it be? The mind boggles. Combine that with the very best of the local hard dance scene, and it’s set to be an epic evening of pummelling beats (and then some) when Magic city unleashes the dragon in May.

Magic City: Unleash the Dragon, hits the Sydney Olympic Sports Centre on Saturday May 29th. Tickets will be on sale from February 22nd from www.magiccity.net.au, stay tuned to ITM for more details!
