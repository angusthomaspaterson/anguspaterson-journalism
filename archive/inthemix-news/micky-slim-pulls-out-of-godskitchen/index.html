Micky Slim pulls out of Godskitchen

Angus Paterson · 27 September 2007 · inthemix

In unfortunate news for anyone who is heading along to the national Godskitchen tour over the coming fortnight, rising name in electro and house Micky Slim has been forced to cancel his trip over to Australia due to visa and immigration issues. It’s both a bummer for us and a bummer for him: when ITM spoke to Micky a few weeks ago HERE he talked of how excited he was at the prospect of heading over our way.

Future Entertainment say that unfortunately they won’t be able to replace hiom with such short notice as the tour kicks off this weekend in Perth and Sydney, but they promise that the other international guests will be playing extended sets to cover his performance time.
