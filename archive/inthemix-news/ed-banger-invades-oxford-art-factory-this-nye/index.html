Ed Banger invades Oxford Art Factory this NYE

Angus Paterson · 29 October 2008 · inthemix

That’s right, baton down the hatches and secure your firearms cause those wicked Frenchies will be attempting a full-blown invasion of one of Sydney’s hottest venues this New Years Eve. Ed Banger Records mainman Busy P has been confirmed as the one who’ll be helping the crowd at Oxford Art Factory bring in the New Year, but that’s not all. He’ll also be joined onstage by DJ Mehdi, ensuring that it’ll be a night of French electro mayhem that won’t be forgotten by anyone in a hurry.

What to say about the Ed Banger Records stable? Busy P AKA Pedro Winters fired up the label earlier in the decade after having already managed the legendary Daft Punk for several years, and quickly he established it as the coolest and most successful of the new wave of independent dance labels emerging out of France. And they like to rock. Something that’s fundamental to all of the material coming from Ed Banger is a heavy ‘rock n’ roll’ influence, as well as an undying love of feedback.

Expect plenty of blistering French electro nastiness this December 31st that continues well into January 1st, and get to rock.

Crazy Ed Banger cohorts Busy P and DJ Mehdi will be tearing it up at Oxford Art Factory this New Years Eve. Stay tuned to ITM for more details!
