Listen & Watch: Sensation ups the spectacle for Into The Wild

Angus Paterson · 8 July 2013 · inthemix

Sensation is one of the defining examples of large-scale dance spectacle, having travelled to every corner of the globe and thrown in excess of 70 parties since it launched 13 years ago. The brand is set for a massive U.S. tour later this year with the ‘Ocean of White’ theme, though on Saturday it returned to its homeland of Amsterdam for the launch of ‘Into the Wild’.

While the organisers have always emphasised that Sensation is more about the “experience” than the line-up, the 40,000 or so white-clad partiers who’d gathered in the Amsterdam Arena on Saturday were nonetheless treated to a solid procession of DJs, all familiar to the experience. Sensation resident Mr White opened the evening, while UK stalwart Nic Fanciulli was up next to deliver a groovy selection of house, with just a few lashings of techno.

The progression over the evening saw the music slowly evolving into the ‘bigger’ stadium-friendly sounds, with the controls eventually were passed over to the artist who has possibly played more Sensation events than anyone else, Dutch house heavy Fedde le Grand who brought the big room sounds, with a set largely comprised of his own custom-made bootlegs, edits and remixes.

Next was a performance from one of the next generation of Dutch house heroes, Nicky Romero. Kicking off on some slower house grooves, he proceeded to ramp things up again with a set that truly captured the big-room sound of the moment. Again full of his own mash-ups, the set captured everything from Axwell and Sebastian Ingrosso, to Eric Prydz and Romero’s own Krewella collaboration.

Sensation indulges in the same sense of the dramatic that has also defined other ID&T events like Tomorrowland, and Saturday was certainly no exception. The ‘Into The Wild’ theme saw the party ramping up the Cirque Du Soleil theatrics possibly more than ever before, with a posse of costumed dancers and performers hitting the stage at various stages during the evening for a series of ‘moments’, with pyrotechnics highlighting the spectacular set-up across the arena.

Party-goers from across the U.S. will have their chance to sample the Sensation experience when the brand travels across the country with an unprecedented seven-date tour later this year.

Sensation The Ocean Of White USA Tour dates:

September 14 – Oracle Arena – Oakland, CA
October 4 & 5 – MGM Grand Garden Arena – Las Vegas, NV
October 11 & 12 – American Airlines Arena – Miami, FL
October 25 & 26 – Barclays Center – Brooklyn, NY
