Parklife lineup lands 5pm Thursday!

Angus Paterson · 4 June 2009 · inthemix

THE LINEUP IS RELEASED – GET THE FULL STORY HERE!

This is the big one people… We’ve been teased and tantalised with lineup leaks over the past few months, but the time has finally come to discover once and for all what artists we’ll see on the bill when Fuzzy’s massively popular Parklife national tour returns later this year. Be sure to head back to inthemix at 5pm today, Thursday June 4th, for the full rundown!

For all of the latest rumours and leaks check out our festival page at inthemix.com.au/parklife. Be sure to bookmark the page as that’s where you’ll read all the latest details on this year’s tour first.

Parklife 2009 tour dates:

Sat 26th Sep – Brisbane
Sun 27th Sep – Perth
Sat 3rd Oct – Melbourne
Sun 4th Oct – Sydney
Mon 5th Oct – Adelaide

inthemix is a proud presenting partner of Parklife in 2009! See you back here at 5pm.
