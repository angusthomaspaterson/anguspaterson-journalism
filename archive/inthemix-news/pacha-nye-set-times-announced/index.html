Pacha NYE set times announced

Angus Paterson · 30 December 2009 · inthemix

The mighty and glamorous Pacha brand will be dropping into the equally mighty and glamorous surrounds of the Ivy on New Years Eve, featuring a massive international lineup of Swedish House Mafioso Sebastian Ingrosso, Claude VonStroke, Sidney Samson and Kenny Dope, plus local heavies like Aston Shuffle, Goodwill, Mo Funk, Carl Kennedy and more. The organisers have now revealed the set times for the evening, check them out now!
