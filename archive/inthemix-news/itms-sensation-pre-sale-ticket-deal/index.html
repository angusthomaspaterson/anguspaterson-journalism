ITM's Sensation pre-sale ticket deal

Angus Paterson · 25 August 2009 · inthemix

ID&T’s world beating spectacular Sensation took home the prize for Melbourne’s favourite event in the Sony inthemix50 last week, and ITM has been able to secure a special pre-sale deal for this coming Wednesday for everyone who’s keen to get in ahead of when it goes on sale to the general public – at 9.09am on the 09/09/09!

Those kind folks over at ID&T are offering ITMers the exclusive opportunity to jump the queue and purchase a strictly limited number of special-offer tickets. In addition, they’re also offering the chance with the pre-sale offer to purchase Deluxe Tickets before they go on sale to the public, with the first 10 deluxe tickets upgraded to a Deluxe Dinner Package for free. To take advantage of the offer, all you have to do is click here from 9am Wednesday 26th August and enter the code ‘ITM’.

A Deluxe Ticket offers a Sensation experience above and beyond what’s enjoyed by the average punter, offering access to a host of special Deluxe Areas, a dedicated entry gate to avoid the long queues and a complimentary glass of sparkling on arrival. The Deluxe Dinner Package adds to this an extravagant 3-Course Dining Package, as well as access to the stadium before the opening of the show.

While Australian audiences enjoyed the iconic ‘Oak of Love’ last New Years Eve in Melbourne, this time around we’ll be seeing the spectacular ‘Ocean of White’ which has already entertained more than 250,000 punters across Europe over the past 12 months. Boasting a 160 metre stage that covers the entire length of the stadium, with huge water features and effects that keep in tune with the theme; there’s also all the lights, lasers and special effects we’ve come to expect from ID&T’s mind-boggling parties.

To take advantage of ITM’s special pre-sale ticket deal, click here from 9am Wednesday 26th August and enter the code ‘ITM’. Sensation goes on sale to the general public from 9.09am on the 9th September, 2009. Start getting excited people!
