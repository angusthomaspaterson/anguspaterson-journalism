Parklife Brisbane sells out!

Angus Paterson · 7 August 2009 · inthemix

Have you got your Parklife tickets yet? Hope so, because sunny Queensland is the first place in the country where the ‘sold out’ sign has gone up, with tickets for Parklife in Brisbane completely selling out. Word on the street is that Melbourne tickets will be all gone by early next week with Sydney also dangerously close to selling out, so anybody else across the country who wants to party with the likes of MSTRKRFT, La Roux, Busy P, Empire of the Sun, Erol Alkan and many more, you’d better get your skates on and make sure you have your tickets sorted!

inthemix is a proud presenting partner of Parklife in 2009! Keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page at inthemix.com.au/parklife
