Roger Sanchez locked for Future Music Festival sideshows

Angus Paterson · 8 February 2008 · inthemix

Are you a fan of the S-Man? House music’s #1 Roger Sanchez will touring around the country with the Future Music Festival when it lands in March, playing to crowds of thousands, but you’ll also have the chance to catch him in a more intimate setting at the following sideshows around the country.

When it comes to the many different shades of house music that’s being played out there in the nightclubs, Roger Sanchez is across it all. Be it vocal, deep, funky, tribal, minimal, electro – he’s playing it all. He’s taken his sets right across the world, from his home of New York to terraces of Ibiza to the darkened basements of London’s Fabric. He’s a demon behind the decks, scratching and mixing across three turntables, and he’s the brains the ‘Release Yourself’ concept: compilation CDs, club nights and a radio show across Europe and the US that reaches more than a million listeners a week. When it comes to house music, you don’t get much bigger than Roger Sanchez.

Check out the Future Music Festival ‘Full Coverage’ page for more info, and catch Roger Sanchez playing at the following shows around the country…

Sat 1st March: Future Music Festival, Brisbane
Sat 1st March: Famous @ Home, Sydney
Sun 2nd March: Future Music Festival, Perth
Thu 6th March: Bedroom, Gold Coast
Fri 7th March: Famous @ Queensbridge, Melbourne
Sat 8th march: Future Music Festival, Sydney
Sun 9th March: Future Music Festival, Melbourne
Mon 10th march: Future Music Festival, Adelaide
