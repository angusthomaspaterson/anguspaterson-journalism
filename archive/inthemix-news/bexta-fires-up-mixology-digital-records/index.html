Bexta fires up Mixology Digital Records

Angus Paterson · 31 January 2007 · inthemix

Australia’s first lady of hard dance Bexta has revealed details on her latest venture: she’s developing her new record label Mixology Digital Records, with the aim of providing exposure to hot new Australian producers. To give fans a taste she’ll be bringing the experience to Sublime next Friday night.

Bexta says the label will feature new artists along with her own material, also playing host to an assortment of remixed tracks. International artists already contributing to Mixology Digital Records includes Public Domain, Dave Joy and Vandertraxx along with local artists JvE, Tydi and Paranoid Dancer ( Patrik Carrera ). Releases are already available to download for those who are ultra keen, with Bexta promising there will be plenty of vinyl releases in the near future.

Sublime’s main room Voodoo will host Bexta next Friday February 9th to celebrate the launch of Mixology Digital Records, and she’ll be playing alongside Amber Savage, Jumping Jack and Patrik Carrera in Voodoo.

See Bexta launching Mixology Digital Records in Sublimes next Friday, or check out the record label at www.mixologydigitalrecords.com
