Schoolies week walloped by $2.5 million ecstasy bust

Angus Paterson · 22 November 2007 · inthemix

Thousands of school leavers planning on partaking in the more salubrious side of partying following the completion of their final High School exams have been thwarted, with police announcing the seizure of $2.5 million worth of ecstasy believed to be headed straight for the Gold Coast.

According to the Sydney Morning Herald, police hauled in 28,500 MDMA tablets and one kilogram of MDMA powder at the conclusion of what was a four-month long investigation. They’ve remarked they have every reason to believe the massive stash was destined for consumption with the Schoolies Week crowd.

“There is little doubt that these illicit drugs which have been seized by strikeforce detectives were destined for sale to teenagers on holiday after completing their HSC exams,” says Detective Superintendent Greig Newbery, commander of the Drug Squad. “We will allege these seizures will have a significant impact on the availability of illicit drugs, particularly in this region of NSW and on Queensland’s Gold Coast.”
