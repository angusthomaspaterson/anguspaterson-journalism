Claude Von Stroke flying back to Australia in January

Angus Paterson · 18 December 2008 · inthemix

San Francisco ‘Dirty Bird’ Claude Von Stroke already has two successful Australian tours under his belt, both in the last 18 months, and it seems he just can’t get enough as he’ll be back in early 2009. He’s one of the big success stories in techno and underground house over the past few years, with is acclaimed Dirtybird label grabbing the attention of heavyweights like Richie Hawtin, and he scored a home run with the release of his full-length debut album Beware of the Bird.

Catch the Dirtybird sound of Claude Von Stroke at the following shows…

Sat 24th Jan – Pulse Radio Yacht Party, Sydney Harbour (daytime)
Sat 24th Jan – Family, Brisbane
Sun 25th Jan – Revolver, Melbourne
