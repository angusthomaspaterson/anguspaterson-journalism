Stereosonic onsale 9am Thursday at ITM!

Angus Paterson · 18 August 2009 · inthemix

This month’s lineup announcement for the Stereosonic Festival tour received an absolutely massive response, with punters absolutely blown away by the lineup that promoters Hardware and Onelove have managed to pull together. With heavyweight names like Deadmau5, Axwell and Fedde Le Grand playing alongside a whole new generation of talent like The Crookers, Surkin, The Bloody Beetroots and more, what’s not to love? If you want to be part of this year’s festival experience, make sure you’re back at ITM with your credit cards at the ready on Thursday morning from 9am as tickets will be going on sale!

First release tickets on sale at ITM from 9am Thursday, 20th August AEST. Check out the national tour dates below……

Sat 28th November – Moore Park, Hordern, RHI & Surrounds, Sydney
Sun 29th November – Claremont Showground, Perth
Sat 5th December – Bonython Park, Adelaide
Sat 5th December – Royal Melbourne Showgrounds, Melbourne
Sun 6th December – Eagle Farm Racecourse, Brisbane

inthemix is a proud presenting partner of Stereosonic in 2009! Keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page and have a look at our Stereosonic lineup showcase gallery for a reminder oif what we’ve got to look forward to!
