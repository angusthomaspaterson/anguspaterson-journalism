Andrew Johns admits to years of drug use

Angus Paterson · 31 August 2007 · inthemix

You’ll find it splashed all over the tabloids this morning: in a candid confession made to the Daily Telegraph, NRL star Andrew Johns has admitted to 12 years of drug use following his arrest earlier this week in London for ecstasy possession. Discussing what was labeled in the paper as “long-term drug and alcohol abuse” as well as his battles with depression, he has admitted taking ecstasy and other drugs throughout his stellar career though he’s managed to avoid detection.

“I took them throughout my career, mainly in the off-season,” he told the paper. “At times it was like playing Russian roulette when I took them during the season. When we’d play Friday night, we wouldn’t train until Monday. I took them then. Hopefully it (the drug) would be gone by Monday.

“It was an escape from the pressure and to get away from being a footballer. People must think it’s great being me and I have led a privileged life. But with that goes a lot of pressure.”

Johns was arrested in London last Sunday when he encountered a drug operation at Kings Cross station, just after he’d left ‘The Church’, a popular hole for Aussie expats. After an ecstasy pill was discovered in his jeans pocket, Johns was arrested and officially cautioned but not charged.
