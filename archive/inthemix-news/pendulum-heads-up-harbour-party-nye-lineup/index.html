Pendulum heads up Harbour Party NYE lineup

Angus Paterson · 2 October 2009 · inthemix

If you were hoping to spend your New Years Eve in Sydney with a spectacular view of the fireworks, Luna Park is the place to be as Harbour Party NYE has revealed the lineup of artists who will be helping us bring in 2010. Heading up the list will be none other than Pendulum, returning from tearing it up overseas at events like Global Gathering and Glastonbury. It’ll be a special treat for fans as it’ll see original members Rob, Gareth and El Hornet hitting the stage along with MC Verse to preview material from their upcoming third album.

Other international guests joining the party will include the Ian Carey Project, as well as Le Tigre member JD Samson, and they’ll be supported by a range of local talent that includes Ajax, Bass Kleph, Lost Valentinos and much more.

Harbour Party NYE returns to Luna Park on Thursday 31st December, presale tickets available on ITM on October 7th. Check out the first-round lineup below…

Pendulum (DJ Set)
Ian Carey Project
JD Samson
Grafton Primary (live)
Lost Valentinos (Live)
Bass Kelph
Ajax
