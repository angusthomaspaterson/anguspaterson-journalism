Future Music Festival photos online

Angus Paterson · 19 March 2007 · inthemix

Future Music Festival was one of the most eagerly awaited events on the Sydney dance music calendar. It hit Randwick Racecourse again on Saturday and brought with it a stellar lineup of international acts and a 20,000 strong crowd.

After all the anticipation the party has now come and gone, but you can relive Saturday’s mega event in the ITM photo section – with a massive six galleries so far and more to come.
