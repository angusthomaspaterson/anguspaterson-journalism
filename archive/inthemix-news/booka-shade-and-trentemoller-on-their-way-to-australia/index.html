Booka Shade & Trentemoller on their way to Australia!

Angus Paterson · 21 September 2007 · inthemix

That’s right, Booka Shade and Trentemoller are both on their way to Australia along with Armand Van Helden, Fedde Le Grand, DJ Mehdi, Tim Deluxe, Kevin Saunderson, Steve Aoki and more. They’ve all just been announced as headliners of the brand new Stereosonic Festival in Melbourne, and you can be sure they’ll be making their way around the rest of the country!

Headline acts Booka Shade and Trentemoller are the ones that we’ve already touted as the hot tips for the new Ministry of Sound festival for Perth and Sydney that was confirmed yesterday. And it’s just been confirmed that Armand Van Helden will be in attendance at the Sydney gig as well (in addition to a gig at Family in Brisbane the Friday evening before). We’ll have more details on Monday, but if our hunch is correct then we may also see be seeing Steve Aoki and Riot in Belgium among others on that bill.

The Stereosonic Festival is confirmed for Melbourne on Saturday December 1st, so when will all these acts be landing in your part of the country? Stay tuned to ITM! And to get you in the mood, check out an clip of Trentemoller doing his thing at Ministry of Sound in London…
