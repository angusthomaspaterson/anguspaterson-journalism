Australia's first Armin van Buuren photos!

Angus Paterson · 4 June 2009 · inthemix

The world’s #1 DJ is in the country. Last night trance superstar Armin van Buuren swept down on Brisbane for an exclusive club show at The Met, the first of the shows for his June Long Weekend tour, and ITM’s trusty snappers were there to capture all then action. Check out Australia’s first Armin van Buuren photos!

> The Met presents Armin van Buuren photos

Armin’s twitter update at 6.30am this morning said it all. “Just had a rocking gig in Brisbane!! Crowd here is insane!! Woohoo!!” From all reports, this enthusiasm was reflected in the response that he got from the Brisbane crowd who were packed into The Met to see him, going nuts everytime he gestured or even looked up from the decks. He delivered a four-hour set of his trademark top-shelf trance and progressive, and we’ve been told the rest of the country has something to look forward to indeed.

Getting ready for the weekend? To get you in the mood, have a listen to Armin’s recent ITM interview, his Australian Tour Mix in ITM-FM, and for more on this weekend’s winter festival action check out our Festival Pages.
