Purple Sneakers scuffle around the country

Angus Paterson · 20 July 2007 · inthemix

Beginning at the Abercrombie Hotel in Sydney a little over two years ago,, the unbeatable indie/dance vibe brought to you by the Purple Sneakers DJs is now taking it around the country in a series of shows. They’ve teamed up with MTV to present the Meet or Delete tour that will include three weeks of shows across Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane along with some extra-special live acts including Red Riders, Children Collide and Operator Please. They’ll also be popping in at some of Australia’s biggest festivals…

It’s certainly been a big year for the Purple Sneakers posse, and in the last year they’ve closed out the Falls Festival, presented stages at The Great Escape and Playground Weekender as well hosting their own room at last years Splendour in the Grass. They’ll be picking up again at this year’s festival in Byron Bay and also be making an appearance at a certain highly anticipated long weekend festival in Sydney… mainly, Fuzzy’s annual Parklife bonanza. High times indeed!

Catch the Purple Sneakers crew at the following shows around the country…

27 July: MTV Meet or Delete Tour Pre-Party, Abercrombie Hotel, Sydney
2 August: MTV Meet or Delete Tour, with Red Riders, @ Manning Bar, Sydney
4 August: Splendour in the Grass – Dance Tent
5 August: Splendour in The Grass – Official After-Party
8 August: MTV Meet or Delete Tour, with Children Collide, @ Melbourne University
9 August: Snowball, with Expatriate, Muscles @ Manning Bar, Sydney University
16 August: MTV Meet or Delete Tour, with Operator Please @ QUT, Brisbane
17 August: MTV Meet or Delete Tour – Wrap Party @ The Empire, Brisbane
30 September: Parklife @ Moore Park, Sydney
