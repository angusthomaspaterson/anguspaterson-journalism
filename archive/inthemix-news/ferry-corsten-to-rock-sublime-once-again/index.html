Ferry Corsten to rock Sublime once again

Angus Paterson · 1 March 2007 · inthemix

The Dutch trance king’s previous performances at Home’s long-running night have become the stuff of legends, and he’ll be returning next Friday night to do it all again. On the eve of the Future Music Festival selling out, Home are proud to announce that Ferry Corsten will be headlining a massive show on Friday 9th of March with support from fellow international guests The Organ Donors and Kutski.

Having worked in the music industry for over 17 years, Ferry Corsten is one of the most enduring names in trance and dance music in general. He last rocked Sublime in both late 2004 and mid 2005, sending the crowd mental with his unmistakable concoction of euphoric trance, peppered with his own hits like Punk and Rock Your Body Rock.

Local support comes from Jumping Jack, Amber Savage, Archie, Kate Monroe, John Glover, Jeff Drake, Klaus ‘Heavyweight’ Hill, and Kemuri + MC Losty with young guns on the night Fast Twitch (Live), Nitrouz, Pablo Anon, and Atlas.
