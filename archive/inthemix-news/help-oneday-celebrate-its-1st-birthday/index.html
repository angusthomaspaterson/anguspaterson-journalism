Help Oneday celebrate its 1st Birthday

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2007 · inthemix

Sydney’s favourite seasonal event Oneday is celebrating its first birthday with a bang on Sunday 21st October, taking over the classy venue The Argyle in the Rocks from midday. Held four times a year to coincide with each season, that Oneday series of events has so far has delivered four amazing parties across a variety of unique Sydney venues, bringing the party to celebrities and fashionable party-types.

Then organisers promise the anniversary event will be bigger and better than ever. They’ll be partnering with funky London shoe label Swear for the evening which means there will be 50 pairs up for grabs, and there will also be a special VIP room and a general area for a capacity of 1500 guests. Some of the musical guests on board will include Adam Bozzetto, Andy Van, Anyo, Bag Raiders, Bobby Disco, Brenden Fing, CtrAlt Del, Dan Bloom, Ember, John Glover, Jonny Powell, The Vandals and more. So get ready to help celebrate the 1st anniversary of Oneday!

Oneday 1st Anniversary hits The Argyle on Sunday 21st October, tickets on sale now from inthemix!
