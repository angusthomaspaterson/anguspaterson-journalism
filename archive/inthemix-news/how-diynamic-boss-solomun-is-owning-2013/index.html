How Diynamic boss Solomun is owning 2013

Angus Paterson · 9 April 2013 · inthemix

Last year really was the year for Diynamic boss Solomun, which saw the deep house vanguard reaching new heights of success, threatening to tip the scales into genuine crossover, a la Luciano’s heights of Ibiza super-stardom. Solomun in particular ruled the White Isle in 2012, with the Diynamic Neon Nights residency at Sankeys making an emphatic impact, though this year it’s set to accelerate even further. Solomun is set for a headline residency at Pacha Ibiza no less, plus he’ll oversee Diynamic launching its own themed festival in Amsterdam.

While Diynamic Neon Nights will return to Sankeys for another Tuesday night residency, the label boss will also be stepping up big time with the Solomun +1 night at Pacha Ibiza. It’ll be part of the unprecedented shake-up of the club’s performance roster, which has also seen Erick Morillo, Pete Tong, Tiesto and Luciano exiting this season.

Pacha have indicated the glitz and glamour will be shelved for Solomun’s residency, in favour of, would you believe it, the music itself being the main focus. The newly-anointed headliner will be playing in a DJ booth that’s repositioned to be directly in the middle of the club, and he’ll also be joined weekly by a single hand-picked guest. Ame, Dennis Ferrer, Miss Kittin, Tensnake and Marek Hemmann are some of the talent that have been named as upcoming +1s for the season.

Solomun will also be heading up a rather tasty looking new one-day festival in Amsterdam in May, with the simply titled Diynamic Festival representing the first time the label has staged such an event, with the label’s entire cast playing across two stages (early-bird tickets sold out in 24 hours). In addition to Solomun scheduling in a three-hour set, the festival will also host live performances from Stimming and Kollektiv Turmstrasse, and DJ sets from H.O.S.H and Adriatique and the rest of the Diynamic gang.
