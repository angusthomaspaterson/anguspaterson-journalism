NSW winner embarks on Endless Summer

Angus Paterson · 9 March 2007 · inthemix

Maria Vamvouklis from Casula, NSW (photos below) is the winner of the inthemix Endless Summer promotion, brougt to you by Student Flights and is gearing up for an overseas clubbing adventure that will take her to vital dance destinations across Berlin, Serbia, Ibiza and Thailand.

First stop is Berlin and although the dance world is still reeling from the news that the Love Parade has been relocated to elsewhere in Germany or Europe, the city is still hotter than hot when it comes to contemporary electronic music so there will still be plenty of opportunities to sample the trademark minimal techno of its thriving club scene.

Maria will then be flown out to Ibiza with tickets to some of the locations biggest clubs. The Spanish island is hotter than ever at the moment and remains one of the world’s premier party destinations, so little more needs to be said beyond that.

Next stop is Serbia where Maria will have a VIP pass to visit the Exit Festival (pictured below), one of the big-ticket musical events in Europe. Held in the picturesque settings of an 18th century fortress by the Danube and growing from its political activist beginnings to a massive cultural event, last year it had over 150,000 people in attendance. For the 2007 edition Basement Jaxx, Beastie Boys, LTJ Bukem, Wu-Tang Clan and Robert Plant have all already been locked in for main stage performances.

The last leg of the Endless Summer tour will be in Thailand for the Full Moon Party. An all-night beach party that takes place on the island of Ko Pha Ngan on the night of every full moon, what started out as a small affair has grown to one that draws a crowd in excess of 10,000 people for every event. The party carries on until the sun rises the next day with an assortment of musical styles, and the spectacular visual display of burning, fuel-soaked balls on chains are a common sight.

The Endless Summer competition was open to anyone who purchased a ticket to any event, festival or club night from inthemix.com.au between November 1st, 2006 and the end of February, 2007.

ITM extends a hearty congratulations to Maria, and we can’t wait to hear the updates and look at photos of the trip!
