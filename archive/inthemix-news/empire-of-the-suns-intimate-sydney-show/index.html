Empire of the Sun's intimate Sydney show

Angus Paterson · 19 October 2009 · inthemix

Whether or not you’re a fan of the ethereal pop sounds that Luke Steele has crafted with Empire of the Sun, there’s little doubt he got tongues wagging when he took his spectacular visual show on the road for the first time with Parklife recently. Beautiful it did indeed look, and the band has been booked to play at several regional festivals over the summer including Foreshore and Pyramid Rock as well as last week’s massive Future Music Festival announcement.

For those who want a chance to check out their sumptuous stage design and visuals in a more intimate context, you’re in luck because they’ll be playing an intimate show at CarriageWorks on November 24th. Tickets are $55+BF and go on sale at 9am on October 29th, check out the gig’s website for more info.
