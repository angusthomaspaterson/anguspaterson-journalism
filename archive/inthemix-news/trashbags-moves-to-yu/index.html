Trashbags moves to Yu

Angus Paterson · 13 August 2007 · inthemix

Changes are afoot for the Saturday night of Kings Cross’ biggest club Yu, as we say farewell to Beware the Cat which has been running for the last 10 months. That night, which has hosted acts as diverse as Spirit Catcher, Audiofly and Jona over the past few months, will be throwing its final party on Saturday September 1st. In its place we’ll be welcoming a new rotational format that will see growing favourite Trashbags hosting its first monthly event on Saturday September 8th.

Yu is still working on finalizing what the schedule will be for the other three weeks of the month, but the fact that the club will be the new home of Trashbags is certainly the biggest news at this stage: the organisers say they’ll be bringing their renown ‘party ethics’ from their current home of Plantation Bar over to Yu as well as a whole load of great internationals for the rest of the year. Toxic Avenger from France will be the first cab off the rank while on the local side of things, ZZZ who recently signed to the Bang Gang 12” will be playing the takeover party.

Stay tuned for further details on what you can expect at Yu over the coming months.
