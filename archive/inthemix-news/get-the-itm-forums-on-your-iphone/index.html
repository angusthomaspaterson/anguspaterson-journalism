Get the ITM forums on your iPhone!

Angus Paterson · 1 July 2009 · inthemix

If you’re one of those crazed forum fiends who simply must be connected to ITM all day long via your iPhone or mobile device, whether you’re on the train, in the supermarket or actually out rocking it in the clubs, then we’ve got some super-hot news for you. ITM has launched its mobile version of the forums, with a special skin that means that surfing (and trolling) your favourite threads with your iPhone is now easier than ever, and it looks a whole lot better to boot.

To access the new version, just head to the normal forum address and if you’re using a mobile device, you’ll automatically get the new-and-improved version of the forums. It’s in its first release, and the tech monkeys working at ITM have indicated that we can expect further improvements in the coming weeks.

So what are you waiting for? If you’re rocking an iPhone, head straight to the ITM forums to check it out for yourself, and if you’ve got any suggestions for improvements then let us know in the ITM Feedback thread.
