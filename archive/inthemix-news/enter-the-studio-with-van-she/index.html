Enter the studio with Van She

Angus Paterson · 4 July 2008 · inthemix

Just about to embark on their ‘party’ tour which sees the band blurring the lines blurring the lines between a live and club environment, Modular Records cool cats Van She are also prepping for the release of the debut long player V in August. It’s been a tense three-year wait for the band’s fans, but it’s finally on the horizon and to give you an insight into what went into the recording process, check out this gallery of the band laying down the tunes for V in their studio in London.

CHECK OUT THE PICS OF VAN SHE RECORDING IN LONDON.

On the road throughout July, Van She will be performing in both live and DJ mode, reflecting the fact that they’re equally as well known for their remixes under the Van She Tech guise, producing stormers like this year’s revisioning of the Utah Saints classic Something Good. The band will be supported by The Dirty Secrets on the extensive national tour, with several of their DJ buddies on rotation along the way including Ajax, Dangerous Dan and Andee Van Damage. Kicking off tonight in Brisbane, it’ll be rolling right across the country before the end of the month.

Van She’s V hits the stores on August 9th, check out the Party Tour at the following dates, presented by inthemix:
