David Guetta gets on the Microsoft payroll

Angus Paterson · 23 July 2007 · inthemix

The French king of cheese and the world’s most evil software empire have gotten into bed together, and the results are astounding and surprising… a crap novelty track! That’s right, David Guetta and Microsoft have hooked up in an unholy union to produce a tune that samples the sound that MSN makes when you’re sent an instant message. Ladies and gentleman, try to restrain your excitement.

While the new song Mix Messenger is apparently “hilarious” (I’m going off the super-enthused press release here), and we could talk about how it sets new standards in corporate dance music synergy, that’s all that ITM is going to say about it. It’s simply too naff to put into words so you’ll just have to take a listen yourself. Or not.

Click below to make your ears bleed by listening to the Guetta/Microsoft monstrosity, and stay tuned for Paul Oakenfold’s crappy remix of the Apple OSX bootup sound.
