Hed Kandi residency comes to Favela

Angus Paterson · 10 June 2009 · inthemix

Glamorous house and disco brand Hed Kandi has turned out to be one of the mainstays of the international clubbing scene, entertaining over two million people across the world last year, and for the very first time it’s found a residency in Sydney at one of our classiest little Kings Cross hotspots Favela. It’ll be spearheaded by veteran Sydney promoter Paul Strange, who has been responsible for throwing a number of Hed Kandi parties in the past including the recent NYE sellout event.

Taking place at Favela every Friday night from June 19th, Jack McCord will be at the wheels as resident, playing alongside a number of international guests as well as mainstays of the Australian house scene.

Hed Kandi comes to Favela in Kings Cross every Friday night from June 19th, $20 on the door. Checkout the June and July lineups below…

June 19th – Launch Night, Disco Heaven feat. Jack Mccord, Johnny Gleeson, Beth Yen, Sean Doyle, Frankie Romano, Muma Megs & guests

June 26th – Back To Love, featuring Steve Divine (UK), Sean Doyle, Jack Mccord, Damien Goundrie & Reel Sessions (live)

July 3rd –Wimbledon, featuring Alex Taylor, Jack Mccord, Damien Goundrie, Sean Doyle, Beth Yen, Muma Megs & guests

July 10th – Beach House, featuring Tim Phin (NZ), Levi 5-Star, Johnny Gleeson, Jack Mccord, Damien Goundrie & Reel Sessions

July 17th – Twisted Disco, featuring Damien Goundrie, Scott Pullen, Jack Mccord, Sean Doyle, Muma Megs & guests

July 24th – Carnival, featuring Jack Mccord, Beth Yen, Sean Doyle, Danny Presti, Damien Goundrie & Reel Sessions
