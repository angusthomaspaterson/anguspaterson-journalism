Le Castle Vania cast a spell over Australia

Angus Paterson · 13 June 2008 · inthemix

Atlanta-based DJ/producer Le Castle Vania, commonly known as Dylan Eiland and one half of Lies In Disguise, has a passion for nightlife that borders on scary, and has gained notoriety around the world with his riotous disco punk sets. With his catchy electro hooks and productions that are tweaked within an inch of their life, Le Castle Vania has spun alongside some his hottest contemporaries including Justice, MSTRKRFT, Busy P, Soulwax and Austrlaia’s own The Presets.

When he’s not on the road he’s holed away in the darkness of his studio, writing tracks and remixing his favourite bands – hunt down and have a listen to his remix of Black Eye by indie darlings Snowden for an example of him at his nasty best. And next weekend he’ll be embarking on a tour of the country courtesy of the crew from Finely Tuned, responsible for the notorious Lost Baggage parties in Sydney.

Catch Le Castle Vania in his element (a darkened room) at the following show…

Thu 19 June, Melbourne @ Pogo
Fri 20 June, Sydney @ Oxford Arts Factory
Sat 21 June, Perth @ Shape Bar
Fri 27 June, Brisbane @ Empire
Sat 5 July, Adelaide @ Dar
