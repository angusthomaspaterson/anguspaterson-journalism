Massif set for massive December tour

Angus Paterson · 24 November 2009 · inthemix

The hard dance experts over at the Massif label have a huge triple-CD compilation due out at the end of the month, to mark their 25th release, and they’ll be kicking off an equally huge tour throughout Australia during December to mark the occasion, with even more dates planned during January and February. Titled Masif Hard Dance Anthems and mixed by a massive posse of stars that includes Steve Hill, Alex Kidd, Kidd Kaos, BK, Luca Antolini, Technikal, MDA & Spherical and the Bootek DJs, it features 75 of the toughest tracks out there.

The big man from Massif that we know as Steve Hill will be manning the tour, with a run that takes in more than a dozen dates. And there’s even more shows planned for Brisbane, Perth, Canberra, Cairns, Townsville and the Sunshine Coast during January and February next year, so stay tuned.

Masif Hard Dance Anthems is out distributed through Stomp from November 27th, check out the national dates below:
