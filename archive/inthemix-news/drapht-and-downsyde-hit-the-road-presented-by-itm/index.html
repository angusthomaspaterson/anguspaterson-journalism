Drapht & Downsyde hit the road, presented by ITM

Angus Paterson · 30 January 2009 · inthemix

With Drapht coming off the back of an impressive top 10 listing in the Triple J Hottest 100 poll for Jimmy Recard, and veterans Downsyde having released yet another impressive longplayer last year, what better time for the two acts to hit the road together? Well that’s exactly what they’ll be doing during March and April, taking it to the masses to promote their recent albums on the extensive ‘City Brothers Tour’ to kick off what will surely be another massive year, presented by inthemix!

The ‘City Brothers Tour’ will make for the largest national jaunt either act has embarked on for quite a long time, so they’re knuckling down to make sure it’ll be an extra special one. They’re currently all locked in a rehearsal room preparing a combined set that’s sure to light up the heart of many a hip hop fan across the nation. And to sweeten the deal even further, fellow Triple J Hottest 100 overachiever Pez will also be joining the fun on support duties, hot off the back of the success of his debut album and its massive hit single The Festival Song.
