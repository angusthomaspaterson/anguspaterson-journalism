Tydi fires up Global Soundsystem tour with Audrey Gallagher

Angus Paterson · 12 November 2009 · inthemix

Queensland golden boy Tydi has enjoyed a fairly large 2009 so far, releasing his debut artist album as well as both retaining the #1 slot in the Sony inthemix50 and sweeping into the DJ Mag Top 100 poll, but he’s about to cap it off with something different entirely. In December he’ll be kicking off the national ‘Global Soundsystem’ tour with renowned trance vocalist Audrey Gallagher in tow.

It’ll be DJ performances complimented by live vocals courtesy of Audrey, who’s worked with some of trance’s biggest names including Armin van Buuren, John O’Callaghan, Agnelli & Nelson and of course with Tydi himself on You Walk Away. And then on New Years Eve in Adelaide at the Heaven on Earth party, there will be an extra special once-off ‘live show’ featuring a full six-piece band, with electronic and live instruments as well performances from string ensemble ‘The String Selection’.

The tour will mark the launch of Tydi’s very own Global Soundsystem radio show, broadcast weekly around the world, and the debut episode is streaming now on ITM. Check it out now in ITM-FM.

Tydi Global Soundsystem dates:

Fri 18th Dec – Brisbane, Family (Feat Aly & Fila)
Sat 19th Dec – Bedroom, Gold Coast (Live PA)
Sat 26th Dec – Rise, Perth (Live PA)
Sun 27th Dec – Gilligan’s, Cairns (Live PA)
Thurs 31st Dec – Heaven On Earth, Adelaide (Full Live Show)
Fri 1st Dec – Summadayze, Melb (Live PA)
Fri 1st Dec – Chasers Nightclub, Melb (Live PA)
