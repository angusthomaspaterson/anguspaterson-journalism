Follow Sony inthemix50 live on Twitter tonight!

Angus Paterson · 19 August 2009 · inthemix

The Sony inthemix50 has certainly been everywhere you look over the past two months; the source of plenty of discussion amongst dance music fans, and very nearly dominating the social networks at times. Well, the time has nearly come – the awards ceremony takes place at Sydney’s Nevermind tonight, with some of dance music’s biggest players set to front up to discover who’s taking home this year’s top trophies. For those who want to keep score of what’s going down, all the action will be broadcast live as it happens on that oh-so immediate social network that is Twitter, from 7.30pm tonight!

CLICK HERE TO FOLLOW INTHEMIX ON TWITTER

The results of this year’s Sony inthemix50 will be keenly awaited, not only by the country’s DJs but also by its producers and live acts, not to mention the promoters behind some of our favourite club nights, parties and festivals. For everyone dying to know, the full results can be seen on the Sony inthemix50 page from 10pm tonight!
