First acts for Harbour Party announced

Angus Paterson · 9 October 2007 · inthemix

Will the madness ever end? It’s party season for sure and now we can reveal the first acts on the lineup for Harbour Party, Sydney’s annual NYE bash on the harbour at Luna Park. Australia’s own Cut Copy and Pnau will be clocking in for live performances while Sneaky Sound System will be DJing.

On the international front, Faithless frontwoman Sister Bliss will be DJing, as well as France’s Joachim Garraud who is known for bringing a dynamic live element to his sets with the use of keyboards and samplers.

It’ll be taking place across two massive arenas with over 100 street performers, you’ll be enjoying unlimited rides as well and of course, there’s the spectacular view of the fireworks happening over the harbour.

Tickets are on sale on ITM from Monday the 15th of October, stay tuned for more details.
