Erick Morillo bringing Subliminal back to Australia this Easter

Angus Paterson · 25 January 2007 · inthemix

After wowing crowds at Two Tribes and Future Music Festival last year, the US house legend Erick Morillo returns in April to celebrate the 10-year anniversary of his seminal Subliminal Records label. He’ll be touching down in Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth and Sydney in a whirlwind tour over the Easter long weekend.

Erick Morillo has remixed everyone from Whitney Houston to Basement Jaxx, from the Sneaker Pimps to Macy Gray. He runs the Subliminal Records empire, he’s a platinum selling artist and after years in the business, remains one of the most sought-after house DJs in the world.

Celebrating 10-years of his record label Subliminal Records, Erick Morillo will be playing on the following dates.

Thu 5th April: Melbourne, Queensbridge
Fri 6th April: Brisbane, The Met
Sat 7th April: Perth, venue TBC
Sun 8th April: Sydney, Home
