ITM's spectacular Defqon.1 photos!

Angus Paterson · 23 June 2009 · inthemix

The absolutely massive Defqon.1 Festival returned to its waterlogged venue in Holland earlier this month, for a event hosting over 40,000 people that this year was boasting a menacing ‘steampunk’ theme. ITM managed to get its hands on some spectacular photos chronicling the event, including what went down during the day, what happened when the sun went down as well as getting down and dirty with a few of the many thousands and thousands of punters who made the trek.

We’re sure you’ll agree, it looks like an event to die for, and it’s made us even more excited about the fact that it’s making it down to Sydney in September. Check them out now…
