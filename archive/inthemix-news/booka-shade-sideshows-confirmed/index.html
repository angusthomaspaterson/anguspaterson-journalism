Booka Shade sideshows confirmed

Angus Paterson · 27 November 2007 · inthemix

Booka Shade will be bringing their extraordinary live show to a festival setting in Sydney and Melbourne this weekend, but do you want the opportunity to catch them in a more intimate setting? Well here’s your chance. The much-loved Berlin duo will be playing the Prince Bandroom in Melbourne next Tuesday December 4th while they’ll be hitting the Gaelic Club the very next night on Wednesday December 5th.

Booka Shade have been hotter than hot in 07: responsible along with DJ T and MANDY for founding the Get Physical label several years ago, with singles like In White Rooms and Body Language they’ve delivered some of the most anthemic examples of the minimal genre. They’re 100 per cent live when they hit the stage and punters were blown away when they toured earlier this year – if you missed them the first time around, don’t miss your chance to see what the fuss is about.

Catch Booka Shade at the following sideshows, and for more details check out our recent feature article HERE and the latest podcast HERE

Tues Dec 4th: Prince Bandroom, Melbourne
Wed Dec 5th: The Gaelic Club, Sydney
