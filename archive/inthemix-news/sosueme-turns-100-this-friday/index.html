Sosueme turns 100 this Friday

Angus Paterson · 22 October 2009 · inthemix

Somehow bucking the trend for weekly events, the crew from Q Bar Friday night Sosueme will be throwing their 100th party this weekend! That’s just under two years of getting loose at the ‘Q every week. Congratulations guys!

Of course, they’ve got a fine selection of their party pals to help them celebrate it in style. We’ll be seeing DJ sets from Muscles, Grafton Primary and Van She, and a bunch of live performances from The Protectors, Lions at Your Door and Indigo Rising. And of course, there’ll also be appearances form a wide selection of other Sosueme regulars including Anna Lunoe, Hey Now, Sosueme DJs and more.

Sosueme 100th Birthday hits Q Bar this Friday 23rd October.
