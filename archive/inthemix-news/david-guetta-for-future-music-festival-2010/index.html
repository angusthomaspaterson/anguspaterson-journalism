David Guetta for Future Music Festival 2010

Angus Paterson · 2 September 2009 · inthemix

You could say (with a sense of the understatement) that French house auteur is David Guetta getting his fair share of attention at the moment, hitting massive crossover success with his new album One Love, and it’s looking like he’ll be fronting up for headline duties at next year’s Future Music Festival tour. Guetta recently dropped the news during interviews with some of our country’s commercial radio networks, so it’s looking like we won’t have to wait too long for his return.

Looking at the strength alone of the A-class guests that appear on Guetta’s new album, he’s scored himself a bonafide crossover success with One Love. We’re talking Kelly Rowland, Akon, Kid Cudi, Sebastian Ingrosso, Dirty South, Julie McKnight, Black Eyed Peas and more. Just goes to show how much sway the mighty Frenchman has! Expect to hear plenty more of David Guetta in your face in the leadup to Future Music Festival next year.

David Guetta’s One Love is out now in Australia through EMI. Stay tuned to ITM for more news on Future Music Festival as it comes to hand!
