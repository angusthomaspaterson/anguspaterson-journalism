Dust Tones takes over Thursday nights

Angus Paterson · 25 July 2007 · inthemix

The crew from one of Sydney’s favourite ‘left-of-centre’ music nights Dust Tones will be taking over the Loft Bar at the University of Technology Sydney from next Thursday night. Bringing together some of Sydney’s best DJs, Producers and live acts, the best of all about the night is that it’s free!

The night kicks off at 5pm with a happy hour running from 5:30–6:30 and the leafy outdoor setting has earned the Loft Bar a reputation as one of Sydney’s best kept secrets. An excellent selection of music will be on offer each and every week with the launch night on Thursday 2nd August featuring performances from Good Buddha Sound System, Rephrase, Will Styles, Noel Boogie, Flux 3, SWAT DJs and of course Bentley. Get amongst it!

The Loft Bar for is part of the UTS Broadway campus just opposite Central Station, things kick off from Thursday August 2nd and entry is free.
