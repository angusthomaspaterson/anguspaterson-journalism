Nik Fish gets black on tour

Angus Paterson · 28 April 2009 · inthemix

20 years on, and he’s still as hard, dark, fast and ‘black’ as ever. Veteran of the hard dance scene Nik Fish is one of Australia’s most enduring DJs, and he continues to be a fixture in our nation’s clubs, particularly at Sydney’s (and the world’s) most enduring weekly night Sublime. He’ll be getting an little bit more attention than normal next month when he releases his brand new compilation Black, a double-disc effort that will include some of the hard dance scene’s latest and greatest weapons. To support hit, the man they call ‘The Fish’ will be hitting the road…
