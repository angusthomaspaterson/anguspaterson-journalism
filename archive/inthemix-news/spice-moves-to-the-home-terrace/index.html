Spice moves to the Home Terrace

Angus Paterson · 22 January 2010 · inthemix

Sydney’s home of quality early-hour underground music Spice has seen a few venue changes in recent months, forced to up and leave to go searching for a new home to keep pumping out the beats in – but don’t make the mistake of thinking this will hold it back. No sir, because after their planned move to the Burdekin Hotel was thwarted by last minute licensing restrictions placed on the venue, the promoters have already been successful in tracking down a replacement in the form of the oh-so excellent Terrace space at Home Nightclub. Wow!

Taking over the Home Terrace from 5am every Sunday morning from 24th January onwards, and resident Murat Kilic will be hosting the room with a minimum 3-hour set every week to help keep things pounding along. Of course, he’ll be joined be local residents Robbie Lowe, Claire Morgan, Aram Chapers, Mitch Crosher, Schwa, Telefunken, Matt Weir, Mattt & Tomass, Ant J Steep, Luke McD and more.

So what about the internationals they’ve got lined up in the short term? Try Vincenzo, Dave DK and Alex Wolfenden, all who will be playing at Spice before the end of February.

Spice will be taking over the Home Terrace from 5am every Sunday morning from 24th January.
