DJ Falcon flies into Australia

Angus Paterson · 1 February 2007 · inthemix

The famed ‘Daft Crew’ member will be hitting up Future Music Festival around the country next month, as well as a number of solo club shows that will include Kink in Sydney, Platinum on the Gold Coast, Famous in Melbourne and Bustin’ in Wollongong.
.
The Parisian is part of Daft Punk’s extended family, pushing the distinctly French ‘twisted filtered disco’ sound to clubbers across the globe and he’s recently been playing gigs at some of the biggest clubs across Europe – Pacha, Back to Basics and Tribal Gatherings no less.

Catch DJ Falcon at the following events:
– Sat 10th Feb: Platinum, Gold Coast
– Sat 17th Feb: KINK, Sydney
– Sun 18th Feb: SMUT, Sydney
– Fri 23th Feb: La La Land, Byron Bay
– Fri 2rd Mar: Bustin, Woolongong
– Sun 4th Mar: Future Music Festival Perth
– Sat 10th Mar: Future Music Festival Brisbane
– Sun 11th Mar: Future Music Festival Melbourne
– Mon 12th Mar: Future Music Festival Adelaide
– Sat 17th Mar: Future Music Festival Sydney
– Sat 17th Mar: Famous, Melbourne
