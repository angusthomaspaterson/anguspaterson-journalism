Pure Pleasure Experience 1st birthday bash

Angus Paterson · 11 September 2007 · inthemix

Pure Pleasure Experience has been bringing quality funky house sounds to Sydney for over a year now, and on their 1st birthday bash on Saturday 22nd September they be hosting a special live performance from Random Soul as well as sets from Graham Cordery, resident Ange, Ben Kelly as well as Lummy from Monkey Tennis playing a versus set with DiscoLou to kick off the night.

The crew will be branching out for their 1st birthday, so expect to hear plenty of pumping funky vocals with a hint of deep, chicago, soul and other quality house related grooves. To top it all off, the first 100 people through the door will receive a free glass of champagne and a Pure Pleasure Gift Bag to celebrate.

Pure Pleasure Experience 1st Birthday hits Level 2 of the Equilibrium Hotel on Saturday 22nd September, festivities kick off from 8.30 pm!
