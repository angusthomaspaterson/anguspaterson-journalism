Police stepping things up for festival season

Angus Paterson · 7 February 2008 · inthemix

It must be Summer. Or at least festival season, because NSW Police have started their anti-drug media push. The Sydney Morning Herald have reported that Police are planning a ‘blitz’ on upcoming dance events, and both Good Vibrations and Mardi Gras have been named as the upcoming events that have been put firmly in their sights.

While it’s not clear whether other big events over the summer will also be targeted, NSW Police have confirmed that they will be allocating as many as 100 officers in the search for drugs at some of the big parties coming up over the next couple of weeks. Don’t be surprised to find undercover cops on the dancefloor with you, and it’ll be commonplace to see sniffer dogs patrolling the streets outside some of our biggest parties.

Catherine Burn, Assistant Commissioner for the NSW Police, has defended the use of sniffer dogs to target drug users at Sydney events. “We will be having drug dogs at these events, there’s no doubt about that, and they are very, very effective,” she was quoted in the SMH. “The message is don’t take drugs because … all our police resources will be there.”

Want to know more about your rights when it comes to sniffer dogs? Check out the website of the NSW Council for Civil Liberties.
