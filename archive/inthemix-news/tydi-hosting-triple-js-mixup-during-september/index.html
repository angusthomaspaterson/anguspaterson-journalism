Tydi hosting triple j's Mixup during September

Angus Paterson · 7 September 2009 · inthemix

Sony inthemix50 #1 Tydi will be adding another notch to his belt this September, stepping up to host an hour-long mix every Saturday night for triple j’s long-running dance program Mixup. He’ll be on air between from 9pm, easing the listeners into the host of other guests to follow. Tydi commenced his residency on Saturday night, warming up for artists like Giuseppe Ottaviani, Joop! and Amber Savage, and you can check out a stream of the show HERE.

It’s been a rather busy and large couple of weeks for the Queensland golden boy, because in addition to taking home the coveted Sony inthemix50 #1 trophy for the second year in a row, he’s also nabbed his very first #1 on the Beatport charts for You Walk Away, also nabbing #2 for another rone of his tunes in the very same week.

Tydi is also the featured artist in ITM’s latest podcast! Check it out to find to find out what he’s been up to since his Sony inthemix50 win a few weeks ago.
