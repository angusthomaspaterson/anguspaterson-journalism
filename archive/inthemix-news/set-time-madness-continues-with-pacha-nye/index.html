Set time madness continues with Pacha NYE

Angus Paterson · 29 December 2006 · inthemix

As if punters didn’t have enough timetables to memorize already, the set times keep rolling in for this massive New Years weekend in Sydney. The organisers behind the Pacha event at Home are the latest to let us know what time its big-name international DJ guests will be hitting the decks.

Featuring a massive lineup of international house talent that includes Darren Emerson, Axwell, Tocadisco, Bodyrockers and Sarah Main, Pacha promises to bring the whole ‘Ibiza’ experience down to Sydney for one massive event. Boasting great views of the fireworks and Pacha’s notorious dancers, the event is also set to deliver the expected ‘visual extravaganza’ that you can only get from a superclub such as Home.

But all this info is going to offer little consolation to those who missed out on tickets – Pacha completely sold only a few days ago. Organisers reported a post-Christmas rush for tickets that left them reeling, so perhaps it was the rumor that Paris Hilton was going to be ‘performing’ at Pacha that convinced the last round of punters to make Darling Harbour their Home on New years Eve.
