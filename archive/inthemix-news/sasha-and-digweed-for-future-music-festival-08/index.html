Sasha and Digweed for Future Music Festival ’08?

Angus Paterson · 2 November 2007 · inthemix

While the prospect of dance music’s biggest duo Sasha and Digweed returning to Australia in the foreseeable future may seem like a pipedream, Future head honcho Mark James himself dropped a bomb in the Melbourne forums when he suggested that they would be back for Future Music Festival next year.

The thread in question was speculating on what artists would be seen at the next installment of Future’s annual Summadayze event. Mark James hopped on to intimate that there would be some big surprises in store for the party. When asked whether the ‘surprise’ for Summadayze would be Sasha and Digweed, Mark replied that it’d be “FMF for those boys.”

Whether the tour eventuates or not, it emphasises the fact that exciting news, or anything interesting to do with dance music in general, is often unmasked in the forums well before any form of official announcement is made.

Cause for excitement, or baseless rumor mongering? Check out the thread HERE for the evidence and decide for yourself.
