Gatecrasher returns to Australia

Angus Paterson · 4 September 2007 · inthemix

The mighty lion is back… Gatecrasher is one of the seminal global clubbing brands, but things have been quite quiet on that front as of late. Well that’s about to change when it hits Home in late October with an all-star lineup.

Sublime will be presenting Gatecrasher… The Return on Friday October 26th and roaring along for the ride will be resident Matt Hardwick along with head honcho Dave Lee, and they’ll also be joined by GTR and Ronald Van Geldren.

Originally created in 1993, the Gatecrasher brand rose to prominence late in the decade and to this day remains inextricably tied to the excitement and euphoria of trance music. With Sublime’s recent recommitment to a main-floor music policy focused solely on trance, what better place to welcome its return to Australia?

Gatecrasher returns to Home on Friday 26th October, tickets on sale from inthemix. First release are $39+BF while second release $49+BF.
