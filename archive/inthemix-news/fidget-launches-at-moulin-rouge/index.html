Fidget launches at Moulin Rouge

Angus Paterson · 22 May 2008 · inthemix

Australian MP3 dance music portal Zooloader is launching its own monthly night Fidget, to go down on the last Friday of the month at Kings Cross favourite Moulin Rouge. Just in time for the cilly winter season, the opening night next weekend on Friday 30th May will bring Grant Smillie, Jen Tutty, Ben Morris, Goodfella and Foundation along for the party.

Fidget launches at Moulin Rouge on Friday 30th May, and will keep rocking on for the last Friday of every month. Damage is $10 before 11pm and $15 after.
