Tiki Taane tours again in December

Angus Paterson · 2 October 2009 · inthemix

Tiki Taane has been at the forefront of the New Zealand music scene for well over a decade, his time as frontman for Salmonella Dub established him as a great songwriter and producer, and his Australian tour earlier this year was something that was rapturously received right across the country; and he’ll be back for more befroe thge year is out. It was his first extensive run of dates as a solo performer, in support of his Past, Present, Future, and this time he’ll be coming back in support of the latest single off the album Faded.

The reports from the first tour were nothing but overwhelmingly positive, so if you’re a fan of what Tiki does both as a solo artist as well as with Salmonella Dub, then you’d be well advised to get on down.

Tiki Taane ‘Faded’ tour dates:

Thursday 3rd – The Hi-Fi, Melbourne
Friday 4th – The Forum, Sydney
Saturday 5th – Homebake, Sydney
Thursday 10th – The Great Northern, Byron Bay
Friday 11th – Coolangatta Hotel, Gold Coast
Saturday 12th – The Hi-Fi, Brisbane
