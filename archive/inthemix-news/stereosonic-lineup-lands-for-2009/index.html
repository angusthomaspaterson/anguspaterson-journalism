Stereosonic lineup lands for 2009!

Angus Paterson · 6 August 2009 · inthemix

CHECK OUT OUR STEREOSONIC LINEUP SHOWCASE GALLERY!

The Stereosonic Festival tour will be back this November, with promoters Hardware and Onelove taking it to the five major cities for the very first time, and ITM now has the full details on who will be playing on the lineup across the country. Check out the full details below!

What stands out most about this year’s Stereosonic lineup is the ‘now’ factor. While it’s toplined by Deadmau5, a favourite with Australian audiences who’s arguably the most successful DJ/producer working in dance music today, alongside rock-solid names in house like Axwell, Fedde Le Grand and John Dahlback, the lineup is just as much defined by acts like Bloody Beetroots, The Crookers and Surkin who represent the new wave of electronica dominating the clubs in 2009.

Joined by a posse of underground acts that includes Miss Kittin & The Hacker, the DFA label’s Tim Sweeney, Umek and more, it’s a selection of artists that keeps the focus firmly on the latest and greatest in global club music.
