Sander Kleinenberg tours for Renaissance

Angus Paterson · 5 October 2007 · inthemix

The Dutch house don Sander Kleinenberg is back in a big way in 2007 with the release of his new This is… Sander Kleinenberg compilation for Renaissance, and he’ll be taking it across the country in early November for a series of dates with Future Entertainment.

This is… Sander Kleinenberg signals a return for the longstanding DJ/producer to the sexy underground house sounds in which he made his name earlier in the decade, and you can see him talking about it in an ITM feature HERE the also recently won the gong for the best Tech House/Progressive DJ at the 10th Anniversary edition of the recent Ibiza DJ Awards, so he’s certainly on his game at the moment.

Even better, he’ll be joined by producer extraordinaire Junkie XL at various gigs around the country! Melbourne punters in particular are in for a treat as the gig takes place on Cup Eve, which will no doubt make for a massive party atmosphere.

Catch him at the following dates around the country! And to get you excited you can listen to his recent ITM-FM mix HERE.

Friday Nov 2nd: Brisbane, Famous @ The Met (with Junkie XL & Mark James)
Saturday Nov 3rd: Sydney, Renaissance Special Event at Famous (with Junkie XL)
Sunday Nov 4th: Gold Coast, Renaissance Special Event @ Platinum Nightclub
Monday Nov 5th: Melbourne, Renaissance Special Event @ Queensbridge (with Junkie XL, Tom Stephan & more)
