Ferry Corsten lands in Australia, check out the first photos!

Angus Paterson · 11 September 2009 · inthemix

Dutch trance master Ferry Corsten landed in Australia this week courtesy of the Cream brand, rolling into Brisbane’s Family Nightclub on Thursday night for a special club set before he hits Sydney and Melbourne this weekend for the full-blown ‘Twice In a Blue Moon: The Experience’ shows. The photos are in from Thursday night’s show, check them out now on ITM!
