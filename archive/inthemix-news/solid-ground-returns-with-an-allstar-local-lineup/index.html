Solid Ground returns with an allstar local lineup

Angus Paterson · 18 April 2008 · inthemix

Coming to Sydney next Thursday night on Anzac Day eve, the crew from Niche Productions will be returning to present the third installment of their Solid Ground series, back to their old stomping ground at Ruby Rabbit on Oxford Street. While they’ve hosted some extraordinary international acts in the past, this time around the focus is on some of the extraordinary talent we’ve got in our own backyard, in the patriotic spirit of Anzac Day of course!

We’ll be seeing performances from Meem, Regal, Hermitude and Mickey Morphingaz; they’re all acts that either have fresh releases in the shops at the moment, or are about to unleash them at some stage in the not too distant future. And it you’re a fan of excellent underground music, you can’t go wrong.

Get on down to Solid Ground on Thursday 24th April at Ruby Rabbit, and sample some excellent tunes form some of Australia’s finest beat diggers…
