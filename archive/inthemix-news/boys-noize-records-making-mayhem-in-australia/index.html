Boys Noize Records making mayhem in Australia

Angus Paterson · 25 June 2009 · inthemix

Some of the miscreants from the notorious Boys Noize Records stable, who are under the musical wing of none other than Boys Noize AKA Alex Rihda himself, will be here to make mayhem in late August, with ‘Records Nights’ already locked in for Sydney and Melbourne. It’s not yet confirmed whether or not other states will be getting the full ‘Records Nights’ experience, but it’s still looking like the crew will be playing at least a few other shows across the country.

On the lineup front, the ‘miscreants’ in question are Shadowdancer, touring on the back of the Golden Traxe artist album, as well as Djedjotronic and Strip Steve. New York City’s Kill The Noise has also just been announced as a special guest for the Sydney show.

You can beat there will be noise. And lots of it. Enough to piss Laurent Garnier off probably, as there’s a reason that Boys Noize Records are known as the Ed Banger of Germany.

Boys Noize Records will be making mayhem in August, stay tuned for full national dates…

Fri August 28th – Roxnanne Parlour, Melbourne
Sat August 29th – Metro Theatre, Sydney
