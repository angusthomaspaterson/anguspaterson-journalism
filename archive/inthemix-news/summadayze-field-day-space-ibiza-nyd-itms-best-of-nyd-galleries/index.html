Summadayze, Field Day, Space Ibiza NYD - ITM's 'best of' NYD galleries!

Angus Paterson · 7 January 2010 · inthemix

Can you remember any other year when we were catered to so well party wise on New Years Day? Whether it was old favourites like Summadayze in Melbourne or Field Day in Sydney, or new entries to the season like Space Ibiza NYD or Adelaide’s very first Summadayze – you had more reason than ever in 2010 to pick yourself up, dust yourself down and just keep kicking on after your NYE festivities.

So did you harden up and get amongst it on New Years Day? ITM certainly did (we’re ‘ardcore like that), and we were at every single key event across the country. Whether you’re keen to relive the magic, or just see what you missed out on, check out the most spectacular party action that went down across Australia at the beginning of 2010…
