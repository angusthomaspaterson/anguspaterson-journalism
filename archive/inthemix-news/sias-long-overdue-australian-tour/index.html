Sia's long overdue Australian tour

Angus Paterson · 25 February 2009 · inthemix

Who would have predicted that Adelaide native Sia would become one of dance music’s most in-demand vocalists? She’s been remixed by artists like Tom Neville, Lifelike, Chris Lake and CSS, and received props from everyone from Katy Perry to Kanye West. And let’s not forget the particular fondness that’s developed for her among some of the world’s top tech-trance talent including Sander van Doorn, John O’Callaghan and in particular Future Music Festival headliner Markus Schulz, who sang her praises in a recent interview with ITM.

What’s even more surprising is that, with three albums already completed and her soulful, unmistakable voice turning heads right around the world, she’s yet to embark on her debut Australian tour. Well that’s about to change, with Sia playing dates across the East Coast during March to promote a limited edition two-CD release of her Some People Have Real Problems album.

Sia tour dates:

March 25th – The Metro, Sydney
March 27th – MTV Music Awards, Sydney
March 29th – Corner Hotel, Melbourne
March 31st – The Gov, Adelaide
