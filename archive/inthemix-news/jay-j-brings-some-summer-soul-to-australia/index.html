Jay-J brings some summer soul to Australia

Angus Paterson · 22 October 2007 · inthemix

Soulful house hero Jay-J will be back later in the year for a series of shows around the country. One of the leading names in the ‘San Francisco sound’ which he’s credited with helping create, he’s put out over 120 releases in the past 15 years and with his famed Moulton Studios has collaborated with the likes of Kaskade, Miguel Migs, Marques Wyatt, Halo, Julius Papp, Mark Grant, Chris Lum many more. There’s no question he remains one of the premier names in house music.

Catch Jay-J at the following dates…

Saturday November 24th: Industrie, Sydney Presented by SHE
Sunday November 25th: The Point, Melbourne
Saturday December 1st: Electric Circus, Adelaide
