Cox, Tenaglia & Sanchez for Shore Thing!

Angus Paterson · 9 October 2009 · inthemix

Just when you thought the end of the year couldn’t get any bigger, Fuzzy have announced the lineup for their annual New Years Eve bash on Bondi Beach, and it’s jampacked with superstar DJ talent. Yep, you heard right. It’s Carl Cox, Danny Tenaglia and Roger Sanchez, all on the one lineup.

It’s definitely the biggest lineup of DJs that Shore Thing has ever hosted. Carl Cox has become an honorary Aussie in the past few years and Roger Sanchez is also a favourite amongst local audiences, but with Danny Tenaglia it’s been over a decade since he toured the country. The scene is definitely set for 2010 to be welcomed in with euphoric style at Bondi this New Years Eve!

Shore Thing returns to Bondi Park on Thursday December 31st, tickets on sale Monday 26 October.
