Above & Beyond stage a sly dig at will.i.am's rip-off

Angus Paterson · 29 May 2013 · inthemix

In the continuation of what’s been one of the grittiest feuds we’ve seen in dance music in the past few months, over the weekend Above & Beyond slyly weighed in on the battle between EDM menace will.i.am and Anjunabeats favourites Mat Zo and Arty, over the former’s blatant copyright infringement.

And this time, instead of a carefully worded press release, label bosses Above & Beyond utilised those ever-useful text readouts on the screens during their recent set at Electric Daisy Carnival Chicago, where they headlined alongside Armin van Buuren, Eric Prydz, Fedde Le Grand, Hardwell and many more.

This time though, instead of getting text-happy by sending soppy messages of love to the audience, and urging them to hug the person next to them, they took the fight to will.i.am. Check out their tongue-in-cheek rebuttal below, delivered while playing Mat Zo and Arty’s 2011 progressive trance stormer Rebound. The message, “This is the new single from will.i.am” lingered on the screen, before the word ‘NOT’ drew a relieved cheer.

The fracas originally blew up back in April when will.i.am unveiled his latest collaboration with the fellow whipping boy Chris Brown, a chart-bound slice of heavily Autotuned EDM-lite called Let’s Go. The track blatantly lifted elements from Arty and Mat Zo’s rather memorable collaboration Rebound, and naturally, Twitter wars ensued.

Above & Beyond’s label Anjunabeats eventually weighed in the debate, via the aforementioned carefully worded press release. “Anjunabeats is the record label behind Arty and Mat Zo and owns the master recording rights to their collaboration Rebound. Mat Zo and Arty have been important members of the Anjunabeats family for a number of years and Rebound was one of the label’s most popular releases in 2011.

“As has been widely reported, a large section of Rebound was sampled on will.i.am’s track Let’s Go featuring Chris Brown and this took place without the permission of Anjunabeats or Arty & Mat Zo.”
