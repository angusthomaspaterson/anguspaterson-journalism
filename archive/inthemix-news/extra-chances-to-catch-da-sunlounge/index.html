Extra chances to catch Da Sunlounge

Angus Paterson · 27 February 2007 · inthemix

Deep house fans will be offered a bunch of additional chances to catch the UK DJ/producer outside of his performance at Sydney’s Playground Weekender festival. Fans can catch him at Electric Circus in Adelaide, Monkey Tennis in Sydney as well as a series of hush-hush gigs that have yet to be finalized.

Da Sunlounge, aka Simon Newby, began DJing in and around the Midlands deep house scene 9 years ago, with a residency at the seminal night Travelling Without Moving where he played alongside DJs like Terry Francis, Inland Knights and Andy Wetherall. He’s been producing deep house for over 5 years with releases on a wide range of excellent international label, with his own Myna Music racking up more than 20 releases and earning him much respect among his DJ colleagues.

Catch Da Sunlounge playing at the following dates:

3rd March: Adelaide, Electric Circus
10th March: Sydney , Playground Weekender, Del Rio Resort
16th March: Melbourne, details TBC
17th March: Sydney, Monkey Tennis
18th March: Sydney, secret gig
