Defqon.1 Festival set times + everything you need to know!

Angus Paterson · 17 September 2009 · inthemix

With the big day for Australia’s hard dance community only a matter of days away, the is Defqon.1 Festival reportedly on the verge of completely selling out – and for everybody who’s locked, loaded and ready to stomp, we’ve got the set times so you can start planning how many hard dance icons you’re going to squeeze into your day. Get ready people…

The team from Q-dance have also released all the info that you need to know about the event – how to get to the Sydney International Regatta Centre, public transport details, opening times and more.

To get the full rundown of who’s playing when and where, along with the venue map, transport details and essential event info head to inthemix’s Defqon.1 festival page. And while you’re at it, be sure to check out all the interviews, news, videos and DJ mixes!

> INTHEMIX’S DEFQON.1 FESTIVAL PAGE

The Defqon.1 Festival comes to the Sydney International Regatta Centre on Saturday September 19th. Limited tickets still on sale from tickets.q-dance.com.au.
