It’s a Mad Classic Fuss!

Angus Paterson · 28 August 2007 · inthemix

It’s an orgy of quality underground house music as the promoters behind Future Classic, Minimal Fuss and Mad Racket will be joining their efforts together to bring out some cutting-edge international talents over the coming long weekend. They’ll be collaborating with German cultural organisation the Goethe Institute to bring out two of Germany’s most innovative DJ/producer teams, as well as an emerging DJ talent from the US.

We’ll be enjoying performances from Âme, who have done excellent work for the Innervisions and Sonar Kollektiv labels and are responsible for club monsters like REJ. There will also be Berlin-based DJs Yannick Labbe and Daniel Becker, working together under the collective of Trickski who bring a wild mixture of Chicago and Detroit tunes and have been turning heads with their remixes of massive names like Gilles Peterson, Carl Craig, Peter Kruder and Jazzanova.

Also onboard will be Justin Martin, one of the brightest stars on Ben Watt’s Buzzin’ Fly stable, who has just delivered a new mix for the label called Chaos Restored. Add to this the solid local lineups across all of the crews and you’ve got the ingredients for one memorable party.

A Mad Classic Fuss takes place at The Metro on Sunday September 30th from 9pm til late, early bird tickets available soon on ITM for $30+BF. Check out the full lineup below!
