High Contrast rolls into Break Inn for some sideshow action

Angus Paterson · 10 January 2008 · inthemix

Drum n’ bass would surely have been pleased with the Good Vibrations lineup when they discovered that the UK’s High Contrast were on the bill. But now they’ve got another chance to catch him in a more intimate environment… That’s right, the man otherwise known as Lincoln Barrett will be rolling into Break Inn on Friday 8th February!

Part of the ever-popular Hospital Records crew, High Contrast has been a big name in drum n’ bass since the beginning of the decade when he first started dropping those massive tunes. Finding plenty of success with his artist albums True Colours and High Society, only last year he dropped the massive Tough Guys Don’t Dance that with singles like Everything’s Different, If We Ever crossover-tastic Kiss Kiss Bang Bang, represents his strongest chance yet of taking himself above and beyond the confines of the drum n’ bass scene to become a bonafide dance music household name.
