Pure Pleasure gets Balearic this weekend

Angus Paterson · 15 January 2008 · inthemix

Pure Pleasure Experience returns this Saturday and along with resident DJ Ange, it’ll also be hosting performances from Alex Ellenger and Marco Loco who hail from the sunny dance music isle. Funky house ahoy!

Alex Ellenger has spent the last decade DJing at some of the biggest Ibizan clubs including Space and Privilege, and as well as being a popular resident at Eden over the past few years he’s also got an extensive production history. Marco Loco on the other hand is a Glasgow-born producer, promoter and DJ who has been based in Ibiza for the past 4 years. Playing a versatile mix of house and electro, he’s also held residencies all over the world including the UK, US and Dubai.

Pure Pleasure Experience returns to Equilibrium this Saturday from 8:30pm, $15 at the door or $10 if you email for guestlist at info@purepleasure.com.au.
