Fuzzy and ITM bring you Afterlife

Angus Paterson · 16 August 2007 · inthemix

Is partying on all day at Parklife not gonna be enough? For those who like to kick on in style, you’re in luck because Fuzzy will be throwing a massive post-festival event at the Hordern Pavilion featuring many of Parklife’s biggest acts. Presented by inthemix!

Justice and Busy P will be going back to back with a DJ set, German indie/electro rockers Digitalism will be gracing the stage for a special DJ set while both MSTRKRFT and The Sounds will return for another post-festival performance behind the decks.

Billed as the definitive after party, Afterlife will be held at the Hordern Pavilion which is just only right across the road from Parklife, and it’ll be the only post-event where the festival’s international guests will be playing. Keeping things nice and easy, there’ll be no public transport required and Fuzzy promise that you’ll be let in wearing your messy festival clothes! Can’t go wrong there.

Afterlife, brought to you by Fuzzy and inthemix, takes place at the Hordern Pavilion on Sunday October 1st from 10pm to 4am. Tickets are $38+BF and are on sale from inthemix from midday Thursday 23rd August.
