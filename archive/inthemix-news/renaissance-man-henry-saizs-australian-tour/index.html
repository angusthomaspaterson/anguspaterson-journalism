Renaissance man Henry Saiz's Australian tour

Angus Paterson · 6 May 2009 · inthemix

Henry Saiz will be touring Australia for the first time during the cold winter months of July and August, but it’s already looking like he’ll be joining the new wave of ‘progressive’ stars in the vein of guns like Guy J who are also blazing into new territories of fusing melodic progressive sounds with the intricate rhythms of techno. With the Spanish DJ/producer’s Artificial Paradises EP out now on Renaissance, he’s also earnt the ear of the likes of John Digweed with releases on the Bedrock label. You can check out what the Renaissance man has to offer in late July…

Henry Saiz tour dates:

Fri July 24 – Melbourne – Brown Alley
Sat July 25 – Sydney – Chinese Laundry
Fri July 31 – Perth – Ambar
Sat Aug 1 – Brisbane – Family
