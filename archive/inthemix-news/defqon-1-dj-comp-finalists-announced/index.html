Defqon.1 DJ comp finalists announced

Angus Paterson · 20 August 2009 · inthemix

The hard dance community is still going freakin’ nuts over the opportunity for five lucky finalists to play the Defqon.1 Festival in Sydney, with the final winner flown to Holland for the main event in 2010, and things weren’t helped at all earlier this week when ITM posted 50+ Defqon.1 DJ Comp mixes online, allowing listeners to vote on their favourite sets. Talk about a hardstyle heart attack! Well, we’re keeping the bass-driven coronary palpations coming with the announcement of the winners who will be playing at the Brisbane, Melbourne and Sydney events over the next few weeks…

All the winners will get the chance to compete at the state heats, where hard dance icon Brennan Heart will be present to judge as well as perform. Check out the winners below…
