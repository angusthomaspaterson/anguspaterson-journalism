Chat live with Armin Van Buuren tonight!

Angus Paterson · 29 May 2007 · inthemix

It’s little more than a week until the massive White Party tour kicks off, and tonight at 6pm EST you’ll have the chance to talk to the man himself Armin Van Buuren as he prepares to tour Australia over the June Long Weekend.

When it comes to global DJing superstars they don’t get much bigger than Armin Van Buuren. He recently celebrated a decade in dance music with the release of his 10 Years compilation, and he’s come a long way since DJs started canning his classic single Blue Fear in 1997. But his stock has risen even further in the past few years which have seen him ascend to the #2 spot on DJ Mag poll, just behind Paul Van Dyk’s and pushing aside fellow Dutchman Tiesto. With his famed A State of Trance radio show and compilation series, it looks like Armin is here to stay.

ITM is hosting Armin tonight for ‘in the spotlight’, where you’ll get the chance to ask him all your burning questions. Everyone who logs in will have the chance to win some tasty Armin goodies, so don’t forget to head to inthemix.com.au/chat/inthespotlight tonight 6pm EST to participate.
