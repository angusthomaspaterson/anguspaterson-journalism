Celebrate the silly season with inthemix – for free!

Angus Paterson · 9 November 2007 · inthemix

Yep, it’s that time of year again – Santa is packing his sleigh, we’re procrastinating about buying presents and we’re all getting ready to spend quality time (and argue) with our family. Christmas is drawing near! And inthemix will be helping you get in the mood right around the nation. As if we needed any excuse, but it’ll be the perfect chance to get boozed up, get all rowdy to some excellent and chew the fat with all the excellent members of the ITM community. Life couldn’t be sweeter…

A national series of free parties presented by Smirnoff, the lineups will be hand-picked by the promoters in each state and if you RSVP, it’s completely for free! All you need to do is be an inthemix member, except for the Sydney party where you don’t even need to do that to RSVP. Yep, we spoil you all rotten don’t we?
