Boundary Sounds takes up residence at Kings Cross Hotel

Angus Paterson · 29 July 2008 · inthemix

With the refurbishments finally complete, the crazy kids behind Purple Sneakers are coming in to take over the refreshed Kings Cross Hotel and the two levels of its enclosed Copperlilly club on a Saturday night. Brought to you by Boundary Sounds, the new night Floorspace will be bringing an indie-rock sound to the lower level and the main room party jams upstairs, with resident DJs Johnny Rad, PhDJ, Steve Lind and Magic Happens providing the tunes.

Expect everything form hip hop, electro, Baltimore, indie, disco, punk funk and everything else in between. And it all kicks off mid Augusts!

Floorspace takes over Kings Cross Hotel every Saturday from August 16th, $15 on the door.
