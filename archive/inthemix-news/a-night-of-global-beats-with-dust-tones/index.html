A night of global beats with Dust Tones

Angus Paterson · 27 June 2007 · inthemix

Sydney crew Dust Tones are taking it global at their next party in early July, exploring sounds from around the world with performances from Ben Walsh from The Bird, rhythm masters Percussion Junction and other tasty treats.

One World Music head honcho Leigh Wood will also be clocking in, DJ Jonah from SBS Alchemy Radio will be teaming up with Altafari and Trevor Parkee from 2ser’s Departure Lounge and naturally, resident Bentley will be performing so it should make a fantastic diversion from what’s usually on offer in Sydney clubbing.

Get on down to The Clare Hotel, Broadway on Saturday 7th July from 9pm, $10 on the door.
