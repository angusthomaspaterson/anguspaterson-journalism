Big Day Out lands in Sydney, check out the photos

Angus Paterson · 25 January 2010 · inthemix

The Big Day Out returned to its heartland of Sydney over the weekend, with two massive sold out (not to mention sweltering) days of awesome performances across the musical spectrum. While the likes of Mars Volta, Powderfinger and Muse held things down at the main stage, there was something special going on this year in the Boiler Room with the likes of Calvin Harris, Sasha, Groove Armada and more. Check out the photos now on ITM!

> Big Day Out, Sydney

We’re only partway through the tour though, as there’s still Melbourne, Adelaide and Perth to go as well as an assortment of sideshows. Make sure you stay tuned into ITM for all your essential Big Day Out coverage!
