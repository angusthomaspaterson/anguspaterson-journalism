Central Station rises from the ashes

Angus Paterson · 4 March 2009 · inthemix

Were you one of the many who shed a tear when Central Station Records was pulled under by the financial misadventures of its parent company Destra late last year? It was a sad day for dance music fans as the label had been responsible for so many quality releases, both locally produced and imported from overseas. It was a rather bleak indicator for what was already a shrinking market. But never fear, because the seminal force in Australian dance that is Central Station is back.

The label has been reborn as part of a joint venture between the original brains of the operation, along with distributor Universal Music Australia and that other great bastion of local dance, Ministry of Sound Australia. Under the new agreement, Universal will manage artist and singles projects, while compilations, marketing and promotions will be handled by the Central Station Records team, including founding members Giuseppe Palumbo and Morgan Williams, as well as general manager Jamie Raeburn. How’s that for an impressive turn about in face?

Keep an eye out for Central Station’s return to your record shelves in 2009.
