X-Qlusive kicks off in Sydney - check out the first photos!

Angus Paterson · 20 December 2008 · inthemix

While the wider dance scene might be gearing up for the debut of Sensation in Australia at the end of the year, fans of the harder styles have something of their own to celebrate this weekend. The revered Q-Dance brand has returned to our shores with the X-Qlusive tour, and on Friday night it kicked off in Sydney. Check out the very first photos here!

> Check out the X-Qlusive Sydney photos.

Acting as a ‘master of ceremonies’ for the party were hardstyle masters Showtek, who played several sets across the evening as well as back-to-back with much of the remaining lineup, which included Zany, Donkey Rollers, Dutch Master as well as South Stylers, who performed live in Australia for the very first time. The sold-out crowd was a testament to the continuing strength of the hard dance scene in Australia, and the production stepped it up even in comparison to Q-Dance’s spectacular debut in Easter – lights, lasers, massive visual screens. All hail to the Dutch!

The X-Qlusive tour wraps up in Melbourne tonight. Stay tuned to ITM as there’s more post-event coverage to come, and check out inthemix’s festival page here for interviews, news, mixes and more…
