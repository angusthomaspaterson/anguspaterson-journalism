Pendulum sideshow shifts to Corner Hotel

Angus Paterson · 13 January 2009 · inthemix

When the Australian kings of live drum n’ bass return to Melbourne later this month to play the mainstage of the Big Day Out, they’ll also be playing a sideshow for their fans. News just in is that it’s been shifted from its original home of The Forum to the Corner Hotel, but otherwise it’s business as usual as all existing tickets purchased will be valid for the relocated performance.

In 2008, Pendulum delivered a hard rockin’ punch with their sophomore album In Silico that saw them transcending their status as a mere ‘drum n’ bass’ act, and taking their sound out to a much wider audience. Their sound now has just as much in common with heavy metal as it does DnB, but nonetheless, their live percussion represents the most fully realised (and ferocious) conversion of synthetic dance beats to the concert arena that we’ve ever heard.

Check out the ITM interview for more info, and catch Pendulum when they play the Corner Hotel on Wednesday 28th January.
