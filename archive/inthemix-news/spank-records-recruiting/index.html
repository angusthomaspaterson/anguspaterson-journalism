Spank Records recruiting

Angus Paterson · 31 July 2007 · inthemix

Spank Records, one of Sydney’s best sources of electronic music, production and DJ equipment is looking to take on another full-time staff member. They’re currently advertising a full-time position available for someone with sales experience in either the DJ industry or one that’s closely related to it.

Specifically, the Spank Records crew are looking for someone with a thorough understanding and experience with a range of DJ equipment and music production on both the software and hardware side. You’ll need to be able to clearly explain related technical concepts at both a very basic level as well as to experienced DJs and producers. If you spend way too much time on music/DJ/software/hardware websites, forums and blogs, then they definitely want to hear from you!

Sound like your thang? Email resumes to leanne@spankrecords.com.au.
