Armin Only on sale this week: get the details here!

Angus Paterson · 4 April 2008 · inthemix

The festival silly season may be coming to an end, but it’s already time to start getting excited about the wintertime events. Holland’s Armin van Buuren rose to seize the #1 spot in the DJ Mag Top 100 poll last year, and this year he’ll be cementing his strong relationship with his Australian audience with an event that’s promising to be just a little bit special… And tickets will be on sale on ITM this coming Wednesday morning at 9am.

Once a year, the ‘Armin Only’ party is thrown in the DJ/producer’s home country of the Netherlands, to crowds of more than 10,000 people. And it’s more than just an extended DJ set: it’s a full-blown arena spectacular with unrivalled production, a bunch of special guests and live performers, as well as a whole bunch of other special touches that never fail to surprise and delight. And this year he’ll be bringing the full ‘Armin Only’ experience to both Sydney and Melbourne, with the events to both be filmed and featured on a DVD to be released at the end of the year.

Last year’s White Party tour was spectacular enough, but you can be sure that things will be ramped up significantly this time around. At this stage it’s all being kept under wraps as to what we can expect, but ITM will be keeping its ear to the ground to bring you news on any big surprises the ‘Armin Only’ tour might possibly bring…

Tickets on sale this Wednesday 9th April at 9am through ITM and Ticketek, with earlybirds priced at $69+BF while second and third release will cost $79+BF and $99+BF respectively…

Friday 6th June – ‘Armin Only’: Hordern Pavillion, Sydney
Sunday 8th June – ‘Armin Only’: Melbourne Park, Melbourne
