Chris Carter rolls back into town

Angus Paterson · 4 July 2007 · inthemix

Nu-school breaks head Chris Carter is heading back to Australia to unleash some fresh tunes on his fans across the country, check him out at the dates listed below.

With a career in DJing, production and playing music, Chris Carter learnt his engineering skills at the ON U SOUND working with the likes of legends like Adrian Sherwood, Asian Dub Foundation and Bim Sherman. Since kicking off his production career in 1997 he’s released a steady stream of singles on a number of breaks labels including Rennie Pilgrim’s TCR, Botchit + Scarper, Forged and his own Record Records label among others.

To give you an idea of his breadth of musicality, he’s also a member of Rennie Pilgrem’s TCR All Stars live act in which he plays guitar. He’s also just completed a collaboration with Aussie breaks hero Dopamine which you’ll sure to be hearing when he hits our clubs again.

Check out the Chris Carter experience at the following dates…

Friday 6 July – Halo, Hobart
Saturday 7 July – Empire Hotel, Brisbane
Friday 13 July – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Saturday 14 July – Baja, Coolangatta
Friday 20 July – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Saturday 21 July – Brown Alley, Melbourne (Day party)
Saturday 21 July – Maxx Nightclub, Joondaup WA
Thursday 26 July – Como Hotel, Perth
