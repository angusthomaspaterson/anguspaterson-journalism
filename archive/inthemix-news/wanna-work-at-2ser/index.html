Wanna work at 2ser?

Angus Paterson · 30 January 2009 · inthemix

One of Sydney’s most enduring and respected community radio stations, none other than 2ser, is looking for a sales representative. They’ll be accountable for securing sponsorship for the station, its programs and related promotional activities and events. The role will also mean you’re responsible for servicing clients, coordinating external sales agents, achieving budgets and maintaining good administrative practices. Sound like your thing?

Wanna know more? Email Kurt at kurt.eckardt@uts.edu.au for more info on this tasty looking role.
