Hybrid national dates confirmed

Angus Paterson · 25 June 2009 · inthemix

Further to Monday’s news that Hybrid would be back for a club tour in July (read about it here), news has just reached ITM on the full national dates for the tour. They’ll be playing several dates along with melodic techno guru Henry Saiz, as well as making a return to the venue that’s hosted some of the duo’s most legendary Australian gigs, in the form of Lot 33 in Canberra.

As mentioned on Monday, things are kicking along well for their next album which is due for release in October, and we can expect to hear some snippets on this tour. “We’ve currently got about 33 individual tracks in varying states of completion, some have vocals, some have orchestration. Most have furiously heavy basslines and to us, feel more vibrant and alive than any of our previous work.”

Hybrid tour dates:

Fri July 24th – Brown Alley, Melbourne (with Henry Saiz)
Sat July 25th – Chinese Laundry, Sydney (with Henry Saiz)
Sat August 1st – Family, Brisbane (with Henry Saiz)
Fri August 7th – Ambar, Perth
Sat August 8th – Lot 33, Canberra
