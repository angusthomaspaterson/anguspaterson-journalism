Set times for Andrew Weatherall

Angus Paterson · 11 January 2008 · inthemix

Legendary DJ, producer and remixer Andrew Weatherall will be playing his first ever Sydney show this Friday night at Toff In Town, check below for set times. If you’re locked and loaded for this weekend’s gig, you’ll finally be given the chance to discover how he’s neatly managed to straddle both rock music and straight-up techno all these years. Brought to you by Civil Society and presented by inthemix, he’ll be putting his years of experience to work and putting the funk back into tech house…

Andrew Weatherall hits Toff In Town tonight, you can check out the recent ITM interview HERE and set times are below…

Band Room
10.00-10.45: Mike Callender
10.45-11.30: Damon Walsh
11.30-12.15: T-Rek
12.15-4.15: Andrew Weatherall
4.15-5.00: Boogs

Side Room
