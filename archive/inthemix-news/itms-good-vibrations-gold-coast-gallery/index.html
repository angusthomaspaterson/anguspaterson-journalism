ITM's Good Vibrations Gold Coast gallery

Angus Paterson · 23 February 2009 · inthemix

It was weekend #2 of the Good Vibrations across Australia, and on Saturday the Gold Coast had its turn to enjoy a sellout show, and – similar to Sydney – make the most of things in spite of poor weather conditions. Check out the photos, now on inthemix!

> Good Vibrations Gold Coast gallery.

Similar to the Sydney show the previous weekend, it was gumboots, ponchos and mudslides on Saturday for the Gold Coast leg of the Good Vibrations tour, with rain setting in for most of the day. From all reports, though, this wasn’t going to get in the way of an up-for-it crowd taking the day for all that it was worth.

Standout performances were similar to the previous weekend; Deadmau5 shook the foundations of the festival grounds to their very core, The Pharcyde again won over audiences at he Roots Tent, while headliner Fatboy Slim defied the controversy of his first Australian show with a performance that, according to the ITM forums, “had the crowd eating out of his hands.”
