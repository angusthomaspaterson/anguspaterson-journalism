Nick Littlemore not coming home for ARIAs

Angus Paterson · 25 October 2009 · inthemix

You’d think that with Empire of the Sun up for a massive six nominations in this year’s ARIA Awards, and with the prospect of them staging a similar ceremony washout that The Presets managed last year, that Nick Littlemore and Luke Steele would put aside any differences they might have and unite on stage to collect their awards. But surprisingly, it’s not the case as it was revealed over the weekend that Littlemore is far too busy working on the new Pnau record with Peter Mayes to consider a return to Australia before the end of they year.

Pnau’s manager Clive Banks (who added the band to his roster with a little bit of encouragement from his other major client, none other than Sir Elton John) issued a statement to Sydney’s Daily Telegraph over the weekend, clarifying that band is simply too busy writing and recording their new album in far flung locations like Atlanta, New York and London.

“Just to set the record straight and avoid confusion, while PNAU members Peter Mayes and Nick Littlemore are extremely honoured to have been among the many nominations for the ARIA Awards, the PNAU creative world work schedule has been set and will continue to prohibit a return to Australia until the new year,” Banks said.

Littlemore or no Littlemore, it’s not going to take the shine off what’s set to be a massive summer for Empire of the Sun. In addition to several appearances at regional festivals in the leadup to New Years, they’ll be playing alongside the likes of Prodigy and Booka Shade on the Future Music Festival tour early next year.
