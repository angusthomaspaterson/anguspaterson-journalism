Eddie Halliwell will be ripping it at Summadayze

Angus Paterson · 29 July 2009 · inthemix

Eddie Halliwell is one of dance music’s tightest performers, a true showman who displays a massive amount of technical talent behind the decks – and as it turns out, we won’t have to wait too much longer to witness him in the flesh again. ITM’s rentboyesq had Eddie on the phone last night to chat about his new Cream Ibiza CD, where the Manchester heavyweight revealed he’ll be in Australia over the New Year period for the Summadayze round of festivals.

“Yeah, I’m going to be out there for the New Year period doing the Summadayze festival rounds – Melbourne, Perth, Gold Coast,” Eddie told ITM. “Yeah, definitely looking forward to it – always look forward to coming to Australia, it’s great.”

Reckon the rest of Australia might see a show or two from Eddie squeezed in, as well as a few sneaky club sideshows along the way? Only time will tell.

Cream Ibiza, mixed by Eddie Halliwell and Sander Van Doorn, is out now through Stomp. Stay tuned for our full interview with Eddie shortly, and keep your eyes glued top ITM for more New Year news over the coming months!
