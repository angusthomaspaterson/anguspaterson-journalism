UPDATED: V Festival official sideshow dates confirmed

Angus Paterson · 20 February 2008 · inthemix

Following yesterday’s unconfirmed information that showed its face on the Planet Rock website regarding sideshows for V Festival heavyweights The Smashing Pumpkins, Duran Duran, Queens Of The Stone Age and Jesus & Mary Chain, ITM now has the official word from the good people at Coppel and we can confirm the official dates for all of the shows.

And for lovers of the more electronic-centred acts that are appearing on the bill, you’re in luck because it’s been announced that both Air and Roisin Murphy will be doing sideshows. Check out the news HERE.

All the acts will be appearing at the V Festival, but are you keen for some sideshow action? Check below for the shows that have just been announced…

Smashing Pumpkins
Sydney, Hordern Pavilion – March 27
Brisbane, Convention Centre – March 31
Melbourne, Festival Hall – April 4

Duran Duran
Sydney, Entertainment Centre – March 28
Melbourne, Palais Theatre – April 3

Queens Of The Stone Age
Brisbane, The Arena – March 27
Sydney, Big Top Luna Park – March 28
Melbourne, Palace Theatre – April 1

Jesus & Mary Chain
Brisbane, The Arena – April 1
Sydney, Enmore Theatre – April 2
Melbourne, Palace Theatre – April 3
