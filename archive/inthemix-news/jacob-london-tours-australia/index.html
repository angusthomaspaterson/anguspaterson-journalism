Jacob London tours Australia

Angus Paterson · 7 June 2007 · inthemix

Need a healthy does of jackin’ house this June? US house act Jacob London will be playing around the country, check the dates below.

With a production resume dating back to the early 90’s, Bob Hansen and Dave Pezzner are veterans of Seattle’s tech-house scene. They formed Jacob London in 1997 as an outlet for their more soulful techno-house hybrids. A few years later they joined forces with up-and-coming deep house label Viva! to create an outlet for their sound, and hence squid:records was born. They’ve been responsible for a number of house hits over the years and you’ll have the chance to check out their sound when they tour around the country.

Catch Jacob London at the following dates…

Saturday 9th June: Monkey Tennis @ Candy’s Apartment, Sydney
Sunday 10th June: HoolaHoop @ Candy Bar, Melbourne
Friday 15th June: TBA
Saturday 16th – Shnoz @ Temple Nightclub, Gold Coast
