Luke Fair carves it up for Balance 011 tour

Angus Paterson · 9 March 2007 · inthemix

Funky progressive don Luke Fair will be returning to Australia for a national tour as his contribution to the ever-popular Balance CD series is about to hit the stores. Out soon on EQ Recordings, Balance 011 promises a diverse, funky and tripped-out journey that featuring tracks and remixes from Charles Webster, Humate, Timewriter, Francois K, Ian Pooley, Tom Middleton and more.

Luke Fair has carved quite a reputation for himself as a producer and DJ oiver the past few years, releasing mix compilations on labels like Yoshitoshi and Bedrock with music that can be described as funky, housey and techy all at the same time. Renowned for playing his own re-edits of tracks in his sets, in the studio, in the studio his productions run the gamut from dubby house to melodic progressive.

This will be Luke Fair’s third tour of Australia, having won over the punters after performances at Sweetchilli, We Love.. Sounds, Gods Kitchen, The Loft, Moonbar and many more.

Check below for date and venue details on Luke Fair’s impending tour of Australia…

Saturday 14th April – Eclipse Touring, Crown & Sceptre, Adelaide
Friday 20th April – Uber, Brisbane
Saturday 21st April – Platinum, Gold Coast
Tuesday 24th April (Anzac Eve) – Cassette, The Prince, Melbourne
