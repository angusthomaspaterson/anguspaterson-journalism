Rooftop Party hits the Eastern this Sunday

Angus Paterson · 4 April 2007 · inthemix

The Easter weekend will be your last chance this party season to enjoy the sunshine and big tunes at The Eastern’s beautiful rooftop bar. Rooftop Party won’t be back until the sunny weather returns in spring, and to make up for it they’ll be throwing everything into it to make it the biggest so far. Residents The Heat will be joined by Sneaky Sound System, Ajax, Bag Raiders* and Sam La More from Tonite Only.

The Rooftop Party series of events kicked off when Sydney electro outfit The Heat decided to celebrate a successful year with a rooftop Xmas party, decking The Eastern’s rooftop bar out with a big sound system & stage. Since then it’s become a fixture of the Sydney summer party calendar, with an impressive range of Australia’s house and electro acts invited to rock the roof out on a monthly basis. The events are definitely remembered by the regular punters for sporting a kickin’ party vibe.

The last Rooftop Party for the season hits The Eastern this Sunday, check below for set times.

2-3: Jonny Pow
3-4: Dusty Sparks
4-5: Disorder (Starfu*kers)
5-6: Black Angus & MC Double D (Sneaky Sound System)
6-7: Caleb & J-Flex
7-8: The Heat (live 3 piece)
8-9: Bag Raiders
9-10: Ajax
10-11: Sam La More (Tonite Only)
