Musik Matters taking over Favela

Angus Paterson · 27 July 2009 · inthemix

After the winter Hed Kandi parties and a number of excellent one-off events that included a Wednesday night appearance from London Electricity, the next installment of Favela’s weekly residencies will be helmed by international guest DJ Matt Cahill. The Kings Cross hotspot will host Musik Matters from Saturday 1st August, with Cahill bringing a ‘back to basics’ approach along with a solid line-up of locals DJs and the odd international guest.

With house music the focus, Musik Matters is pitching itself as a ‘mature’ night that is aiming to bring back the passionate era of house music, with the DJs not just selecting the music but also being part of the night and joining in with the vibe of the party. We’ll be hearing quality house in the upstairs club, with a mix of funk, hip hop, breaks and nu-disco downstairs.

Musik Matters featuring Matt Cahill launches at Favela from Saturday August 1st.
