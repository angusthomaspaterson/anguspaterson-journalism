Fat Freddy’s Drop swing back to Australia

Angus Paterson · 6 September 2007 · inthemix

Fat Freddy’s Drop are one of the brightest stars in the current crop of New Zealand acts making waves at the moment, and with a new album on the horizon they’ll be back in Australia in November.

Known for their high-tech dub sound, the upcoming gigs will come at the end of what has been an amazing year for the band who took to the stage at Glastonbury Festival in June, as well as a number of other killer shows as they took their live show around the world. Their last shows in Australia were at sunny summer festivals like Womadelaide, Playground Weekender and East Coast Blues & Roots Festival while back home, their B.O.A.T.S album continues to do big business.

Tickets on sale Tuesday 11th September, catch em at the following venues!

Fri 2nd Nov: Melbourne Metro
Sat 3rd Nov: Sydney Enmore Theatre
Sun 4th Nov: Brisbane Tivoli
