The Likes of You shifts to The Metro

Angus Paterson · 5 May 2009 · inthemix

Future Entertainment’s massive debut of its The Likes of You brand has been shifted from its original choice of The Manning Bar at Sydney Uni, to the dependable party option that is The Metro. The new venue equals extended sets from the international guests, as well as trading hours of the event extended to 6am.

The Likes of You has already been building up a solid reputation in Melbourne over the last 12 months, hosting marquee names in techno and progressive like Stephan Bodzin, Carl Craig, Richie Hawtin among others. The brand’s debut in Sydney is set to be a spectacular one: not only will it feature South American progressive god Hernan Cattaneo and the debut performance of minimal heavyweight Loco Dice, it’ll also be rounded out with appearances by Jesse Rose, Oliver $, King Roc as well as a slew of quality local supports.

The Likes of You hits The Metro on Saturday 16th May. All tickets purchased remain valid for the new venue, and they’re still on sale through ITM. Check out the revised set times below…
