Chris Duckenfield plays Mad Racket this Sunday

Angus Paterson · 2 November 2007 · inthemix

Locked in for another one of their ‘Day Roll’ events, Chris Duckenfield will be playing a return performance at Marrickville Bowling Club this Sunday afternoon. Kicking off at midday and continuing to sunset, it’ll be the first Day Roll of the season and you’ll be able to have a drink, a sausage and a bowl.

Get on down to Marrickville Marrickville Bowling Club this Sunday, kids are welcome and it’s free entry!
