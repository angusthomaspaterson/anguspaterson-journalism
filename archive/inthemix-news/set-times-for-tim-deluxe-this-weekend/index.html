Set times for Tim Deluxe this weekend

Angus Paterson · 30 November 2007 · inthemix

So did you miss Tim Deluxe at Harbourlife last weekend? Well from all reports he smashed it, and he’ll be playing a special encore set when he lands at Sounds on Sunday at The Greenwood this weekend. Check below for set times!

A regular on Darren Emerson’s Underwater label, Deluxe is known the world over for his funky house anthem It Just Won’t Do, which was featured on his Little Ginger Club Kid album, and he’s also done memorable remixes for the likes of White Stripes and Blur. He’s got a new album due out called Ego Death, which shows the more fidgety (but fun) tech/electro direction he’s been heading in as of late: you may have also heard his recent anthem Let The Beats Roll.

At Sounds on Sunday he’ll be supported by Ajax, Tim Sea, Kato, Ember, so make sure you come and check out what everyone was talking about after Harbourlife last weekend.

Tim Deluxe hits The Greenwood this Sunday 2nd December, tickets are available now from ITM. Set times are below ,and check out the clip for Let The Beats Roll while you’re at it…
