Sasha plays Sydney's Chinese Laundry in November

Angus Paterson · 20 July 2009 · inthemix

One of dance music’s most enigmatic figures Sasha will be back in Australia in November for a tour that will see him playing the kind of spaces he’s arguably best suited for – our nightclubs. And the first of these shows to be announced is a Saturday night gig in late November at the Chinese Laundry in Sydney, that will see the Global Underground veteran taking control for an extended set in the open-air settings of the venue’s Courtyard. It’s shaping up to be the kind of stuff that legends are made of.

Where to begin with Sasha? DJing in the UK since the ‘summer of love’ and leading the charge when progressive house was at its most epic and melodic back in the late 90s, he’s since shrugged off any genre restrictions and remained on the bleeding edge of electronic music, as well as being one of the first high-profile DJ/producers to adopt Ableton into his live sets. As a producer his output has been scarce at times, but it always carries an impact when it eventually makes it to the public. Last year’s Invol2ver was enough of a masterpiece to snare the #2 spot in ITM’s Top 20 Releases of 2008!

For Sydney-siders who want to secure their place in the countryard, pre-sale tickets are available on ITM from 9am on Thursday July 23rd to Chinese Laundry database members for $64.95 +BF, with general-release tickets on sale from 9am on Thursday July 30th for $74.95 +BF. In additon, Finland’s Rico Tubbs will also be returning to in with the the party after his tour with Good Vibrations earlier this year, as well as the tantalising promise of another surprise international special guest…

Sasha plays Chinese Laundry, Sydney on Saturday 21st November. Stay tuned to ITM this week for further details where else we’ll be seeing Sasha setting up his Ableton-enabled laptop around the country during November.
