Renaissance clocks up its 15th Anniversary

Angus Paterson · 13 April 2007 · inthemix

The veteran house/progressive brand is popping the champagne corks as it reaches yet another milestone, and of course they’re not going to be letting they’re 15th anniversary pass without a massive party. Renaissance will be celebrating with a special Wild in the Country party with some of its biggest DJ names.

Dubbed as an ‘original boutique festival’, Wild in the Country is set for Saturday 30th June and will take place against the backdrop of Hertfordshire’s Knebworth House. There will be three main areas hosting some of the biggest names in electronic music, with Sasha and Digweed at the top of the bill – two names that will forever be associated with the brand.

Launched in 1992 as a weekly club night in the Midlands, Renaissance has since grown into one of the biggest and most enduring electronic music brands. While Wild in the Country will be a celebration of this history, it will also emphasise some of the new names that are making waves on the dance scene at the moment.

Things kick off in the country on Saturday 30th June, take a look at www.renaissance.com for more details and peek below for the lineup…
