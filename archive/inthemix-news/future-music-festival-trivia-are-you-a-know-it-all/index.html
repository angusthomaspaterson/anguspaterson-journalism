Future Music Festival trivia; are you a know it all?

Angus Paterson · 25 February 2009 · inthemix

For dance fans who love quality music it doesn’t get much better than the Future Music Festival. Kicking off in Sydney and Perth this weekend, it features some of the world’s best live acts and DJs, including superstar veterans like Basement Jaxx, Richie Hawtin and Paul Oakenfold, the Swedish House Mafia’s Steve Angello and Sebastian Ingrosso, and trance identity Markus Schulz.

Do you think you know everything there is to know about the 2009 Future Music Festival lineup? We’re sure there’s at least a few new facts you could learn, and we’ve put together a special Future Music Festival Trivia feature to help in the education process. Which DJ/producer’s big break came through remixing folk singer Jewel? Whose father had links to organised crime in Greece? And which dance music trailblazer got his start in Harlem’s hip hop scene in the 1980s? It may not be who you think…
