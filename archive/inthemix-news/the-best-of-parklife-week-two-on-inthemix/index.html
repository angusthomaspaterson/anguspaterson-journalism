The best of Parklife 'week two' on inthemix

Angus Paterson · 7 October 2009 · inthemix

While the first week of the Parklife tour was characterised by sunny skies and sweltering heat, the dark clouds came out late last week, leading to fears we’d be subjected to a fully-fledged festival washout. But while it might not have been the most perfect festival weather, the rain held out over the weekend and the punters refused to let it dampen their spirits, leading to another long weekend of fun across Melbourne, Adelaide and the festival’s original home of Sydney.

Again, inthemix’s team photographers and reviewers were there to capture the finest moments of the tour as we welcomed in the summer festival season. We’ve compiled some of the finest moments in our ‘best of week two’ Snap galleries below…
