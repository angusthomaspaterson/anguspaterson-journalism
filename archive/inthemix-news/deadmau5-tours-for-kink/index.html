Deadmau5 tours for Kink

Angus Paterson · 24 July 2007 · inthemix

A massive name in electro/progressive production, Deadmau5 will be swooping down on Australia for a series of tour dates courtesy of the crew from Kink. Hailing from Canada, tracks from him like Faxing Berlin, Jaded and Vanishing Point have been played by the likes of Pete Tong, Chris Lake, Sebastien Leger, Nic Fanciulli and many more and is now responsible for the record label Xfer Records.

Catch Deadmau5 at the following dates and have a squiz at the video below to see what you’re in store for…

7th September: Kink, Gold Coast
8th September: Kink, Brisbane
14th September: Kink, Melbourne
15th September: Kink, Sydney
16th September: Smut, Sydney
