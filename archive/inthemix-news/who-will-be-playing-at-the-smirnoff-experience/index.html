Who will be playing at the Smirnoff Experience?

Angus Paterson · 19 June 2008 · inthemix

The Smirnoff Experience returns to Australia in July, with a tour that takes in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane. The hype has been building with people doing whatever they can to get their hands on those elusive tickets, and for good reason: anybody who attended last year’s year’s inaugural event will tell you how spectacular it was, but the lineup of artists in 2008 promises to be unforgettable as well. So who will be playing at the Smirnoff Experience? Here’s what we know so far…

First up is DJ Yoda, who in a first for Australian audiences will be bringing his highly acclaimed ‘Magic Cinema Show’ along with him. One of the few DJs worldwide to be packing a Pioneer SMV-1000, he’s been voted as ‘One of the Top Three DJ’s in the World’ by Hip Hop Connection mag as well as being known by the public as one of the most eclectic DJs working the circuit. Injecting heavy does into his set, he’s just as likely to spin underground hip hop as he is TV themes, nursery rhymes, country, 80s pop and whatever else he decides to seamlessly cut into his set.

The next act we know will be playing the Smirnoff Experience is UK DJ Mark Pritchard, a performer who’s earnt himself a huge amount of respect and admiration for his ever-changing sound, and drive to adapt to new directions in music. Working under a huge aray of different aliases and producing everything from experimental techno, deep ambient to instrumental hip hop – and is currently digging the exciting possibilities offered by dubstep.

On the local front, we’ll also be seeing something unique – Goodwill and Hook n Sling will be putting their formidable DJ heads together and teaming up with vocalist Kristy Peters to perform in a once-off collaboration. They’ll also be joined by four-times DMC Australian champion turntablist Dexter.

Nobody can purchase a ticket to Smirnoff Experience and the only way in is to win, so head to www.thewayin.com.au for your chance to get your hands on tickets. And stay tuned to ITM as we’ll be running interviews with all of the artists in the leadup to the events in July.

Be back at ITM on Monday when we’ll be able to reveal the rest of the lineup, here are the dates when the Smirnoff Experience will be landing in Australia…

Sydney – Thursday 10th July
Melbourne – Friday 11 July
Brisbane – Sunday 13th July
