Nickodemus plays for free at UTS Loft Thursday night

Angus Paterson · 6 March 2008 · inthemix

Brought to you by the ever-reliable Dust Tones crew, Thursday night at the Loft Bar at UTS we’ll be seeing an encore performance from none other than New York’s Nickodemus, fresh form his performance at the Turntables on the Harbour party last weekend. And best of all, it’s free!

A mainstay of the New York underground, Nickodemus has been polishing his craft since a young age. The DJ and producer co-founded the now legendary Turntable on the Hudson parties alongside his buddy Mariano, with the simple edict of playing “music with soul”. In a city where clubs come and go, the parties have stayed strong for a decade. It’s even spawned a sought-after six volume mix compilation series, earning Nickodemus something of a cult following in his hometown.

Nickodemus plays tonight at the UTS Loft Bar. Things kick 5pm with a happy hour running from 5:30pm-6:30pm, see you there!
