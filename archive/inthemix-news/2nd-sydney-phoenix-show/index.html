2nd Sydney Phoenix show

Angus Paterson · 20 March 2007 · inthemix

With the first show already sold out, fans of Phoenix now have another chance to catch the French four-piece in addition to their performance at the upcoming V Festival. They’ll now be hitting The Factory on Thursday April 5th.

Since their debut CD United first hit stores in 2001, the band Phoenix has built up a following on the international pop scene. Their latest album is called Its Never been Like That, bringing with it the singles Consolation Prizes and Long Distance Calls.
