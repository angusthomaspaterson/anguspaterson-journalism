Lee Mortimer tours during May

Angus Paterson · 11 May 2009 · inthemix

Fidget house up-and-comer Lee Mortimer (known in production circles as Sawtooth Sucka) will be kicking off an Australian tour this weekend, with a tour that will see him ripping though five states over the next two weeks. So far he’s had tracks and remixes released on CR2, Front Room Recordings, Made To Play, as well as Chris Duckenfield’s label Odori and Passenger Records, run by Aquasky. The next stop is an EP on Switch’s unstoppable Dubsided Records imprint, so Mortimer is obviously impressing the right people.

Lee Mortimer tour dates:

Friday 15 May – Lot33, Canberra
Saturday 16 May – Cadence Bar & Lounge, Cairns
Sunday 17 May – Twisted Discoteca 2nd Birthday, Sydney
Friday 22 May – Ambar, Perth
Saturday 23 May – Empire, Brisbane
Sunday 24 May – Revolver Upstairs, Melbourne
