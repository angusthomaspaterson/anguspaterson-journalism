Fierce Angel flies into Ivy over long weekend

Angus Paterson · 22 September 2009 · inthemix

Fierce Angel is the latest global dance brand to grab the imagination of international clubbers, gaining favour in dance hotspots like Miami, Amsterdam and Ibiza, and over the October Long Weekend it’ll be taking over the opulent surrounds of Ivy for the brand’s Australian debut. Two levels will be utilised for a party that will be the first of a series of events, which promise extravagance to burn.

The organisers are promising incredible décor and production to match the reputation the brand has been building up across the globe, and it’s the brainchild of Hed Kandi creator Mark Doyle who says he was looking to craft a genuinely spectacular clubbing experience that remains down to earth and friendly. “Treat everyone the same, don’t think you are a rock star, remember you are there to entertain people and not expand your ego and most of all do everything spectacularly well.”

The show at Ivy on Sunday 4th October will be the first of a series parties across Australia and New Zealand to take place every six to eight weeks. Sydney’s debut event will see K–Klass perform a DJ and live PA set, as well as DJ Paul Goodyear and local support from Melbourne’s John Course alongside more than 10 of Sydney’s favourite DJs and live performers.

Fierce Angel makes its debut at Ivy in Sydney on Sunday 4th October from 4pm. Grab your tickets at ITM!
