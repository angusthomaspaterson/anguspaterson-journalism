Lily Allen slapped with terror charges

Angus Paterson · 10 August 2007 · inthemix

Well, sort of… Lily Allen is the most provocative pop starlet doing the rounds at the moment, shooting off her mouth to the press at every opportunity. But she’s taken it to the next level with the news that she’s had her US work visa cancelled, after being detained at the Los Angeles International Airport for a massive five hours. Some have even gone as far as to label here as the biggest national security threat America has ever seen. Yep, she’s sure got a potty mouth on her!

“Lily was questioned and her work visa was revoked,” says her spokesperson. “She was detained for five hours but not strip searched. Understandably she was upset by it.”

While Lily was scheduled for a tour of America next month, that’s all looking like it’s up in the air now. She was detained at the LA airport in connection to a recent arrest in London, which was related to an incident outside a nightclub a few months earlier where she allegedly got into a bit of biffo with a photographer. Yikes!

In other Lily Allen news, she has put yet another musical colleague on her shitlist after posting one of her infamous diatribes on her MySpace page. This time against veteran rock widow Courtney Love! While she later backed down with a claim that her page had been hacked into by one of those nasty internet nerds, the following comment made an appearance on her blog: “I am not bfs (best friends) w/ (with) C (Courtney Love), one night with her made me realise why Kurt (Cobain) killed himself.”

Courtney Love of course issued a quick response to the provocation (a predictable outcome if ever there was one) and again, delivering her message of retribution via, you guessed it, her MySpace page. Grab your popcorn, looks like it’s on!

“Thanks to Lily Allen for her lovely apologising for having had her MySpace broken into. I wouldn’t pick on me if I was Miss Lily, as it wouldn’t take TOO much to swat, but we’ve known each other far too long, like each other far too much and if she was gonna say something nasty she has the acerbic wit and intelligence to say something cutting, that would actually really hurt, not some clichéd hoary old chestnut some obsessed blogger would say.

“She tells me her MySpace was broken into – even if I REALLY think she did blog it mindlessly some 8am after being up far too long for a young healthy woman I don’t care, and I appreciate and accept her thoughtfulness for apologising for what was printed, if she is telling the truth and it really doesn’t much matter – everyone slams everyone early in their careers, (Sonic Youth)’s Kim Gordon still slams me, I mean how much more boring can you get? (than Kim Gordon – slam!) Does anyone care?”

Stay tuned for round two of Courtney Love vs. Lily Allen.
