Why Armin van Buuren's psyched about Stereosonic's epic line-up

Angus Paterson · 17 July 2013 · inthemix

Over the weekend inthemix caught up with trance kingpin Armin van Buuren. A firm favourite of Australian audiences for years, he was of course recently announced to be returning to play the mainstage of the national Stereosonic tour at the end of the year. We doubt you missed the line-up. Our chat with the winner of Favourite International DJ in the 2013 inthemix Awards happened half an hour before Armin hit the stage at the debut of Ultra Europe in Croatia; though he said he’s already buzzing about his return to Australian stages in our summer.

“It’s gonna be really amazing – I think they’ve got everyone who ever put out a record on that line-up,” he told inthemix. “Australia is one of my most important markets. The scene is probably bigger than it’s ever been, there’s so many festivals and events. There’s only 20 million people in Australia, and they’re all living and breathing dance music.”

Armin also spoke about playing festival mainstages alongside the new wave of ‘EDM’ big leaguers. On Saturday at Ultra Europe he was set to hit the stage immediately after Afrojack, which is coincidentally one of the very same artists he’ll be playing alongside on the Stereosonic tour.

“It makes it very interesting,” he laughed. “But I don’t see it as a challenge, I see it as fun. They book Armin van Buuren, so they get Armin van Buuren. Afrojack, Avicii, Hardwell, these guys are doing amazingly well. But it’s not the kind of music that I think I should be playing.”

Armin’s sound was certainly intact in his centrepiece set on the weekend, playing inside a Stereosonic-sized stadium. A range of festival-friendly vocal tracks from his new album Intense had a strong presence, though the set was also brimming with high-energy 138-BPM trance. Armin delivered a polished two-hour showcase that kept the energy of the crowd buzzing at all times, while also honouring his trance roots.

Armin dropped hints about bringing Armin Only back to Australia in 2014 (“it depends on venues, it depends on availability, it’ll depend on the promoters,” he said), but otherwise indicated he’ll be winding back his tour schedule a little in the future to focus on his family life.

“I’ll be DJing less, that’s for sure,” he told inthemix. “I’ll be spending more time at home and in the studio; I’m very lucky to have one in my house. I also want to say that having children has made me more balanced as a person as well. You come home and it’s not about ‘me’ anymore. It’s about the children, and this is how it’s supposed to be.” Tickets to the 2013 Stereosonic tour go on-sale 9am Thursday 1 August, head to the official Stereo website for more.
