Timo Maas to rock Tank in exclusive pre-festival set

Angus Paterson · 25 January 2007 · inthemix

For those who reckon a single does of the German uber-producer just won’t be enough, Timo Maas will be firing up for a 4-hour set at Tank on Thursday 15th Feb – offering punters the perfect chance to warm up for the Good Vibrations festival the following Saturday.

Self-styled purveyor of ‘percussive wet funk’, Timo Maas is up there in the upper echelons of the best dance producers in the world – with his excellent Pictures album last year offering a particularly good example of exactly what he’s capable of. It’s been a good few years since he’s toured Australia, but Sydney punters have a double-shot chance of seeing him work his skills behind the decks.

For more information on Timo Maas and the rest of the Good Vibrations line up, check out the ITM festival pages HERE.
