Markus Schulz's 'Global DJ Broadcast' from FMF

Angus Paterson · 13 February 2009 · inthemix

If you’re a fan of progressive trance icon Markus Schulz, then you’ll surely be familiar with his weekly Global DJ Broadcast radio show. Keeping himself (and his listeners) on the cutting edge of everything that trance has to offer, once a month he also gives them a chance to check out his DJs skills in a live setting, with a set recorded live during his busy touring schedule. Shanghai, Buenes Aries, Paris, Toronto, Athens – these have all been locations where his sets have been recorded and subsequently broadcast, and in the next few weeks Australia will be joining the list.

“One of the things that we’re working on right now is to do one of those stops from this tour,” Schulz told ITM this week. “At the end of the year, when I look at the cities that have been represented on the world tour… I definitely think that Australia needs to have a city represented as far as that goes. So I look forward to doing one of the world tour sets from Australia next month.”

It was only this time last year that Schulz was in the country as part of the Future Music Festival, but he’ll be back to make a return appearance shortly as part this year’s tour. “I’m really excited to come back,” he told ITM. “I wasn’t expecting it to be honest with you, because usually I do every other year. But wow, what a pleasant surprise. I’m really excited…. I really look forward to, and believe in building something there in Australia.”
