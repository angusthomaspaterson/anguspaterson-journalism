Wanna see Splendour in the Grass return in ’08?

Angus Paterson · 18 July 2007 · inthemix

The organisers of one of Australia’s festivals Splendour in the Grass will be relocating to a new venue for next year’s event, and to help guarantee that it goes ahead they need your help. They’ll be relocating the party next year to a nearby site and in order to ensure that things keep kickin’ as you’d hope, they’d like your support.

The festival’s current venue in the Belongil Fields will soon become unavailable as the owners are rezoning it residential use, so in order to the event in its beautiful traditional surrounds the organisers have joined a collective of people and purchased 650 acres of land in Yelgun only 15 minutes away. This is going to allow a lot more space for the event in the future, and to put it in perspective the current site only allows 47 acres for both the music and camping areas, so it’ll allow a lot more space for the festival to grow and develop in the future.

But concerns exist in the surrounding community on whether the festival is wanted and what benefits it would bring to the area as a whole. Specifically, the organisers claim there is some misinformation circulating in regards to their consideration of the property’s ecology and its wildlife (Fasterlouder readers will most likely be aware of their stated and ongoing commitment to making the event environmentally friendly).

Splendour in the Grass asserts that debate is healthy and assists the process, but they’ve called for their loyal supporters to jump online the council website and have your say about music festivals in the Byron Shire.

Check the survey HERE, it only takes two minutes to fill in and could help secure the future of one of Australia’s favourite musical events.
