Erick Morillo celebrates 10 years of Subliminal over Easter

Angus Paterson · 23 March 2007 · inthemix

House legend Erick Morillo will be celebrating his Subliminal Records reaching its 10-year milestone by flying in for a party at Home Nightclub on Easter Sunday, 8th April. With Morillo at the controls it’ll be a legendary night of house music with stunning production and plenty of Birthday surprises. The icing on the cake is that he’ll be christening the reinvented Home Sydney main room, as the first international to play following its recent renovation.

Erick Morillo is a platinum selling artist and topped the charts worldwide early in his career as the producer behind Reel 2 Reel’s hit I Like To Move It. He’s remixed everyone from Whitney Houston to Basement Jaxx, from the Sneaker Pimps to Macy Gray. He runs the Subliminal Records empire and has collaborated with the likes of Puff Daddy, Boy George and the Audio Bullys.

As a high profile DJ, Erick is currently one of the most instantly recognisable in the world. From the success of his weekly Sessions parties in New York, to hosting events like the annual Subliminal Sessions parties in Miami for the Winter Music Conference, to his coveted residency at Ministry of Sound, making him one of the few US DJs to ever hold one.

Erick Morillo hits Home on Easter Sunday 8th April, grab your tickets from ITM quick as they’re already in final release!
