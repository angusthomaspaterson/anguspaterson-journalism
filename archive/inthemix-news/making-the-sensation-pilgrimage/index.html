Making the Sensation pilgrimage?

Angus Paterson · 24 November 2009 · inthemix

The debut of Sensation in Melbourne last year would suggest that there’s a huge amount of people around the country who are keen to make the pilgrimage to attend the event on New Years Eve, with more than 45 per cent of ticket holders travelling from interstate for the party. For everyone who’s planning on making the journey this year to experience the ‘Ocean of White’, there are some special packages available.

The packages include two nights accommodation at a range of Melbourne hotels and apartments, with extra nights available for those who are keen to stay a little bit longer, and it’ll be packaged together with General Admission tickets to the party, with an option for ticket upgrades also available.

This New Years Eve we’ll be enjoying an ‘Ocean of White’ themed party, the same event that has ventured across Europe over the past 12 months. Boasting a 160 metre stage that covers the entire length of the stadium, with huge water features and effects, there’s also all the lights, lasers and special effects we’ve come to expect from ID&T.
