Liquid Love kicks off this weekend

Angus Paterson · 30 November 2007 · inthemix

Bringing the soulful house sounds to The Arthouse this weekend, with a wide range of DJs and guest vocalists, the Liquid Love party is being thrown in conjunction with the Beyond Blue organisation to help raise awareness of the effects of depression and anxiety in our community.

Some of the DJs who will be spinning will include Damien Goundrie, Dan Murphy and Movin’ Marty and to make things special, they’ll be joined by guest vocalists Kristen Pearson, Chloe West and Adam Michael Scassa. You can catch all the action in Gallery Bar of the Arthouse Hotel and it’s being put on in conjunction with Paul Strange, one of Sydney’s biggest names when it comes to house music promotions and events.

Liquid Love hits The Arthouse this Saturday, for more info on the party and what it’s all about visit www.liquidlove.com.au.
