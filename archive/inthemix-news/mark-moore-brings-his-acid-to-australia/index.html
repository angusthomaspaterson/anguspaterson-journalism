Mark Moore brings his acid to Australia

Angus Paterson · 11 September 2007 · inthemix

Pointed to as one of the innovators that kickstarted the UK acid house scene, Mark Moore will be bringing it to Australia for the Onelove crew later in the month. Shoot to fame as part of S’Express and their anthem The Theme From S’Express in the late 80s, the track was responsible for boosting acid house into cultural consciousness around the world.

Over the years he’s worked in various projects across his three record labels, working with and remixing everyone from Prince, Elton John, Grace Jones, Malcolm McLaren, Carl Craig, Sonique, Phillip Glass, Scissor Sisters and many more. Also tagged as an early pioneer of the current electro house sound, expect a mix of the before mentioned style along with disco, rock and twisted house that eschews any obvious or cheesy anthems.

Catch Mark Moore playing the following dates for Onelove across the country.

Saturday September 22: Limelite Fremantle, Perth
Saturday September 22: Tank Nightclub, Sydney
Friday September 28: Electric Circus, Adelaide
Saturday September 29: Prince Bandroom, St. Kilda
