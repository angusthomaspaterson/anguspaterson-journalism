Pandemonium hits Havana

Angus Paterson · 10 October 2007 · inthemix

Did you enjoy the Fowl Play live nights at Havana? Well now it’s time for the DJs to cause havoc as Pandemonium takes control this Friday night October 12th. Expect a night out of the ordinary as giant visual screens are brought into the club for the occasion, and you’ll be enjoying sets from The Goose & Azza United, Adam Phillis & Spanners, Thrustn, Luke J & Lonsdale and Teez. It all kicks off this Friday and then it’ll be back every second Friday of the month.
Pandemonium comes to Havana this Friday 12th October, free entry and free Champagne before 10pm. RSVP at www.fowlplay.net.au/pandemonium.
