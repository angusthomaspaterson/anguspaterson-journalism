Hear Aston Shuffle's new single on ITM podcast

Angus Paterson · 9 November 2009 · inthemix

Ministry of Sound’s The Annual 2010 has just hit the stores, with a national tour going down right at the moment and to help mark the occasion, ITM has talked to one of the acts responsible for the release, Canberra’s The Aston Shuffle who of course also screamed into the Sony inthemix50 #3 spot this year. You’ll also have a chance to listen to their upcoming new single I Wanna See You, a wicked slice of squelchy, acidy Aston mayhem that’s surely gonna prove a massive hit over summer.

If you tune in and download, you’ll be hear a massive selection of new tunes from the likes of Sasha, Martin Solveig, Bass Kleph as well as taking a look at Deepchild’s excursions into dubstep.
