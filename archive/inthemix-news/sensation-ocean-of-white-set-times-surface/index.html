Sensation 'Ocean of White' set times surface

Angus Paterson · 23 December 2009 · inthemix

The majestic ‘Ocean of White’ is set to take over the Etihad Stadium in Melbourne for the second New Years Eve in a row, with the spectacular underwater theme having already entertained hundreds of thousands of people across Europe over the past 18 months. For everyone who’s already witnessed its majestic splendor, the evaluation is pretty much unanimous – it’s big, big, big, and enough to take your breathe away. If you’re one of Melbourne’s clubbing community who’s heading along this year, or you’re one of the many travelling from interstate for the party, then we’ve got the set times locked and loaded for you to check out.

Sensation ‘Ocean of White’ comes to Etihad Stadium in Melbourne this Thursday 31st December, tickets on sale now at ITM. Keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page for all the info you could possibly need, and check out set times below…
