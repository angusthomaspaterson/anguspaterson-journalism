Midnight Juggernauts DJs for Snowball this Thursday

Angus Paterson · 7 August 2007 · inthemix

They’ve just dropped their debut album Dystopia and this Thursday the Midnight Juggernauts will be playing a special surprise DJ set at Sydney University’s massive seasonal shindig Snowball. Tickets on sale now at ITM!

Every year there are two massive parties at Sydney University – in March there’s the Beach Ball bash to celebrate the start of the university year but this Thursday night is the annual winter edition known as Snowball. A highlight on campus music calendar, there’s both a dance and an indie rock stage to keep everyone happy as well as a ‘Purple Sneakers’ stage featuring, you guessed it, the ubiquitous Purple Sneakers DJs! Another late addition to the lineup are the Telefunken DJs.

Snowball kicks off this Thursday night and tickets are on sale on ITM, so grab ‘em now. Check the full lineup for the gig below…
