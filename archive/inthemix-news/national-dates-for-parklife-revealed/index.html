National dates for Parklife revealed

Angus Paterson · 16 April 2009 · inthemix

Following last week’s first leak of two of the acts that’ll be touring with Fuzzy’s tent pole national festival next year, ITM can now reveal that dates of when Parklife will be touching down in your part of the country. Again, it’s an extensive run of shows that touches down across the five cities, so everybody gets a chance to party.

Last week ITM revealed that Lady Sovereign and MSTRKRFT were the first acts to leak out to the public as being part of the Parklife lineup, but for the moment Fuzzy are remaining tightlipped on the other artists we’ll be enjoying. “While we can’t tell you the line-up just yet, we can promise you that you’re going to be blown away by yet another unrivalled array of the finest international and local artists in amazing settings,” Fuzzy claimed in a statement.

Anybody got any educated guesses? Tell us in the comment field below.

Catch Parklife at the following dates around the country, and keep your eyes glued to ITM for more details as they come to hand…

Saturday 26th September – Brisbane
Sunday 27th September – Perth
Saturday 3rd October – Melbourne
Sunday 4th October – Sydney
Monday 5th October – Adelaide
