Musique launches at Mandalay Room

Angus Paterson · 26 February 2008 · inthemix

Saturday 8th March will see the launch of a new night in Kings Cross called Musique, a free event for anyone that registers for guestlist and will feature some of Sydney’s most consistent headliners along with the next generation of DJs. The launch party of in early March will feature Craig Obey while Fowl Play and John Glover will be playing in the weeks to follow, and they’ll be ably supported by the night’s list of resident DJs that includes Adam Phillis, The Gauge Breakers, Nick Müller, Andy B and a whole bunch of other up and coming talents.

Musique kicks off at the Mandalay Room on Saturday March 8th, check out www.feelthismusic.com.au for free guestlist info.
