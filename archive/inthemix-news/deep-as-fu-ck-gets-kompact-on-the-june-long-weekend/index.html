Deep as Fu*ck gets Kompact on the June Long Weekend

Angus Paterson · 28 May 2009 · inthemix

It’s time for Deep as Fu#k to celebrate 6 years of bringing amazing underground house and techno to Sydney punters, and to mark the occasion they’ll be taking over the entirety of The Cross at the tail end of the June Long Weekend for a huge event that will see them playing host to the Kompakt World Tour. The event will span across four areas throughout The Cross and The Bunker, including a Kompakt Records stage to honour the Cologne-based minimal techno label.

Kompakt representatives will include French DJ/producer Jennifer Cardini, Canada’s Pan Tone and Sid Le Roc playing live as well as Germany’s Popnoname. In the other rooms there will also be plenty of talent to choose from, including Chris Fortier, Magik Johnson and New Zealand’s Fiord AKA Antix.

Add to that a support cast that includes Jamie Lloyd, Robbie Lowe as well as Deep As Fu*k residents or course, and it’ll surely make for an event lives up to their unbeatable reputation.

Deep As Fu#k Turns 6 at The Cross and The Bunker, Sunday 7th June. Tickets on sale now!
