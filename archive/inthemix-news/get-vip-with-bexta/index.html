Get VIP with Bexta

Angus Paterson · 14 February 2008 · inthemix

Australia’s reigning queen of trance and hard dance has got a new single out, and she’ll be hitting the road for a series of promotional shows around the country. Her new tune V.I.P. single is the result of a collaboration with Adelaide vocalist Amii Jackson and interestingly, former Psuedo Echo frontman Brian Canham was also brought into assist on production duties to create a song that’s been described as a “mix between Kylie and the Potbellyz.” Pop dance with a hint of electro, it represents a bit of a change of tact for Bexta! But hardened fans never fear, because it’s accompanied by a variety of club mixes to bring a different take.

Catch Bexta launching VIP at the following dates…

Fri 15th Feb: Sublime @ Home, Sydney
Fri 22nd Feb: Stargarden @ Chasers, Melbourne
Sun 9th March: Future Music Festival, Melbourne
Sat 29th Mar: Family, Brisbane
Fri 4th Apr: Rise, Perth
Fri 18th Apr: King St, Newcastle
