James Zabiela talks his 'Master Series' debut

Angus Paterson · 14 April 2009 · inthemix

UK next generation superstar DJ James Zabiela chatted last week about his new 2CD Renaissance compilation, his first for the revered The Masters Series, and revealed that he took it a lot more seriously than any of his regular podcast mixes. “It took me three months especially just to complete the first disk,” said James. “With a podcast I just do a DJ set in my studio in couple of hours and then I hand it in.”

He also chatted in details about the significance of his unusually long flowing blonde hair, claiming he’s minimally interested in matters of personal appearance. “I don’t know, is image important?” he said. “There are a lot of ugly DJs out there who are pretty successful and a lot of them are larger and balder. I think what’s important for a DJ is what music you play,” he continued.

“I admit, there is a side to it that involves fans or whatever. I guess there are some people that would get upset if I’d cut all my hair off. I know for sure that my mum would get upset,” the 30 year old confessed. “I’m the least fashionable person. I have no idea of designer clothes and stuff like that. I actually hate clothes shopping. I’m the sort of person that if I buy jeans and they don’t fit, I just go out and buy a belt or turn up the bottoms if they’re too long.”

Aussie fave James Zabiela will be back for a national tour over the June Long Weekend that includes the We Love Sounds, Warehouse and Winter Sound System events. Check out ITM’s Festival Guide for more info.
