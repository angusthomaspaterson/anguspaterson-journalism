Transmission fires up its Next Gen DJ comp

Angus Paterson · 5 February 2007 · inthemix

Two lucky DJs will be playing in front of thousands of people at Magic City this May, check in for ‘round one’ this weekend. The potential ‘next generation’ of DJs will be battling it out at The Temple, a trance night held every second Sunday at Vbar.

The competition will continue until 20th May, with DJs competing across two categories: trance/hardstyle and house/electro/breaks. One winner will be playing on the main trance stage in front of 5,000 people and the other winner will be playing on the house stage in front of 2,000 people, supporting Transmission’s finest international and local DJs. All semi finalists in the comp will play in the Next Gen DJ Arenas.

All entrants get a ticket to the party and a special competitor t-shirt. CLICK HERE to register in the competition. Spots limit to 50 people, so get in quick or miss out!
