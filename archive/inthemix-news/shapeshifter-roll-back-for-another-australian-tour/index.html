Shapeshifter roll back for another Australian tour

Angus Paterson · 14 July 2009 · inthemix

Those elite New Zealand kings of live drum n’ bass Shapeshifter are rolling back for another tour this September, fresh back from conquering overseas crowds in the UK and Europe. It’ll also be the five-piece’s first venture out to audiences since being holed up in the recording studio for album number four, which they will be giving us a sneak peak finally of what we can expect.

Along with the new stuff, we’ll of course be getting all of the classics, from albums Soulstice and prior to that, which we’ve grown to know and love. There’s a reason why they’re considered one of the finest bunch of performers on the Australian and NZ live circuit, and another tour is always welcome!

Shapeshifter tour dates:

Wednesday 9th September – Great Northern, Byron Bay
Thursday 10th September – Coolangatta Hotel, Gold Coast
Friday 11th September – The Hi-Fi, Brisbane
Saturday 12th September – The Forum, Melbourne
Thursday 17th September – Villa Nightclub, Perth
Friday 18th September – HQ, Adelaide
Saturday 19th September – The Forum, Sydney
