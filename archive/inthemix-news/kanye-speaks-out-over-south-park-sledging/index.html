Kanye speaks out over South Park sledging

Angus Paterson · 15 April 2009 · inthemix

The man in visor shades that every ITMer loves to hate, none other than Kanye West, has spoken out on the inevitable animated sledging he’s received from the South Park crew. He feels he was “murdered”, but admits it was still “pretty funny”. We agree.

The episode titled ‘Fish Sticks’, focuses on a joke of the same name that everybody in the world except Kanye was able to grasp. Throughout the episode, Kanye gets worked up at being unable to understand the punch line. “It hurts my feelings,” Kanye revealed on his blog (CAPS LOCK removed for the sake of your sanity), “but what can you expect from South Park!” Too right Kanye. Tom Cruise and Paris Hilton got a sledging, why would they spare you?

The controversial rapper revealed in the same entry that he’s been working on his oh-so notorious ego recently. “I just wanna be a doper person without telling people how dope I think I am.” However, South Park creators Matt Stone and Trey Parker didn’t quite succeed in triggering an ego epiphany. “I actually only watched a piece of the episode, which was funny. I’ve been working on myself for a while now, which is hard to do in the public eye. I just used [this blog] as a platform to express where my head is at.” Just nod your head and smile politely people.
