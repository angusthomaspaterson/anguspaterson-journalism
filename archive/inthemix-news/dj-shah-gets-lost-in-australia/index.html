DJ Shah gets 'lost' in Australia

Angus Paterson · 3 March 2009 · inthemix

Whether you know him for his prolific work under the Sunlounger, or simply as that guy who helped Armin produce the trance singalong fave Going Wrong, if you’re hooked into the trance scene then you no doubt be familiar with DJ Shah. It’s even possible you might have heard of a little tune called Lost: it proved a massive success, taking the #1 listener voted spot for the year on Armin van Buuren’s A State of Trance radio show.

2008 certainly was a massive year for DJ Shah, putting out a massive three albums on trance giant Armada as well as debuting at #58 in the DJ Mag Top 100 poll. If you’re interested in catching him in the flesh then you’ll have your chance in May, with confirmed three-hour club shows with a semi-live performance, bringing his keyboards and laptops along for the ride.

DJ Shah Australian tour dates:

Friday 15th May – Sublime, Sydney
Saturday 16th May – Subconcious, St Pauls, Adelaide
Friday 22nd May – White Sands, Room 680, Melbourne
Saturday 23 May – Rise Nightclub, Perth
