Days Like This! set times are in

Angus Paterson · 29 December 2009 · inthemix

Sydney’s favourite new boutique festival Days Like This! will be back in early January, backing it up after the warm reception the debut party received this year. The lineup for next year’s party lives up to the debut’s reputation of spanning a massive selection of quality music – from hip hop to electronica, indie-rock, roots and plenty more – and if you want to start planning out who you’ll be checking out, set times have arrived for you to have a look at.
