Fire up for Stereosonic with the ITM podcast!

Angus Paterson · 22 November 2009 · inthemix

A nation of dance music lovers are getting fired up at the moment as the start of the Stereosonic tour draws near this weekend, and to make that wait just a little more bearable we’ve got a massive special edition of the inthemix podcast that takes a look at some of the artists playing at the festival. We chat to Sweden’s John Dahlback about what we can expect from his sets on the tour, and about the massive transformation he’s had over the past year into a formidable house music don.

That’s just the start though, as there’s also fresh tunes from a wicked selection of music from other acts on the lineup, including Laurent Garnier, The Bloody Beetroots, Chicane, Fedde Le Grand, Zombie Nation and more.
