Kate Monroe gets some Pure Pleasure this weekend

Angus Paterson · 22 June 2007 · inthemix

Australia’s first lady of house Kate Monroe will be headlining the next installment of Pure Pleasure Experience this weekend as part of her CD tour with record label MAT Recordings. Taking place on the second level of the Equilibrium Hotel it’s one of the last sources of quality funky house in Sydney. The focus is on creating a relaxed environment with pumping tunes from some of Sydney’s best DJs.

Of course she’ll be joined by resident Ange as well as Nick Vidal, Winston and David J Matlak. For fans of Pure Pleasure Experience, they can also look forward to the event’s 1st Birthday Bash Sept 22nd.

Get on down to the Equilibrium Hotel, level 2 from 8.30pm this Saturday night for Pure Pleasure Experience.
