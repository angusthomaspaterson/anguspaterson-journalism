Guns N Bombs + Danger added to We Love Noize

Angus Paterson · 1 June 2009 · inthemix

The city will be alive with crazy dance music action on Saturday night, but the notorious Trashbags crew will be creating mayhem all of their own over at at The Arthouse with We Love Noize, a party that we already knew would be featuring Toxic Avenger and Bobmo. ITM can now reveal the identity of the other two secret guests from the We Love Sounds festival who will be playing at the show – LA electro madmen Guns N Bombs, as well as France’s Danger.

Expect noise. And lots of it. In fact, be very, very afraid….

We Love Noize hits this The Arthouse this Saturday 6th June, featuring Toxic Avenger, Bobmo, Guns N Bombs, Danger and more.
