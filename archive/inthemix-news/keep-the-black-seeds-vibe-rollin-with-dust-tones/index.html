Keep the Black Seeds vibe rollin’ with Dust Tones

Angus Paterson · 30 May 2007 · inthemix

Keenly anticipating the Black Seeds gig over the June Long Weekend, and looking for somewhere suitable to kick on afterwards? The crew from Dust Tones have just the after party for you. They’ll be taking over the Clare Hotel in Broadway from 9pm til late with an all star local line up for a night of dance hall, hip hop & global beats.

The night will feature performances from FBi’s Basslines crew, The Versionaries in DJ mode along with a very special interstate guest in the form of Firehouse, one of Australia’s best reggae and dancehall posse’s. You can also look forward to a global music showdown between Mashy P and Stuart Buchanan from the Fat Planet radio program, a set from Coy Pez Cartel and the Bump DJs and finally, a set from the Dust Tones DJs featuring Flux 3 & Bentley.

It should make for a mighty fine way of keeping the party sounds of the Black Seeds going well into the night.

Check it out at the Clare Hotel from 9pm on Saturday 9th June, or check out www.dusttones.com.
