Secret Groove Armada DJ set?

Angus Paterson · 31 December 2007 · inthemix

Excited by the possibility of catching Andy Cato and Tom Findlay from Groove Armada playing an exclusive DJ set, post their massive NYE performance at Shore Thing? Well, the whispers are building that the dynamic duo will be spinning at a top secret gig at The Loft on Tuesday 2nd January, with some of the Field Day superstars also rumoured to be rocking up for the party… Nothing has been confirmed as of yet, but it’s an thrilling prospect to say the least!

The rumour mill is pointing towards Tom and Andy performing at The Loft from 9pm this Wednesday January 2nd. ITM takes all care and no responsibility, but if it does go ahead remember that you heard it here fist!
