So You Think You Can Trance; take inthemix's Trance Energy trivia challenge!

Angus Paterson · 8 April 2009 · inthemix

Think you know your Sied van Riel from your Sander van Doorn? Do you know the difference between Agnelli & Nelson and Aly & Fila, or your Scandinavians from your Canadians? If you thought you knew everything there was to know about this weekend’s Trance Energy tour, then think again because ITM is here to show you there’s plenty more interesting facts awaiting discovery. Are you ready for So You Think You Can Trance!?

Take a look at our special feature and you’ll find out which Trance Energy DJ can trace his dance music background to when his father purchased a nightclub from The Beatles. Also, which esteemed producer’s daughter was kidnapped in 2007, only to be found safe and well a few weeks later? Which dance music veteran used to moonlight as a Gonzo journalist known as ‘The Dark Knight’? All these answers plus plenty more fascinating tidbits are included within.

> Trance Energy Trivia: So You Think You Can Trance!

By checking out our So You Think You Can Trance gallery you also have the chance to win 1 of 10 copies of the official Trance Energy tour CD, mixed by Sean Tyas, Simon Patterson and Trent McDermott. Keep your eye out for the secret code word to win! Also, don’t forget for all the latest on Trance Energy be sure to make inthemix’s festival page your first port of call.
