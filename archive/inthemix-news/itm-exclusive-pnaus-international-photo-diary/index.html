ITM Exclusive: Pnau’s international photo diary

Angus Paterson · 18 December 2008 · inthemix

One of Australia’s fave live dance acts Pnau are back in the country and will be playing a select bunch of gigs over the New Year period in Newcastle and Victoria, returning from a hugely successful string of overseas gigs that saw them play at Airwave Festival in Iceland, along with shows in Bristol, London, Paris and more. ITM got their hands on an exclusive ‘photo diary’ of the band’s travels, so you can see for yourself all the mischief they got up to taking the sound of their hugely successful Pnau album around the world.
