Tickled Pink turns two!

Angus Paterson · 8 June 2007 · inthemix

That’s two years of partying for Tickled Pink, and to celebrate they’re throwing a massive party on June 29. This month they’ve got Ben Cromack, Max Koenig and Danni B locked in to get the crowd into the party vibe. There will also be free drinks between 11 and midnight to make the evening extra special.

Get on down to Circus on Friday June 29, $10 on the door from 10pm. Check out www.tickledpink.net.au for more info.
