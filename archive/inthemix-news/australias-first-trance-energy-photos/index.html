Australia's first Trance Energy photos!

Angus Paterson · 10 April 2009 · inthemix

ID&T’s mighty Trance Energy brand finally touched down in Australia for the very first time on Thursday night, bringing with it an international DJ lineup to die for and production that was able to stand toe-to-toe with the out of this world experience that is the Dutch event. Check out Australia’s first Trance Energy photos on ITM!

>Trance Energy photos Sydney.

The most interesting thing about Australia’s debut Trance Energy gig was how much of a slow build the night featured – it was nearly a gruelling test of the audience’s patience, as the full extent of the amazing lights, lasers and production were held right back until well after 2am when were hit by the full force of the technology in the mainroom. This slow build was also reflected in the suberb music programming for the night , with BT blowing minds early on with a superb set that jumped from deep progressive to hands-in-the-air anthems like Love Comes Again, but Marco V and Sander van Doorn keeping the tension screwed on extra tight for the next couple of hours with sets that were extremely tech-heavy affairs.

The white-knuckle tension was finally released with the start of Judge Jules’ relentlessly banging (but poorly mixed) performance, with the amazing production ramped to a degree that it could only truly be appreciated from the back of the grandstands. Sets from Richard Durand, Simon Patterson and Sean Tyas ensured the euphoria was kept pumping throughout the rest of the night.
