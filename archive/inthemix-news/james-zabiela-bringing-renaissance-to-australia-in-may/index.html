James Zabiela bringing Renaissance to Australia in May

Angus Paterson · 1 February 2010 · inthemix

Following last week’s announcement that technology wonder-wizard James Zabiela would be releasing a sequel to last year’s terrific Masters Series release – which would see the mix being given away for free at dates on his upcoming world tour and bundled into the normal ticket price – it’s now confirmed that Aussie fans will have their own opportunity to get their mitts on a copy. Zabs will be here for a club tour in may, that’ll see him ripping across some of our finest clubs.

Last week the crew from Renaissance confirmed they’d be employing a unique new tactic in marketing the new Masters Series release. “At the club shows the compilation will be given away for free to a majority, if not all, the music-lovers who have bought their regularly priced ticket to the event. The album will be presented in a shrink-wrapped, full-colour wallet on a flexi-DVD that’s both eco-friendly (50% less carbon emissions) and lightweight (again, 50% less than standard CD/DVDs).”

The compilation will come bursting at the seams with all kinds of cool extras. “Alongside the 2 mixes that comprise the compilation, the package will also feature two exclusive videos: a tour diary and making of the Masters Series, alongside custom screensavers, desktop wallpapers, icons and more.” Sounds pretty sweet to us!

Catch James Zabiela on tour to launch his new Master Series release at the following shows:

Thur May 20th – Villa, Perth
Fri May 21st – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Sat May 22nd – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Sun May 23rd – Lot 33, Canberra
