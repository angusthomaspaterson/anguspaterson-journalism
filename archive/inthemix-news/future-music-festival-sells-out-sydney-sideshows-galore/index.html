Future Music Festival sells out Sydney + sideshows galore

Angus Paterson · 18 January 2010 · inthemix

In a massive day of Future Music Festival news, with Future Entertainment already revealing this morning that acts like Way Out West and Spank Rock have been added to the second-round lineup, it’s now been announced the Sydney show at Randwick Racecourse has completely sold out, bar a small selection of VIP tickets. Not only that, they’ve taken the wraps off the next round of sideshows for the festival, with live favourites Booka Shade performing in both Sydney and Melbourne, and Dubfire and Sven Vath playing an extra special gig together in Sydney. Huzzah!

Booka Shade are known as one of the most visible and successful examples of the minimal and tech house sound, as well as one of the most popular live electronic acts working in dance music today, and they’ve already wowed Australian crowds on a number of occasions. Expect to hear a few sneak peaks off their upcoming fourth studio album, due out in Australia on Bandroom Records in April.

Techno fans collectively wet themselves when it was announced that Cocoon maestro Sven Vath would be returning for another roll at Future Music Festival in 2010, and Sydney punters will have a chance to catch his unbelievable charisma when he plays a sideshow the night before the festival, joined by the recently crowned ‘Prince of Darkness’ Dubfire for one special night of underground sounds.

They’ll be joining the sideshows already announced for The Prodigy and Franz Ferdinand. Here’s hoping for a couple more before the tour winds up!

Future Music Festival sideshows:

Thu Mar 4 – Booka Shade @ The Metro, Sydney
Fri Mar 5 – Booka Shade @ The Forum, Melbourne
Fri Mar 5 – Sven Vath + Dubfire @ The Metro, Sydney

In addition to previously announced sideshows:

Wed Mar 3 – The Prodigy @ Hordern Pavilion, Sydney
Wed Mar 3 – Franz Ferdinand @ The Forum, Melbourne
Thu Mar 4 – The Prodigy @ Hisense Arena, Melbourne
Thu Mar 4 – Franz Ferdinand @ Luna Park Big Top, Sydney
