Angus Paterson · 5 March 2007 · inthemix

With less than two weeks to go until the massive Future Music Festival returns to Randwick Racecourse, eager fans now have the chance to plan out their day.

Meanwhile in the lead up to the event, check out ITM’s full coverage page HERE for everything you need to know about the Future Music Festival – there’s streaming audio interviews and feature articles on the best of the festival’s headline acts, as well as up to date news, reviews, ITM-FM and ITM-TV streams and most recently, a Chris Lake feature podcast.
