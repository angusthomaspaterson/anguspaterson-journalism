Seaman and Fanciulli locked for massive Renaissance bonanza

Angus Paterson · 8 February 2008 · inthemix

Have you figured out your party plans for Easter Thursday yet? Well, the Sweetchilli crew have been cooking up a pretty massive gig for a while now… None other than both Dave Seaman and Nic Fanciulli have been booked for a massive Renaissance Masters party that will be taking the roof off Space Nightclub.

And neither of the headliners for the event really need any introduction: Dave Seaman was the very first editor of Mixmag and has been DJing for over 25 years, and in that time has released over 20 mix compilations. Nowadays he’s the main man behind independent labels Stress Records and Audio Therapy, and he’s been touring Australia consistently for more than a decade. Nic Fanciulli on the other hand is one of dance’s rising stars, but he’s already wielding a massive amount of respect through his prolific production work, his ‘One+One’ partnership with James Zabiela as well as his own compilation effort for the Renaissance crew.

Renaissance Masters feat. Dave Seaman & Nic Fanciulli hits Space Nightclub on Thursday March 20th. Tickets are $45+BF and go on sale Monday11th February on inthemix.
