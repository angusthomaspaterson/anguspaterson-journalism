Pacha NYE completely sold out

Angus Paterson · 28 December 2006 · inthemix

Christmas has already come and gone and with New Years Eve rushing up faster than anyone could possibly fathom, punters that have been dragging their heels now have just a little bit less choice as to what they’ll be doing on the big night – Pacha at Home has completely sold out.

Featuring a massive lineup of international house talent that includes Darren Emerson, Axwell, Tocadisco, Bodyrockers and Sarah Main, Pacha promises to bring the whole ‘Ibiza’ experience down to Sydney for one massive event. Boasting great views of the fireworks and Pacha’s notorious dancers, the event is also set to deliver the expected ‘visual extravaganza’ that you can only get from a superclub such as Home.

But all this info is going to offer little consolation to those who missed out on tickets. Organisers have reported a post-Christmas rush for tickets that left them reeling, so perhaps it was the rumor that Paris Hilton was going to be ‘performing’ at Pacha that convinced the last round of punters to make Darling Harbour their Home on New years Eve.

With only a few days to go, set times for Pacha NYE will be up on ITM shortly.
