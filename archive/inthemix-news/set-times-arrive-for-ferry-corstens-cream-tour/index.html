Set times arrive for Ferry Corsten's Cream tour

Angus Paterson · 10 September 2009 · inthemix

Dutch trance veteran Ferry Corsten returns Australia this weekend for a tour under the Cream banner, bringing the full ‘Twice In A Blue Moon: The Experience’ setup that he has been touring around the world this year, and finally we have the set times for all of his performances across the country. Fans will be more than pleased, as he’s playing a minimum of at least three hours at every show.

Brisbane set times:
2100 – 2230: Tydi
2230 – 2400: Giuseppe Ottaviani
0000 – 0300: Ferry Corsten
0300 – close: Dynasty

Melbourne set times:
2200 – 2300: Ben Evans & Steve Strangis
2300 – 2330: Trent McDermott
2330 – 0030: Giuseppe Ottaviani
0030 – 0100: Trent McDermott
0100 – 0500: Ferry Corsten

Sydney set times:
2000 – 2045: Matt Nugent vs Chris Fraser
2045 – 2150: Nik Fish vs Pewee Ferris
2150 – 2250: Giuseppe Ottaviani
2250 – 2300: Peewee Ferris
2300 – 0300: Ferry Corsten

Cream pres. Ferry Corsten tour dates:

Thu Sep 10 – Brisbane, Family
Fri Sep 11 – Melbourne, Palace Metro Theatre
Sat Sep 12 – Sydney, Hordern Pavilion
