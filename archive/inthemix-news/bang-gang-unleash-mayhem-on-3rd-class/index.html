Bang Gang unleash mayhem on 3rd Class

Angus Paterson · 25 September 2007 · inthemix

Be very, very afraid people… One of Sydney’s most revered sources of dance floor insanity Bang Gang will be taking it to Third Class this Friday night. Behind the decks will be inthemix50 favourite Ajax along with Jaime Doom and Gus Da Hoodrat and they’ll be brining the party like nobody else can.

Check it out this Friday night at 3rd Class, presales are $20 and there’ll be more on the door. Meanwhile, check out the special Sydney dance the Bang Gang crew inspired! And laugh at how stupid your Northern countrymen really are.
