Reckless Republic bringing The Likes of You to Sydney

Angus Paterson · 31 August 2009 · inthemix

After already establishing a healthy foothold in the Melbourne scene, Future Entertainment brought their The Likes of You brand to Sydney earlier this year, and they’ve stepped it up a notch with the news they’ve inked a partnership with Sydney crew Reckless Republic to help make it happen. Reckless Republic are infamous in their city’s scene for their involvement with events like Spice, Lost Baggage as well as Earthdance in Sydney.

The Likes of You’s debut Sydney party back in May featured Hernan Cattaneo and Loco Dice, but what can we expect from the first event with Reckless Republic at the controls? No less than Mathew Dear AKA Audion, who’ll be taking over the Civic Underground on Saturday September 12th along with Oxia. Hot!

ITM spoke with Brett Robinson from Future Entertainment in Sydney earlier this year about The Likes of You, where he claimed the decision to bring the brand up north came after they witnessed the incredible response to the acts who played in the arena at this year’s Future Music Festival. “When we had Etienne De Crecy, Richie Hawtin, Sander Kleinenberg and Stephan Bodzin in that tent, it was the response that we got and the type of people that we saw fill that tent,” Robinson said.

“The ‘Likes of You’ arena really appealed to the proggy/techy/minimal type crowd, and they came out in droves to support it…. And I thought, there’s definitely a very serious following for that kind of music here, and it’d be good to get something going on a more regular basis.”

The Likes of You returns to Sydney on Saturday September 12th at Civic Underground, with Audion + Oxia.
