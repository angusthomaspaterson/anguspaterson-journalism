Sublime pops the cork on 11th Birthday celebrations

Angus Paterson · 10 July 2007 · inthemix

Later this month the longest running club night in Australia (one of the longest running in the world even) will be celebrating its 11th Birthday. With all of the DJs returning that have been part of Sublime’s long history, get onboard and join them!

Making the night even more enticing is the fact that anyone joining the party will go in the draw to win a trip to Ibiza courtesy of Sublime and Virgin Atlantic. Every clubber through the door for the 11th Birthday will be in the running to fly with the Sublime crew to Ibiza to hit some of the other biggest dance nights in the world.

Join 44 of Sublime’s finest on Friday 20th July as they take you on a time-twister through the biggest moments in dance over the past 11 years. Pre-sales available now on ITM, check below for the lineup
