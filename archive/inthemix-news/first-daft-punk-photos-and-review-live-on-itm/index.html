First Daft Punk photos & review live on ITM

Angus Paterson · 14 December 2007 · inthemix

That’s right, the mystical pyramid has finally landed in Australia, and last night Melbourne punters were given the chance to see those wacky French robots doing their thing. It turned out to be one almighty spectacle, and our photos from the first Melbourne Daft Punk gig are online now, check them out HERE, while you can read up on the experience HERE!

And from all the excitement hype that’s erupted in the Melbourne forums, it seems the show somehow managed to live up to all the hype. Comments range from the succinct, “That was huge,” to the more descriptive, “BEST SHOW OF MY FUCKING LIFE… The pyramid blew my fucking mind, but not nearly as much as their light up suits, holy shit that was dope.”

And it doesn’t stop there. “Single greatest performance ever. Unbelievable,” said another ITMer. Another user extended his good wishes to all the lucky punters heading along to the Friday night Melbourne show. “For everyone going tonight, be prepared for a mind explosion. Absolutely unfuckinbelievable. No highlights, it was all just one incredible experience. I want to say so much but don’t want to spoil it. Have a great night, it is truly something special.”

And as we revealed last night, inthemix will be allowing you to go ‘Behind The Scenes’ on NeverEverLand tour. The first part of the video series is now online, where we sit down with a selection of the tour’s support artists and speak with them about their excitement for the tour and the profound impact Daft Punk’s had over their approach to music. So bookmark www.inthemix.com.au/nevereverland now!

But the madness is only beginning… Stay tuned to our NeverEverLand page over coming weeks for more new, photos and reviews as the tour takes it across the country! And check out this mental YouTube from the performance on Thursday night…
