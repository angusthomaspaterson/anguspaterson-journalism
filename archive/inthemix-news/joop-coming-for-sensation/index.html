JOOP coming for Sensation

Angus Paterson · 2 December 2009 · inthemix

If you’re a trance fan and you’re planning on heading to Sensation this New Years Eve, you’ve got another reason to look forward to it. In addition to the already announced trance names like Richard Durand, Sid Van Riel and Binary Finary, it’s also just been announced that JOOP will be coming along to help hold things down in the ‘In Riel Time’ arena.

Known for pushing his own unique brand of progressive trance, JOOP is possibly best know for being responsible for the Trance Energy 2007 anthem The Future. In addition to that he’s also been responsible for bombs like Act Of God, The World/Another World and Pradera, as well as playing some of ID&T’s biggest events across Europe including Sensation, Trance Energy and beyond.
