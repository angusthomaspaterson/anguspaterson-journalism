Andy C rolls back into Sydney this January

Angus Paterson · 11 December 2007 · inthemix

Feel like you’ve been waiting forever for Andy C to return? Well wait no more, because legendary local drum n’ bass crew The Fracture Factory will be hosting him at Home on a Thursday night early next year. Joined on vocal duties by MC GQ, he’ll be bringing a serious dose of the good stuff on Thursday Jan 24th.

One of the drum n’ bass scene’s most enduring names, Andy C will be celebrating 15 years of his Ram Records label as well as the release of his latest mix compilation Nightlife 4. He continues to rack up the awards in 2007 and was the highest ranking drum n’ bass DJ in this year’s DJ Mag results, so the gig will make a great choice for those who want to get the new year rollin’ in style. And seeing that it’s a Fracture Factory gig, it’ll features installations from prominent street artists.

Andy C + MC GQ will be rolling into Home on Thursday Jan 24th. Tickets on sale now, and check out this massive clip from the duo filmed last NYE…
