Underworld, Prydz & Presets for Shore Thing!

Angus Paterson · 2 October 2008 · inthemix

If you’re in Sydney this New Years Eve, there’s no question there’ll be some heavy-duty party action going down at Bondi Beach. Fuzzy have secured the massive lineup of Underworld, Eric Prydz and The Presets to bring in the new year! There’s a been a few Sydney-siders out there that have been feeling a wee bit left out when it comes to the large events and heavy-duty headliners we’ll be seeing during the New Year period, but now they’re guaranteed something huge to rock out too while the clock is ticking over to the first hours of 2009.

What to say about that lineup? Underworld are known as one of the most seminal and successful live dance acts to ever hit the stage, due equally to the enigmatic (and energetic) performances of Karl Hyde and the forward-thinking production of Rick Smith. Similarly, Sweden’s Eric Prydz is revered by many as one of the greatest dance music producers to ever walk the planet, and he sent fans around the country into a frenzy when it was announced that he’d be touring Australia for the very first time.

2008 has been the year of The Presets, with the Australian duo whipping crowds into a state of hysteria wherever they’ve played over the past 12 months. So it’s fitting that they’ll be helping Sydney usher in the New Year. All up it makes for what’s arguably the finest lineup that Shore Thing has ever hosted, taking place in what’s arguably one of Australia’s most iconic locations. How could you lose?

Tickets to Shore Thing are $129+BF, onsale at ITM from Friday 10th October. Take a look at our photos from last year’s Shore Thing to get an idea of what’s in store!
