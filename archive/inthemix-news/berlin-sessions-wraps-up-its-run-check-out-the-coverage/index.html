Berlin Sessions wraps up its run - check out the coverage

Angus Paterson · 7 May 2009 · inthemix

The Beck’s Berlin Sessions tour completed its national run with shows in Melbourne and Adelaide last weekend, and ITM was there to capture it all. There’s photo galleries, reviews, episodes of ITM-TV as well as well as live DJ sets recorded at every show. It’s about as comprehensive as you can get!

International guests Justus Köhncke, Marcus Worgull and man of the moment Stimming were all present across the four shows to bring a taste of Berlin to Australia, with able support from some of our own local proponents of nu-disco, deep house and techno. Make sure you check it all out…
