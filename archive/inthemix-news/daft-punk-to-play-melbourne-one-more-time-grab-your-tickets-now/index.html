Daft Punk to play Melbourne one more time - grab your tickets now!

Angus Paterson · 13 September 2007 · inthemix

With the first show already completely sold out, Daft Punk fans in Melbourne who missed out the first time around can breathe a sigh of relief. The loveable French robots will be playing a second show on Thursday December 13th, tickets on sale now through inthemix!

The show will have the same mighty lineup as the rest of the ‘Never Ever Land’ tour, with a world-class roster of supports to back up the robot rockers. Melbourne crowds will also be enjoying performances from Cut Copy, The Presets, Muscles, the Bang Gang DJs along with international guests SebastiAn & Kavinsky.

Tickets are on sale NOW through ITM, so don’t delay! Grabe em now before they disappear.
