V Festival takes it across the country in ‘08

Angus Paterson · 27 November 2007 · inthemix

Last year’s V Festival events in Sydney and Gold Coast were the pleasant surprise of the season for many punters – flying in a diverse lineup of quality international names, along with a festival vibe of the likes of which we hadn’t seen since the mid 90s. In 2008 the rest of the country will have a chance to check it as well! It’ll be expanding out to bring Melbourne and Perth into the fold, have a look below for the dates.

Last year was the first time that the festival, spawned by billionaire Richard Branson, had made it over to Australia and the lineup was certainly one to die for. As well as sending indie rock fans into spasms of joy with the announcement that The Pixies would be visiting for the very first time, we also enjoyed performances from the likes of Beck, Jarvis Cocker, Groove Armada, Pet Shop Boys, Soulwax and heaps more. Check out a whole heap of related coverage HERE.

So who are we going to be seeing at this year’s event? The organisers have remained tight lipped so far, and there’s been no confirmed leaks as of yet but one name that continues to keep coming up is the Smashing Pumpkins – who reformed and returned to the spotlight earlier this year with their new album Zeitgeist. But whether there’s any substance to that rumour remains to be seen…

Stay tuned to ITM for details as they come to hand! Meanwhile, check out the dates for when the V Festival will be rolling into your neck of the woods…

Saturday March 29 – Sydney
Sunday March 30 – Gold Coast
Saturday April 5 – Melbourne
Sunday April 6 – Perth
