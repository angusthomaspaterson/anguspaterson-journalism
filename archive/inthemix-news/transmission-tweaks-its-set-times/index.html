Transmission tweaks its set times

Angus Paterson · 7 December 2007 · inthemix

Heading along to see the master Paul van Dyk play this weekend? The organisers of Transmission have had to tweak the set times slightly so that he can hop on a Lear Jet and make it to the Ultraworld Festival in Melbourne in time for his sunrise set. But never fear: he’ll still be playing his scheduled three-hour set. Everything has just been pushed back 15 minutes, and the party is now open from 8.30pm onwards so everyone will be able to catch every beat.

Check out the amended Main Arena set times for Saturday night. And wanna get hyped for the party? Check out our recent interview with PVD HERE.
