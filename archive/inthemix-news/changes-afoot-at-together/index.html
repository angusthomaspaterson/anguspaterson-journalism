Changes afoot at Together

Angus Paterson · 1 July 2008 · inthemix

Veteran Sydney promoter Paul Strange took the reigns at The Arthouse on Saturday nights late last year, making the classy venue the place to go for soulful and funky house sounds. And news just in, Paul Strange and co will be broadening their music policy for their regular Together Saturday nights. Referred to now as ‘Big Room Party,’ the refreshed music policy kicked off alst Saturday night wyhen they hosted The Potbelleez and Goodwill for the ‘Ministry of Sound Sessions’ tour, and some of the headliners they’ve got coming up include Hook N Sling, Dirty Laundry, Stonebridge, Mark Dynamix, Wei Shen and more.

“If it rocks a big room we will be playing it,” says Paul, but he ensures us that Together will continue to concentrate on the sexier and classier side of house music that fits in well with the venue. Other news is that fellow promoter Leighton Avery has been given the task of running the Gallery Bar, with up-and-comers Sushi & Tekneex, Alex Brown, Matt Mvp, Marzy, Sassin and Matt Nukewood providing the tunes with a few more surprises promised.

Together takes over The Arthouse every Saturday night.
