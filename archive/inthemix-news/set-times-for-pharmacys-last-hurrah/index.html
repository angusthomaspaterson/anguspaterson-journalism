Set times for Pharmacy's last hurrah

Angus Paterson · 20 May 2009 · inthemix

Hard dance fans in Melbourne were sad to hear that the legendary Pharmacy series of events were coming to an end on Friday May 22nd. For the past ten years they’ve been a regular feature on the clubbing calendar, and to go out with a bang the Pharmacy crew have pulled the big guns for the final event. Set times are now in for this Friday’s sold out event, check them out below!
