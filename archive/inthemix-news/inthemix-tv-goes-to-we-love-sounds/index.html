inthemix.tv goes to We Love Sounds!

Angus Paterson · 12 June 2009 · inthemix

Australia’s premier winter festival We Love Sounds returned over the June Long Weekend, with some of the world’s biggest names in dance including Armin van Buuren, Arman Van Helden, Laidback Luke, Chris Lake, Guy Gerber, Kissy Sell Out and more. inthemix.tv was there to speak to all the massive international headliners who played at the party, as well as capture the event in all its glory…
