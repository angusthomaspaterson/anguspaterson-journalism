Lily Allen sideshows announced

Angus Paterson · 27 April 2007 · inthemix

We already knew she was gonna be here for Splendour in the Grass, now we’ll be seeing her play a number of sideshow dates during July and August. Lily Allen will be back to play songs from her smash album Alright Still, it’ll be no less than her third visit to Australia but she’ll be jetting over to Perth for the first time.

It’s been a hectic 12 months of international touring and media commitments for Lily, but her shows in Australia will represent the end of the touring cycle for the album and afterwards she’ll be taking a break before beginning work on her second album.

Catch Lily Allen playing at the following dates, supported by Macromantics.
Tuesday 31st July: Metropolis, Fremantle
Thursday 2nd August: Prince of Wales, Melbourne
Friday 3rd August: The Metro, Sydney
Saturday 4th August: Splendour in the Grass
