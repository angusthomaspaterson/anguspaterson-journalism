Way Out West's new album done & dusted

Angus Paterson · 20 March 2009 · inthemix

It’s been in the works for a lengthy four years, but last week inthemix spoke on the phone with Global Underground regular Nick Warren who confirmed that Way Out West, the dance duo that he operates alongside production partner Jody Wisternoff, have just put the finishing touches on their brand new album.

“We finished the Way Out West album today, so all is rosy over in Bristol,” Warren told ITM on Friday morning. He says they’re extremely pleased with the results, and that it’s their best effort so far by a mile. “It’s a slight change of tact for us. We’ve been very influenced by the whole ‘Cosmic Disco’ thing in Europe, guys like Lindstrøm from Scandanavia… It’s only male vocals on this album, but it ranges from zero BPM up until 130BPM. It’s almost like Pet Shop Boys making a very cool underground record.”

Expect a long-player that’s very much in touch with Warren and Wisternoff’s clubbing roots. “There’s gonna be around 14 tracks on the album, and seven or eight of them are club based. So it’s still very much of a club-based, electronica album.”

Want a taste of what we can expect? Warren says he’ll be roadtesting a lot of the material on his upcoming run of dates over Easter. “I’ll be playing a lot of it on the tour, so make sure you come out.”

It’ll be an agonising long wait until October until Way Out West’s We Love Machine see release, but in the meantime you can catch Nick Warren at the following shows over Easter…

Saturday April 11th – Adelaide, Live at the Zoo
Saturday April 11th – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Sunday April 12th – The Met, Brisbane
