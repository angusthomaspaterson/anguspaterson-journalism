Famous descends on Macquarie University

Angus Paterson · 22 August 2007 · inthemix

Macquarie University’s massive annual Conception Day bash is coming up again in September, and this year Future Entertainment’s Famous will be there will be there rocking the rowdy uni crowds. All of its residents are locked in for sets including Grant Smillie, Carl Kennedy and Goodwill.

Clocking in every year at Macquarie University as spring is drawing near, Conception Day has become a major festival and entertainment event, drawing huge crowds and amazing musical acts and giving the students a chance to cut loose on campus.

Catch the Famous crew when they take over Macquarie University on the 14th of September.
