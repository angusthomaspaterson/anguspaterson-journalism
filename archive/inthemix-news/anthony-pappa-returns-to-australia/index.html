Anthony Pappa returns to Australia

Angus Paterson · 21 June 2007 · inthemix

Anthony Pappa is easily the most successful Australian DJ to ever hit the international touring circuit, and in July he’ll be returning to his homelands for a national tour that will include Sydney institution Sweetchilli’s massive 8th birthday bash as well dates in Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth.

Much has been made of Pappa’s background as a DJ of the very highest technical calibre. After becoming a DMC Australia mix champion at the amazingly young age of 15, he moved to London in the early 90’s to pursue a career as a DJ on the club circuit. What this means is that he brings the amazing mixing and technical flair of a hip hop turntablist to his chosen genre of progressive house, always rocking the crowd whether it be a tiny intimate venue or a massive superclub.

He was chosen by the prestigious Global Underground label to mix the first of their Nubreed series of compilations, a collection that later also put the spotlight on the likes of Danny Howels, Sander Kleinenberg and Steve Lawler. And like the rest of those Nubreed names he went on enjoy a huge amount of success.

It’ll be an extra special gig for the Sweetchilli crew in Sydney, who have already hosted Pappa several times before at a slew of memorable gigs. It’ll mark the eighth anniversary that they’ve been bringing quality progressive house (and other forward thinking music) to their slavishly loyal punters in the Sydney dance community.

Pappa has a huge following all throughout Australia, and you’ll be able to join them when he plays the following dates…

Friday 20th July: Sydney, The Cross (Sweetchilli 8th Birthday)
Saturday 21st July: Melbourne, Brown Alley (Darkbeat & Sunny)
Friday 27th July: Brisbane, Mass (Ritual Events)
Friday 3rd August: Perth, Ambar (Electriq)
