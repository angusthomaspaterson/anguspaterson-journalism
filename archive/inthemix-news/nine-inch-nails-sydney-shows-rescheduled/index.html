Nine Inch Nails Sydney shows rescheduled

Angus Paterson · 11 May 2007 · inthemix

Music fans go into mourning today with the news that they’ll have to wait until September to see Trent Reznor hit the stage again. We learnt late Thursday afternoon that the show wasn’t going ahead that evening, and it has turned out to be the same story for the Friday night show as well.

But all is not lost as Nine Inch nails will be back in the country in September to play the rescheduled shows. Tickets for Thursday 10th May are now valid for Saturday 15th of September and tickets for Friday 11th May are valid for Sunday the 16th September.

Any ticket holders unable to attend the rescheduled performances can obtain a refund from their point of purchase.
