Check out ITM’s Splendour in the Grass galleries

Angus Paterson · 4 August 2008 · inthemix

Splendour in the Grass is one of the country’s most adored annual music events, and for an festival that’s so very popular and sells out at record speeds every year, it’s also still one of the most intimate – capped at well under 20,000, there’s still room to find your friends and feel the beautiful Byron Bay vibes at Splendour in the Grass. The annual returned in style over the weekend, and ITM has the photographic evidence…

>Splendour in the Grass photo galleries.

From the reports that are plastered all over ITM sibling site and festival media partner Fasterlouder, this year’s Splendour was one of the best ever. For those of us who weren’t able to make the trek, it’s all laid out for us in vivid detail. “And so Splendour 2008 winds down for the night,” says the review of the second day’s action-packed festivaities. “There’s still beats from the various dance areas which will echo on into the night, but for most, this signals the end of a very long weekend, filled with sun, songs and sublime performances. There was something for everyone, and we can only hope next year’s Splendour continues in the fine footsteps of Splendours passed.”

Wanna find out more about some of the amazing artists who played over the weekend? Check out ITM’s Splendour in the Grass festival page.
