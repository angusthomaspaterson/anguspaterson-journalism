Sensation unveils 'Ocean of White' for NYE

Angus Paterson · 29 June 2009 · inthemix

ID&T’s flagship event Sensation was most definitely the biggest addition to the Australian clubbing calendar last year, and this NYE in Melbourne it’ll be back with a brand new theme that looks set to eclipse the ‘Oak of Love’ we saw at the debut event. The ‘Ocean of White’ edition of Sensation will transform the Etihad Stadium into a stunning underwater world, in the same fashion that has already bedazzled more than 250,000 punters across Europe over the past 12 months.

The ‘Ocean of White’ show boasts a 160 metre stage that covers the entire length of the stadium, with huge water features and effects that keep in tune with the theme; as well as all the lights, lasers and special effects we’ve come to expect from ID&T’s mind-boggling parties. Want a preview of exactly what ‘Ocean of White’ looks like? Check out the adventures of inthemix.tv in Copenhagen last year!

Senation’s debut was more of a success that anyone could have even predicated, selling out 40,000 tickets and drawing punters from all over the country. There’s every reason to expect we’ll see even more national revelers descending on the city to bring in 2010 with Sensation, securing Melbourne’s place as easily the place to be to party over the New Year period. Bring it on we say…

Will you be there for Sensation this NYE? Stay tuned to ITM for ticketing info in the coming weeks, and keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page for all the Sensation info you could possibly need.
