Summadayze photos go live on ITM

Angus Paterson · 2 January 2008 · inthemix

Were you at Summadayze on NYD? Relive the madness and see whether you made an appearance in the ITM photo galleries! Check em out HERE.

Summadayze celebrated it’s 10th anniversary this year, with a scorching day and a smoking hot crowd. With a massive lineup featuring the likes of Groove Armada, David Guetta, New Young Pony Club, Steve Angello & Sebastian Ingrosso, Felix Da Housecat, Sister Bliss, Dave Spoon, Benny Benassi and many, many more, the party live up to its repuatation as one of Melbourne’s premier events.

Stay tuned to ITM for a stack of New Year photos, reviews and other coverage over the next few days!
