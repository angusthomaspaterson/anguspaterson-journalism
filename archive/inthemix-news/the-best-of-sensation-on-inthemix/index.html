The best of Sensation on inthemix

Angus Paterson · 6 January 2009 · inthemix

Were you one of the 40,000+ punters who headed along to Melbourne’s Telstra Dome for the spectacular debut of Sensation on NYE? Whether you were one of the thousands dressed in white, or you weren’t able to afford the interstate flights, either way it’s worth reflecting back on one of the most spectacular dance events ever seen in Australia. ITM was on the ground for the entire evening, and we’ve compiled several Snap! featured ‘Best Of’ galleries with the hottest pics from the party!

After months and months of hype and anticipation, the world-renowned Sensation finally made it’s long awaited debut at Telstra Dome in Melbourne last Wednesday December 31st. After years of looking up the iconic ‘Oak of Love’ from afar, Australian punters finally got to view the spectacle in person. Punters were treated to the full Dutch experience, and it’s sure to make Melbourne a premier NYE destination for many years to come.
