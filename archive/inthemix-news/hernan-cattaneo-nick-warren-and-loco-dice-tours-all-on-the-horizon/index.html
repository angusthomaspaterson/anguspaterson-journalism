Hernan Cattaneo, Nick Warren & Loco Dice tours all on the horizon

Angus Paterson · 19 March 2009 · inthemix

There’s been details and dates leaking out here and there on the upcoming tours of dance music heavyweights Hernan Cattaneo, Nick Warren and Loco Dice, with each of the three DJ/producers crisscrossing the country with tours that’ll give punters the chance to see them where they arguably do it best – in the clubs. Although some of the venue details might still be forthcoming, ITM can now confirm full national dates of when you’ll be seeing them in your neck of the woods.

Both Hernan Cattaneo and Nick Warren will be touring in support of some pretty high-profile compilations. While Warren late last year released his acclaimed eighth compilation for the Global Underground crew, South American progressive guru Cattaneo will shortly be releasing a Masters Series installment for the Renaissance brand. It’s been nearly two years since we saw the release of one of his highly-acclaimed Sequential CDs, so fans are frothing at the mouth for this one.

Loco Dice on the other hand is a whole different story. It was only just last month that one of the biggest names in German tech was mentioned in ITM’s Most Wanted Tours of 2009 as one of the artists we’d be dying to see reach Australia this year. It’ll be his first ever tour, so expectations are high for this one!

Hernan Cattaneo tour dates:

Friday May 15 – The Likes Of You, Melbourne
Saturday May 16 – The Art House, Sydney
Sunday May 17 – TBA , Brisbane

Nick Warren tour dates:

Friday April 10th – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Saturday April 11th – Adelaide, Live at the Zoo
Saturday April 11th – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
Sunday April 12th – The Met, Brisbane
Thursday April 16th – Lot 33, Canberra
Friday April 17th – The Heritage, Cairns

Loco Dice tour dates:

Friday May 15 – The Likes Of You, Melbourne
Saturday May 16 – TBA, Sydney
Sunday May 17 – TBA , Brisbane
