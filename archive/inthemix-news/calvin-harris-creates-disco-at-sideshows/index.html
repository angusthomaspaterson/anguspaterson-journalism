Calvin Harris creates disco… at sideshows!

Angus Paterson · 5 December 2007 · inthemix

He was Acceptable in the 80s, he was booked to play at the Good Vibrations festival around the country and now, he’s playing sideshows in both Sydney and Melbourne. Don’t forget those unsubstantiated rumours that he was dating Kylie Minogue. So hot right now!

One of the artists to breakthrough on the MySpace wave, Scotland’s Calvin Harris scored himself a UK top 10 hit earlier this year with the afore-mentioned Acceptable In The 80s, a memorable electro-pop single that had him compared to Mylo, that other electro maestro that also happens to hail from Scotland. His also released debut album I Created Disco this year, and you’ll now be able to catch him at a solo show outside of the festival circuit.

Catch Calvin Harris creating disco at the following sideshows, tickets are on sale Thursday December 13th…

Friday, February 8th: Hi Fi Bar, Melbourne
Thursday, February 14: The Forum, Sydney
