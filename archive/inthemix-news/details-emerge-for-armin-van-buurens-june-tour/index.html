Details emerge for Armin van Buuren's June tour

Angus Paterson · 25 March 2008 · inthemix

We already knew that the world’s #1 DJ Armin van Buuren would be returning to Australia over the June long weekend, bringing all the ‘Armin Only’ madness for two shows in Sydney and Melbourne. Well now we have the dates and venues, as well as confirmation that he’ll be bringing it to Perth and Brisbane as well!

While Sydney and Melbourne will be treated to the full ‘Armin Only’ experience over the long weekend, with the footage to be included on this year’s DVD and featuring the tantalizing promise of “special guests and performers,” Perth and Brisbane won’t be missing out after all because he’ll be visiting the following weekend for a standard DJ set. Last year’s ‘White Party’ tour was spectacular enough, but what surprises can we expect this time around? We’ll be bringing you more details as they come to hand, but ITM is currently cooking up a once-in-a-lifetime promotion with Future Entertainment to celebrate the tour. So keep your ears to the ground for that one!

Armin Van Buuren will be playing the following shows when he returns to Australia this June, stay tuned to ITM for info on when tickets go on sale…

Friday 6th June – ‘Armin Only’: Hordern Pavillion, Sydney
Sunday 8th June – ‘Armin Only’: Melbourne Park, Melbourne
Friday 13th June – Metro City, Perth
Saturday 14th June – Commerce Building, Brisbane
