The Presets' official afterparty plans

Angus Paterson · 26 May 2009 · inthemix

For all those who are gearing up for The Presets’ big return to Sydney this weekend, there’s a rare opportunity to party on with the band themselves after the gig at the Hordern Pavilion finishes up. The Presets Official Tour Afterparty will be going down at the Kit & Kaboodle Supper Club this Saturday 30th May from 9pm til very late, and The Presets themselves and friends will be taking over all 3 levels; Sugarmill, the Kit & Kaboodle Cocktail Bar as well as the Supper Club.

The duo, arguably Australia’s biggest band at the moment, are currently ripping through a string of tour dates across Australia, but this will make for their only afterparty appearance in Sydney. Confirmed to appear on the decks so far will be K.I.M himself, as well some special “friends” in the form of Van She Tech, AJAX, Spruce Lee and more.

The Presets Official Tour Afterparty will be hitting the Kit & Kaboodle Supper Club this Saturday 30th May from 9pm, there are limited tickets available from available through www.modularpeople.com.
