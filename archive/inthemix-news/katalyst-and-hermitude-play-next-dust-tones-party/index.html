Katalyst and Hermitude play next Dust Tones party

Angus Paterson · 21 March 2007 · inthemix

The Dust Tones series of parties are growing in popularity among Sydney-siders and coming up next up they’ve got one of their biggest lineups yet. Bringing a slice of reggae, dub and hip hop to the local scene, their party on Saturday April 21st at the Manning Bar will also feature Dust Tones residents Rephrase, Percussion Junction, Mark Walton, Noodles, Noel Boogie and Bentley.

Katalyst’s new album What’s Happening is in stores soon while the first single All You’ve Got will hot the streets in April. Rephrase’s new album Little Victories is on the shelves and gaining praise now.

Rock on down to Dust Tones on Saturday 21st April from 9pm – 3am, at the The Manning Bar, Sydney Uni. Tickets are $22.50 + BF but more on the door.
