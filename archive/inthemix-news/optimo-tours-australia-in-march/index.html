Optimo tours Australia in March

Angus Paterson · 25 February 2008 · inthemix

You’ll get to see more than 20 years worth of DJing experience put to work when Glaswegian DJ duo Optimo tour Australia in early March. Consisting of Twitch and Jonnie Wilkes, the pair began working together in 1997, at which point they’d already been DJing separately for ten years. In 1997 they founded their club night (also named Optimo) as a reaction to 4/4 techno all night long, intent on mixing things up with a more diverse music policy.

Still kicking in Glasgow with regular guest spots form the likes of LCD Soundsystem, Atom Heart, Luciano, Four Tet and Justus Kohncke, techno is still a grounding point for the pair, but in their sets you can expect to hear anything from “sleazy funk to post punk, electro, 50’s swing and anything inbetween”.

Catch the Optimo experience at the following dates…

March 6th @ 3rd Class, Melbourne
March 8th @ Mandarin, Sydney
March 9th @ Golden Plains Festival, Merrideth
