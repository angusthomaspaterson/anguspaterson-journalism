Australia’s first Q-Dance photos hit ITM

Angus Paterson · 22 March 2008 · inthemix

World-renowned hard dance event Q-Dance is visiting Australia for the very first time this Easter Long Weekend, courtesy of legendary Dutch promoters ID&T who are also responsible for the massive Sensation White series of parties. The first leg of the tour went down in Sydney on the evening of Easter Thursday – were you there to witness the audio/visual spectacular? Or are you perhaps waiting to experience it for yourself in the other states over the next few days? Check out the stunning photos HERE.

Some of the world’s finest hard dance performers were present for the first leg of Q-Dance on Thursday night – names like Donkey Rollers, The Prophet, Luna, Brennan Heart, Mark Sherry and many more. But whether you’re a hardstyle aficionado that’s already well familiar with these acts, or you just went along for the party, there was a whole lot more to enjoy than just the ridiculously intense musical selections that just refused to let up all night long. Fans were treated to spectacular light shows, flamethrowers, an over-the-top laser show as well as all the little production touches that only ID&T can offer. And with a sold out crowd packing out the 6000+ Hordern Pavilion, it showed the hard dance scene in Australia is alive, healthy and kicking.

Stay tuned to ITM for more Q Dance coverage shortly…
