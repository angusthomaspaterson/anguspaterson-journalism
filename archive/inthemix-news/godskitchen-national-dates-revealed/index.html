Godskitchen national dates revealed

Angus Paterson · 11 June 2009 · inthemix

A few weeks ago ITM revealed that the Godskitchen ‘Boombox Tour’ would be hitting Sydney and Melbourne in October, complete with the stunning piece of audiovisual kit that’s been plastered all over the net since it debuted in Ukraine earlier this year. While Sydney and Melbourne will unfortunately be the only cities who will be treated to the full ‘Boombox’ experience, both Brisbane and Perth will still be getting club shows with the full Godskitchen lineup, to give you a chance to experience things in a more intimate environment.

Trance superstars Cosmic Gate will be headlining the tour, and they’ll be joined by fellow veterans Blank & Jones, psytrance heavy John ‘00’ Fleming and it’ll also be the first ever Australian tour of JES who’s worked as a live vocalist for Tiesto.

For those in Sydney and Melbourne who are looking forward to checking out the Boombox in all its glory (or even if you’re thinking of making the trek from one of the other states) check out ITM’s gallery below…
