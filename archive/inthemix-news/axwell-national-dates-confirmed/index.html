Axwell national dates confirmed!

Angus Paterson · 25 October 2007 · inthemix

As reported on ITM last week HERE, the ‘Swedish House Mafia’ don Axwell will be returning to Australia early next year for a national tour, and we’ve now got the dates! It was only at the beginning of this year that he was last in the country, and at the time he was already a leading name in the global electro house scene. But a whole lot has happened since then to see his star rise even higher…

Launching his brand new label Axtone with the Dirty South tune Let It Go released right off the bat, it’s been his recent release I Found U that has really been turning heads. It’s part of the 90s house revival (with a modern twist of course) that was massive in Ibiza this season, and it’s been predicted to sweep the world’s clubs before too long… Whether that eventuates or not, the song is bound to be one of the year’s biggest anthems.

Catch Axwell at the following dates! And because it deserves another run, check out the clip for I Found U below…

Friday 25th January: Brisbane, The Met
Saturday 26th January: Adelaide, HQ
Sunday 27th January: Sydney, Famous @ Home
Friday 1st February: Perth, Metro
Saturday 2nd February: Melbourne, Famous @ Queensbridge
