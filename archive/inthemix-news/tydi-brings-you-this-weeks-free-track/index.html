Tydi brings you this week’s free track!

Angus Paterson · 7 July 2008 · inthemix

Six weeks out from its official release ITM brings you an exclusive sneak peak of Tydi’s debut artist album Look Closer. With the original taking the trance path, here we’ve got an exclusive remix of Running Back from UK house superstar Tom Neville, who brings a funky big room vibe.

Running Back was originally written by Tydi over 2 years ago and was discovered by the new label boss at Generationext Music in a recycle bin folder on their computer server labelled ‘18 yr old DJ – Brisbane’. Upon further investigation they found an untitled gem, which they dusted off. We think you’ll agree it was a wise decision! The single is released on August 20th with seven remixes to compliment. Extended vocal, instrumental and dub versions of the Tom Neville remix will also be available for download via the usual sites, so DJs mark it in your diary.
