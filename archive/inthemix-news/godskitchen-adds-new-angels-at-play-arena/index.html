Godskitchen adds new 'Angels At Play' arena

Angus Paterson · 7 August 2009 · inthemix

Tickets have been selling like hotcakes for Melbourne’s next Godskitchen event, which will see the debut in the city of the audio/visual spectacular that is the ‘Boombox’, and promoters Future Entertainment have just announced a second arena for the event. Featuring international guest Jes heading up the arena, she’ll be joined by current Sony inthemix50 #1 Tydi, Melbourne veteran Papa Smurf and a whole bunch of other classy local talent.

‘Angels at Play’ arena:
Jes (live)
Tydi
Papa Smurf
Bexta
Dr Willis
Trent McDermott
Ben Evans
Ahmet Atasever

Godskitchen Main Arena:
Cosmic Gate
Blank & Jones
John 00 Fleming
Steve Strangis
Chardy
Boombox Competition Winner

The Godskitchen ‘Boombox’ tour hits Melbourne Park on October 10th, check out ITM’s Festival Page for more details.
