Hybrid will be back in July

Angus Paterson · 22 June 2009 · inthemix

One of progressive house and break’s most consistent DJ/producer teams Hybrid are currently in the studio nailing down album #4 for release in early October, but between now and then they’ll be squeezing in a trip to Australia for a series of club gigs across the country. Shows in Sydney and Melbourne have already been confirmed, and you can be sure they’ll squeeze in at least a couple more performances around the country.

So how are things proceeding for the next album? “Last year saw most of album 4 arrive, get frowned at, poked about and finally abandoned as it wasn’t quite hitting the spot,” the group revealed recently on their internet blog. “One year later and it’s a different story altogether…. We’ve currently got about 33 individual tracks in varying states of completion, some have vocals, some have orchestration. Most have furiously heavy basslines and to us, feel more vibrant and alive than any of our previous work.”

Hybrid say they’ll be posting snippets of the new material this year, and have reassured us they’ll be sticking to their deadline of completion. “First week of October. No, really, this time we mean it.”

Hybrid return to Australia in July, stay tuned for full national dates…

Fri 24th July – Brown Alley, Melbourne
Sat 25th July – Chinese Laundry, Sydney
