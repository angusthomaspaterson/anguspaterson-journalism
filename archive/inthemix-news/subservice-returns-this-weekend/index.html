Subservice returns this weekend

Angus Paterson · 15 January 2008 · inthemix

Been wondering where the Subservice boys have been over the past couple of hot summer months? Well this weekend the boys are back, in a brand venue and with both Phil Smart and Ben Korbel in tow for guest performances. After a short hiatus the Subservice DJs, consisting of Dennis Williams, Dave Nolan and Andrew Wowk will be taking over the clubbing floor of the Cross in Kings Cross for another smattering of tech, minimal. Progressive, breaks and whatever other forms of underground dance music they decide to throw in there.

Subservice hits The Cross this Friday, register for discount entry at www.subservice.com.au.
