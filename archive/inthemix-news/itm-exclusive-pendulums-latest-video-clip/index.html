ITM Exclusive: Pendulum's latest video clip

Angus Paterson · 6 January 2009 · inthemix

Just in time for their high-profile slot on the Big Day Out lineup, check out the clip for Pendulum’s latest stormer! The revered Perth drum n’ bass outfit returned to the spotlight in a big way last year with their hard rockin’ follow up LP In Silico and a hard hitting live show that has obliterated audiences all over the world in the last 12 months. Australia will have the chance to be devastated again when they return to perform at the mainstage of the Big Day Out, as well as several sideshows across the country.
