Godskitchen's intimate club gig on sale now!

Angus Paterson · 1 July 2009 · inthemix

Brisbane will be getting an intimate club show in early October featuring the full Godskitchen lineup, allowing punters a chance to live out the experience in a more intimate environment. Tickets are on sale on ITM now! Trance superstars Cosmic Gate will be headlining the proceedings, and they’ll be joined by fellow veterans Blank & Jones, psytrance heavy John ‘00’ Fleming and it’ll also be the first ever Australian tour of JES who’s worked as a live vocalist for Tiesto.

Godskitchen hits the Met in Brisbane on Friday October 2nd, tickets on sale now!
