Beetroots, Deadmau5 & Axwell make mayhem in Foreshore photos

Angus Paterson · 30 November 2009 · inthemix

So you thought Stereosonic was the only big party in town over the weekend? Well you’d be wrong, as there was a mighty fine event going down in Canberra too in the form of the Foreshore Festival, a party that’s considered by many to be one of the best of its kind in Australia. Set in a pristine setting on the water with views across the lake of Parliament House, it was also sporting a damn impressive lineup this year too – Bloody Beetroots, Deadmau5, Axwell, Empire of the Sun and many more.
