Get yer set times for the inthemix50 party

Angus Paterson · 3 October 2007 · inthemix

The inthemix50 DJ Poll is now in its 5th year and we want you to help celebrate and applaud our great Aussie talent doing what they do best! The national inthemix50 tour is now on its last leg and Melbourne did better than ever this year: votes in Victoria were up a massive 37 per cent and the results were packed with local talent. As a reflection of this we’ll have some of the city’s best talent onboard this Sunday.

Your favourite DJs like Derek K, John Course, Grant Smilie, Andy Murphy and more will be joined by some of Australia’s most popular producers spinning their latest & greatest tracks, so make sure you’re there.

It all takes place at Q Bar this Sunday from 9pm. Presales are $15+BF, ITM-VIPs are $10+BF while on the door its $20. Check below for set times!

9.00-10.30: Chardy
10.30-12: Derek K
12:00-1:30: John Course
1:30-3:00: Grant Smilie
3:00-5:00: Andy Murphy
5.00-7.00: Boogs

Red Room

2.00-4.00: Acid Jacks
4.00-6.00: Miles J
