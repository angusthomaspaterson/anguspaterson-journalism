Booka Shade locked in for a LIVE performance this Easter

Angus Paterson · 30 January 2007 · inthemix

It’s been the subject of much speculation, but it can now be confirmed that one of the hottest production duos in dance at the moment will be hitting Sydney on Easter Thursday this April. Killer will be presenting Booka Shade LIVE at The Metro on Thursday April 5th in what will be their first tour of Australia.

Booka Shade are comprised of Walter Merziger and Arno Kammermeier, who together with M.A.N.D.Y. and DJ T established the minimal/tech record label Get Physical which has recently been making waves in the global dance community. They’ve turned heads with their albums Memento and Movements, won over the clubbing massive with hits like Stupid Questions and Body Language and completed a solid catalogue of remix work. They’ve been a regular inclusion in DJ sets and compilation tracklisting over the last two years, so fans can look forward to hearing them in the flesh.

Booka Shade will be hosting Killer @ The Metro on Thursday April 5th. Tickets are $39 +bf and on sale from the 13th February, check www.fuzzy.com.au for more details.
