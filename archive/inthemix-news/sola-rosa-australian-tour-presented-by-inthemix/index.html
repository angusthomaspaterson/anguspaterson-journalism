Sola Rosa Australian tour, presented by inthemix

Angus Paterson · 28 May 2007 · inthemix

Auckland’s multi genre funk/hiphop/dub/reggae/breaks/brass band Sola Rosa are coming back for another welcome jaunt of Australia, in support of their fantastic new album Moves On. And the tour is proudly brought to you by inthemix!

Moves On has already been earning them accolades from sources as diverse as their own local New Zealand street press to the Sydney Morning Herald, with inthemix’s own review of the album describing it as, “island sweet and full of funk but also effortlessly cool and ambitious in the right places.”

On the live stage, Sola Rosa is a six-piece act that features former Jah Wobble sideman Spikey Tee, and they’ll be hitting the road for four shows in July to showcase tracks from the new album, dishing up the same live performance that won them rapturous applause at their recent festival appearances in New Zealand.

Tickets are on sale on ITM next Monday June 4th from 9am, catch em at the following dates!

Thursday July 19th: The Basement, Sydney
Friday July 20th: ANU Bar, Canberra
Saturday July 21st: The Evelyn Hotel, Melbourne
Sunday July 22nd: The Espy, Gershwin Room, Melbourne
