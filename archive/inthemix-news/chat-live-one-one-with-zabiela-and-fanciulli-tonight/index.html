Chat live One+One with Zabiela & Fanciulli tonight!

Angus Paterson · 6 June 2007 · inthemix

They’ve been ripping it up at a hectic series of back-to-back gigs around the country, and tonight you’ll have your chance to talk to what’s easily the most exciting partnership in dance music at the moment. We’ll have James Zabiela and Nic Fanciulli aka One+One live and online tonight at 6pm EST, don’t miss it!

The idea for One+One was actually born in Sydney back in ‘05 on Home Nightclub’s Terrace at a legendary Renaissance party, when Zabiela and Fanciulli – already loved and respected DJs in their own right – went back to back for an epic sunrise set. The first One+One CD is in the stores and their musical union is already being compared to that of the iconic Sasha & Digweed, but we’ve been checking it out for ourselves as they jaunt across the country as part of the We Love Sounds festival.

Fanboys rejoice as ITM is hosting them tonight for ‘in the spotlight’, where you’ll get the chance to ask them whatever you fancy ahead of their festival (and afterparty) appearances over the coming Long Weekend. Everyone who logs in will have the chance to win some tasty One+One goodies, so head to inthemix.com.au/chat/inthespotlight tonight at 6pm EST to participate.
