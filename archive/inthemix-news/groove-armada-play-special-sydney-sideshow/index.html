Groove Armada play special Sydney sideshow

Angus Paterson · 7 March 2007 · inthemix

Punters will have an extra chance to get into the whole V Festival experience as one of dance music’s seminal live acts takes over The Metro. Groove Armada will be playing on Friday March 30 and for extra festival action, you can also catch Phoenix at The Factory on Thursday March 29.

When it comes to live dance acts, Groove Armada are certainly up there with the big guns like Basement Jaxx, Chemical Brothers and Underworld. Crossing the divide between underground dance culture and mainstream music listening with effortless aplomb, they blend a range of organic influences into an electronic sound. The band has shifted millions of copies of albums like Love Box and Goodbye Country (Hello Nightclub), as well as certified club smash hits like Superstylin’ and Easy. The last time we saw them in Australia was all the way back in 2004.
