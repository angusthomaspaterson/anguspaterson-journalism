DJ Roc Raida spins his last record

Angus Paterson · 21 September 2009 · inthemix

Busta Rhymes broke the sad news on the weekend via his Twitter feed – legendary hip hop DJ Roc Raida passed away on Saturday afternoon. “I am sorry 2 say that on this day at 2:05 Sept 19th we lost another incredible life…Dj Roc Raida died 2day my personal Dj is gone… I just wanna thank everyone 4 ur love and support and ur prayers…We will never let ur name die Roc…We love u and will 4ever miss u…RIP.”

Roc Raida’s family released a statement over the weekend that went into more detail on his passing. “He is survived by his wife, 3 lovely daughters, mother and friends. Raida was recently in an mixed martial arts accident, something that he has been practicing for several years. Although he had under gone 2 surgeries with great success, was released to an inpatient physical therapy facility and was in great spirits the past few days. This morning he started to have complications and passed.”

New York native Roc Raida has been a legendary figure in hip hop culture since the early 90s, famous for his part in The X-Ecutioners collective. In 1995 he famously won the DMC World DJ Championship, inducted into the DMC hall of fam several years later and his last solo album was the 2007 effort Beats, Cuts and Skits.
