Sydney gets an Afterlife

Angus Paterson · 21 August 2009 · inthemix

If you were looking for somewhere to party after Parklife, you’ve now got the perfect choice. The lineup and details for the official afterparty Afterlife have just been announced, and it’s a doozy of a lineup indeed featuring Busy P, MSTRKRFT, a DJ set from The Rapture and more in the main room.

This will be the place to be for all those who managed to get a ticket to the sold out festival, or those who missed out but still want to sample a few tasty Parklife delights.

Afterlife hits The Metro on Sunday 4th October from 10pm. inthemix is a proud presenting partner of Parklife in 2009! Keep your eyes peeled to our Festival Page at inthemix.com.au/parklife.
