Jamie Jones lifts the lid on Hot Natured's debut album

Angus Paterson · 22 July 2013 · inthemix

“Personally I’m very very proud that with the Hot Natured band project we were able to touch so many people with this record. But I also want to assure people that this in NO way selling out,” house hero Jamie Jones assured his fans on his Facebook page late last year, shortly after the group smashed into the Top 40 with their hit Benediction. Last week, Jones also revealed details of the group’s debut album that we can expect soon.

“The album’s coming out the first week of the September and we handed in the masters last week,” Jones told Mixmag, speaking of the group he’d formed with Hot Creations label partner Lee Foss along with Ali Love and Luca C. “We had too many tracks but managed to squeeze about 15 onto the album with only like 30 seconds to spare.”

The LP will be called Different Sides Of The Sun and will arrive on September 2nd. While Benediction makes a repeat appearance, they’ve also revealed the next single that goes by the name of Isis (Magic Carpet Ride). Take a listen below.

Entering the zeitgeist along with Disclosure as one of the next generation of house acts who have cracked the mainstream, Hot Natured have banded together to play a series of high-profile live gigs including Brixton Academy in London, Glastonbury and Sonar. At the end of the year, they’ll make their debut trek as a team to Australia for the Hot Creations arena at Stereosonic. Jones also spoke to Mixmag about the pressure of taking the group out of the studio.

“The first few shows we did at Brixton Academy were the most nerve-wracking things I’ve ever done. It’s really cool, in a way it’s easier because I know all of the songs, I don’t have to get any of the music ready, we have a set amount of songs and the more I do it the more confident I get playing my bass parts.”
