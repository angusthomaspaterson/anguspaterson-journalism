Fat Freddy's Drop album shakedown tour

Angus Paterson · 25 August 2009 · inthemix

Premier New Zealand funk/soul act Fat Freddy’s Drop received a ridiculously warm response for their sophomore Dr Boondigga & The Big BW album, hitting Platinum in their home country within four days and landing the #1 spot on the charts for five weeks straight, and they’ll be bringing it back to Australia for a quick pit-stop of our major cities this October.

It’ll be their third visit to Australia this year, having played the inaugural Days Like This! festival in January before they gave listeners a sneak peak of the new album during their tour in March, so there’s plenty enthusiasm in our country for what Fat Freddy’s Drop do. Come and see what all the fuss is about…

Fat Freddy’s Drop tour dates:

Friday 16 October – The Palace, Melbourne
Saturday 17 October – The Forum, Sydney
Sunday 18 October – The Forum, Sydney
