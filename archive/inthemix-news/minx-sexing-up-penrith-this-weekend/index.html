Minx sexing up Penrith this weekend

Angus Paterson · 3 December 2007 · inthemix

Minx is a brand new bar and dance venue within the Panthers complex in Penrith, and it’ll be firing up this weekend for the very first time! It’ll be catering to the area’s growing dance market, bringing a proper clubbing experience to those who don’t want to travel all the way to the city on the weekend. The organisers say to expect a steady stream of some of Australia’s finest DJs, with a bunch of live acts thrown into the mix as well.

The interior of Minx features a huge dance floor, stage big enough to accommodate bands and live performances, plenty of intimate seating areas as well as a gaming section complete with plasma screens playing the latest music videos and live sport. The opening night this weekend will feature a feline dance group taking centre stage, and the Jim Beam Party Crew will also be along for heaps of giveaways.

“We’re really excited to provide a safe, secure environment for the young people in the area to come and enjoy a great night out with their friends,” says Panthers general manager Don Stewart. “We want to make this the ultimate meeting place on a Friday and Saturday night and there has been plenty of thought put into the layout and theme.”

Minx will be open every Friday and Saturday night from December 8, check out www.minxatpanthers.com.au for more info.
