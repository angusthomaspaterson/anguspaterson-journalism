The Prodigy play sideshows for FMF! Presented by ITM

Angus Paterson · 30 November 2009 · inthemix

The big guns at the top of the lineup for this year’s Future Music Festival were a drawcard for many, and with the festival close to selling out across the country, fans will be overjoyed with the news they’ll have another chance to catch them doing their thing at sideshows. There will be shows across Sydney, Melbourne as well as Newcastle, with next-gen rock/dance kids Does it Offend You Yeah? providing the able support for the veterans.

If there was ever any doubt that The Prodigy are the most elite live act performing in dance music today, these were shattered when they returned after a four-year break earlier this year to play the Boiler Room, with brute force to spare. Packing tunes from their new album Invaders Must Die, they dominated every show they played and showed they’ve got just as much energy as they did in the early 90s. Suffice to say, plenty were happy with the news they’d be coming back in early 2010.

The Prodigy play sideshows for Future Music Festival, presented by inthemix. Tickets on sale midday Dec 10th, check out the dates below:

Wed 3rd Mar – Hordern Pavilion, Sydney
Thu 4th Mar – Hisense Arena (GA Mode), Melbourne
Wed 10th Mar – Entertainment Centre, Newcastle
