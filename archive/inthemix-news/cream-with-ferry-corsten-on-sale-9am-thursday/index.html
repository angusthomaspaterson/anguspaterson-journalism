Cream with Ferry Corsten on sale 9am Thursday

Angus Paterson · 15 July 2009 · inthemix

Trance fans around the country were shocked in the best possible way last week with the news that Ferry Corsten would be here with the Cream brand in September for his Twice In A Blue Moon: The Experience tour, and for all those who are dying to have their place on the dancefloor, tickets are on sale at ITM from 9am on Thursday morning! We announced last week we’d be selling tickets for Brisbane and Melbourne, and ITM has now been able to secure a limited allocation for Sydney as well so make sure you’re back at the site on Thursday morning to grab your tickets.

The Cream shows in September will showcase Dutch trance veteran Ferry Corsten’s first ‘live’ performance as a DJ/Producer, featuring the vocal talents of Betsie Larkin as well as the promise of ‘spectacular show elements’ and an ‘unprecedented visual experience’. It was first launched in May in this year and has since rocked thousands of clubbers worldwide, so it’s set to be a massive reintroduction of the Cream brand to Australian audiences!

He’ll be joined by Italian trance maestro Giuseppe Ottaviani playing live, in support of his new artist album G!, and as one of the most accomplished producers working in the genre he’ll make for a more than worthy support for Mr Corsten.

Cream September tour dates:

Thu Sep 10 – Brisbane, Family
Fri Sep 11 – Melbourne, Palace Metro Theatre
Sat Sep 12 – Sydney, Hordern Pavilion

Tickets are onsale at ITM 9am Thursday July 16th, so make sure you’re back here at inthemix! Check out the tour trailer for Twice In A Blue Moon: The Experience below…
