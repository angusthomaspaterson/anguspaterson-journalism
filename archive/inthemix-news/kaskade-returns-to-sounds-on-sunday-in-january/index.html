Kaskade returns to Sounds on Sunday in January

Angus Paterson · 15 December 2006 · inthemix

After packing the Greenwood out earlier in the year to the point where it was bursting at the seams, US house legend Kaskade will be returning to play the first Sounds on Sunday of the New Year on the 7th of January.

Kaskade is already a familiar name to Sydney party goers, a regular fixture at Fuzzy events like Field Day and Parklife, well known for his club hits like It’s You, It’s Me, Steppin’ Out and Gonna Make It. He has played a massive 150+ shows this year and also released his third studio album Love Mysterious.

Stay tuned to ITM for presale ticketing details.
