Best of Trance Energy on inthemix

Angus Paterson · 16 April 2009 · inthemix

inthemix had photographers at every show of Trance Energy’s massive debut tour last weekend, with several galleries from each city telling the story of the spectacular experience that unfolded in all its laser-fuelled glory. And we’ve scoured through the pages and pages of pics to find the best of the bunch from events nationwide, putting together some extra special ‘Best Of…’ Snap! galleries for you to feast your eyes on.

Trance Energy whipped through the country quicker than you could say ‘euphoric breakdown’, but that doesn’t mean we have to forget about all the lights, lasers and special effects just yet. Check out the best ‘People’, ‘Party’ and ‘Artist’ shots by following the links below!
