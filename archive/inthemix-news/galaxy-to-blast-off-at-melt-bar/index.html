Galaxy to blast off at Melt Bar

Angus Paterson · 15 March 2007 · inthemix

Saturday March 31st will see the launch of Galaxy, a night of interstellar boogie and cosmic disco that promises some fabulous international guests playing in a totally different context. Melt Bar will host a night that draws inspiration from the ethos of David Mancuso’s legendary New York Loft party, providing people with the perfect underground disco vibe featuring classics of yesterday & today.

The organisers emphasise that the music will come first, bringing a positive and open-minded attitude. Secret and unannounced international guests are promised, and they will be given the chance to play an eclectic personal set they would often not get the chance to play, but still with their eye always firmly on the dance floor. An esteemed set of local DJs will also be clocking to keep the party going all night.

Galaxy blasts off at Melt Bar on Saturday March 31st, the lineup is as follows…
Simon Caldwell
Joe Stanley
Better Days Sound System
