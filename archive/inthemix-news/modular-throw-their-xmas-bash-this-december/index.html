Modular throw their Xmas bash this December

Angus Paterson · 19 November 2009 · inthemix

While it’s looking like the Nevereverland festival will be sitting it out this year, that doesn’t mean the crew from Modular are going to pass up the opportunity to throw a big party before the end of 2009. Of course not, that would just be ridiculous and hugely out of character. Instead, we’ll be getting some tasty end-of-year party action in the form of the Modular Xmas Party.

Landing in both Sydney and Melbourne, it’ll feature a selection of the label’s latest and greatest coming together, with names like Ladyhawke, Bang Gang, Cut Copy and Muscles, as well as some super fresh faces too.

Sydney, Saturday December 12th, The Beach Road Hotel.
