Deekline and Wizard breakin’ it in the New Year

Angus Paterson · 12 December 2007 · inthemix

One of the biggest success stories in breaks in 2007, Deekline and Wizard have capitalized on the earlier success of their Breaks Beats and Blondes album on the Botchit and Scarper label and taken up permanent residency in the boxes of DJs from around the world. You’ll know them from their remixes of Armand Van Helden’s My My My, Ol’ Dirty Bastard’s Got Your Money and Stanton Warriors’ Dip and Get Low, as well as a solid string of solo hits.

Pushing a Miami ‘booty bass’ vibe along with the nu-skool breaks sound, the boys also mash a number of other styles into the mix like electro, grime, ragga and punk rock. But you’ll be able to see for yourself when they arrive just in time for the New Year shenanigans!

Catch Deekline and Wizard at the following shows, and stay tuned for info on when they’ll reach Melbourne!

31st Dec – Perth – Metropolis City
10th Jan – Goldcoast – Berlin Lounge
11th Jan – Brisbane – GPO
12th Jan – Dust Tones, Sydney
