Last Field Day & Shore Thing tickets up for grabs from ITM!

Angus Paterson · 15 December 2006 · inthemix

Missed out on tickets to the hottest Fuzzy events of the summer? Here’s your chance to win yourself entry into both big-ticket New Year events.

Field Day and Shore Thing both sold out in record time, and crazed punters all over the city are falling over each other in a bid to secure tickets. inthemix is offering one lucky punter the chance to win themselves entry into both events and is giving away a massive prize pack that includes one double pass to Shore Thing feat basement Jaxx and one double VIP pass to Field Day.

Field Day is without a doubt one of the most anticipated events on the Sydney clubbing calendar and the 2007 installment looks set to continue that reputation, with a massive lineup of house, breaks, electro and a stack of live acts. And after bringing the Fatboy Slim experience to the iconic Bondi Beach last NYE, Fuzzy look set to repeat the success with Basement Jaxx this time bringing in the New Year.

To win the prize pack, all you have to do is tell us in 25 words or less why you deserve to win the tickets. Click here to enter.
