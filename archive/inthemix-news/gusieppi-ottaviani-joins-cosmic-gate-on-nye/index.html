Gusieppi Ottaviani joins Cosmic Gate on NYE

Angus Paterson · 9 November 2007 · inthemix

If you were looking forward to rockin’ it on New Year’s Eve with Cosmic Gate, things have just ramped up a tad: Italian trance star Gusieppi Ottaviani has just been added to the lineup! He’ll be playing an exclusive live set, and showing the world why he’s increasingly becoming one of the most loved names in trance.

That’s along with the already announced drawcard for the event, seminal DJ/production duo Cosmic Gate. The Tranceport NYE party will feature them playing a 4-hour set, and it’ll be the only Sydney show they’ll be playing while on their world tour promoting their upcoming album.

It all takes place at Space on NYE, tickets are available now on ITM!
