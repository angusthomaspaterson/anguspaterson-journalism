Iemma threatens to throw the book at Big Day Out

Angus Paterson · 23 January 2007 · inthemix

The Big Day Out ‘flag ban’ controversy rolls on today, with the NSW Premier Morris Iemma claiming he is seeking legal action to overturn the organiser’s decision to discourage punters from taking Australian flags to the event on Thursday. This is in spite of a statement issued yesterday claiming a ban had never been instituted in the first place, labeling it as “a request, not a command.”

But this didn’t stop Iemma pushing on with statements to the media, where he encouraged those attending the Sydney Big Day Out to ignore attempts to prevent them showing the flag. “You can’t stop people from showing pride in their country in this way. Kids will have the tattoo on their nose, they’ll have the T-shirts and they’ll fly the flag. It’s an insult to all Australians and they ought to withdraw it immediately,” he told the Sydney Morning Herald.
