New Young Pony Club postpone tour

Angus Paterson · 23 July 2007 · inthemix

The good news is that the London indie/electro crossover act New Young Pony Club has picked up a nomination for the prestigious Mercury Music Prize. The bad news is that it means the band’s forthcoming return to Australia to launch their Fantastic Playroom will have to be postponed until later in the year.

When they do eventually return to follow up their performances at the inaugural V Festival earlier this year, the band is still expected to play dates across Perth, Adelaide, Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne.

New dates will be announced shortly, and Fantastic Playroom will be available through iTunes on July 28 then record stores from Aug 11. Meanwhile, have a look at the clip for their single Ice Cream.
