Sweet Chilli returns to rock the Civic

Angus Paterson · 7 March 2007 · inthemix

‘Minichilli’ will be firing up again this Friday night as Sydney’s premier progressive house team take over the Civic for yet another FREE party. The Sweet Chilli series of parties are known for inspiring a level of devotion among their fans that can compete with anything else that Sydney clubbing has to offer, and punters will be given another chance to see the boys hit the decks before they host a stage at next week’s Future Music Festival.

Sweetchilli is giving away three double passes to Future Music Festival on the night, all people have to do to enter is register for ‘Free Entry’ online. All the usual residents will be spinning tunes as well as welcoming a newbie to the fold, Luke Stoker.

The party is FREE before 12.30am if you send your names to minichilli@sweetchilli.com.au, check below for set times…

Crispin: 10-12am
Carlos Zarate: 12-2am
Luke Stoker: 2 – 3:30am
Daniel Crocetti: 3:30-Close
