Sasha's Chinese Laundry gig now sold out

Angus Paterson · 1 September 2009 · inthemix

One of dance music’s most enigmatic figures Sasha will be back in Australia in November for a highly-anticipated tour that will see him taking control of some of our more intimate venues, with one of these of course including his extended set at the Chinese Laundry in Sydney – and word has just come in that the gig is completely sold out.

For those who don’t already have tickets, the club will be reopening its doors to the public at 11pm when Sasha’s set has finished, to give punters the chance to see Rico Tubbs along with the other surprise international act (the source of much speculation indeed as to who the identity may be).

Sasha plays Chinese Laundry, Sydney on Saturday 21st November. Tickets now completely sold out!
