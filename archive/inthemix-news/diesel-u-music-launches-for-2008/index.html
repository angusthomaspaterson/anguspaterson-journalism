Diesel:U:Music launches for 2008

Angus Paterson · 22 May 2008 · inthemix

An initiative aimed at unearthing and breaking quality new music and now back in its 8th year, Diesel:U:Music is this year launching a international platform as part of an effort to reach out to new audiences. Submissions are now being taken and music from across all musical genres is welcome, with the organisers offering entrants the promise of connecting with a community of worldwide musicians, information on how to establish a music career as well as the tools to get out there.

The chosen winner from the Australian entrants will have the chance to take part in the Diesel’s 30th Anniversary party in Sydney this October. Past winners of the Diesel:U:Music have included Mylo, Duke Dumont, DJ Yoda and more.

Check out www.diesel-u-music.com for more details
