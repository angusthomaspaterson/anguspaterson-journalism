Editorial Assistant internship up for grabs at ITM!

Angus Paterson · 4 August 2009 · inthemix

The first V Raw internship offered at inthemix a few months back got a huge response, and now it’s time to offer up the second for the 2009 program. How would you like to join our Sydney team at ITM HQ as an Editorial Assistant intern? If you’re looking to launch your career in music journalism or editing, then it’s an opportunity you won’t want to miss.

The internship will see you working closely with the Editor and Deputy Editor to manage the national contributor base, as well as assist in sub-editing and approval of articles submitted through our content management system. We’re looking for someone with sharp editorial skills and a strong grasp of the English language, but most importantly, someone who also has a broad understanding, knowledge and passion for music and dance culture. A typical ITMer, in other words!
