Stereosonic lineup lands 8am Thursday!

Angus Paterson · 5 August 2009 · inthemix

THE LINEUP IS RELEASED – GET THE FULL STORY HERE!

The Stereosonic tour reaches its third edition in November, with promoters Hardware and Onelove taking it to five major cities for the very first time, and it’s sure to be the biggest festival ticket for the latter months of the year. For those who’ve been hanging out for the lineup announcement, it’s not long to go now as it’ll be announced on ITM at 8am Thursday morning!

For all of the latest rumours and leaks for the tour, check out our Stereosonic Festival Page. Be sure to bookmark the page, as that’s where you’ll read all the latest details on this year’s tour first. See you back here at 8am!

Stereosonic national tour dates:

Sat 28th November – Sydney
Sun 29th November – Perth
Sat 5th December – Adelaide
Sat 5th December – Melbourne
Sun 6th December – Brisbane
