Shore Thing completely sold out!

Angus Paterson · 9 October 2007 · inthemix

Don’t say we didn’t warn you… ITM reported a few weeks ago HERE that tickets to Shore Thing were selling extremely swiftly, and with more than two and a half months to go before the actual event, Fuzzy’s NYE extravaganza has already sold out of tickets.

For those that did manage to grab tickets in time, they’ll be gearing up for the third consecutive NYE party at Bondi, with much-loved UK live act Groove Armada at the helm for this year’s installment. The Soundboy Rock crew will also be joined by local mega-successful pop act Sneaky Sound System.

Missed out on your chance to spend NYE at Bondi? Stay tuned to ITM as all the other New Year announcements come flying in thick and fast.
