High-Jinks fires up a triple header for the silly season

Angus Paterson · 13 December 2006 · inthemix

After keeping a low profile throughout 2006, High-Jinks returns for a combined third birthday and Christmas party bonanza at the Sly Fox in late December.

The party takes place at the Sly Fox Hotel in Enmore on Saturday 23rd December and is being billed as three parties at once – the 3rd birthday of High-Jinks, a Christmas celebration as well as a kid’s party! Naturally, those attending are expected to stay in tune with the festive atmosphere by dressing up as something to do with the number three, something Christmassy, a kid, or all three at once for those that are really keen. Prizes are promised for those wearing the best costumes.

Old favourites of the High-Jinks parties will return: a ‘Sleazy Santa’ has been invited, there will be a few rounds of ‘Booty Techno Musical Chairs’ as well as a Pass-The-Parcel around the whole pub! Throw in the fact that the timing will mean that many people will be 24 hours into a 6-day bender and you have a sure-fire recipe for disaster.

Providing the soundtrack will be the ‘High-Jinks Three’ – Some Freak, Señor Spitch and K. Bleisner. Accompanying them will be Ding, G-Mo and Tom Smythe, billed by the organisers as both excellent music folk and solid drinkers.

Entry is free and the party kicks on from 7pm-3am, with cheap cocktails from 7-9.
