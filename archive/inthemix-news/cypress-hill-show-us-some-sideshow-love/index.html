Cypress Hill show us some sideshow love

Angus Paterson · 8 January 2008 · inthemix

They’re one of the big drawcards on the Good Vibrations tour that’s traveling across Australia this February, and it’s time to roll that roach and load up that conepiece because Cypress Hill will be playing sideshows right around the country.

Responsible for some of the most classic tunes that we’ve ever heard in Latino hip hop, as well as their widely-known (and advertised) love for err… a little bit of the green stuff, Cypress Hill hail from South Central LA and have kept the hits rolling since 93’s landmark, multi-million selling Black Sunday album. When the Australian tour rolls around in February they’ll be emerging from the studio, where they’ve been holed up and working with Mike Shinoda from Linkin Park. Word on the street is we can expect a new album in 2008!

Catch Cypress Hill at the following sideshows, with special guests across the different states that include Pharoahe Monch and Lyrics Born!

Wednesday 13 February, 2008 – Enmore Theatre, Sydney with special guest Pharoahe Monch
Thursday, 14 February, 2008 – HQ Complex, Adelaide with special guest Pharoahe Monch
Friday, 15 February, 2008 – Palace Theatre, Melbourne with special guest Lyrics Born
