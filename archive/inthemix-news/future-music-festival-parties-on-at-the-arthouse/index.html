Future Music Festival parties on at The Arthouse

Angus Paterson · 25 February 2009 · inthemix

Looking to kick on after the party winds up at Randwick Racecourse this weekend? If you’re keen on catching some of the Future Music Festival’s excellent acts in a more intimate setting then it looks like The Arthouse will be the place to be, with minimal/tech legend Stephan Bodzin locked for a performance as well as “Special Guests” who’ll be announced on the day of the festival.

Whoever could they be? Speculation is rife, but the promoters say that on will be revealed on Saturday.

Paul Strange Presents the Official Future Music Afterparty at The Arthouse this Saturday 28th February, tickets on sale now on ITM.
