Want $10,000 to put on your dream party?

Angus Paterson · 7 September 2009 · inthemix

We’re sure there are plenty of promoters out there who are confident they could throw a ridiculously over-the-top awesome party, if only they had the cash to do it properly. So now’s your chance to show what you’ve got with the Smirnoff Promoter Grants scheme, which will be awarding a hefty $10,000 to the up-and-coming nightlife promoter who’s able to devise the most amazing event concept!

The initiative is aimed at anyone who reckons they have a good idea for a party, and will reward those with the most original and forward-thinking ideas. And Smirnoff have enlisted the help of some industry heavyweights to decide who is most worthy of receiving all that dosh! The judging panel will include Johnathan Wall from Fuzzy, Brett Robinson from Future Entertainment as well as ITM’s own General Manager Tim Hardaker! If you can sell your idea to these guys, chances are you you’ll be able to sell it to the punters too.

Applications for the Smirnoff Promoter Grant scheme are open now. Check out Smirnoff’s website for more information, and get brainstorming!
