Limited round of tickets available to Big Day Out

Angus Paterson · 7 January 2010 · inthemix

If you’re still breathless from New Years, no rest for the wicked as the mammoth Big Day Out tour is just around the corner, catering to rock, dance and general music lovers alike. For those who wanted to get along to the second Sydney show on the 23rd January but missed out on tickets, you’re in luck because there’s a limited round available now. It’s your final chance to get tickets and is comprised completely of incomplete and rejected ticket transactions, so get in fast.

The Big Day Out festival will be celebrating its 100th show this year, and to mark the momentous occasion, organisers have confirmed that Sydney fans will be treated to a special fireworks spectacular at the close of each night. Simply hang around after headliners Muse exit the main stage for your chance to check it out.

Get your tickets to the January 23rd Big Day Out in Sydney at Ticketmaster now, and check out our Festival Page for plenty of news, interviews, videos and more.
