Elefant Traks descends on Bondi Beach

Angus Paterson · 4 July 2007 · inthemix

One of Australia’s seminal hip hop and alternative beats labels Elefant Traks will be stampeding through the Eastern Suburbs of Sydney right throughout July with special shows at the Beach Road Hotel every Friday night.

The four shows across the month of July will be hosting performances from A-Grade local acts of the likes of Hermitude, Astronomy Class, Urthboy, Unkle Ho, Kobra Kai and the best thing about it is that all nights are free. Things kick off with the support DJs from 8pm while the live kicks off from 10pm.

Get amongst it at the Beach Road Hotel every Friday night during July, lineup details are below…

July 6 – Hermitude + Dj Prince V
July 13 – Astronomy Class + Dj Prince V
July 20 – Urthboy + Unkle Ho + Dj Diaz + Dj Prince V
July 27 – Kobra Kai + Dj Skoob
