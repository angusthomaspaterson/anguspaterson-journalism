Field Day set times arrive

Angus Paterson · 21 December 2009 · inthemix

Planning on spending your New Years Day out in the sunny surrounds of The Domain? Sydney favourite Field Day returns in the opening hours of 2010, sporting a typically heavy lineup with the likes of 2manyDJs, Major Lazer, LCD Soundsystem, Fake Blood, Krafty Kuts and more all set to help provide the musical backdrop to what’s been a huge success since it fired up in 2002. If you’ll be returning for another dose in January 1st, check out the set times and map so you can start planning out your musical adventures…
