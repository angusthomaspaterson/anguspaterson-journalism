Harbourlife photos hit ITM

Angus Paterson · 22 November 2009 · inthemix

Sydney experienced absolutely scorching weather conditions over the weekend, so what better time to get out there and amongst it for an outdoor harbourside party? Fuzzy’s annual Harbourlife event returned on the weekend to entertain a sold-out crowd who boogied under the beating sun, and the first photos have arrived on inthemix.

Harbourlife photos, Sydney

Harbourlife sold out in record time again this year, showing that demand is just as high as ever to party in the stunning harbourside surrounds of Mrs Macquarie’s Chair near the Royal Botanic Gardens. While it’s a a party that has always been more about the stunning harbourside view than it is about the lineup, this year’s featured a respectable showing from breakbeat duo the Stanton Warriors and house master Martin Solveig, both who reportedly turned in excellent performances. Local acts like Beni, KIM and Bag Raiders were also given an impressive showing this year, as well as deep house stalwart Illya who returned for an early afternoon set.
