Rogue Element rocks back to Australia

Angus Paterson · 13 June 2008 · inthemix

It’s not long now… Just the thing to get you over your post long weekend blues, the Rogue Element will be back next weekend to rock the country in his inimitable style. After a successful tour last year, he’s once again hitting our shores for a spell of dates for the Destination? crew. A multiple Breakspoll award winner, this year will see the release of his second studio album with his debut of course nominated for Breakspoll’s ‘Best Album’ following its release in late 2005.

Rogue Element’s releases have received support from heavy-hitting likes of Plump DJs, Rennie Pilgrim, Hyper, Stanton Warriors and more, along with memorable remixes for acts like Krafty Kuts, Crystal Method, Dylan Rhymes and Tom Real. We all may be in tretreat following last weekend’s efforts, but here’s a reason to get out there again!

Check out the Rogue Element at the following dates…

20th June – Ambar, Perth
21st June – Destination?: The Cross, Sydney
27th June – Mixed Messages: Candy Bar, Melbourne
28th June – Halo, Hobart
4th July – Breaks and Enter: GPO, Brisbane
