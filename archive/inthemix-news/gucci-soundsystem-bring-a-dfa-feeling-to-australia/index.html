Gucci Soundsystem bring a DFA feeling to Australia

Angus Paterson · 14 January 2009 · inthemix

DFA Records signing Ben Rymer will be visiting Australia for the very first time later this month, performing under the name Gucci Soundsystem, a project that he forms with fellow DJ/producer partner Riton. While he’s built himself up a reputation as a DJ/producer in the vein of LCD Soundsystem’s indie/electro/disco fusion, he’s just as well known for his successful London club night Druzzis Baltimore Rave Club that’s been packing it out for over four years now.

With such guests as luminaries Tiefschwarz, James Murphy, Andrew Weatherall, Ivan Smagge, 2 many DJ’s, Erol Alkan, Optimo and more regularly getting behind the decks as guest performers, you’d assume this would function as a fairly excellent vote of confidence in what the Gucci Soundsystem do?

Gucci Soundsystem tour dates…

January 23rd – Bandits, Sydney
January 24th – Pulse Radio Boat Party, Sydney (daytime)
January 24th – La Di Da, Melbourne (night)
January 29th – Edingborough Castle, Adelaide
January 30th – Elsewhere, Gold Coast
January 31st – Lick It @ Barsoma, Brisbane
