Deep As Fu#k relaunches this weekend

Angus Paterson · 13 June 2008 · inthemix

Ready for a weekly dose of minimal, tech, glitch and all things underground, in one of Sydney’s finest intimate clubbing spaces? One of Sydney’s finest sources of underground sounds Deep As Fu#k is relaunching this Saturday night under the new moniker of Deep Impressions. Taking place at The Bunker in Kings Cross for the minimal price of $10, the launch for the new monthly night will feature Juri Menicucci from Italy, who’s built a reputation in his adopted home of the UK for his energetic techno sets and his work with Stream Recordings.

What’s more, the organisers are promising to transform The Bunker into an environment akin to DC10, or a Hieronymus Bosch painting – whichever you’re more familiar with! It’ll make for some excellent post long weekend fun.

Deep As Fu#k presents Deep Impressions, at The Bunker underneath The Bourbon on Saturday 14 June. Kicking off at 10pm, it’s only $10 on the door.
