We Love Sounds sellout imminent

Angus Paterson · 25 May 2007 · inthemix

Haven’t got your tickets yet to the biggest festival happening this side of the summer season? Then get cracking cause it’s about to sell out this weekend. The organisers of We Love Sounds have issued a friendly warning to punters that there a less than 300 tickets reaming at the time of writing, and there will be no further release of tickets or any available on the door. All remaining tickets are through Ticketek.

If you miss out then never fear, because you’ll have the chance to catch headliners DJ Shadow and Tiga courtesy of ITM at their only Sydney sideshows, playing respective gigs at Home on Friday June 1st and at Yu on Thursday June 7th. Tickets are on sale now.

The Sounds crew will also be releasing details next week on a double header of festival after parties at Yu and The Cross on the Sunday night, so stay tuned to ITM for details.

Grab your tickets while you still can…
