ITM's winter festival action - the photos are in!

Angus Paterson · 9 June 2009 · inthemix

In keeping with tradition, the June Long Weekend went off again around the country with what is arguably the strongest lineup of dance artists that we enjoy all year, covering off everything from trance, progressive, techno, house, electro and more. While the biggest drawcard was world #1 DJ Armin van Buuren, he was supported by a massive cast of players that included Armand Van Helden, Laidback Luke, Chris Lake, Justin Martin, Popof, Guy Gerber, Guns N Bombs and many more.

From We Love Sounds in Sydney and Adelaide, Winter Sound System in Melbourne, Warehouse in Canberra to some massive club gigs in Perth and Brisbane, ITM was there to cover every last key event so check out the photos below…
