Clare from Kings Cross dominates May's most read on inthemix!

Angus Paterson · 2 June 2009 · inthemix

In what’s traditionally a fairly slow month on the news and announcements front, as temperatures start to drop and everybody keeps their eye on the approaching parties over the June Long Weekend, May saw interest piqued among ITMers by someone who became known as the ‘Chk Chk Boom’ girl. Apparently witnessing what was actually quite a violent shooting in Sydney’s busy clubbing district of Kings Cross, 19-year-old Clare Werbeloff ran up to a Ninemsn/Channel 9 video reporter and gave her decidedly ‘bogan’ account of the incident. While it was later revealed that she never actually saw the shooting, her account quickly took on a life of its own.

ITM’s notorious internet meme competition took out both the #1 and #2 places for most read stories on inthemix, with the original story on the Kings Cross shooting that linked to the ‘witness account’ video coming in at #5, proving that Clare is indeed the Corey Worthington of 2009. While ITM promises we won’t be covering any further her rise to the top of the D-grade celebrity pile (unless she does something totally awesome like covers a Beastie Boys single or something), it sure was fun while it lasted… actually, make that hilarious. Just check out some of those memes!

Other popular stories included a raft of lineup leaks for the Parklife national tour later this year, as well as news on the Defqon.1 Festival (including the legendary ‘MCG streaker’ attempt to win ITM’s Maximum Exposure Competition), showing there’s a whole lot of hunger for news on events that are due to hit when the weather starts warming up again. ITM’s ‘Oldest DJs still in the game’ list also elicited more than a few laughs, coming in at #3.
