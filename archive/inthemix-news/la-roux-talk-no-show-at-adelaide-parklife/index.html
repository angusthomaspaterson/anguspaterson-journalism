La Roux talk no-show at Adelaide Parklife

Angus Paterson · 9 October 2009 · inthemix

One of the most hyped and talked about acts on this year’s Parklife lineup was La Roux, whose electronic pop sounds and blew up big since the lineup was announced back in June. Frontwoman Elly Jackson won punters over all around the country, with the exception of Adelaide last Monday, which sadly missed out on a performance. Wondering what happened to the popsters? This week Elly spoke out on why Adelaide was denied a La Roux performance. She’d been struck down a nasty virus when performing in Sydney, it seems!

“To everyone that came to see us at Parklife Adelaide, I’m so, so sorry that we had to cancel the show,” ellu said on La Roux’s Facebook page this week. “I got really sick in Sydney and couldn’t make it to the last stop on the tour. Had a temperature, a sore throat, the lot. We’d had such a great time in Australia and it was such a crappy way to have to end it. I’m really sorry if we disappointed anyone. We’ll come and see you next time we’re down under.”
