Axwell mixing Ministry of Sound's 'Clubbers Guide to 2010'

Angus Paterson · 15 December 2009 · inthemix

It certainly seems that Swedish House Mafioso Axwell has a lot of love for Australia. In addition to wowing crowds on the recent Stereosonic Festival, he’s also chosen to make Australia his home over the summer, hanging around in Bondi for several months to soak up the sunshine as well as playing a special gig at Pacha on New Years Eve alongside Sebastian Ingrosso. But that’s not all – he’s also been recruited by the Ministry of Sound to mix a disco on the upcoming Clubbers Guide to 2010!

As if that wasn’t enough of a selling point, he’ll be joined by Sydney DJ/producer whiz and MOS favourite Hook N Sling on the second disc, along with third disc that’s been mixed by Anna Lunoe – who takes the honours as the first ever female to mix a compilation for the Australian Ministry of Sound crew!

The Ministry say to expect a tracklisting of super, super fresh of-the-moment tunes, featuring artists like Miami Horror, Chuckie, Claude Vonstroke, Crookers, Sharam Fake Blood, Major Lazer, The Bloody Beetroots, Miike Snow, Bag Raiders and John Dahlback. And with 63 tracks across three CDs, you can expect whole lot more than that still…
