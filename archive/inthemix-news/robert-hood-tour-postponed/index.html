Robert Hood tour postponed

Angus Paterson · 21 September 2009 · inthemix

Those who were waiting patiently for October’s tour with minimal techno legend Robert Hood will be forced to endure an even longer wait, with promoters Finely Tuned informing us that the US DJ/producer has suffered a personal tragedy, and will no longer be able to make his shows.

“It is my deepest regret that I have to postpone my trip to Australia & Asia in October due to a family tragedy that means I cannot stay away from home for a tour,” Hood said in a statement. “There has been a tragic accident which I have to help grieving family members with.”

Finely Tuned have promised they’ll reveal a replacement act shortly for the dates, and have claimed Robert Hood will be back in December for a replacement tour.
