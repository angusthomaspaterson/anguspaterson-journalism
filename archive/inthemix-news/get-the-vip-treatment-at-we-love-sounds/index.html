Get the VIP treatment at We Love Sounds

Angus Paterson · 21 May 2007 · inthemix

ITM VIP members are used to getting pampered and the upcoming Sydney winter festival We Love… Sounds will be no different, offering both a space to chill out as well as to get up close and personal with the performers.

inthemix will be running a VIP area that accommodates both the artists playing at We Love… Sounds as well as members the ITM VIP program. It will be an ideal place to take a break from the festival action, to catch up with your ITM friends away from the crowds, or even just to grab an opportunity to hang out with the artists playing on the day.

Because the venue already holds great toilet facilities there won’t be separate bathrooms, but it’ll have its own bar and a beautifully decorated tent for your festival pleasure.

If you’re not a member already, check out the ITM VIP Program for your opportunity to get pampered at We Love… Sounds.
