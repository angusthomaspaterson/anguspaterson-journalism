Chat with Ferry Corsten tonight

Angus Paterson · 16 March 2007 · inthemix

Tonight sees the return of inthemix’s ‘in the spotlight’ series of live webchats, where you get chance to go head to head with some of dance’s biggest celebrities. Tonight we’ve got uplifting trance king Ferry Corsten at 5pm AEDST.

Ferry Corsten – Thu Mar 15th
05.00pm: NSW/ACT/VIC
04.30pm: SA
04.00pm: QLD
02.00pm: WA
06.00am: London
01.00am: New York
10.00pm: Los Angeles (March 16th)

The Dutch DJ/producer has held onto his status as one of uplifting trance’s most revered identities throughout is career – making music for over 15 years and riding the Gatecrasher wave all the way to the top in the late 90s and managing to stay there. Sitting comfortably amongst top tier DJs like Tiesto, Paul Van Dyk and Armin Van Buuren, Ferry Corsten has in recent times prided himself on a new trailblazing direction, fusing elements of electro, tech and breaks, with his renowned style of trance.

Blowing minds throughout March as part of the nationwide Future Music Festival, this will be your chance to ask Ferry why trance continues to excite him so much.

Be there at 5pm AEDST this afternoon, for more info check out www.inthemix.com.au/chat/inthespotlight.
