Erick Morillo talks Sensation

Angus Paterson · 7 November 2008 · inthemix

Without doubt one of the biggest revelations in dance music this year was the news that after years of rumours, Sensation would be coming to Melbourne this NYE. In some respects it’s also been the most controversial: in particular, the lineup announcement for the 40,000+ party was met with a less than enthusiastic response from some quarters, with the dissenting voices insisting it differed sharply from what we’ve come to expect from events of its calibre. One of the big hitters who’ll be taking control at Telstra Dome on NYE is Erick Morillo, and recently the Subliminal Records frontman spoke with ITM about what we can expect when the Sensation brand hits our shores.

“To me, it’s probably one of the best organised events that I’ve ever seen in my life,” Morillo told inthemix. “When it comes to dance festivals, these guys really spend a lot of money on production. It’s not just a tent, or an arena with a bunch of banners. It’s actual production, with fireworks, rockets, people walking around on stilts, lasers… There’s just a lot of spectacle and for me, to be a part of an event like this is an honour because I’ve worked with these guys before, and I’ve seen what they do. It’s truly amazing, it’s eye candy and it’s breathtaking.”

Arguably, it seemed like a punt on the part of the Dutch promoters to enter the Australian scene with such a huge event, when the local market is already well catered for with an impressive number of established and much-loved events. However, Morillo insists that word or mouth will ensure Sensation’s continued success after its debut this NYE. “I’m sure once people come and experience it once, the word will get out. And if they continue to do this year in and year out, it’ll continue to grow in the way that it has in Amsterdam and in other territories. I think everybody is gonna be blown the hell away. They’re gonna be like, ‘what the hell is this? Holy shit..!’”

Whether Sensation live up to the lofty expectations of Australian dance fans or not, New Years Eve will be a massive night for Morillo regardless. After his set at Sensation he’ll be hopping in a Lear Jet to fly to Sydney, driving it home at Pacha at Home Nightclub with a performance he promises will go well into the late hours of the morning.

Stay tuned for ITM’s feature-length interview with Erick Morillo in the lead up to New Years Eve! Also head to our Sensation Festival Page on inthemix for all the latest news updates, interviews, DJ mixes and videos as its hotly anticipated debut approaches.
