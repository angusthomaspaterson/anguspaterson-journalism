MTV Australia goes into Overdrive

Angus Paterson · 8 March 2007 · inthemix

Online content delivery gets a shot in the arm with the launch of the free broadband channel MTV Overdrive. It’s a bold step to take advantage of new media forms and will feature locally produced MTV News, live music, movies, gaming and top rating MTV shows, including the upcoming MTV Australia Video Music Awards.

The launch date is set for April and MTV is pegging the new initiative to more than double online traffic within the next six months with its core market of 16–29 year olds, and it points to the considerable success the format has already received in the US, UK, Canadian and European markets.

Only a fairly standard speed computer is needed to experience everything MTV Overdrive has to offer, and users will need the most recent versions of Microsoft Internet Explorer and Macromedia Flash.

To see what all the fuss is about, check out www.mtv.com.au/overdrive.
