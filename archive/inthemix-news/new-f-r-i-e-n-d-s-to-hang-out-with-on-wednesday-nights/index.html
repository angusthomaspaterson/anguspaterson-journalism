New F.R.I.E.N.D.S to hang out with on Wednesday nights

Angus Paterson · 25 June 2009 · inthemix

Are you a fan of Q Bar weekly Friday night Sosueme, but just wish you had the chance to go out and get hammered in the same fashion on Wednesday nights too? What’s that you say, just can’t wait until the weekend? Well, beyond having a good long think about the life you’re leading, and the choices you’re making (there’s people you can talk to about that kind of stuff), ITM reckons we have the answer. The same team who brought you Sosueme will be bringing you a new Wednesday night party F.R.I.E.N.D.S at Fringe Bar from next week!

The launch party will be hitting Fringe Bar this Wednesday 1st July, with Ghostwood playing live plus DJ performances from Bag Raiders, Joyride and more. Coming weeks will also see performances from Hoodrat, Beni and Ajax (who now has ‘Dance Legend’ after his name for some strange reason, maybe our new F.R.I.E.N.D.S can shed some light on why).

Come and hang with your F.R.I.E.N.D.S at Fringe bar every Wednesday from 9pm, $10 with a free drink upon entry.
