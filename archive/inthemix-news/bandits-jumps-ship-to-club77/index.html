Bandits jumps ship to Club77

Angus Paterson · 23 May 2007 · inthemix

One of Sydney’s most eclectic and interesting electro/indie/rock nights is about to make the big jump from the Phoenix Bar to William Street’s classic venue Club 77. Just in time for its first birthday! The organisers are promising more bands, more DJs and umm… Better toilet facilities!

Check it out when the refreshed bandits kicks off at Club 77 on Friday June 1st.
