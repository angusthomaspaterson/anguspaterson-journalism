ITM's summer hitlist and predictions!

Angus Paterson · 2 September 2009 · inthemix

You have no idea how close we are… It may only be the first week of spring, but precious news on our biggest summer festivals is just around the corner, with the lineups for Summadayze and Field Day both immanent as well as the likes of Big Day Out, Future Music Festival, Good Vibrations and more circling closely on the horizon. Here at ITM, we thought it was time to compile our ‘hitlist and predictions’ for the coming summer, rounding up the artists we already know will be here, and who’s rumored to be making the trek…Along with a healthy dose of speculation!

It’s an impressive list of DJs and live acts, that’s for sure. We’re talking superstars like Groove Armada, Swedish House Mafia, Massive Attack, Sven Vath and even that ol’ heartbreaker Eric Prydz pops his cheeky head in.

It’s set to be a massive summer for sure, so without further ado, take a look at who we know will be here, who’s rumored to be here and who we hope will be here…
