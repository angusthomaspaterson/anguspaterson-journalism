Panther: Schizophrenic and solo

Angus Paterson · 24 July 2007 · inthemix

Based in Portland, Oregon, Panther is something of an anomaly on the US music scene. Even for an underground artist, Panther’s doing things a little different to the norm. He plays live onstage all on his lonesome, looping vocals and effects through a single keyboard. It might sound a little boring and stale, but he describes his sound as “Nina Simone meets Albert Ayler meets Tina Turner meets Dead C”, and in all honesty, anything that schizophrenic has to be good. Touring Australia in August for the first time with Yacht, ITM investigates.

Hi Charlie, where are you at the moment?

Hello, I am in Portland, Oregon, right now, just got back from New York visiting my family before I go on a million tours.

You’ve just released your second album which is called ‘Secret Lawns’.
But for the uninitiated, how would you describe your music?

I would describe it as a marriage between punk, free jazz and disco, not unlike Nina Simone meets Albert Ayler meets Tina Turner meets Dead C.

Are there many acts out there at the moment that you would identify with as sharing a fairly similar musical direction or philosophy?

As of right now, I can’t think of anyone who is trying to meld pop and free music together, but early Royal Trux has been an inspiration for a while now. I’m sure there are bands with a similar philosophy but a different sound.

When did you first start working on the new album, and how difficult was the process of getting it finished and in the can?

I first started last January and worked on it in bits in pieces when I had time. I initially recorded about 40 songs and kind of picked through the ones [I liked]. After deciding on the ones I wanted, I brought a few of the more poppy ones over to my friend E Rocks’ house, and we worked on more traditional structures.

There’s been a fair amount of hype building around you as an act. Tell me about how you got to where you are at the moment?

I was in a dance prog band called the Planet The, and when we broke up I decided to make a full length Panther record. Right after the break up, I have been touring and writing ever since. Panther started out as a response to someone wanting me to be a part of a singer songwriter night, and I thought it would be fun to sabotage it with huge blown out drum machines, synths and me singing and dancing over the top.

You’ve got sort of an indie/electro crossover thing going on in your music. This kind of broad sound has a lot of energy support behind it in the UK and Australia at the moment, but how receptive are people in the US towards it?

People here are generally receptive toward it. I have been lucky that people are into it because Americans find what I am doing a little harder to digest. It’s fun though because I get comments that they have never heard anything like it. I feel like American audiences are more mainstream, but I am hoping things are changing.

So what’s it been like getting your musical career off the ground in the US?

It has been good, I am still new, so I have to work harder. I have recently signed to Kill Rock Stars in the US, and found an amazing booking agent, so things get easier. I am based in Portland and the scene here is by far the most vibrant in the nation, the world is kind of going Portland crazy right now, all eyes on Portland. It’s weird.

You’ve got a tour of Australia coming up. Is this the first time you’ve been to the country?

I have never been and I am really looking forward to it. I also am super stoked that Pop Frenzy released it [‘Secret Lawns’], they are super awesome. I am excited to see the different parts of Australia. Everyone I know who has gone cannot say enough good things!

We know you’re a solo act, but what goes on when you play live? Do you have other people up on the stage with you?

It’s just me dancing and singing and playing a small circuit bent keyboard, I also loop vocals live too. For the next record, which has already been written, it’s me and a drummer. I really want every record to be different.

Panther’s album ‘Secret Lawns’ is out now through Pop Frenzy. He tours the east coast in August with Yacht:

Aug 1 – Brisbane, Alhambra Lounge
Aug 2 – Sydney, Bar Broadway
Aug 3 – Melbourne, Roxanne
