Kyau & Albert: Keeping things Euphonic

Angus Paterson · 2 December 2009 · inthemix

German progressive trance duo Ralph Kyau and Steven Moebius Albert, otherwise known as Kyau & Albert, have been working together for 13 years now, and while a lot of fans will remember classic anthems like Velvet Morning and Falling Anywhere, it’s a reflection of their talent that they keep coming up with new stormers every year. Always a Fool, Hooked on Infinity and their stunning new crunchy tech-trance single I Love You; the consistent thing here is that with every new release, they’ve managed to keep taking their sound forward to the next level.

That’s not to mention their excellent work with the Euphonic record label, described by UK trance trio Above & Beyond as the German equivalent of their Anjunabeats stable. Euphonic Records has been responsible for a constant stream of progressive trance treats over the eyars, the best of which you can hear on their monthly Euphonic Sessions radio show. As a reflection of this, the pair have had a hectic 2009 but somehow they’re managing to squeeze in a special one-off Australian performance on New Years Eve at Heaven on Earth in Adelaide. Here Kyau & Albert* talk to ITM about what they’ve got planned.

You’ve had a pretty big year with some fairly massive tunes, following up from consistent success in the years before that as well. How’s it all been going for you?

Albert: Our booking calender in 2009 was very full. Finally it will be the year with the most shows we ever played in our career. Beside the touring and producing, as always we had a lot to do with Euphonic, plus arranging two Euphonic nights this year.

The consistent thing we’ve been hearing in all of your productions is that while there’s always melody, and it’s quite accessible too, the approach is always very progressive and probably more so with each new single. What’s been driving and inspiring you here?

Albert: With all the new elements we brought into our productions in the past, we sometimes got criticised by a few old fans, who did not really want our musical change at that time or simply did not understand it.
Kyau: That is true. Honestly this hurts a bit sometime. On the other hand people were talking about our track, so we got attention through this and definitively new audiences too. I have the feeling that people are more and more prepared now, when first listening to a new Kyau & Albert production. We just got a lot of very good feedbacks on our new single I Love You.

Some of your classic tracks like Falling Anywhere where a lot more straightforward, you could say. Are you always trying to move forward?
Kyau: Of course we try and I exactly know what you mean with Falling Anywhere, which was the B-side of our single Made Of Sun, a completely different track by the way. Both tracks were already created in mid 2004, a half decade ago. A quite long time in Electronic Dance Music. I definitively think our remix for Cressida’s 6AM was also a kind of straight forward tune, which was uplifting in its own way. Looking back, we just had balls to speed it down BPM-wise. Some artists just use their production template for their whole career, which is indeed effective, but very boring from the artistic view.
Albert: Everybody who was listening to our productions over the last 5 years or so have noticed, that we really try to move forward, but to be realistic, as of course it’s not possible to do this on every new production. It’s more an evolution thing. Sometimes it is just the right combination of different things we recently love. We still have fun and many ideas in our minds. And I like challenges.

You’ve been working together as a duo for quite a while. What is it about the Kyau and Albert partnership that works so well?

Albert: As human beings and individual characters we are quite different, but our view on the music is very similar. Looking back, the very good thing about our business partnership is that most times it was better to have two opinions about one things, before deciding the final way. Therefore it always needs quite long for us to finish a track, but in the end only the final result counts and this has to be perfect.
Kyau: Because we are spending so much time together all over the year, we never really hang out at our free time. Also we have quite different interests outside the music. It is a proper working relationship for over 13 years now. Possibly we will do this until we become pensioners!

Above & Beyond have often described your Euphonic label as the German equivalent of Anjunabeats. Do you agree with this, and what’s your artistic aim with the label?
Kyau: That was nice from the guys. We like them a lot and had a strong working partnership with Anjunabeats back in the days of vinyl releases. We also distributed a lot of Anjunabeats album CDs through our distribution deal with Sony Germany. In return they did some Kyau & Albert singles and distributed our last longplay CD Worldvibe through their channels. Nowadays territory deals only make sense with really commercial music. Especially club music releases have most times global presence and are available digitally worldwide. We are happy, that Above & Beyond recently invited us to one of their TATW 300 radio show celebration events. It will be in Moscow on December 12th. We really look forward to it.
Albert: I think the big difference between both labels is that Anjunabeats’ output in releases is much higher. They separated the styles between Anjunabeats and Anjunadeep to create clearer brands, which was a clever idea.
Kyau: With Euphonic we always tried to have a platform for creative artists, which in best case could also be our friends. When we started Euphonic in 1997, we did releases in different styles, even house, some techno and chillout tracks. Mainly through artists such as Ronski Speed, Mirco de Govia, Sonorous and later Stoneface & Terminal, Euphonic became the well-known Trance brand.

You’re coming to Australia, to play the single gig in Adelaide! How come the rest of the country misses out, and what can we expect from that show?

Kyau: We really look forward to this show and we’re arriving in Adelaide on the 29th. So we have time to relax some days at summer time, will meet some friends there too and finally with me as a home brewer, I want to make a tour at Coopers brewery then. I’ve been promised that we’ll check this out, so fingers crossed.
Albert: It is also the first time we are celebrating New Year’s Eve in the Southern hemisphere. Our whole life we have always been up north for NYE. We’ve always played always in different countries, for example UK, India, China, Germany and Poland. Alongside the long list of acts for Heaven on Earth, there is also Fragma on the lineup. The singer from Fragma, Damae, was already singing in duet with me on the original video version of our single Velvet Morning back in 2003.
Kyau: Yeah, really look forward to seeing Damae again. She is a really lovely person. Except for talking on the phone with her, we did not saw her for quite a long time. Also friends from Sydney are coming to see us. So this will be big fun!

Kyau and Albert play Heaven on Earth at the Royal Adelaide Showgrounds on Thursday 31st of December. Tickets on sale at ITM!
