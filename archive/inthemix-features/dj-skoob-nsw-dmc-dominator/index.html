DJ Skoob: NSW DMC dominator

Angus Paterson · 15 July 2008 · inthemix

There can only be one. And each and every year since 1986, the world’s most talented and technically skilled DJs step up to the plate to try and earn the right to be called ‘the one’, as the winner of the DMC World Championships. With past champions including the likes of Mixmaster Mike, Cash Money, DJ Craze, Q-Bert and on a local level Pee Wee Ferris, John Course, Anthony Pappa, Dexter and more, the competition has certainly yielded its share of elite DJ talent and future favourites.

So ladies and gentlemen, welcome DJ Skoob to the fold. Kicking off his DJ career in 2000 around Newcastle, he was voted that city’s DJ of the year for 2003 and by 2005, had taken third place in the Australian DMC Finals. And then in the following two years, breezing in to take the crown twice in a row at the NSW DMC state finals. Will his domination of the state competition continue this year? ITM talks to Skoob.

Hi there Skoob, this is Angus from inthemix. How are you feeling at the moment, Are you pretty focused at the moment on the lead up to the DMC Championships?

At the moment I have the flu, so I’m feeling pretty bad. In terms of the lead up, I’ve been practicing flat chat for three months straight day and night. I really want to take out the Australian title this year. And the NSW heats as well…

I’m sure the sort of practicing regimes you undertake would rival the dedication required of an Olympic athlete. Tell me a little bit about what goes into the preparation before a competition like the DMC?

That’s funny, but it’s true. I always start with the records I want to use. The ideas stem from that. I then take those records and I go over it, refine it, go over it some more and practice every day and every night. By the time the competition roles around, I have worn all the records out, so I then have to source new ones!

Can you give us a bit of a peek into some surprises we’ll be getting on Thursday night?

No real surprises, but this year I have practiced harder than ever and have put together the best set possible. It’s going to be a hard, loud and aggressive set. I’ve been heavily influenced by the music coming out of Europe, particularly Switzerland. I promise you really loud drums!

You’ve slowly worked your way up to a stage of completely dominating the state DMC champs. Has this opened a lot of doors for you?

For sure. The last two years, there have been heaps of opportunity for work and plenty of doors that have opened, things like stuff at Triple J etc. In 2005 there were no state heats, but I got third in the National final. That’s when things really started kicking off.

You’ve toured with some of the finest names in Aussie hip hop, including The Herd, TZU, Hilltop Hoods, Muph & Plutonic and more. Do you feel like you’re gradually rising to a state of Australian hip hop royalty?

I hope so. Everyone wants to be royalty, don’t they? I’ve quit my job as a journalist and just signed to DJ for the Tongue indefinitely. The money is way too good to quit.

Do you think you have what it takes to compete on an international level?

That is my focus this year. I want to at least put something together that has the ability to get me there. I think after Perplex’s set last year that everyone is going to come with the same idea for their sets. I mean, he beat Snare by 15 points, so I think there will be a lot of very similar sets.

What’s more important for you: technical mixing, or track selection?

They are both equally important. For the NSW event I think I will focus more on track selection, but for the Australian final, I think it’s got to be more focused on the technical stuff. But really, people have got to like what they’re hearing if you want to go well.

You’re also now the live DJ for Elefant Tracks’ The Tongue. Tell me a little bit about how that relationship works.

I meet him around the same time of the DMC comp in 05. I was doing some DJ work for Urthboy’s first solo album. Through that, whenever he came to Newcastle, I always put him up and we just got on really well. I helped out with an Elefant Traks tour last year and when his DJ Diaz quit, it become a permanent fixture. We just work really well together.

Finally, good luck for Thursday night’s gig! Do you have a message for any of your sneaky competitors who might be hoping to rise up and snatch your crown away?

If they think they can do it…they’re going to have to try pretty hard. But good luck to them!

Catch DJ Skoob when the 2008 DMC World Championships ‘NSW Finals’ hits the Gaelic Club this Thursday night from 8pm.
