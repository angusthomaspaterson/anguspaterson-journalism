All About Me

Hi guys,

My name is Angus & I love writing for inthemix as a means of keeping me interested in everything that's going on in the music world out there, and it's got a fantastic community that's involved with the site as well.

I love all forms of contemporary culture, be it books, television, movies - but particularly music. I like to hear a bit of everything (the eclectic playlist of JJJ suits my tastes quite well), but I have a particular passion for music of the electronic persuasion. Be it breaks, house, progressive, trance or something a little more suited to listening outside of the club. Whatever!

I really enjoy writing for inthemix - anyone wanting to provide feedback at the bottom of articles would be much appreciated. Whether you think it's good, bad or whatever, all comments are welcome!

Cheers,
Angus :)
