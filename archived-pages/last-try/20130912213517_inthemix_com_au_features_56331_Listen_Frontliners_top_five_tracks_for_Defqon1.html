Listen: Frontliner’s top five tracks for Defqon.1

Wed 11th Sep, 2013 by Nick Jarvis in Features 2302 views

Dutch hardstyle producer Frontliner is the only person in the world (that we know of) who’s thought to make hardstyle bootlegs of the likes of Rihanna and Macklemore. The man’s sound is versatile, to say the least, and as we announced a couple of weeks ago, Sydney will be lucky enough to see him in the flesh at last (after he was unable to make it out for IQON earlier this year) when he appears as a special guest at Defqon.1 this Saturday. We asked him which five tracks he’ll definitely be bringing to play.

Frontliner feat. John Harris – Halos

“It was a bullseye hit teaming up with John Harris. Excellent singer and writer! He stole my musical heart with his perfect matching vocal on this track, and the energy I get back from the audience while playing this makes us so proud!”

Showtek – FTS

“This track represents hardstyle in its purest form. Without doubt it tops the list of my all-time favourite hardstyle tracks!”

Frontliner – I’m the Melodyman

“A melody from my heart – and it shows a big piece of it. I can’t think of another track like it!”

Noisecontrollers – Pillars of Creation

“The melody and its overall sound hit the exact right spot with me! There won’t be a moment in my life that I can’t hear this track anymore.”

Frontliner – Symbols

“When I drop this track, mayhem and chaos ensue and the whole venue shakes – no more words needed. Music is our weapon!”

Sep 14th
Defqon.1 Australia 2013

Sydney International Regatta Centre
