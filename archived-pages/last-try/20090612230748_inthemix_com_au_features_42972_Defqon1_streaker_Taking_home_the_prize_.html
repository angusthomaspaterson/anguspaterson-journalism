When Ryan from Melbourne streaked onto the field of Melbourne’s hallowed MCG during the Essendon vs Richmond match a few weeks ago, the questions was no longer what people would do to win a trip to Holland to attend the Defqon.1 Festival – it was what wouldn’t they do to win the prize.

But while inthemix has seen some impressive entries into the ‘Defqon.1 Maximum Exposure Competition’, none were quite able to match Ryan’s spectacular effort. He was called this week with the news that he’ll be flying out with a mate to attend the festival in Holland later this month.

Defqon.1 Festival comes to the Sydney International Regatta Centre on Saturday September 19th. Tickets on now from q-dance.com.au, head on over to our Festival Page for more coverage.
