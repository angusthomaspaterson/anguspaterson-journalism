Tours: Tim Rogers, Oscar KeySung, Brant Bjork

Sat 8th Mar, 2014 in Local News

Tim Rogers

It may have been two years ago that You Am I’s Tim Rogers released his last solo album Rogers Sings Rogerstein but are you going to tell him he can’t be touring it now? Christ, no. You’re going to get along, perhaps to one of the many regional dates on the man’s tour running through March and April, and you are going to have yourself a hell of a time. Rogers is touring with guitarist Shane O’Mara, and they’ll be supported by Melody Feder and Michael Muchow.

Rogers Sings Rogerstein tour dates

Thursday, March 20 – Sooki Lounge, Belgrave
Friday, March 21 – Theatre Royal, Castlemaine
Friday, March 28 -Brass Monkey, Cronulla
Sunday, March 30 -The Heritage, Bulli
Friday, April 4 – The Baroque Room, Katoomba
Saturday, April 5 – Lizottes, Dee Why
Sunday, April 6 – Lizottes, Kincumber
Saturday, April 12 – Milton Theatre, Milton
Tuesday, April 15 – Lizottes, Newcastle
Thursday, April 24 – Flinders Social, Townsville
Saturday, April 26 – Railway Club, Darwin

Oscar Key Sung

Oscar Key Sung, the front half of Melbourne duo Oscar & Martin, combines sparse beats and smooth vocals to make indie R&B jams. His debut EP Holograms is out now so he’ll be heading out on a national tour in support of it spread over the next few months. Tickets are on sale now.

Hologram EP tour dates

Friday, March 14 – Trinity Bar, Canberra
Friday, April 11 – Star, Bendigo
Saturday, April 19 – Howler, Melbourne
Thursday, April 24 – Goodgod Small Club, Sydney
Saturday, April 26 – Alhambra, Brisbane
Thursday, May 1 – The Bird, Perth
Saturday, May 3 – Rocket Bar, Adelaide

Brant Bjork

Brant Bjork, founding member of Kyuss and current member of Vista Chino (formerly Kyuss Lives!) has put together a touring band for his solo material. It features Dave Dinsmore (Che, B’last) on bass, Bubba Dupree (Void, Hater) on guitar, and Tont Tornay (Fatso Jetson, Chuck Dukowski) on drums. They’ll all be coming to Australia in May/June for their first tour here, and as well as their own shows they’ll be joining the Meat Puppets to co-headline both the CherryRock014 festival in Sydney and Astor Rocks in Perth.

Brant Bjork tour dates

Friday, May 23 – The Zoo, Brisbane
Saturday, May 24 – Great Northern Hotel, Byron Bay
Wednesday, May 28 – Enigma Bar, Adelaide
Thursday, May 29 – Ding Dong Lounge, Melbourne
Saturday, May 31 – CherryRock014, Sydney
Monday, June 2 – Astor Rocks, Perth
