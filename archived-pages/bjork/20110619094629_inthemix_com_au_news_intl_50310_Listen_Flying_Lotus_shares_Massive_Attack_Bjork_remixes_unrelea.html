Otherworldly electronic adventurer Flying Lotus took to Twitter last night to announce that though he “doesn’t like Soundcloud” he had uploaded a slew of material to the music streaming site.

Among the batch of new, old and in-progress are some real treats for the ears including the Los Angeles’ producer’s typically arresting takes on work from Massive Attack and Bjork.

As well as those mixes and some older reworkings for Mr Oizo there are a few gems from FlyLo’s hard drive like the sprightly glitch of Serum Acquired and the smoothed out Happening Really.

We’ve embedded a few selections for you to lose yourself in below and you can check out more treats from Flying Lotus’ vault here.

Bjork – Pagan Of Poetry (Flying Lotus Remix)

Massive Attack – Girl I Love You (Flying Lotus Remix)

Bilal – Levels (Flying Lotus Remix)
