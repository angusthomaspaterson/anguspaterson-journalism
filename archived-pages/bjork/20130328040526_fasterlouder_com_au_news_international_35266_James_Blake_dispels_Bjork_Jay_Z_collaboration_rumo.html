James Blake dispels Bjork,Jay-Z collaboration rumours

Tue 26th Mar, 2013 in International News

Ahead of the release of his second album Overgrown, James Blake has dispelled rumours he’s collaborated with some of the world’s biggest acts.

In a soon-to-be-published interview with FL, Blake said the likes of Bjork, Drake, Joni Mitchell, Jay-Z and Kanye West will not be appearing on his new album, contrary to an erroneous press release.

“None of those people helped me with the album – Bjork, Drake and Bon Iver. They were just names that for some reason ended up on a biography that got sent out and I’ve got absolutely no idea why,” Blake told FL. “It’s a bit name drop-y and I didn’t intend it to come across like that. That wasn’t my intention.”

While Kanye and Jay-Z might not be on the album, Yeezy is apparently a fan of Blake’s latest single ‘Retrograde’. Blake told FL that although he has never been in the studio with Kanye he did play a few tracks for the hip-hop superstar. “He seemed to like them – that’s about it really,” Blake explained. “He’s been playing ‘Retrograde’ a lot apparently and really likes that.”

The new album does still feature a couple of high-profile guests, including Blake’s collaboration with renowned producer Brian Eno on ‘Digital Lion’ and ‘Take a Fall For Me’, which features a verse from the RZA of Wu-Tang Clan fame. RZA’s contribution reportedly features the line “fish and chips with vinegar, with a cold glass of stout”. Make of that what you will.

Blake has already “leaked” his place on the lineup for this year’s Splendour in the Grass lineup, along with Cold War Kids.

The full interview with Blake will be published on FL soon.
