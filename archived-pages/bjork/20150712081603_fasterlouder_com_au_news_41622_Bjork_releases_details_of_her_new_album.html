Bjork releases details of hernew album

  • 1
Wed 14th Jan, 2015 in News

Bjork has announced details her new album. Titled Vulnicura the followup to 2011’s Biophilia will be released in March and according to a post on the singer’s Facebook wall features the following songs: ‘Tonemilker’, ‘Lionsong’, ‘History of Touches’, ‘Black Lake’, ‘Family’, ‘Notget’, ‘Atom Dance’, ‘Mouth Mantra’, and ‘Quicksand’. Read her full note below.

Long-time Bjork collaborator, Venezuelan producer Arca aka Alejandro Ghersi (Kanye West, FKA Twigs), produced six of the album’s songs and co-wrote two with the singer. John Flyn wrote one, with string arrangements from Bjork. Production credits include Bjork, Arca and Haxan Cloak.

Bjork has just been announced as the headliner for New York’s Governors Ball in June, alongside Drake and The Black Keys.
