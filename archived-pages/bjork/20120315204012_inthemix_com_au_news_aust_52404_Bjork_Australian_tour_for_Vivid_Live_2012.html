Bjork Australian tour for Vivid Live 2012?

About the Author

Tim D

view my other 23 articles

  • 3
  • 2
  • 423

Social

  • Tweet
Mon 12th Mar, 2012 in Local News

Could Australian tour dates be on the cards for Bjork to headline Vivid LIVE 2012 at the Sydney Opera House? If a sneaky Google AdWords campaign is correct, the Icelandic songstress might be bringing her groundbreaking Biophilia project to Sydney as part of the 10 day celebration of ambitious popular music, which runs from 25 May to 11 June 2012.

Google AdWords that pointed towards the Vivid LIVE website appeared yesterday correlated next to search terms on Google, including “Bjork Australian tour 2012”, “Bjork Australia” and “Bjork Vivid”. As of today, those AdWords terms had been switched off.

But it gives us a glimmer of hope that Bjork could be bringing the multimedia tour that the Guardian called “the future of the entire music industry” to Australia. According to her official tour dates, there is a gap between shows at Sonar in Brazil on May 12 and Primavera Sound Festival in Brazil on May 30 where Bjork could squeeze in a few Opera House dates.

Of course, this follows Friday’s rumours that Amon Tobin may have a home on the Vivid bill- but we’ll have to wait until Thursday for the official line-up announcement. Dare we hope?
