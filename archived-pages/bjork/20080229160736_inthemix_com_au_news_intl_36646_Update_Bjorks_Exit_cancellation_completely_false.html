Following our announcement yesterday that Bjork had been dropped from the Exit Festival, a spokesperson from the Novi Sad event has said “the story is totally fabricated”.

NME.com reported that Bjork had been taken off the Exit bill after she dedicated her song Declare Independence to Kosovo during a performance in Japan. Once again, those pesky kids at NME got it wrong. We apologise for following their lead…

“Exit never, nor would ever do anything like that, it goes against their ethos,” Dance Arena organiser Karen Young told us.
