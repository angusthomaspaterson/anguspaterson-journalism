About this archive

This site collects journalism and content work published between 2000 and 2019, most of it about electronic music.

A lot of it no longer exists where it was first published. inthemix, which ran a good deal of this work, stopped operating as a standalone site and folded into Music Junkee. 3D World ceased publication. DJ Broadcast went offline, and its articles with it. angusthomaspaterson.com, which had collected the work since 2011, lapsed when its hosting expired in 2024.

This is that site rebuilt from a database backup in 2026, kept as close to the original as the surviving material allows. The layout, the categories and the URLs are the ones it used, including a few category names that never quite matched their addresses.

Some photographs are gone for good. Where an image was hosted by a publication that has since closed, and no copy survives in the Internet Archive, the page says so rather than showing a broken image. Everything the site itself hosted has been recovered.

It is kept here for the record.
